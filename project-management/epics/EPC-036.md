# EPC-036: Project-declared enforcement

## Summary

Adopters need outward commands and enforcement that the shipped presets do not cover. This epic lets a project declare them beside the presets. It is one of the feature epics the operator approved on 2026-11-03, when the 4.0.0 milestone epic (EPC-022) and the 4.1.0 epic (EPC-023) were regrouped by outcome. Planned version: 4.1.0. Why that version: Operator 2026-10-18 (EPC-023 features).

## Outcome

After this merges, a project can declare its own outward commands and enforcement beside the shipped presets.
