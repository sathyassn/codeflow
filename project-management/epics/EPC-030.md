# EPC-030: Baseline replacement and read-only update

## Summary

3.x adopters carry a baseline directory and an update that writes as it previews. This epic replaces the baseline with the binary's own bases and makes the preview read-only. It is one of the feature epics the operator approved on 2026-11-03, when the 4.0.0 milestone epic (EPC-022) and the 4.1.0 epic (EPC-023) were regrouped by outcome. Planned version: 4.0.0. Why that version: Operator 2026-10-28 20:15: the line ships as 4.0.0 absorbing baseline replacement and read-only update.

## Outcome

After this merges, an adopter on 3.x can move to 4.0.0 with one update that merges against the binary's bases, previews read-only and leaves no baseline directory.
