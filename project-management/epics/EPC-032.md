# EPC-032: Seats run autonomously under their sandboxes

## Summary

Peer and Claude seats stop on prompts or need an unsandboxed retry for routine read-only and build work. This epic lets them run under their sandboxes, each reaching only its own sockets. It is one of the feature epics the operator approved on 2026-11-03, when the 4.0.0 milestone epic (EPC-022) and the 4.1.0 epic (EPC-023) were regrouped by outcome. Planned version: 4.0.0. Why that version: Operator 2026-11-02 d12 (harness autonomy, loopback) and 2026-10-31 hook-trust rule; TSK-1070 moved here from Baseline.

## Outcome

After this merges, an operator can run peer and Claude seats through their read-only and build work without prompts, each seat reaching only its own sockets, with no unsandboxed retry.
