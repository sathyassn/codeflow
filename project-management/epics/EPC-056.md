# EPC-056: Planning derives the epic graph and states what each landing leaves on main

## Summary

Epics depend on each other only through task edges that nobody sees as an epic graph: in CodeFlow's records 208 of 681 task dependencies cross an epic, and one circle of two epics is live. This epic derives the epic graph from task dependencies, refuses a new circle, shows a task waiting for another epic's work on the default branch, reports open epics for re-cutting, and makes every new landing state what it leaves on `main` (ADR-0102).

## Outcome

After this merges, a planner can see which epics wait on which, is refused a new dependency circle between epics, sees in `work next` which task waits for another epic's work on the default branch, and records for every landing what becomes reachable, what stays inert and what other lines must do.

## Deliverables

- the task records below: `project-management/tasks/`
- the derived epic graph, explicit links and circle refusal: `crates/codeflow-core/src/validate/docs.rs` and the planning-range check in `codeflow ci`
- the cross-epic wait: `crates/codeflow-core/src/workgraph/work_start.rs` and `crates/codeflow-core/src/workgraph/readiness.rs`
- the epic report: a new read-only command (proposed `codeflow epic report`; its builder settles the name)
- the landing answers, the size rule and the marker text: `project-management/templates/`, `assets/base/pm/`, `assets/base/rule-map.toml`, the AGENTS templates, and the cf-plan and cf-method skills
- release notes: `changelog.d/`

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-1 (journey) With the built binary, a planted circle shall be refused; `work next` shall name a cross-epic wait; and the report on CodeFlow's records pinned at `f8aabc62c` shall list the EPC-027 and EPC-028 circle and the records fixes; evidence: the command output.
- AC-2 A fresh `codeflow task new` and `codeflow epic new` shall carry the Landing section and the marker documentation; evidence: the command output.
- AC-3 The epic shall close only when every task is complete, computed by `codeflow epic status`, its criteria are as planned or amended by a recorded planning amendment, and one whole-epic cross-lineage review names the `main` commit that holds its last task; evidence: `codeflow epic status` and the review row.
