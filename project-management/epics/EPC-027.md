# EPC-027: CI verdicts carry trusted policy authority

## Summary

The CI and push gates read their policy from a requester-controlled head in several places. This epic makes every verdict, merge admission and authority query rest on the default branch's policy at one SHA. It is one of the feature epics the operator approved on 2026-11-03, when the 4.0.0 milestone epic (EPC-022) and the 4.1.0 epic (EPC-023) were regrouped by outcome. Planned version: 4.0.0. Why that version: Operator 2026-10-27 merge admission option A and 2026-11-02 19:35 CI decisions; security gaps ship in 4.0.0 (2026-10-18).

## Outcome

After this merges, a maintainer can trust that every hosted verdict rests on the default branch's policy and test config at one SHA resolved from the base ref's current tip, and that merge admission, the secret scan, the destination lookup and the CI note use only that trusted route.

- Amended 2026-11-04 by the stricter consolidation pass: the local hook, push and remote tasks moved to EPC-054, and TSK-1039 to EPC-048. Earlier outcome: "a maintainer can trust that every hosted and push-time verdict rests on the default branch's policy at one SHA, and that merge admission, authority queries and the CI note use only that trusted route."
