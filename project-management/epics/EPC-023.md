# EPC-023: the 3.2.0 adopter features

## Summary

An adopter running many integration lines asked for nine capabilities
(issues 164, 166, 167 beyond its binding defect, 168, 169, 170, 171, 172
and 174). This epic delivers them in 3.2.0, as the operator directed on
2026-10-13: a generated program report over every fetched line, a
project-declared class and lane for tasks, record-hygiene reports, a
reworded planning rule with scope-drift reporting, a checked carry of a
landed change to another line and a proven cleanup of pushed branches, a
managed bootstrap that installs the pinned binary on a fresh clone,
project-declared outward commands, a person-applied route for enforcement
changes an agent proposes, and blocked names and protected paths at any
depth. Nothing a project or agent writes narrows the shipped floor.

It serves adopters and contributors who run several lines, onboard new
clones, or need project-specific enforcement. Done means each task below
has landed on `integration/EPC-023-the-3-2-0-adopter-features` with its
cross-lineage review, the integrated body passes the full gate and the
journey below on the installed candidate, and its pull request to `main`
is ready for a human merge.

Source: the adopter feature design, revision 6, written by Claude and Codex
from independent discovery, reviewed in six rounds by Codex and Grok until
both approved, and approved by the operator with all 17 decisions as
recommended on 2026-10-13
(`.worktrees/triage-next-release/target/adopter-design-merged-06.md` in
the codeflow checkout, outside the repository; workspace `RELEASE-PLAN.md`,
"After 3.1.1 (triage of 2026-10-13)"). The task records carry the decided
specifics, so a builder needs no other source.

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-1 `codeflow report program` shall show every epic and task on every
  fetched ref with its home, agreement, currency, placement and derived
  readiness, and each epic's estimate against the actual where outcomes
  exist, with explicit incomplete-data fields otherwise, choosing
  authority only from the trusted remote's lines, and `status --json` shall
  share its versioned fact model; evidence: TSK-311 and TSK-312.
- AC-2 A project shall declare classes and lanes in
  `project-management/vocabulary.json`, tasks shall carry them, every view
  shall group by them, and a class shall change no verdict; evidence:
  TSK-313 and TSK-314.
- AC-3 A record off its target, a status written off its home, a
  completion on no line and a prose wait shall each be reported, never
  above warn by default, with the guidance written once; evidence: TSK-315.
- AC-4 The planning rule shall read as operator decision 1 approved it,
  and `ci` shall report scope drift against a task's Deliverables and, when
  a project opts in, citations for authority paths, never blocking by
  default; evidence: TSK-316 and TSK-317.
- AC-5 A landed task shall reach another line as one checked commit whose
  bytes equal the reviewed delta, and a pushed branch shall be deleted only
  on a recorded-tip proof by a compare-and-swap; evidence: TSK-318 and
  TSK-319.
- AC-6 A project shall declare its own outward commands, with an
  unproven-match refusal and an opt-in infrastructure preset; evidence:
  TSK-320.
- AC-7 A person shall apply an agent's proposed enforcement change by
  digest, rendering into the native rule files shall only add, and the
  cooperating writers shall never lose each other's work; evidence:
  TSK-321.
- AC-8 Blocked names and protected prefixes shall be refused at any depth
  before commit and at merge time, and ignored or oversized files when a
  project opts in; evidence: TSK-322 and TSK-323.
- AC-9 A fresh clone shall install the pinned binary verified by digest
  with one managed script, never downgrade the shared binary, and wire the
  hooks; evidence: TSK-324.
- AC-10 The policy workflow, the CI setup hook, the scanner files and the
  bootstrap scripts shall be in-session edit denies; evidence: TSK-325.
- AC-11 Nothing a project or agent writes shall narrow the shipped floor:
  `policy apply` and `policy render`, the head-versus-head render check,
  the script and shim byte comparison and in-session hook-binary selection
  shall be fixed refusals beside `security.dangerous_commands`, and the
  path gate shall never judge its own inputs; evidence: TSK-320, TSK-321,
  TSK-322, TSK-324 and TSK-325.
- AC-12 (journey) On the installed combined candidate, a fresh clone of a
  sample adopter shall bootstrap, set up and pass the full gate; the
  program report shall match a planted multi-line fixture; a carry shall be
  admitted and land; a proposed enforcement change shall be applied by a
  person and refused to an agent; and the path gate shall refuse a nested
  secret name; evidence: the final integration run, with every task
  reviewed by the other lineage, and the pull request to `main` prepared
  for a human merge.
