# EPC-020: delivery system for 3.0.0

## Summary

The delivery audit of 2026-09-26 found that CodeFlow's own work records drift
from the work: ids issued as max+1 over one checkout collide across lines,
status words are hand-edited and stale, `status --delivery` ignores
dependencies and standalone tasks, blocked and complete records need no
content, criteria can pass while the result misses (TSK-066: green in the
repository, 773 findings on a fresh scaffold), main was left with invalid
release state twice, and adopters meet failing bot PRs and ignored templates.
This epic delivers the Plan v4 answer for 3.0.0: a shared id registry on a
protected data branch, recorded facts with status verbs and derived views,
one readiness predicate, classification of every product pull request,
acceptance bound to the reviewed commit, release-state checks before merge,
adopter fit, and proof by deterministic journeys and native behavioural
evals.

It serves every agent and human who plans, claims, reviews or releases work
in a repository that tracks work with CodeFlow, CodeFlow itself first. Done
means every task below has landed on `integration/EPC-020-delivery-system`
with its cross-lineage review, the aggregate journey gate and the live eval
pack pass on the installed combined candidate, and the integrated body is
prepared for a human to merge into `main`.

Source: Plan v4, `design/delivery-system/design-delivery-system-v4.md` in the
agentic-systems workspace, reviewed by Fable 5.1, a Codex GPT-6 seat and
Grok 4.7. Operator decisions of 2026-09-26: 1a (registry branch), 2a
(advisory claims), 3a (keep the split and retire the diagram input through
TSK-114), 4a (change fragments in 3.1, on EPC-021), 5a (36 live sessions);
decision 6 (the EPC-015 line) is taken later. SPC-013 pins the contract and
must be approved by both primary seats before any task starts.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] AC-1 When any record is added in a pull request, CI shall block the
      merge until its number is reserved in the authority's
      `codeflow/registry` history and bound to the record's `uid`; issue is
      unique while the authority is reachable, and a deleted reservation is
      never reissued; evidence: TSK-100 on a real host and the TSK-101
      journeys.
- [ ] AC-2 When a task, epic or spec changes state, the change shall go
      through a status verb or a hand edit judged by the same validator,
      blocked shall carry a reason, owner and revisit event, and active,
      ready, waiting, landed and epic progress shall be derived, never
      stored; evidence: TSK-102 and TSK-103 transition journeys.
- [ ] AC-3 `work next`, `work start`, `status` and CI shall share one
      readiness predicate that includes standalone tasks and cross-line
      dependencies; evidence: TSK-103.
- [ ] AC-4 When a product pull request carries no work classification, CI
      shall block it, and the anchored preflight shall run for tracked work
      on any branch; evidence: TSK-104.
- [ ] AC-5 When a task completes, CI shall verify its acceptance block
      against the reviewed commit, its criteria against the target, and a
      journey criterion for adopter-facing ranges; evidence: TSK-105.
- [ ] AC-6 Release state shall be checked before push and on integration
      lines, a stale base shall be repairable by a typed repair, and the
      3.0.0 notes shall be rendered and reviewed on the final assembled
      source; evidence: TSK-106.
- [ ] AC-7 (journey) When an adopter installs or updates CodeFlow, trusted
      automation and release-tool pull requests shall pass, a kept brownfield
      pull request template shall follow the four mapping states, project
      record templates shall be used, and no release calculator shall be
      imposed; evidence: TSK-107 journeys on fresh init and update.
- [ ] AC-8 The skills describe one lifecycle that matches the shipped verbs,
      within the byte budgets; evidence: TSK-108.
- [ ] AC-9 On this line, carrying `main`'s records, `validate --docs` shall
      be clean apart from the recorded baseline, `status` shall show no
      stale flags, and `doctor` shall reconcile the registry with the
      inventory; the cleanup of the EPC-016 line, the EPC-018 line and the
      backlog plan is held by the follow-up records TSK-124, TSK-125 and
      TSK-126, each with an owner, its line as target and the line's next
      sync from `main` as trigger; evidence: TSK-109 (SPC-013 R-102).
- [ ] AC-10 (journey) The installed combined candidate shall pass the
      aggregate deterministic journey gate with a recorded benchmark
      baseline, and the live eval pack of six cases, three trials each on
      both primaries (36 native sessions), by the pass bar of SPC-013 R-116
      on one pinned candidate; evidence: TSK-110, TSK-111 (grader and
      fixtures) and TSK-116 (the sessions).
- [ ] AC-11 No schema published with 3.0.0 shall accept the present
      `diagram` input, and stored revisions that hold one stay readable;
      evidence: TSK-114.
- [ ] AC-12 The integrated body passes the full local gate, has cross-lineage
      review of every task and review of the combined diff by both primary
      seats, and its pull request to `main` is prepared for a human merge;
      evidence: the final integration review and the pull request.
