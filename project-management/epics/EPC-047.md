# EPC-047: The gate gives its real verdict on any host

## Summary

A loaded runner, a denied network, a browser that cannot launch on this host or a hook binary older than the repository can each turn a correct change red or slow, and CodeFlow's limits are declared in many places. It was split out of EPC-028 on 2026-11-04 by the stricter consolidation pass (the operator's rule, relayed by the Director at 21:5x: epics of "around 5 - 10" tasks that reach `main` "in very few days", with "medium to hard" tasks). Planned version: 4.0.0, the version of EPC-028, whose placement reasons carry over.

Value line: a builder trusts a red gate to mean the change is wrong, on any host, because load, the network, a host browser fault and an old binary no longer redden it, and every limit sits in one registry.

## Outcome

After this merges, a builder gets the gate's real verdict, quickly, on a loaded, offline or older host, with local coverage run as shards, with no false timeout, flake or stale-binary finding, and finds every limit declared once.
