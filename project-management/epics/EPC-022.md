# EPC-022: the 3.2.0 milestone fixes

## Summary

The 3.2.0 release plan addresses issues filed by this repository and
by adopters. The original 2026-10-12 scope covered 26 issues against
CodeFlow 3.0.0 and 3.1.0. The combined scope covers `codeflow ci`
verdicts that depend on how a range is spelled, planning and exemption pull
requests that cannot be classified, guards that miss commands, writers and
history reads that can be redirected in a local clone, CodeFlow's own pushes
using a personal credential, managed CI steps an adopter must edit by hand,
a landing mode, tests that touch an enclosing repository, and one
post-release evaluation. This epic fixes or settles each of them for the
3.2.0 release.

It serves every adopter and contributor who runs `codeflow ci`, the hooks,
the guards and the managed CI. Done means each task below has landed on
`integration/EPC-022-the-3-2-0-milestone-fixes` with its cross-lineage
review, the integrated body passes the full gate and the journey below on
the installed candidate, and its pull request to `main` is ready for a
human merge.

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-1 When `codeflow ci` judges a range, its verdict shall not depend on
  how the base is spelled or where the command runs, and a planning
  amendment that updates the document index or an exemption-only pull
  request into the base shall each get a class; evidence: TSK-275, TSK-276
  and TSK-277.
- AC-2 When a closed record holds text a project must remove, a reviewed
  record-only change shall replace it while every result, the reviewed
  commit and the review provenance stay, and the acceptance waiver shall be
  documented; evidence: TSK-278.
- AC-3 Before a push, an author shall get the release-impact verdict hosted
  CI gives, and a new policy key shall follow one written placement rule
  that a check holds; evidence: TSK-279 and TSK-280.
- AC-4 No CodeFlow writer or judging history read shall be redirected by a
  link at a temporary name, a graft, a replace ref or a shallow boundary in
  a local clone; evidence: TSK-281 and TSK-287.
- AC-5 The guards shall judge what a command will actually do: git's
  effective configuration, files that run code in a later shell or login,
  package-manager script shells, and deletion inputs by each native tool's
  grammar. They shall refuse the alias environment of issue 98, the PATH,
  `environment.d` and login-item writes, the package-manager script-shell
  settings and the deletion bypasses. A write to a file that git's
  configuration includes follows TSK-283 AC-4: the write is refused, or
  `codeflow doctor` reports the writable include and what the guard cannot
  see; evidence: TSK-283, TSK-284, TSK-285 and TSK-286.
- AC-6 The test suite shall never write outside its own temporary folders
  or hang on a small pipe buffer, whatever `TMPDIR` is; evidence: TSK-282.
- AC-9 When a project configures a non-personal credential, every push
  CodeFlow makes for it shall use that credential and never the ambient
  one, and the setting shall be one a pull request cannot change for its
  own pushes; evidence: TSK-291.
- AC-10 A project shall refuse inline `gitleaks:allow` and route
  dependency-audit exceptions through its own reviewed check, both read
  from the trusted commit, with no local edit to the managed workflow;
  evidence: TSK-292 and TSK-294.
- AC-11 `codeflow integrate` shall land a reviewed head with a
  no-fast-forward merge on request, keeping the reviewed commit and its
  acceptance binding unchanged; evidence: TSK-293.
- AC-12 (journey) On the installed combined candidate, a fresh
  `codeflow init --standard` project and a project updated from 3.1.0 shall
  pass the full gate, the guards shall refuse each planted case these tasks
  add except the include-file case, which the journey plants and closes the
  way TSK-283 chose (refused, or reported by doctor with what the guard
  cannot see), a configured push credential shall carry `codeflow task new`'s
  reservation, and `codeflow ci` shall give one verdict for a sync merge
  spelled both ways; evidence: the final integration run, with every task
  reviewed by the other lineage, and the pull request to `main` prepared
  for a human merge.
- AC-14 When `git.test_gate_on_push` is `block`, a pushed ref whose tree
  the hook cannot check (dirty tracked files, a ref other than `HEAD`,
  several refs, a sparse or incomplete checkout, a submodule mismatch)
  shall be checked on the pushed commit or refused, never passed with a
  note; evidence: TSK-304.
- AC-15 When the destination does not answer the default-branch query,
  the run shall still refuse, under one rule that names the cause and the
  network, never under a criteria or records rule; evidence: TSK-305.
- AC-16 When a line replaced a task version that `main` inherited from it
  unchanged, the line's version shall be the criteria authority, while
  concurrent amendments, reverts, merge resolutions and unreadable history
  still give no answer; evidence: TSK-306.
- AC-17 A direct commit on an epic line that a landed `line_adoptions`
  entry adopts shall not refuse the acceptance binding of tasks landed
  before it, and a forged or unlanded entry shall still refuse; evidence:
  TSK-308.
- AC-18 An acceptance file without its top-level key shall be refused by
  the file's name, not the record's; evidence: TSK-307.
- AC-19 `codeflow update` run by a binary older than the project's pin
  shall refuse and change nothing; evidence: TSK-309.
- AC-21 No scheduled or per-landing workflow of this repository shall
  target a release branch that does not exist or that no open
  release-integration task owns; evidence: TSK-310.
- AC-22 (journey) On the installed combined candidate, a dirty-tree push
  of a commit that fails the push set shall be refused, a task branch on a
  line that replaced `main`'s inherited task version shall push cleanly,
  and an adopted direct commit shall leave earlier bindings intact;
  evidence: the journeys of TSK-304, TSK-306 and TSK-308 rerun in the
  final integration run.
- AC-23 When a git ref, remote, environment assignment or program in a
  command comes from a variable assigned from data or from `xargs`,
  git-guard and exec-guard shall judge it as unresolved and refuse it; a
  ref built with bracket arithmetic shall be resolved as the shell will and
  judged against policy by git-guard, so a protected ref is refused and an
  unprotected one passes; evidence: TSK-326.
- AC-24 The pre-push destination query shall not be redirected by on-disk
  URL rewriting, nor read its range base or ssh transport from state the
  hook did not choose, and a setting it cannot isolate shall be handled as
  an unchecked destination; evidence: TSK-327.
- AC-25 The presentation close and show tests that scan other processes
  shall pass reliably under host load, with the cause of the failure of the
  TSK-283 gate run found; evidence: TSK-328.
- AC-26 (journey) The `model-eval-kit` gate target shall succeed in each
  supported gate mode, with only its declared prerequisites and from an
  empty artifact directory, whatever order the scheduler picks, and a gate
  target that runs a binary under `target/` shall declare the target that
  produces it; evidence: TSK-329.
- AC-27 While the index holds unmerged entries for `.codeflow/project.toml`
  or `.codeflow/policy.json`, a hook's otherwise failing local read of that
  file shall be replaced by the copy committed at `HEAD`, with authority
  selection, the stricter composition with fetched lines, protected refs,
  root-branch selection and levels unchanged, so the commands that resolve
  or abandon the conflict get the ordinary effective-policy verdict and no
  guard is weaker; an unreadable file with no unmerged entry, or an
  unreadable authority, still refuses and never falls back to defaults;
  evidence: TSK-330.
- AC-28 On one machine, an essential or full `codeflow test` run shall
  never overlap another essential or full run, and the second shall refuse
  naming the holder and its mode, while quick runs and the pre-push gate
  take no lock; evidence: TSK-331.
- AC-29 A docs portal workflow lease whose workflow on the same host has
  stopped (its coordinating process and every writer it started) shall be
  reclaimed at once with a message; a live coordinator or a live writer
  shall still block, including a writer that outlives a killed
  coordinator, and a lease whose coordinator or writers cannot be checked
  shall keep the four-hour age rule; evidence: TSK-332.
- AC-61 Every documented policy key shall have a reader or be reported as
  unsupported, and `security.script_bypass`, `security.sandbox_retry` and
  `security.sandbox_retry_allow` shall no longer be shipped or documented
  as controls; evidence: TSK-363.
- AC-62 A substituted hook binary that answers the capability handshake
  shall be refused at every hook entry point with the repository
  unchanged, and integrate-token and human-override use shall be recorded;
  evidence: TSK-364, building on TSK-324's in-session refusal.
- AC-68 (journey) On the final candidate, an impostor hook binary shall
  be refused with a staged synthetic secret and leave the repository
  unchanged; evidence: the final integration run.
- AC-30 Every comparison of versions and every writer of a pin shall use
  one parser, and no caller shall order an unparseable version as a
  string; evidence: TSK-333.
- AC-48 When a gate run on a task branch names no base, it shall select
  against the merge base with the task's declared line and skip a target
  only when it is `content` class and recorded evidence covers its exact
  command, tools, platform, relevant environment, membership, producer
  outputs and unchanged inputs, falling back to running the target, with
  the reason named, whenever any of that is unproven; a landing candidate's
  gate shall always execute every target; evidence: TSK-352.
- AC-49 This repository's gate targets shall declare the inputs they read,
  so a records-only or docs-only change runs only the checks that read
  those paths, while hook, guard, policy, scaffold and Rust changes run
  every relevant check; evidence: TSK-353's path-to-target matrix.
- AC-50 Essential Rust verification shall run under nextest with explicit
  doctests and the same test membership as `cargo test --workspace`;
  evidence: TSK-354.
- AC-51 A completed target execution whose evidence matches the current
  check shall be reused and name the run that executed it, while
  history-dependent checks and every pre-push protection run live, and a
  run with any reused target never counts as a complete gate; evidence:
  TSK-355.
- AC-52 The full gate shall keep every test case while repeated identical
  fixture work runs once and exhaustive matrices are assigned to it
  explicitly, and its serial-group time shall drop; evidence: TSK-356.
- AC-53 Heavy targets inside one gate shall share one machine worker
  budget, this repository's Cargo producers shall not wait on each other's
  build directory lock, and heavy work started elsewhere through CodeFlow
  (heavy quick targets, the quick-to-essential fallback and the sanctioned
  wrapper for direct heavy commands) shall wait or refuse while a landing
  gate holds the machine; evidence: TSK-331 AC-2, AC-8, AC-9 and AC-10.
- AC-54 This repository's Rust build profiles and test layout shall cut
  target disk use and build or test time without a new tool, each change
  kept only when its measurement meets its stated criterion, and the
  compiler cache verdict shall be recorded with its revisit event;
  evidence: TSK-357.
- AC-55 A pull request into an integration line shall get hosted Linux and
  Windows verdicts before it lands once it is ready for review, a draft
  shall not start those jobs, and ordinary landings on a line shall wait
  until its current tip has green hosted verdicts, with only the repair and
  evidence-producing work allowed to land meanwhile; once the line is
  green, a ruleset shall require both hosted checks on `integration/*`;
  evidence: TSK-358 and TSK-360.
- AC-56 A push shall reuse a hosted verdict only from the run that tested
  the exact merged pull request into the exact destination, on the pushed
  tree, under the same workflow content and job contract, with the secret
  scan, canary and security review still live, or the work shall record
  that no such pair occurs; evidence: TSK-359.
- AC-57 Builders shall cite targeted tests and the quick gate instead of
  routine whole essential or full gates, one full gate shall run on each
  landing candidate, and both this repository's contract and the shipped
  pipeline default shall say so; evidence: TSK-360 and TSK-370.
- AC-58 (journey) After TSK-352 to TSK-360, TSK-370 and the amended TSK-331
  land, a records-only task branch cut from the line shall push with a
  quick gate that runs only the checks that read records and reuses
  unchanged results, its ready pull request shall get green hosted Linux
  and Windows verdicts before landing, the landing push shall reuse that
  verdict where the tree is identical, and the landing candidate's full
  gate shall still run every target; the elapsed times of the quick run,
  the full gate and the hosted runs shall be recorded before and after;
  evidence: one recorded run of that path on the line.
- AC-31 Supported cloud and registry credential stores, helper output and raw credential variables shall be denied in the supported lanes while credentialless tools remain usable. Evidence: TSK-334's synthetic fixtures and fresh/update native journeys.
- AC-32 Bare, relative and absolute hard-secret paths shall receive equivalent shell verdicts, with documented carve-outs preserved. Evidence: TSK-335's matcher matrix and installed hook journey.
- AC-33 Only trusted authority and the full required contract shall emit the required hosted policy verdict; shipped checkouts shall leave no persisted token available to later repository code. Evidence: TSK-336's actual GitHub check-state and fresh/update workflow journeys.
- AC-34 Prescribed Codex launches shall reach an authenticated prompt with the intended effective guard or builder boundary on the supported release. Evidence: TSK-337's native startup and permission canaries.
- AC-35 An existing repository whose main never contained CodeFlow policy shall adopt through a guarded task PR, push and completion. Removed, unreadable and unverifiable policy histories shall remain refused. Evidence: TSK-338's adoption journey and negative controls.
- AC-36 Imported unchanged completions shall retain their proven home landing and accepted criteria, while receiving-line changes remain accountable. Evidence: TSK-339's main, sibling-line and task-branch matrix and tamper controls.
- AC-37 Generated blocker changes, repeated task completion and subsequent epic Closeout shall have an ordinary command sequence accepted locally and for the final range. Evidence: TSK-340's repeated-closeout journey, provenance and tamper controls.
- AC-38 Claim, start, push and CI shall agree on reviewed predecessor chains, validating every supported link or refusing unsupported depth at claim time. Evidence: TSK-341's two-deep chain and broken-link journey.
- AC-39 Writer output options and nested sed execution shall refuse protected writes while ordinary uses remain allowed. Evidence: TSK-342's program sweep and installed hook journey.
- AC-40 Reference macOS guard fuzz and coverage runs shall meet their timing criteria without weakening refusal coverage or ordinary-command latency. Evidence: TSK-343's comparable timings and coverage journey.
- AC-41 Native Windows evidence shall settle Cygwin/MSYS2 startup-path handling, preserve Git Bash protection and state unsupported cases accurately. Evidence: TSK-345.
- AC-45 Scanning with a root HEAD filename shall still detect planted findings, and legacy remerge fingerprints shall receive a reviewable migration candidate without silently changing exemptions. Evidence: amended TSK-292 AC-3 and AC-6.
- AC-46 Desktop parallel work shall use separate app-managed sessions, with context-correct worktree guidance and qualified durable local-evidence access that preserves session isolation. Evidence: TSK-349's native fresh/update journey, nested-worktree and cleanup controls.
- AC-47 Own-task criteria classification shall depend on the judged head and explicit authorities, with the same verdict from main, detached and task checkouts. Checkout-only records shall grant no authority. Evidence: TSK-350's classed PR, push and negative-control journeys after TSK-275 lands.
- AC-69 Blocked names and protected prefixes shall be refused at any depth
  before commit and at merge time, and ignored or oversized files when a

- AC-73 (journey) On the installed combined candidate, a fresh clone of a

- AC-74 When a script runs through `deno`, `bun`, `tsx`, `ts-node` or `uv`
