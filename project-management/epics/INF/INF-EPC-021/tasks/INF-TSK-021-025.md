   - `git rm` for all 7 pre-tool-use hooks, 5 post-tool-use hooks, 1 task-completed hook, 2 stop hooks, 2 user-prompt-submit hooks
3. Delete retired production scripts:
   - State scripts: `ledger.sh` (408), `cf-pathflow-state.sh` (678), `cf-work-state.sh` (216), `memory.sh` (195), `cf-tracking-generate.sh` (267)
