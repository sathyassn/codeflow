# EPC-034: Present and docs portal design

## Summary

cf-present and the docs portal are text-heavy and styled from separate sources. This epic makes them figure-led, branded from one token source, with a product site mode. It is one of the feature epics the operator approved on 2026-11-03, when the 4.0.0 milestone epic (EPC-022) and the 4.1.0 epic (EPC-023) were regrouped by outcome. Planned version: 4.0.0. Why that version: Operator 2026-11-02 d1 to d12 (product site, product docs mode, brand design system).

## Outcome

After this merges, a reader gets cf-present pages and a docs portal that are figure-led and branded from one token source, with a product site mode.
