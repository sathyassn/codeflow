# EPC-044: Estimation method and outcomes

## Summary

Estimates name hours by hand and no record compares them with what happened. This epic ships the estimation method in the references, templates and plan flow, turns on CodeFlow's time-box profile from issue 334, and makes `codeflow estimate outcomes` report each task against its frozen band and true clock. It was split from EPC-035 on 2026-11-04 by the second consolidation amendment, because estimation is a separate outcome from status reporting: a planner adopts the method and reads outcomes without any status view, its proof is the estimate checker and outcome fixtures, and it waits on no status task, so it can reach main on its own. Planned version: 4.0.0.

## Outcome

After this merges, a planner writes bands from the shipped method, and `codeflow estimate outcomes` reports each task against its frozen band and clock. Its tasks carry rubric grades (`codeflow-agentic-2`); only tasks created on or after profile v1's effective date (2026-11-04) carry a band, and older tasks are never back-filled.
