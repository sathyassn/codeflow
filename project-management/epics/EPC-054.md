# EPC-054: Local hooks and pushes trust only the landed policy and the real remote

## Summary

Local hooks can scan at a lower level than the landed policy, the pre-push destination query reads local configuration and transport, a rewritten remote can hide the real one, CodeFlow's own pushes use a personal credential, and remote protect can change rules it does not own. It was split out of EPC-027 on 2026-11-04 by the stricter consolidation pass (the operator's rule, relayed by the Director at 21:5x: epics of "around 5 - 10" tasks that reach `main` "in very few days", with "medium to hard" tasks). Planned version: 4.0.0, the version of EPC-027, whose placement reasons carry over.

Value line: an operator knows the local plane cannot be weakened by local configuration: hooks use the landed policy, pushes ask the real remote, and CodeFlow's own credentials and rules stay its own.

## Outcome

After this merges, an operator can rely on local hooks and pushes judging at the landed policy's level against the real remote, with CodeFlow's own pushes on a non-personal credential and remote protect touching only its own rules.
