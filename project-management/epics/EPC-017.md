# EPC-017: operating doctrine follow-through

## Summary

Five behaviours recur in agent sessions on this repository and the doctrine
does not prevent any of them: after opening a pull request the agent does not
watch its checks, repair red ones or report readiness unless asked; chat
replies and written artifacts use mannered prose (dashes as drama, slogans,
rhetorical framing, paragraph walls) where short plain prose with bullets and
tables was wanted; a flow or relationship is explained in prose when a figure
would carry it, and the review surface is neither offered nor opened when a
substantial comparison would be clearer there; pull requests, portals and
pages are mentioned without the link the tool printed; and record identifiers
or acronyms stand as titles. Independent discovery by both families found the
same shape: merge law exists but no post-open loop; the editorial skill
governs substantial prose but excludes ordinary chat and protects the em dash
as a false positive under ADR-0032; the figure duty exists but its case can
pass on recognition without a figure; no rule asks for a URL; titles are
governed against slogans but not against bare identifiers. The always-loaded
contract has 385 bytes of headroom and the main skills sit at their byte
ratchets, so each rule lands in the reference that already owns its stage,
with one policy sentence in the contract, one accepted decision record, and a
blind evaluation case with a faulty control for each behaviour. Done is: the
five behaviours are written rules in their owning references, the policy
characters are checked mechanically where an accepted decision allows it, the
evaluation kit fails a faulty control for each, every mirror and hash is
consistent, and the body is reviewed and prepared for a human to land.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] After `gh pr create`, the ship skill carries one step that polls the
      pull request's required checks under a stated time budget, classifies
      redness with the quality contract's existing taxonomy (assertion-red
      returns to the owning stage, infrastructure-incomplete is missing
      evidence), repairs assertion-red checks without asking, never merges,
      and reports readiness to the operator with the pull request URL and the
      green evidence without being asked; evidence: the step, its mechanics
      in `pr-evidence.md`, and the blind case whose faulty control waits for
      the operator to name the red job.
- [ ] One accepted decision record extends project policy in the family of
      the existing no-emoji rule: em and en dashes are absent from tracked
      documents, planning records, commit messages, pull request bodies and
      operator-facing replies; titles, headings and pull request titles name
      the subject in words and never consist of a stable identifier or an
      acronym alone; a summary is two to four sentences of plain prose
      followed by bullets or a table only where they carry facts better; the
      record narrows ADR-0032 so that policy characters may be checked
      mechanically while editorial judgment stays contextual and on demand;
      evidence: the record, the contract's policy sentence, and the
      editorial smells reference with its false-positive sentence amended.
- [ ] `codeflow ci` and the commit-msg hook fail on a policy character (U+2013,
      U+2014) in a commit message, a pull request body or a tracked document
      under `docs/`, `project-management/` and the skill trees, with the
      sanctioned fix named, and the existing legitimate-punctuation canary
      still passes; evidence: the check, its tests and the canary.
- [ ] The lifecycle reference states the reply rule once: when the point is a
      flow or relationship, the reply carries a figure (fenced ASCII in chat,
      an HTML or SVG stage in present or the portal); when a substantial
      comparison or review would be clearer on one surface, the reply opens
      or offers `cf-present` with the reason; a simple answer stays simple;
      evidence: the paragraph and the blind cases in both directions.
- [ ] When the operator is told about a pull request, portal or page, the
      reply carries the URL the creating or fetching tool printed and never a
      guessed one; evidence: the closeout rule in the lifecycle reference,
      the ship completion step, and the blind case whose faulty control
      invents a pull request number.
- [ ] The evaluation kit carries one hard requirement or extension per
      behaviour with markers that resolve verbatim, at least ten blind cases
      whose prompts never name the rule under test, each with a faulty
      control that fails, the existing over-correction and simple-answer
      canaries still green; evidence: the requirement fidelity test,
      `validate-suite` and the grading inventory.
- [ ] Every edited skill file is byte identical across `.claude/skills`,
      `.agents/skills` and `assets/base`, the manifest hashes match, no
      SKILL.md exceeds its byte ratchet without a recorded ratchet change,
      and the contract stays under its 31 KiB cap; evidence:
      `manifest_consistency` and `artifact_budget_contract`.
- [ ] The combined body passes the full gate and both validators, editorial
      review and Grok adversarial review, with Codex review where its seat
      allows, and the EPC-017 pull request to `main` is prepared with its
      evidence and unverified facts named; evidence: the TSK-069 record.

## Out of scope

  epic only states when a reply must carry a figure.

  characters and identifier-only titles.
- Retrofitting existing documents; the mechanical check runs on the tracked
  tree at the head this epic ships, and the sweep that makes it green is part
  of TSK-066.
