# EPC-025: orchestration ledger and lanes

## Summary

Issue 272 asked for an orchestrator follow-up ledger: every handed-over
item logged, checked and closed on proof. The operator shaped it on
2026-10-22 into project-level working state that CodeFlow owns for any
orchestrating model: a clone-local event ledger with a `follow`
command, lanes with ids and a lifecycle, hooks on every harness, and
the rules and skills that require them (ADR-0086). It is its own small
epic, sized by issue 280's guidance, and it targets 3.2.0 as the
operator placed issue 272 there: its tasks land on EPC-022's line,
`integration/EPC-022-the-3-2-0-milestone-fixes`, and are proven on
that line's final candidate beside EPC-022's, with the line's pull
request to `main` describing both epics' outcomes.

Value line: after this merges, any orchestrating session in a CodeFlow
project keeps its open commitments and its lanes in the clone, sees
what is owed, overdue or orphaned at every session start and stop, and
hands a lane to a new seat without losing its state.

The design the operator is reviewing is the planning session's
"Orchestration ledger and lanes" page (claude.ai artifact
`JNdVjLApNR2UqttrtpiAZT`); the records follow it. Already decided:
location A, JSON Lines, one ledger per project, any session may add
rows, only the holder closes, closure on proof. Also decided on
2026-10-22: a lane carries a scope (task, tasks, epic or topic, with
refs) that `lane scope` changes over time as events, with the slug
derived from it and the number as the identity; two project settings,
`check interval` (default 10 minutes) and `heartbeat timeout` (default
30 minutes, always longer than the interval), with heartbeats refreshed
by every hook and command; and takeover only by an explicit `adopt`,
with a returning session that finds its role adopted stopping. Open for
the operator, marked in the records: the turn-end check strength
(advisory by default, or blocking in this repository), whether a lane
may open sub-lanes (proposed: host only), and the Codex hook route
(TSK-440 establishes it).

Enforcement is five layers that reinforce each other, only the first
of which depends on the model remembering: the managed rule and skill
(TSK-423); the session-start hook that claims or reports the role and
prints owed, overdue, orphaned and operator rows plus stale lanes
(TSK-440); the bookkeeping commands (`work claim` opens or links a
lane, `cf-herdr` refuses a seat without a lane id, a merged pull
request or completed task auto-closes linked rows, `follow add` refuses
duplicates; TSK-422, TSK-439); the turn-end check, advisory by default
and blocking under a policy key (TSK-440); and status and `orient`
built from `follow list` (TSK-440). A session timer is an optional
aid.

## Outcome

After this merges, any orchestrating session in a CodeFlow project can
keep its open commitments and lanes in the clone, see what is owed at
every session start and stop, and hand a lane to a new seat without
losing its state.

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-1 The follow-up ledger shall live in the clone's common git directory as schema-validated JSON Lines events with a folded view, rows `FU-NNN` never reused, any session shall add rows, only a row's holder (`host` or a lane id) shall close it and only on proof, a row shall name what it waits on (a task, a pull request, a lane, another row, the operator, an outside event or a time) with CodeFlow deriving when it is due, a merged pull request or completed task shall close its linked rows with that proof, `add` shall refuse a duplicate of an open record until linked or rejected and refuse a cycle among rows, and `codeflow work status` shall list per task the worktrees with uncommitted changes, unpushed commits and commits on no branch; evidence: TSK-422.
- AC-2 A lane shall have an id `LN-NNN-<slug>` allocated per project, monotonic and never reused, with the slug derived from a scope (task, tasks, epic or topic, with refs) that `lane scope` changes as events, record its kind, model, current seat, heartbeat and rows, move only through open, active, idle, closed (with reason and proof; superseded names the successor) and archived, never be deleted, build by its scope kind's readiness, and be driven by `codeflow lane open|attach|scope|adopt|close|list`; the host role shall be one session's lease with a heartbeat; `check interval` and `heartbeat timeout` shall be project settings with the timeout longer than the interval, refreshed by every hook and command; a stale host or seat shall be reported and taken over only by an explicit `adopt`, with a returning session that finds its role adopted stopping; `cf-herdr` shall refuse a seat without a lane id; and a standalone session shall be its own host; evidence: TSK-439.
- AC-3 On every harness, one implementation shall claim or report the role and show the owed, overdue, orphaned and operator rows plus stale lanes at session start, report overdue rows, rows with no next check and an unlogged operator ask at turn end (advisory by default, blocking under a policy key), print the overdue count from any command and build status and `orient` from `follow list`, through the Claude and Grok adapters and the established Codex route or its stated gap, never through a session timer; evidence: TSK-440.
- AC-4 The managed block, `workflow-discipline.md`, `cf-model-orchestrator`, `cf-herdr`, `cf-delegate`, `cf-method`, the per-harness notes, `docs/architecture.md` and the CLI help shall state how the ledger and lanes are used, and a fresh-context qualification shall show a host reporting three handed-over items, one stalled, unasked; evidence: TSK-423.
- AC-5 (journey) On the installed combined candidate, a host session and two lane seats on two harnesses in a scratch adopter project shall log, hand back, orphan (by an expired heartbeat) and adopt rows, with the next session start on each harness showing the same owed and overdue rows; evidence: the final integration run.
