# EPC-025: the coordination ledger: follow-ups and lanes

## Summary

Issue 272 asked for an orchestrator follow-up ledger: every handed-over
item logged, checked and closed on proof. The operator shaped it on
2026-10-22 into project-level working state that CodeFlow owns for any
orchestrating model, and an independent Codex review of the design
(sound with changes, 22 findings, 14 high) settled its contracts: the
coordination ledger, a clone-local single event log with follow-ups
(`FU-NNN`, `codeflow follow`) and lanes (`LN-NNN`, `codeflow lane`),
the coordinator as lead role, hooks on every harness, and the rules
and skills that adopt them (ADR-0086). It is its own small epic, sized
by issue 280's guidance, and it targets 3.2.0 as the operator placed
issue 272 there. It lands on its own integration line,
`integration/EPC-025-the-coordination-ledger-follow-ups-and-lanes`, cut
from the EPC-022 line, because the epic-line binding
(`crates/codeflow-core/src/workgraph/work_start.rs:880-917`) requires a
task's epic to equal the line's epic, so an EPC-025 task cannot bind to
the EPC-022 line (found at the first EPC-025 push on 2026-10-23;
amended the same day, a reversible primary decision). The closing
step: once its four tasks have landed and the epic's criteria are
proven on its own installed candidate, the EPC-025 line merges into
the EPC-022 line through one reviewed pull request, gated once as a
candidate there, before the 3.2.0 pull request to `main`; that pull
request describes both epics' outcomes, and the operator merges it.
Amended 2026-10-24: `codeflow ci` lands an `integration/` head only on
the default branch, so each merge of this line into the EPC-022 line is
carried by an EPC-022 task branch that merges it (the first is
TSK-1000), and the operator chose that day to merge the line once now,
with TSK-422 landed, so the 3.2.0 pull request can open.

The design the operator reviewed is the planning session's page
"Coordination ledger: follow-ups and lanes" (claude.ai artifact
`JNdVjLApNR2UqttrtpiAZT`, final version 9, with the sections
"Independent review: what it changed", "Switching harnesses and
models" and "Several coordinators at once: work streams"), and the
reviewed presentation is the primary's cf-present session for the
ledger, rebuilt after the review; the Codex review is
`review-codex-01.md` in that session's scratchpad. The
records state what they settled; the ledger is a coordination aid and
never a safety control.

## Outcome

After this merges into the EPC-022 line and then `main`, any
orchestrating or lone session, on any harness or model, can see and
follow up every open commitment and every lane from disk, across
restarts, usage limits, lost transcripts and a change of coordinator,
and nothing handed over goes quiet.

The operator approved the design on 2026-10-23 (cf-present session
`dac94188`, `approve_with_notes` at revision 9) with three notes, folded
in: direct work by the coordinator is tracked as a follow-up with no
lane; a seat the new coordinator's harness cannot reach is judged by its
own heartbeat and reports, never assumed dead, and replaced on its
resources only with stopped-work evidence or a resolved overlap; and the data model ships
with a field-level schema reference and a figure. Decided by the
operator on 2026-10-22: the name "coordination ledger";
location A (clone-local), JSON Lines, one ledger per project (per
clone, one machine), work streams `WS-NNN` with one live coordinator
each so several coordinators run at once within and across harnesses, any session may add follow-ups, only the
holder closes, closure on proof; lane scope is descriptive (task, tasks, epic
or topic) and changes as events; `check interval` 10 minutes and
`heartbeat timeout` 30 minutes, configurable, timeout longer than
interval; takeover only by explicit `adopt`; the turn-end check warns
by default and a policy key can make it blocking outside tracked
delegate sessions; only a stream's coordinator opens or closes its lanes. Still open for
the operator: the Codex hook route (TSK-440 establishes it).

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-1 The coordination ledger shall live in the clone's common git directory as one append-only JSON Lines log with one sequence, liveness in overwritten status files, reserved counters, a stable sidecar lock and an instance UUID, with crash, lock and torn-tail rules that keep every acknowledged event and make damage read-only with a diagnostic; a follow-up shall be a full record for a cold reader with typed links (context, prerequisite, fulfils) or a no-link reason and an effective schedule, held by a lane or by the coordinator itself with no lane; a field-level schema reference with a figure of the entities shall ship with the store; a prerequisite change shall make it due and only a fulfils condition with target-aware evidence shall close it; closure, drop and hand-over shall follow one permission matrix with evidence marked asserted or machine-verified; exact duplicates shall be refused and similar ones suggested; every free-text field shall be scanned and bounded; status shall be read-only with heartbeats refreshed only by bound mutating operations; the ledger shall never block git, records, CI or landing and records shall never cite FU, LN or WS ids, with actors bound in tests through a fixture while the role and stream commands are TSK-439's and `coord status` is TSK-440's; and `codeflow work status` shall list per task the worktrees with uncommitted changes, unpushed commits and commits on no branch; evidence: TSK-422.
- AC-2 A lane shall be harness-neutral with a local display id never reused, a descriptive scope that changes as events and never amends tracked records, attempts that each record task, worktree, branch, revision, expected result and native provenance, seats with a recorded kind (external, resumable, in-process) re-checked live before they are driven, a seat the new harness cannot reach judged by its own heartbeat and reports (left running while fresh; when silent, investigated and replaced on its branch and worktree only with stopped-work evidence or an explicit resolution of the overlap, else kept as unresolved and possibly live and reported), a `lane_reported` result and a lifecycle whose `done` carries evidence and a disposition for open follow-ups; a work stream `WS-NNN` shall be local to the ledger with a descriptive scope and one live coordinator held by lease and generation, several coordinators shall run at once within one harness and across harnesses, each lane shall belong to one stream and move only by a two-sided handover, a task active in another stream's lane shall produce a warning naming the owner, adoption shall be per stream, the coordinator shall be taken over only by explicit `adopt` after an inventory, with a stale holder reported and never cleaned up from a timeout; the timing settings shall hold with their validation; a new coordinator on any harness shall keep the same lane for the same work; and each row of the design's switching table shall hold; evidence: TSK-439.
- AC-3 On every harness, one implementation shall report at session start, read-only and bounded, the session's own stream in detail and one line per other stream, never guessing a stream, who leads and whether it is stale, the follow-ups owed, overdue or awaiting the operator and the lanes with stale seats, and at turn end warn on overdue items, items with no schedule and an unlogged operator ask, never blocking inside a tracked delegate session, through the Claude, Codex and Grok adapters as a published and tested capability matrix states, with observations bounded, cached and offline-aware and never through a session timer, and `codeflow coord status` shall show every stream with its coordinator's liveness, lanes, owed follow-ups and operator items, flagging, as an advisory, more than one distinct epic with active build attempts in a stream unless the earlier epic records an outside wait; evidence: TSK-440.
- AC-4 The managed block, `workflow-discipline.md`, `cf-model-orchestrator` (its task-graph doctrine amended), `cf-herdr`, `cf-delegate`, `cf-method`, the per-harness notes and `docs/architecture.md` shall state how the ledger and lanes are used in one vocabulary with `host` mapped to `coordinator`, every requirement of #272 and its comment shall map to a task or a named deferral, and a fresh-context qualification shall show a coordinator reporting three handed-over items, one stalled, unasked, beside the lone session, the lost transcript, the stale-but-live holder and the delegated Stop lifecycle; evidence: TSK-423.
- AC-5 (journey) On this epic's own installed candidate, and again on the EPC-022 line's final candidate after the EPC-025 line merges into it, two coordinator sessions on two streams in one harness and two lane seats on two harnesses in a scratch adopter project shall log, hand over, lose a coordinator to a usage limit, report the stale lease, adopt after the inventory with the next generation, continue the same lanes from disk on another harness, and close one follow-up on machine-verified proof, with `codeflow ci` on the project's branches unchanged by the ledger's presence; evidence: the final integration run.
- AC-6 A stream shall carry an optional parent so a Director's stream has Coordinator streams under it, every coordinator claim, adoption and lane seat shall record the session holding it (harness, name, resume id) with `rename` and read-only `resume` lookups for both, a coordinator shall report to its parent with `stream report` and a lane with `lane report`, each report identified by its ledger sequence with harvest marks naming it and a crash-safe retry key, the schema shall move to version 2 in one cutover that folds every version 1 ledger, `coord status --tree` and the start digest shall show the tree with unharvested reports and their age and terminate on any malformed shape, and the guidance shall state the roles Director, Coordinator and Lane with Seat as instructions only, with same-family lanes allowed as recorded interactive sessions, headless forms still forbidden and the other-lineage review still required; evidence: TSK-439 (AC-2, AC-4, AC-7, AC-10 to AC-12), TSK-1126, TSK-440 (AC-1, AC-7, AC-9), TSK-423 (AC-1, AC-2, AC-4, AC-6).

## Hierarchy amendment (2026-10-31)

The operator asked on 2026-10-31 for a hierarchy over the ledger: one
session the user talks to across many topics (a Director), a coordinator
per topic, and lanes doing the work, every session reachable directly and
resumable. The proposal "Director, Coordinators and Lanes" (Fable 5.1,
version 8; reviewed by Fable 5.1, Codex GPT-6 Astra and Grok 4.7 over
seven rounds and approved at version 7, version 8 applying Grok's three
nits, every finding dispositioned in its appendix A) and its page were
accepted by the operator with the recommended answers: the names Director,
Coordinator and Lane, with Seat for the session on a lane; two supervision
levels as guidance; same-family lanes as recorded interactive sessions,
headless forms still forbidden and the other-lineage review still required
(ADR-0094, amending ADR-0077 and ADR-0086); schema version 2 in one
cutover; the report data shipped now, the cf-present view and the Claude
report hook decided later (tracked once above). Roles are guidance, never
a check in the tooling; every coordination act is a `codeflow` command
from a plain shell, and the existing floors hold for every role.

Clarifications the operator agreed on the page after version 8, carried
in the records: all three levels are the same kind of named, resumable
session, differing only in name, guidance and ledger record; a stream
report's body is the status report format (`codeflow status --json`,
TSK-1095, EPC-022) unchanged, the ledger adding only the envelope
(TSK-1126 stores it opaque, so no edge on TSK-1095); the Coordinator
creates its topic's `integration/<epic>` branch, lands reviewed lane pull
requests there and raises the line's pull request to `main` for the
operator to merge; a Director or Coordinator that does work opens a lane
and takes the seat (TSK-423). Two additions were suggested on the page and
not reviewed by the proposal's rounds; they are filed as proposed criteria
so the planning review settles them: TSK-423 AC-1 states the
open-a-lane-for-yourself rule, and TSK-440 AC-9 flags a dirty root
checkout or a task branch with no lane.
