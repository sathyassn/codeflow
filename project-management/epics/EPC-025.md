# EPC-025: the coordination ledger: follow-ups and lanes

## Summary

Issue 272 asked for an orchestrator follow-up ledger: every handed-over
item logged, checked and closed on proof. The operator shaped it on
2026-10-22 into project-level working state that CodeFlow owns for any
orchestrating model, and an independent Codex review of the design
(sound with changes, 22 findings, 14 high) settled its contracts: the
coordination ledger, a clone-local single event log with follow-ups
(`FU-NNN`, `codeflow follow`) and lanes (`LN-NNN`, `codeflow lane`),
the coordinator as lead role, hooks on every harness, and the rules
and skills that adopt them (ADR-0086). It is its own small epic, sized
by issue 280's guidance, and it targets 3.2.0 as the operator placed
issue 272 there: its tasks land on EPC-022's line,
`integration/EPC-022-the-3-2-0-milestone-fixes`, and are proven on that
line's final candidate beside EPC-022's, with the line's pull request
to `main` describing both epics' outcomes.

The design the operator reviewed is the planning session's page
"Coordination ledger: follow-ups and lanes" (claude.ai artifact
`JNdVjLApNR2UqttrtpiAZT`, final version 9, with the sections
"Independent review: what it changed", "Switching harnesses and
models" and "Several coordinators at once: work streams"), and the
reviewed presentation is the primary's cf-present session for the
ledger, rebuilt after the review; the Codex review is
`review-codex-01.md` in that session's scratchpad. The
records state what they settled; the ledger is a coordination aid and
never a safety control.

## Outcome

After this merges, any orchestrating or lone session, on any harness or
model, can see and follow up every open commitment and every lane from
disk, across restarts, usage limits, lost transcripts and a change of
coordinator, and nothing handed over goes quiet.

Decided by the operator on 2026-10-22: the name "coordination ledger";
location A (clone-local), JSON Lines, one ledger per project (per
clone, one machine), work streams `WS-NNN` with one live coordinator
each so several coordinators run at once within and across harnesses, any session may add follow-ups, only the
holder closes, closure on proof; lane scope is descriptive (task, tasks, epic
or topic) and changes as events; `check interval` 10 minutes and
`heartbeat timeout` 30 minutes, configurable, timeout longer than
interval; takeover only by explicit `adopt`; the turn-end check warns
by default and a policy key can make it blocking outside tracked
delegate sessions; only a stream's coordinator opens or closes its lanes. Still open for
the operator: the Codex hook route (TSK-440 establishes it).

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-1 The coordination ledger shall live in the clone's common git directory as one append-only JSON Lines log with one sequence, liveness in overwritten status files, reserved counters, a stable sidecar lock and an instance UUID, with crash, lock and torn-tail rules that keep every acknowledged event and make damage read-only with a diagnostic; a follow-up shall be a full record for a cold reader with typed links (context, prerequisite, fulfils) or a no-link reason and an effective schedule; a prerequisite change shall make it due and only a fulfils condition with target-aware evidence shall close it; closure, drop and hand-over shall follow one permission matrix with evidence marked asserted or machine-verified; exact duplicates shall be refused and similar ones suggested; every free-text field shall be scanned and bounded; status shall be read-only with heartbeats refreshed only by bound mutating operations; the ledger shall never block git, records, CI or landing and records shall never cite FU, LN or WS ids, with actors bound in tests through a fixture while the role and stream commands are TSK-439's and `coord status` is TSK-440's; and `codeflow work status` shall list per task the worktrees with uncommitted changes, unpushed commits and commits on no branch; evidence: TSK-422.
- AC-2 A lane shall be harness-neutral with a local display id never reused, a descriptive scope that changes as events and never amends tracked records, attempts that each record task, worktree, branch, revision, expected result and native provenance, seats with a recorded kind (external, resumable, in-process) re-checked live before they are driven, a `lane_reported` result and a lifecycle whose `done` carries evidence and a disposition for open follow-ups; a work stream `WS-NNN` shall be local to the ledger with a descriptive scope and one live coordinator held by lease and generation, several coordinators shall run at once within one harness and across harnesses, each lane shall belong to one stream and move only by a two-sided handover, a task active in another stream's lane shall produce a warning naming the owner, adoption shall be per stream, the coordinator shall be taken over only by explicit `adopt` after an inventory, with a stale holder reported and never cleaned up from a timeout; the timing settings shall hold with their validation; a new coordinator on any harness shall keep the same lane for the same work; and each row of the design's switching table shall hold; evidence: TSK-439.
- AC-3 On every harness, one implementation shall report at session start, read-only and bounded, the session's own stream in detail and one line per other stream, never guessing a stream, who leads and whether it is stale, the follow-ups owed, overdue or awaiting the operator and the lanes with stale seats, and at turn end warn on overdue items, items with no schedule and an unlogged operator ask, never blocking inside a tracked delegate session, through the Claude, Codex and Grok adapters as a published and tested capability matrix states, with observations bounded, cached and offline-aware and never through a session timer, and `codeflow coord status` shall show every stream with its coordinator's liveness, lanes, owed follow-ups and operator items, flagging more than one epic built at once in a stream; evidence: TSK-440.
- AC-4 The managed block, `workflow-discipline.md`, `cf-model-orchestrator` (its task-graph doctrine amended), `cf-herdr`, `cf-delegate`, `cf-method`, the per-harness notes and `docs/architecture.md` shall state how the ledger and lanes are used in one vocabulary with `host` mapped to `coordinator`, every requirement of #272 and its comment shall map to a task or a named deferral, and a fresh-context qualification shall show a coordinator reporting three handed-over items, one stalled, unasked, beside the lone session, the lost transcript, the stale-but-live holder and the delegated Stop lifecycle; evidence: TSK-423.
- AC-5 (journey) On the installed combined candidate, two coordinator sessions on two streams in one harness and two lane seats on two harnesses in a scratch adopter project shall log, hand over, lose a coordinator to a usage limit, report the stale lease, adopt after the inventory with the next generation, continue the same lanes from disk on another harness, and close one follow-up on machine-verified proof, with `codeflow ci` on the project's branches unchanged by the ledger's presence; evidence: the final integration run.
