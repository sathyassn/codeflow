# EPC-035: Status reporting

## Summary

Status pages and estimates assemble timing and landing state by hand. This epic reports them from the records, with the operator's estimate bands (issue 334). It is one of the feature epics the operator approved on 2026-11-03, when the 4.0.0 milestone epic (EPC-022) and the 4.1.0 epic (EPC-023) were regrouped by outcome. Planned version: 4.0.0. Why that version: Issue 334 bands (operator) and the 2026-10-29 decisions; depends on epic 9 figures and epic 3 (286), epic 5 (1173, 436).

## Outcome

After this merges, an operator can read status from the records through `codeflow status`, as facts, a figure-led dashboard document or plain text, and ask the cf-status skill.
