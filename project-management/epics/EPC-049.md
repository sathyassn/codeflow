# EPC-049: Away sessions keep work going on the project's models and seat accounts

## Summary

An operator who steps away cannot leave a session to keep work going: nothing records the charter and owed actions across compaction, admits each unattended step, or routes work to seats on the project's models within each account's limits. This epic builds the away activation, its report and its admitted effects, the skill that drives them, and the model and account routing they rely on. It was split from EPC-032 by the stricter consolidation pass of 2026-11-04. Planned version: 4.0.0. Why that version: the operator's standing direction that features land in the current release (TSK-1045 Execution contract).

## Outcome

After this merges, an operator can step away and leave a session that keeps work moving on seats with capacity, on the project's chosen models, admitting every claim, seat and landing first, and hands back one report of what happened and what they must do.
