# EPC-016: visual guide doctrine and portal rebuild

## Summary

The documentation portal that EPC-014 landed presents the guide's sources, but
it explains them with text in boxes: a row of labelled cards for Concept, a
table for Architecture, prose for Technical. The TSK-014 design boards under
`docs/verification/tsk-014-w5/` had already shown, on this repository's own
facts, what a figure that explains looks like: extent along an axis, travel
that stops, coverage as a grid of distinct marks, layers whose bar length is
their cadence with a spine crossing them. None of that reached the skills, so
neither portal nor present can ask for it. This epic writes the visual
explanation doctrine and its figure grammar into cf-docs-portal and
cf-present, gives the portal a figure runtime and a gate that enforces the
grammar, replaces the three skins with ones that separate cleanly in light and
dark, makes the side panels collapsible and resizable, composes every page of
the CodeFlow guide with a figure at each altitude, closes the guide's coverage
gaps, rebuilds the Agent OS guide on the same skill, and grades the doctrine in
the evaluation kit. Done is: every page reviewed rendered and observed
answer-blind, the full gate and both validators green, and an EPC-016 PR
prepared for a human to land after EPC-014's.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] One visual explanation doctrine, with nine figure families keyed to the
      relationship they encode, twelve figure rules, and the altitude contract,
      lives byte-identically in `cf-docs-portal/resources/figure-grammar.md`
      and `cf-present/resources/figure-grammar.md`, carries one inline SVG
      specimen per family, names `docs/verification/tsk-014-w5/` as the
      evidence board with its baselines as the negative controls, and is
      recorded by a new ADR that supersedes the stage grammar as the default
      form; evidence: the two files, the ADR, the manifest hashes and the
      mirror test.
- [ ] The portal and the present review surface render a figure block from
      one shared grammar module in two binding modes, authored and derived,
      drawing with the token sheet only, with a legend, an accessible
      description and a table twin on every figure; `browser-verify` fails a
      build in which an explanatory page lacks a figure at any altitude, a
      figure has an unkeyed mark or a literal colour, any two drawn states
      differ on fewer than two rendered channels measured off the render in
      both modes, any drawn inner mark falls under the declared floor at
      either width, any text overprints text or a mark, the narrow render
      reuses the wide mark set instead of its declared narrow variant, or a
      derived figure disagrees with its source; evidence: the adapter and
      verifier tests with one failing fixture per rule that a compliant page
      cannot mask.
- [ ] Three skins, each with one identity in light and dark and no blue cast
      on the neutral one, named for what they are rather than for a hue word
      (Graphite, Slate, and a third the operator names), value identical per
      token role across the live portal, the starter and the embedded present
      distribution, holding text and muted at or above 4.5:1 in all six pairs
      and separated from each other by a declared minimum on every canvas
      channel in both modes; both side panels
      collapse with the state remembered and the navigation panel resizes by
      drag within bounds; the altitude tabs use the body typeface in sentence
      case with weight and an accent underline for the selected state;
      evidence: the six-pair token contract test, the per-role equality
      checks, and the design primary's rendered chrome review.
- [ ] Every explanatory page of the CodeFlow guide carries one figure per
      altitude that passes the masked-title test and the two-channel rule,
      Concept owns what the subject is, who it is for and what it is not, no
      Technical altitude carries more than 1500 words of prose outside its
      tables, and the guide gains the pages a reader cannot do without: a
      skills catalog, a policy and hooks reference, a troubleshooting page for
      the doctor checks, a glossary, the decision map, the governance documents
      (contributing, security, conduct, license), a present user guide and a
      delegation and Herdr page, each new route classified as explanatory,
      derived lookup or pass-through text so the figure gate asks the right
      thing of it; the seven charter citations and the v1 and legacy
      internals are gone from public prose; evidence: the rendered review per
      page per altitude, the figure gate, and `validate --docs`.
- [ ] The evaluation kit carries requirements and blind cases that grade
      family choice, the no-box rule, the rendered two-channel rule, narrow
      recomposition, overprint, fidelity and the inner mark floor from a
      rendered fixture, each with a faulty control that must fail; evidence:
      the grading inventory test and `validate-suite`.
- [ ] The Agent OS repository guide is rebuilt from the integration starter
      and doctrine with a figure per altitude on its explanatory pages, the
      tier question (minimal tier versus the portal workflow) settled and
      recorded, and its own `validate --portal` and browser verification
      green on the Agent OS branch; the Agent OS PR that lands it waits for
      the CodeFlow release and is an operator follow-on outside this body;
      evidence: the Agent OS branch, its verification record and its ADR.
- [ ] The combined body passes the full gate, both validators, the present
      checksum, the design primary's rendered review in three skins, two modes
      and two widths, and an answer-blind observation of at least three pages
      per section against the W5 observation gates G1, G6, G7, G11 and G12;
      evidence: the TSK-064 verification record.
- [ ] The combined diff passes editorial review and Grok adversarial review,
      with Codex review where its seat allows; the EPC-016 PR to `main` is
      prepared with its evidence and its unverified facts named, follows the
      EPC-014 PR, and is landed by a human only on actually-green required
      checks.

The figure declaration schema is a new interface. The figure grammar ADR
carries it as the decision of record and `figure-grammar.md` carries the
field reference; no separate spec is allocated unless the ADR says one is
needed, in which case TSK-059 allocates it before implementation starts.

Landing rule: agents prepare reviewed PRs and merge only into
`integration/EPC-016-visual-guide`; humans land protected targets on
actually-green required checks. `integration/EPC-016-visual-guide` was cut
from `integration/EPC-014-public-docs` at `877580b20`, so the EPC-016 PR to
main follows EPC-014's and must not be landed before it.

Identifier note: this branch does not carry `integration/EPC-015-engineering-bar`,
whose tree allocates EPC-015 and TSK-053 to TSK-057 and ADR-0065. The
allocator numbers from the tree, so the records here were allocated and then
renumbered to the global next identifiers (EPC-016, TSK-058 to TSK-064) to
keep the sequences unique when both branches reach `main`. That skip is not a
reservation: the figure grammar ADR is not numbered here. TSK-058 allocates
it at its start from the highest ADR number across this tree,
`integration/EPC-015-engineering-bar` and `origin/main`, and never renames it
afterwards; the other plane re-reads the same three trees before it allocates
its next identifier.
