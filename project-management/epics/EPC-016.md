# EPC-016: visual guide doctrine and portal rebuild

## Summary

The documentation portal that EPC-014 landed presents the guide's sources, but
it explains them with text in boxes: a row of labelled cards for Concept, a
table for Architecture, prose for Technical. The TSK-014 design boards under
`docs/verification/tsk-014-w5/` had already shown, on this repository's own
facts, what a figure that explains looks like: extent along an axis, travel
that stops, coverage as a grid of distinct marks, layers whose bar length is
their cadence with a spine crossing them. None of that reached the skills, so
neither portal nor present can ask for it. This epic writes the visual
explanation doctrine and its figure grammar into cf-docs-portal and
cf-present, gives the portal a figure runtime and a gate that enforces the
grammar, replaces the three skins with ones that separate cleanly in light and
dark, makes the side panels collapsible and resizable, composes every page of
the CodeFlow guide with a figure at each altitude, closes the guide's coverage
gaps, rebuilds the Agent OS guide on the same skill, and grades the doctrine in
the evaluation kit. Done is: every page reviewed rendered and observed
answer-blind, the full gate and both validators green, and an EPC-016 PR
prepared for a human to land after EPC-014's.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] One visual explanation doctrine, with nine figure families keyed to the
      relationship they encode, ten figure rules, and the altitude contract,
      lives byte-identically in `cf-docs-portal/resources/figure-grammar.md`
      and `cf-present/resources/figure-grammar.md`, carries one inline SVG
      specimen per family, names `docs/verification/tsk-014-w5/` as the
      evidence board with its baselines as the negative controls, and is
      recorded by a new ADR that supersedes the stage grammar as the default
      form; evidence: the two files, the ADR, the manifest hashes and the
      mirror test.
- [ ] The portal renders a figure block from a shared grammar module in two
      binding modes, authored and derived, drawing with the token sheet only,
      with a legend, an accessible description and a table twin on every
      figure; `browser-verify` fails a build in which an explanatory page
      lacks a figure at any altitude, a figure has an unkeyed mark or a
      literal colour, a mark falls under the declared floor at either width,
      or the narrow render exceeds the declared height multiple; evidence:
      the adapter and verifier tests with fixtures where one compliant page
      cannot mask a noncompliant one.
- [ ] Three skins, each with one identity in light and dark and no blue cast
      on the neutral one, named for what they are rather than for a hue word
      (Graphite, Slate, and a third the operator names), identical across the
      live portal, the starter and the embedded present distribution, holding
      text and muted at or above 4.5:1 in all six pairs; both side panels
      collapse with the state remembered and the navigation panel resizes by
      drag within bounds; the altitude tabs use the body typeface in sentence
      case with weight and an accent underline for the selected state;
      evidence: the six-pair token contract test, the starter and present
      equality checks, and the design primary's rendered review.
- [ ] Every explanatory page of the CodeFlow guide carries one figure per
      altitude that passes the masked-title test and the two-channel rule,
      Technical altitudes carry at least 250 words of prose around their
      tables, and the guide gains the pages a reader cannot do without: a
      skills catalog, a policy and hooks reference, a troubleshooting page for
      the doctor checks, a glossary, the decision map, the governance documents
      (contributing, security, conduct, license), a present user guide and a
      delegation and Herdr page; the seven charter citations and the v1 and
      legacy internals are gone from public prose; evidence: the rendered
      review per page per altitude, the figure gate, and `validate --docs`.
- [ ] The evaluation kit carries requirements and blind cases that grade
      family choice, the no-box rule, the two-channel rule and narrow
      recomposition from a rendered fixture, each with a faulty control that
      must fail; evidence: the grading inventory test and `validate-suite`.
- [ ] The Agent OS repository guide is rebuilt from the updated starter and
      doctrine with a figure per altitude on its explanatory pages, the tier
      question (minimal tier versus the portal workflow) settled and recorded,
      and its own `validate --portal` and browser verification green;
      evidence: the Agent OS PR and its verification record.
- [ ] The combined body passes the full gate, both validators, the present
      checksum, the design primary's rendered review in three skins, two modes
      and two widths, and an answer-blind observation of at least three pages
      per section against the W5 observation gates G1, G6, G7, G11 and G12;
      evidence: the TSK-064 verification record.
- [ ] The combined diff passes editorial review and Grok adversarial review,
      with Codex review where its seat allows; the EPC-016 PR to `main` is
      prepared with its evidence and its unverified facts named, follows the
      EPC-014 PR, and is landed by a human only on actually-green required
      checks.
