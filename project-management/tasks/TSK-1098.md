# TSK-1098: codeflow status --present renders the figure-led progress document and --md its plain-text form

## Description

The operator asked on 2026-10-30 that status be presented in a visually
rich way with supporting text on `cf-present`, and added on the same day
(20:40): timing must read at a glance on a time axis; it must be clear
what is on `main`, what is not and when it gets there; a details
disclosure must open to well-presented visual content, never a dense
paragraph and a plain table ("layering information, not sweeping text
there"); a table that is needed must be easy to consume and look good,
elegant and restrained; and the report must answer, in a logical
progression, what someone expecting a report would want to know. TSK-1095
computes the facts; this task renders them so the same facts open as a
`cf-present` document on any adopting project with no agent, and print as
a plain-text form for terminals, pull request bodies and chat. The
rendering is in the binary so it is deterministic, tested and the same
everywhere; the skill (TSK-1099) adds only the session's context and
session-only facts through `--session`.

The reader's questions, in the order the document answers them, from
the status report design brief revision 2 (the design lead's, revised
2026-10-30 21:05 EDT, section 2), generalised for any adopting project:

```text
  1 how current is this; do I owe anything      verdict: as-of time, owed
    now, and when next                           items or an explicit all-clear
                                                 (or not established), the next
                                                 operator moment and its window
  2 what is on main, complete on the line, in    one partition of the scope on
    a PR, not started; when does the line         one denominator (extent rows)
    reach main                                    + the flow of four stations
                                                 with the hop to main dated or
                                                 marked no date
  3 what happens next, at what clock time,      schedule figure: next landing,
    what is late or stopped                      the chain, the operator step,
                                                 the hop to main; stopped hatched
  4 what is blocked, why, who clears it         blocked list: mark, item, cause,
                                                 unblocker chip, window
  5 what changed since the previous report      since strip: landings, new red
                                                 checks, tasks added, blockers,
                                                 decisions, with times
  6 how is each lane and each task doing        lanes as extent rows with a
                                                 disclosure per lane to its
                                                 encoded task table
  7 is the finish getting closer; what if       pace and intake per day; the
    intake continues                             finish under the two named
                                                 scenarios, in one place
  8 which numbers can I trust; what was not     evidence matrix (coverage
    checked                                      figure) and the refs
```

Figure: the eight reader questions and the carrier that answers each, in
document order; a reader who stops after question 4 has the decision
picture; every number appears first as a mark and stays as text.

Decisions, each reversible and disclosed here:

- `codeflow status --present <path>` (with the scope flags and
  `--session <file>` of TSK-1095) writes a `schema_version: 2` document
  the skill opens with `codeflow present open`; `--md` prints the
  plain-text form with the fenced ASCII chat forms of the same families
  (`figure-grammar.md` section 7) and the ladder, for a terminal, a PR
  body or a reply; the text views of TSK-1095 are unchanged.
- The document follows the bar of TSK-1097 and the design brief
  revision 2 (its reader model, page outline, mark set, chips, timing
  design, location design, layered detail rule and table style), with
  the brief's repository-specific numbers and rows replaced by the facts
  of the scope at hand, and with every `html` stage the brief fell back
  to replaced by the figure families and the encoded table the grammar
  now has (TSK-1096, TSK-1100): anchor first, each section a titled
  narrative with a lead line naming its question, the figure or encoded
  visual, short acting text; a disclosure opens to an encoded table or a
  figure with its lead, never a bare paragraph and a plain table, and
  its summary carries a mark or count. The anchor's lines are the
  as-of time with its zone, the owed items (or `none owed`, or `not
  established` with the unchecked source), the next operator moment with
  its window, the scope and the location summary on one denominator (so
  many on `main`, on the line, in a pull request, not started); the skill
  may prepend one context block, and the document carries a `summary`.
  The order is the recorded reader's question list above, named in each
  section's lead; TSK-1097's bar requires that an author record the
  reader and the questions, and this list is the one this document
  records.
- Carriers: the overall and per-epic percent as an extent figure with
  `max` 100 and limit ticks at the stage boundaries; location as a flow
  figure of four stations (`branch`, `pull request`, `line`, `main`)
  with the task count under each on one denominator, the arrows labelled
  with what moves work along and their windows, the arrow into `main` in
  the accent as the governing path with the pull request and its state
  or `no PR, no date` in words, and an extent per location beside it;
  timing as the schedule family (TSK-1100) on a clock axis with the as-of
  rule: the next landing, the chain, the operator step as a readiness
  window ending in the ring with the merge undated off the axis, and the
  hop to `main`, stopped work hatched with its condition, dependencies
  drawn, the critical path in accent, and the per-item windows in a
  disclosure as an encoded table; the blocked list as an encoded table
  (state mark, item, cause, unblocker chip, window); the operator set as
  a `status` block, a success `callout` of one line only when the facts
  say `none owed` with every source checked, and a pending `status` item
  naming the unchecked source when `not established`; the since strip as
  an encoded table of events with times when `--since` was given, and
  one lead line saying no previous report was given otherwise; lanes as
  an extent per lane with the session facts (seat,
  verdict in flight, blocker) as marks and the overall row, each lane
  with a disclosure opening to its encoded task table (criteria bar,
  checks as marks, review round, location chip, next milestone and
  window, last moved); tasks as the encoded table of TSK-1096 grouped by
  epic with the stage mark, a bar for the percent, the location chip,
  the planning window and an issue mark; pace and intake as extent rows
  per day (landed against added, with the open count at the end of each
  day) with the finish under the two named scenarios (intake stops;
  intake continues at the observed rate, a non-finite finish said in
  words), the two labelled rates and the windows' basis as the lead, the
  only home of the finish forecast; evidence as a coverage figure (measures by sources, the five cell
  marks) inside a disclosure with an encoded table of refs and commands.
  No `html` stage, no callout per task, no stat tile, no table without
  an encoding outside a figure twin. Craft rules from the four reviews of
  the hand-built report (2026-10-30 21:42 EDT): each lane's disclosure
  follows its own lane row, never collected at the end; a lane's lead is
  tasks landed of total, with criteria verified second and the word
  `criteria` on its bar, `stale` when the reviewed SHA is not the head,
  `not recorded` when absent; the location chip says `open PR` or
  `draft`, the state says what it waits for, the next step says `then
  the operator's decision`; every named set (the chain, a lane, the path)
  is defined once from the facts and the same count appears everywhere;
  the anchor defines `on main` (the merge is in `main`'s history), `on
  the line` (landed since) and `complete` where first used, and says that
  neither is a release; the since strip is one column in time order with
  a neutral event mark and the stop mark only for a stop; a caption
  carries one new fact and never restates its figure.
- Governing path in accent: the critical path, the arrow into `main` and
  the scope row; location in the cat1 and cat2 companions through the
  chips; warn for a window marked stale or an adverse run; stop for a
  blocker; never hue alone; the legend drawn once per figure as the
  grammar requires.
- A scope with more than 24 open tasks groups the table by epic and draws
  the schedule for the critical path and its neighbours only, saying so
  in the caption; the full order and every window stay in a disclosure as
  encoded tables.
- Every figure fact names its source in the facts JSON, so the twin and
  the description hold the exact values; nothing is drawn that the facts
  do not state; `unknown` draws as the `unknown` mark (TSK-1096) with the
  word, `unverified` as the `notrun` hatch with the word, and neither as
  `na` (not applicable), which keeps its own meaning, nor as zero.
- The derived binding: the schedule, flow, extent and coverage
  declarations carry `source` entries into the facts JSON (the field per
  drawn value), so `codeflow present check` can compare a figure against
  the facts file beside it and refuse a drawn value the facts do not
  hold.

Non-goals: the facts (TSK-1095); the skill, its wiring and its context
prose (TSK-1099); the schedule family (TSK-1100) and the encoded table
(TSK-1096) themselves; opening the browser (the skill does); a stored or
scheduled report.

## Outcome

After this merges, one command turns the status facts of any adopting
project into a present document a reader grasps in a few seconds, in the
order a reader asks its questions, with every value reachable in visual
detail, and into a plain-text form for terminals and pull requests.
