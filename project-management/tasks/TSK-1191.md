# TSK-1191: The update warning separates steps that can alter the secret scan from steps that cannot

## Description

Issue 516. The `codeflow update` warning says a workflow step "can hide a leak" for steps that cannot alter the secret scan, such as the reporter's exit-only guard step before checkout. Code: `crates/codeflow-core/src/scaffold/update.rs:557-562`, with the text at `:489-493`. This task narrows the warning, so the unproven case stays a warning (fail closed).

Security task: it narrows a security warning.

## Outcome

after this merges, a person updating a project gets the "can hide a leak" warning only for steps that can alter the secret scan, and the warning says which reason applied.

## Deliverables

- the step classification and warning text: `crates/codeflow-core/src/scaffold/update.rs`
- tests beside the check
- release note: `changelog.d/TSK-1191.md`

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-1 The reporter's exit-only guard step before checkout shall get no "can hide a leak" warning; evidence: a fixture that warns before the change.
- AC-2 A step after a checkout, a `uses:` step, or any step naming `GITHUB_ENV` or `GITHUB_PATH` shall still warn, and the text shall name which reason applied; evidence: one fixture per reason.
- AC-3 Anything the check cannot prove harmless shall keep the warning; evidence: a fixture with an unparsed step.
- AC-4 (journey) (added 2026-11-02, final review round 1, finding 1: the task had no journey criterion; this one drives its own change with the built binary) With the built binary, `codeflow update` over a fixture project whose reporter has an exit-only guard before checkout shall print no leak warning, and over a project with a step after checkout, a `uses:` step or a `GITHUB_ENV` write shall print the warning naming that reason; evidence: the outputs of the runs.
- AC-5 (added 2026-11-02, source: `for-TSK-1191-from-T10.md` (study item T10, issue 516); reason: AC-1 names the reporter's step only in words, and the warning's remedy scheme is not stated) The issue's guard step, `Require a supported pull request base`, shall be a test fixture verbatim and shall produce no warning, and the warning text and its remedy shall stay in the existing remedy-code scheme and shall not tell a reader to move a step that was not found to be a risk; evidence: a test with the issue's step verbatim, the remedy test and the text diff. (serves EPC-022 AC-235)
- AC-6 (added 2026-11-02, source: `for-TSK-1191-from-T10.md`; reason: the landed record has no step that finds every copy of the warning text before code) Before any change, the builder shall record in a `## Findings` section where the warning is produced, the conditions it uses today, and every other place that repeats its text; evidence: the Findings, committed before the first code change.
