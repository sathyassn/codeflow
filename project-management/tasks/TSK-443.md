# TSK-443: The present review surface draws, selects and checks what it renders

## Description

Five runtime defects of the utility presentation surface
(`crates/codeflow-present`), seen in the operator's renders of
2026-10-22 (session `5307bd0e`, revisions 2 and 3) and diagnosed on the
line at `87010c7c1` (`present-selection-diagnosis.md` in the planning
session's scratchpad). Each gets a test that fails before and passes
after.

- **Hover in Comment mode glows every mark.** Empty space inside a
  figure resolves to the `<svg>` root, and `svg.cf-hot` carries a
  drop-shadow (`web/src/styles.css:1408-1418`, the
  `svg :not(svg).cf-hot` rule), so the glow paints around every mark
  and label; a regression of commit `343ec9a8f` (TSK-118). The svg
  root's label is also every word, where it should be the figure title
  (`selection.ts:174`, `excerpt.ts:53`).
- **Tabs do not work.** A `tabs` block renders as a bare row of tab
  labels ("After Before") followed by one closed disclosure per tab,
  with no tab control, and the section list labels it "After, Before".
- **`present check` passes figures that cannot draw.** A sequence
  figure whose JSON-sourced facts used the contains check, and a
  coverage figure with `wide.width` 760 against the 720 limit, both
  passed the check and rendered "The figure was not drawn".
- **An `html` stage block rendered blank** (the old example's
  hand-written SVG stage) in `codeflow present export` viewed in
  headless Chrome; not yet checked in a live session.
- **An operator decision to record**: whether words inside a figure
  become selectable text for anchored notes. Today a press on a figure
  label pins the part it labels, by design (SPC-014 B3; the annotation
  matrix in `cf-present/references/document-authoring.md:352`; TSK-118
  AC-12). If the operator says yes, the anchor text, the matrix,
  SPC-014 and the re-anchoring rules change in this task.

Outcome: **hovering a figure in Comment mode glows only the part under
the pointer and names the svg root by its figure title; a `tabs` block
is a working tab control or is withdrawn; `present check` applies the
renderer's figure rules so a figure that cannot draw fails the check;
an `html` stage block renders in export and live, or its limit is
stated; and the figure-text selection question is decided by the
operator and recorded, with the change made if the answer is yes.**

Non-goals: the design direction and type scale (TSK-444); the skill
method (TSK-445); the pre-handoff figure-count warning, whose author
side is TSK-445's and whose `present check` side is this task's.

Added by the EPC-022 amendment of 2026-10-22, restructured the same day
from TSK-441 at the operator's direction so each outcome is one task.

- the tabs control, or its withdrawal from the schema and the skill: `crates/codeflow-present/web/src/` and `crates/codeflow-present/src/document.rs`, and the `tabs` row of `cf-present/references/document-authoring.md`
- the check: `crates/codeflow-cli/src/cmd/present.rs` (`check`) validating figure declarations by the renderer's rules (the fact check kinds, `wide.width` 120 to 720) and warning when a concept-shaped document has no figure or narrative dominates, with the rules in one place both consult
- the html stage block: the export path in `crates/codeflow-present/` and a live check, or the stated limit in `document-authoring.md`
- the selection decision: the operator's answer recorded in this task; if yes, `crates/codeflow-present/web/src/chrome.tsx`, `selection.ts`, the annotation matrix, `project-management/specs/SPC-014.md` (a dated amendment) and the re-anchoring rules

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-1 In Comment mode, hovering empty space inside a figure shall outline the figure and glow no mark, hovering a part shall glow that part only, and the svg root's label shall be the figure title, not its words; evidence: the entity browser check, failing before the change. (serves EPC-022 AC-121)
- AC-2 A `tabs` block shall render as a working tab control (one panel shown, the others switchable, the section list labelling the block by its title), or the block shall be withdrawn from the schema and the authoring reference until it works; evidence: a render and web test with a two-tab block, failing before the change. (serves EPC-022 AC-121)
- AC-3 `codeflow present check` shall validate figure declarations with the rules the renderer applies, failing the sequence figure whose facts used the contains check and the coverage figure with `wide.width` 760, and shall warn when a concept-shaped document has no figure or narrative text dominates; evidence: CLI tests per instance, the two failing before the change. (serves EPC-022 AC-121)
- AC-4 An `html` stage block shall render its content in `codeflow present export` viewed in headless Chrome and in a live session, or the limit shall be stated in the authoring reference with the block withdrawn from the example; evidence: an export test asserting the content, failing before the change, or the stated limit with a test for the refusal. (serves EPC-022 AC-121)
- AC-5 Figure words shall be selectable (operator decision, 2026-10-23: yes): a drag across a figure label shall anchor the note to those words as a text anchor, and a click shall still pin the part, with the matrix, SPC-014 B3 and the re-anchoring rules amended in the same pull request; evidence: a real-browser test that drags across a label and asserts a text anchor on those words, and clicks a part and asserts a part pin. (serves EPC-022 AC-121)
- AC-6 (journey) With the installed build, `codeflow present` on a document holding a sequence figure, a tabs block and an html stage shall render each as the criteria state, `present check` shall refuse the two undrawable figures, and a Comment-mode hover shall glow one part; evidence: the journey captures. (journey)
- AC-7 The figure render checks (the dimension and fact-kind rules, `wide.width` bounds) shall be shared with the portal build where the portal draws the same figures, so a figure the renderer cannot draw fails both `present check` and the portal build; evidence: a portal-side test that builds a portal page with an undrawable figure and asserts the build fails with the same message, and passes with a valid figure. (serves EPC-022 AC-121)
- AC-8 The Comment flow shall be smooth and unambiguous for a first-time reviewer: entering and leaving Comment mode, selecting prose words, figure words, a figure part, a region and the whole document, writing, editing and removing a note, seeing what is queued, sending a review with a verdict, and seeing that it was sent; each step shall have a visible affordance and state, and no step shall leave the user unsure what was captured; evidence: a real-browser journey test covering every step in light and dark and at phone width; a fresh-context usability walkthrough by an agent that has not seen the code, driving the UI and reporting every point of confusion, with each fixed or stated; and screenshots of each step checked by the builder and the reviewer. (serves EPC-022 AC-121)

Reason for the AC-5 rewording: the operator has decided, so the open decision and its "if no" branch are removed.
