# TSK-443: The present review surface draws, selects and checks what it renders

## Description

Five runtime defects of the utility presentation surface
(`crates/codeflow-present`), seen in the operator's renders of
2026-10-22 (session `5307bd0e`, revisions 2 and 3) and diagnosed on the
line at `87010c7c1` (`present-selection-diagnosis.md` in the planning
session's scratchpad). Each gets a test that fails before and passes
after.

- **Hover in Comment mode glows every mark.** Empty space inside a
  figure resolves to the `<svg>` root, and `svg.cf-hot` carries a
  drop-shadow (`web/src/styles.css:1408-1418`, the
  `svg :not(svg).cf-hot` rule), so the glow paints around every mark
  and label; a regression of commit `343ec9a8f` (TSK-118). The svg
  root's label is also every word, where it should be the figure title
  (`selection.ts:174`, `excerpt.ts:53`).
- **Tabs do not work.** A `tabs` block renders as a bare row of tab
  labels ("After Before") followed by one closed disclosure per tab,
  with no tab control, and the section list labels it "After, Before".
- **`present check` passes figures that cannot draw.** A sequence
  figure whose JSON-sourced facts used the contains check, and a
  coverage figure with `wide.width` 760 against the 720 limit, both
  passed the check and rendered "The figure was not drawn".
- **An `html` stage block rendered blank** (the old example's
  hand-written SVG stage) in `codeflow present export` viewed in
  headless Chrome; not yet checked in a live session.
- **An operator decision to record**: whether words inside a figure
  become selectable text for anchored notes. Today a press on a figure
  label pins the part it labels, by design (SPC-014 B3; the annotation
  matrix in `cf-present/references/document-authoring.md:352`; TSK-118
  AC-12). If the operator says yes, the anchor text, the matrix,
  SPC-014 and the re-anchoring rules change in this task.

Outcome: **hovering a figure in Comment mode glows only the part under
the pointer and names the svg root by its figure title; a `tabs` block
is a working tab control or is withdrawn; `present check` applies the
renderer's figure rules so a figure that cannot draw fails the check;
an `html` stage block renders in export and live, or its limit is
stated; and the figure-text selection question is decided by the
operator and recorded, with the change made if the answer is yes.**

Non-goals: the design direction and type scale (TSK-444); the skill
method (TSK-445); the pre-handoff figure-count warning, whose author
side is TSK-445's and whose `present check` side is this task's.

Added by the EPC-022 amendment of 2026-10-22, restructured the same day
from TSK-441 at the operator's direction so each outcome is one task.

## Operator decision (2026-10-23)

The operator decided in chat that figure words are selectable. A drag across
words in a label, axis name or legend entry creates a text anchor on those
words, as in prose. A plain click still pins the part, and Comment-mode hover
still highlights one part. A drag that starts on empty figure space remains
a region gesture. This decision authorizes the AC-5 implementation in this
pull request; it is not an acceptance or completion record.

## SPC-014 amendment (2026-10-23)

This is the explicit superseding record for figure text in SPC-014 B1, B3
and B5, authorized by the operator's AC-5 decision above. SPC-014's
implemented body stays frozen as history; this amendment governs figure
words from this change onward. The lifecycle gate names an explicit
superseding record as the alternative to replacing an implemented spec.

### B1: canonical figure text and re-anchoring

A figure's canonical review text begins with its title and caption,
preserving their existing positions, followed by the wide composition's
text items in draw order, the narrow composition's text items in draw
order, and the declared legend meanings in state order. Authored layouts
use the labels and formatted values their compositions generate. Each
part uses the existing line-break separator. Repeated words remain
separate occurrences. Each rendered label or legend entry maps to its own
UTF-16 position in that block text; hidden variant text, SVG metadata and
runtime controls cannot initiate a selection. A text drag stays within
one label or legend entry, as a prose drag stays within one review-text
root.

These notes carry the existing text selector. The same-revision position
check and B1's exact/context and fuzzy rules apply unchanged. Moving a
mark without changing its words preserves the text anchor. If a quote
can no longer be matched, the note falls back to its figure block with
the reason; if that block is removed, it is orphaned. A former part-pin
note stays a part-pin note.

### B3: gesture priority

The first resolver rule, a non-empty selection inside a block's review
text, includes drawn figure labels, axis names and legend words. A drag
over those words makes a text note. A plain click still resolves the part
by the following rules, and hover still highlights that part. A drag
beginning on empty drawing space remains a region gesture even if it
crosses words; Shift-drag requests a region when starting on words.

### B5: legend exception and export

Drawn figure legend words are an exception to B5's framing exclusion:
they take text notes on a drag and keep their existing element target on
a click. Title, caption and Details framing keeps its element-note
behavior. Exported documents permit native text selection and, with
`--with-notes`, include the stored text selectors. Offline export has no
comment submission controls.
