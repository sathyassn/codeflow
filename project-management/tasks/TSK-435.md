# TSK-435: Records state their Outcome and epics their Deliverables

## Description

Operator decision of 2026-10-22, part of the issue #280 work: an
explicit Outcome on records, and Deliverables on epics. Issue 280 asks
that an epic state one value line ("after this merges, X can do Y");
today the task template has no home for it (records put an "Outcome:"
sentence in the Description by convention), the epic template has no
Deliverables section, and `codeflow validate --docs` checks a task's
Deliverables (`crates/codeflow-core/src/workgraph/record_text.rs:581-596`)
but no Outcome anywhere.

Outcome: **after this lands, a task or epic record created with the
templates carries an `## Outcome` section with one line saying what
someone can do once it lands, an epic carries a `## Deliverables`
section like a task's, `codeflow validate --docs` requires the Outcome
section on records created after the change, and existing records stay
valid: closed records are never changed or flagged, and an open record
created before the change gets a warning, not a refusal, and gains the
section when next amended.**

Rules:

- **Templates.** `assets/base/pm/task.md.tmpl` and `epic.md.tmpl` gain
  `## Outcome` (one line, in the form "after this merges, X can do Y")
  between the Description and the Deliverables; `epic.md.tmpl` gains
  `## Deliverables` with the task template's comment. `codeflow task
  new` and `epic new` write both.
- **Validation.** A record whose `created` date is on or after the
  change's cut date (a constant the task records, the date the change
  lands on the line) and whose Outcome section is missing or empty is
  refused by `validate --docs`; an open record created before it gets a
  warning naming the section; a record whose status is terminal
  (complete, cancelled, archived) is never flagged. The cut date lives
  beside the check, documented in `docs/policy-reference.md`.
- **Compatibility.** No existing record is edited by this task; the
  "Outcome:" sentence convention in Descriptions stays valid for old
  records.
- **Guidance.** `cf-plan` and `cf-method` "Planning an epic" name the
  section as the home of the value line; TSK-419's sizing guidance
  points at it, so TSK-419 depends on this task.

Non-goals: rewording existing records (TSK-418's class covers that when
a project wants it); any new field in frontmatter.

Added by the EPC-022 amendment of 2026-10-22 at the operator's
direction of that day.

## Deliverables

- the templates: `assets/base/pm/task.md.tmpl`, `assets/base/pm/epic.md.tmpl` and this repository's `project-management/templates/`, with `codeflow task new` and `epic new` in `crates/codeflow-core/src/workgraph/record_template.rs` and `allocate.rs`
- the check and the cut date: `crates/codeflow-core/src/validate/mod.rs` and `crates/codeflow-core/src/workgraph/record_text.rs` (an `outcome` reader beside the Deliverables one)

- guidance: `cf-plan/SKILL.md` and `cf-method/SKILL.md` "Planning an epic" in `assets/base/`, `.claude/skills` and `.agents/skills`, byte-identical; `docs/policy-reference.md` and the records reference in `docs/`

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-1 `codeflow task new` and `codeflow epic new` shall write an `## Outcome` section with its one-line comment, and `epic new` shall also write `## Deliverables` with the task template's comment; evidence: scaffold tests reading both records. (serves EPC-022 AC-117)
- AC-2 `codeflow validate --docs` shall refuse a task or epic record created on or after the cut date whose Outcome section is missing or empty, naming the record and the section; evidence: a validate test, failing before the change. (serves EPC-022 AC-117)
- AC-3 `codeflow validate --docs` shall warn, not refuse, for an open record created before the cut date without the section, and shall say nothing for a complete, cancelled or archived record with or without it; evidence: one test per case, and this repository's existing records validating with warnings only. (serves EPC-022 AC-117)
- AC-4 `cf-plan` and `cf-method` "Planning an epic" shall name the Outcome section as the home of the epic's value line and the task's outcome, and `docs/policy-reference.md` shall state the cut date and the compatibility rule; evidence: the text diff and `codeflow validate --docs`.
- AC-5 The three skill homes shall be byte-identical and the `manifest_consistency` test shall pass; evidence: the test output.
- AC-6 (journey) With the installed build in a scratch adopter project holding one closed and one open record from before the change, `codeflow task new` shall produce a record that validates, removing its Outcome shall make `validate --docs` refuse it, and the two old records shall give one warning and no refusal; evidence: the journey output.
