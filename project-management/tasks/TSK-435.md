# TSK-435: Records state their Outcome and epics their Deliverables

## Description

Operator decision of 2026-10-22, part of the issue #280 work: an
explicit Outcome on records, and Deliverables on epics. Issue 280 asks
that an epic state one value line ("after this merges, X can do Y");
today the task template has no home for it (records put an "Outcome:"
sentence in the Description by convention), the epic template has no
Deliverables section, and `codeflow validate --docs` checks a task's
Deliverables (`crates/codeflow-core/src/workgraph/record_text.rs:581-596`)
but no Outcome anywhere.

Outcome: **after this lands, a task or epic record created with the
templates carries an `## Outcome` section with one line saying what
someone can do once it lands, an epic carries a `## Deliverables`
section like a task's, `codeflow validate --docs` requires the Outcome
section on records created after the change, and existing records stay
valid: closed records are never changed or flagged, and an open record
created before the change gets a warning, not a refusal, and gains the
section when next amended.**

Rules:

- **Templates.** `assets/base/pm/task.md.tmpl` and `epic.md.tmpl` gain
  `## Outcome` (one line, in the form "after this merges, X can do Y")
  between the Description and the Deliverables; `epic.md.tmpl` gains
  `## Deliverables` with the task template's comment. `codeflow task
  new` and `epic new` write both.
- **Validation, adoption-aware.** The compatibility boundary is the
  project's own adoption of this release, not the date the change lands
  on CodeFlow's line: `codeflow update` records the adoption date in
  the project's configuration (provisional `records.outcome_required_from`
  in `.codeflow/project.toml`, written when the project first updates
  to a release that has the section). A record created on or after that
  date whose Outcome section is missing or empty is refused by
  `validate --docs`, unless its status is terminal; an open record
  created before it, including one an older release created after the
  product's cut date, gets a warning naming the section and gains the
  section when next amended; a record whose status is terminal
  (complete, cancelled, archived) is never flagged, whatever its date.
  With no adoption date recorded (a project not yet updated), every
  missing section warns and nothing refuses.
- **Compatibility.** No existing record is edited by this task; the
  "Outcome:" sentence convention in Descriptions stays valid for old
  records.
- **Guidance.** `cf-plan` and `cf-method` "Planning an epic" name the
  section as the home of the value line; TSK-419's sizing guidance
  points at it, so TSK-419 depends on this task.

Non-goals: rewording existing records (TSK-418's class covers that when
a project wants it); any new field in frontmatter.

Added by the EPC-022 amendment of 2026-10-22 at the operator's
direction of that day.

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-1 `codeflow task new` and `codeflow epic new` shall write an `## Outcome` section with its one-line comment, and `epic new` shall also write `## Deliverables` with the task template's comment; evidence: scaffold tests reading both records. (serves EPC-022 AC-117)
- AC-2 `codeflow validate --docs` shall refuse a non-terminal task or epic record created on or after the project's recorded adoption date whose Outcome section is missing or empty, naming the record and the section; evidence: a validate test, failing before the change. (serves EPC-022 AC-117)
- AC-3 `codeflow validate --docs` shall warn, not refuse, for an open record created before the project's adoption date without the section (including a record an older release created after the product's cut date), shall warn and never refuse when no adoption date is recorded, and shall say nothing for a complete, cancelled or archived record with or without the section, whatever its date; evidence: one test per case, and this repository's existing records validating with warnings only. (serves EPC-022 AC-117)
- AC-4 `cf-plan` and `cf-method` "Planning an epic" shall name the Outcome section as the home of the epic's value line and the task's outcome, and `docs/policy-reference.md` shall state the adoption-date rule and the compatibility rule; evidence: the text diff and `codeflow validate --docs`.
- AC-5 The three skill homes shall be byte-identical and the `manifest_consistency` test shall pass; evidence: the test output.
- AC-6 (journey) With the installed build in a scratch adopter project holding one closed and one open record from the previous release, plus one open record the previous release created after the product's cut date, `codeflow update` shall record the adoption date, `codeflow task new` shall produce a record that validates, removing its Outcome shall make `validate --docs` refuse it, and the three older records shall give warnings and no refusal; evidence: the journey output. (journey)
