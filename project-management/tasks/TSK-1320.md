# TSK-1320: A reviewed completion survives a landed planning move of its epic

## Description

`work.acceptance_binding` refuses a reviewed task once its branch merges a line where a landed planning PR changed only the task's epic. The check compares the record's reviewed part at the reviewed commit with the record at the head (`crates/codeflow-core/src/workgraph/acceptance.rs:1418-1436`, `reviewed_part`), so any later change outside status and Closeout reads as unreviewed work. The regroup commit cc62e9381 changed `epic_id` in 221 task records and nothing else in most of them. Since then PRs 523 (TSK-1101), 535 (TSK-1255) and 525 (TSK-1162) refuse after merging line tip 9c5f665cf, and each needs a fresh whole-unit review only because of that edit. PR 518 (TSK-1039) also refuses, but its record has reshuffled blocks and an unlanded amendment, so it must keep refusing.

This task admits that one shape and keeps every other change refused. The admitted edit is a change to `epic_id`, and lines added outside the front matter, the acceptance criteria, the acceptance block, the Deliverables and the Closeout. It must come from a commit on the target line's fetched first-parent chain, identified by exact SHA and read from the target's contents. The task's landing line must be the same before and after: its `integration_target`, and the line its new epic declares (`parent_line`, TSK-1286). Non-goals: TSK-1316 (a planning merge that carries a completed record into another line's check), and record edits a task branch makes itself, which stay reviewed content.

Threat kept closed: a builder or a branch moving a reviewed task, or changing what its completion attests, without a fresh review. Admission depends only on commits already landed on the target through a reviewed planning PR. Cost of not fixing: a fresh whole-unit review for every reviewed, unlanded task that the regroup touched, and the same again after any later planning move of epics.

## Outcome

After this merges, a builder whose reviewed task was moved to another epic by a landed planning PR can merge the line and keep the completion, without a fresh review, while every change to what the completion attests still refuses.

## Deliverables

- the binding change and its fixtures: `crates/codeflow-core/src/workgraph/acceptance.rs` and its tests
- the rule text: SPC-013 R-60 and `docs/capabilities.md` CAP-010, where they describe what a reviewed record may change

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-1 When a reviewed task's record at the head differs from its reviewed part only by the admitted edit, made by a commit on the target's fetched first-parent chain, and its landing line is unchanged, the binding check shall accept the completion; evidence: a fixture shaped like cc62e9381, refused before the change and accepted after.
- AC-2 Each of these shall stay refused, each with its own fixture: a change to the criteria, the acceptance block, the status, the Deliverables or the `integration_target`; an `epic_id` change whose new epic declares another line; the same edit from a commit not on the target's first-parent chain (an unlanded branch, a local-only commit, or an unknown or unreadable commit); a task branch's own commit making the same edit; and history the check cannot read; evidence: the fixtures, each failing closed.
- AC-3 The decision shall read only commits and file contents from the target's fetched first-parent chain, never a branch name, PR title, commit message or date; evidence: the code and a test that varies those names.
- AC-4 A refusal outside the admitted shape shall name the commit and the part of the record that changed; evidence: a test on the message.
- AC-5 (journey) With the change built, `codeflow ci` on one of PRs 523, 535 or 525, at a head that merged line tip 9c5f665cf and was not re-reviewed, shall pass `work.acceptance_binding`, the same check with the criteria edited by hand shall refuse, and PR 518's head shall still refuse; evidence: the commands and their exit codes.
- AC-6 The change narrows a gate, so the PR shall carry a security review with the `cf-security-reviewer` checklist (Grok where it has quota, else a fresh Fable subagent with reduced assurance recorded) and the operator's approval before it lands, and shall state the threat it keeps closed and the cost of not fixing it; evidence: the review file and the operator's recorded approval.
