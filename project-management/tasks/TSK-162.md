# TSK-162: present form ignores a revision notice it already uses

## Description

In the TSK-120 round 1 runs, `check:forms` failed once at the wait for the
"editing" state after a stale correction was refused. The form showed "A
newer revision exists" although it already used the newest revision. The
page learns revision 3 from the stale refusal and moves the form to it, but
the poll's revision-3 notice can arrive later, since the service checks
every 250 ms and load adds delay. The notice carries only the word
"revision", so `FormController.newerRevision()` cannot tell that the form
already uses that revision and marks it stale. An instrumented run caught
the notice 4 ms after the update. Submit still works, but the reviewer reads
a false line, and the check fails when the timing lines up. The fault
predates TSK-120; its trace is in the TSK-120 scratch record
`t120-forms-repro/`. This task makes a revision notice carry its revision
number and makes a form ignore a notice for a revision it already uses.

## Affected surfaces and interfaces

- `crates/codeflow-present/web/src/chrome.tsx` (the revision notice in
  `handleEvent`, not the gesture code) and `web/src/forms.ts`
  (`newerRevision`); the forms browser check; the present rebuild and
  checksum.

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-1 When a revision notice arrives for a revision the form already
  uses, the form keeps its state and shows no newer-revision line; a notice
  for a later revision still marks the form stale; evidence: a browser case

  form, deterministically, and a case with a truly newer revision.
- AC-2 The new case fails on the pre-fix head and passes after it;
