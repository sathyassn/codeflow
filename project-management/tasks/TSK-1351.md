# TSK-1351: A landing candidate branch is pushable and judged against its line

## Description

Split 2026-11-05 from TSK-1161 AC-14 by the CI records planning pull request (Director 15:3x). coord/ci's push of landing candidate `candidate/epc-022-c4a` was refused with 16 blocking findings (`coordination/coordinators/parallel/reviews/candidate-push-refusal-c4a.md`, workspace repository): pre-push has no candidate mode, so a branch with no task id is judged against the default branch, and the range base comes from the narrowest-boundary heuristic, which picked PR 525's head. coord/catalog's design read (`coordination/coordinators/capabilities/builders/candidate-push-design.md`, workspace f155b086) gives the binding in AC-2: a candidate is judged as the line's next tip only when its name, first-parent chain, advertised tip, line binding and merge shape all hold, and is refused with the cause named otherwise. TSK-1188 AC-7 route (b) credits the line push that lands a candidate. Non-goal: TSK-1161's general push judging for task branches; this task owns the shared first-parent line-tip helper, and TSK-1161 AC-8 to AC-10 and AC-13 reuse it. Remaining trust: the hook cannot prove offline that a member head was reviewed (design read, line 68), so a clean merge of an unreviewed branch that carries a criteria change is admitted, the same trust as a push to the line itself; the binding refuses only a change that enters by a direct commit on the candidate or by an evil merge, and AC-1 is no proof of review.

## Outcome

After this merges, a maintainer landing a batch of reviewed pull requests can push the landing candidate branch and have it judged against its line, while a candidate that is not a clean stack of member merges on a bound line tip is refused with its cause.

## Deliverables

- the candidate binding and the shared first-parent line-tip helper: `crates/codeflow-cli/src/cmd/push_set.rs` (or `crates/codeflow-core/src/workgraph/line_adoption.rs` so `codeflow ci` reuses it)
- line judging for a verified candidate: `crates/codeflow-cli/src/cmd/ci.rs`, `ci/line_tip.rs`, `ci/acceptance.rs`
- the branch-name check: `crates/codeflow-core/src/hooks/policy.rs` (`branch_name_ok` accepts the candidate prefix)
- tests: pre-push and `codeflow ci` fixtures beside `crates/codeflow-cli/tests/acceptance_cli/line_sync_push.rs` and `ci_cli/line_tip.rs`
- docs: `docs/cli.md` and `.codeflow/rules/git-rules.md` "Bodies of work"

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-1 A landing candidate, a branch of the project's candidate naming (`candidate/` by default; TSK-1308 AC-5 makes the naming a project setting) cut from a line tip with reviewed pull request heads merged onto it, shall be an allowed branch name and shall be judged at pre-push against that line: the base shall be the line tip on the candidate's first-parent chain, never a merged member's head or another fetched task branch; the target shall be the line, not the default branch; and each member's merge shall be judged as a landing of its reviewed head. A criteria or acceptance change that no member's reviewed head made shall still be refused. Evidence: coord/ci's refusal of 2026-11-05 as a fixture refused before and admitted after (`candidate/epc-022-c4a` at 13e4d88fa, cut from line tip a5cd83c69, judged with base cbc18c2a2, PR 525's head, and into `main`: 16 blocking findings, `coordination/coordinators/parallel/reviews/candidate-push-refusal-c4a.md`); and a negative fixture with a foreign criteria change on the candidate, refused. A candidate branch push starts no hosted run; after this lands, the line push that lands a real candidate is the batch's hosted proof and TSK-1188 AC-7 route (b)'s evidence, not this criterion's.
