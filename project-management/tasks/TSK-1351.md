# TSK-1351: A landing candidate branch is pushable and judged against its line

## Description

Split 2026-11-05 from TSK-1161 AC-14 by the CI records planning pull request (Director 15:3x). coord/ci's push of landing candidate `candidate/epc-022-c4a` was refused with 16 blocking findings (`coordination/coordinators/parallel/reviews/candidate-push-refusal-c4a.md`, workspace repository): pre-push has no candidate mode, so a branch with no task id is judged against the default branch, and the range base comes from the narrowest-boundary heuristic, which picked PR 525's head. coord/catalog's design read (`coordination/coordinators/capabilities/builders/candidate-push-design.md`, workspace f155b086) gives the binding in AC-2: a candidate is judged as the line's next tip only when its name, first-parent chain, advertised tip, line binding and merge shape all hold, and is refused with the cause named otherwise. TSK-1188 AC-7 route (b) credits the line push that lands a candidate. Non-goal: TSK-1161's general push judging for task branches; this task owns the shared first-parent line-tip helper, and TSK-1161 AC-8 to AC-10 and AC-13 reuse it. Member binding (Director's rulings, 2026-11-10 20:0x and 20:3x EST): each member merge's second parent must be the exact head SHA of an open pull request into the line, with main's trusted admission check green at that SHA, read from the forge. The pusher cannot write that SHA binding or that check, and a completion commit or a planning member needs no special case, since the bound head is the pull request's final head. This proves admission at the head (its CI verdicts and record checks), not that a person reviewed it: the completion blocks in a member's commits are recheckable but not authenticated (`acceptance.rs:15-17`). Where the forge cannot be read, or a project has no forge admission check to read, the candidate push refuses, and landing one pull request at a time stays the path, so the feature never fails open for an adopter. The binding also refuses a change that enters by a direct commit on the candidate or by an evil merge.

## Outcome

After this merges, a maintainer landing a batch of reviewed pull requests can push the landing candidate branch and have it judged against its line, while a candidate that is not a clean stack of member merges on a bound line tip is refused with its cause.

## Deliverables

- the candidate binding and the shared first-parent line-tip helper: `crates/codeflow-cli/src/cmd/push_set.rs` (or `crates/codeflow-core/src/workgraph/line_adoption.rs` so `codeflow ci` reuses it)
- line judging for a verified candidate: `crates/codeflow-cli/src/cmd/ci.rs`, `ci/line_tip.rs`, `ci/acceptance.rs`
- the branch-name check: `crates/codeflow-core/src/hooks/policy.rs` (`branch_name_ok` accepts the candidate prefix)
- tests: pre-push and `codeflow ci` fixtures beside `crates/codeflow-cli/tests/acceptance_cli/line_sync_push.rs` and `ci_cli/line_tip.rs`
- docs: `docs/cli.md` and `.codeflow/rules/git-rules.md` "Bodies of work"

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-1 A landing candidate, a branch of the project's candidate naming (`candidate/` by default; TSK-1308 AC-5 makes the naming a project setting) cut from a line tip with reviewed pull request heads merged onto it, shall be an allowed branch name and shall be judged at pre-push against that line: the base shall be the line tip on the candidate's first-parent chain, never a merged member's head or another fetched task branch; the target shall be the line, not the default branch; and each member's merge shall be judged as a landing of its admitted head: its second parent shall be the exact head SHA of an open pull request into the line, with main's trusted admission check green at that SHA, both read from the forge at pre-push. The push shall refuse, naming the cause, when the forge cannot be read, when the project has no forge admission check to read (one-at-a-time landing stays the path), or when any member fails the binding. This proves admission at the head, not that a person reviewed it, and the pull request body says so. A criteria or acceptance change that no member's reviewed head made shall still be refused. Evidence: coord/ci's refusal of 2026-11-05 as a fixture refused before and admitted after (`candidate/epc-022-c4a` at 13e4d88fa, cut from line tip a5cd83c69, judged with base cbc18c2a2, PR 525's head, and into `main`: 16 blocking findings, `coordination/coordinators/parallel/reviews/candidate-push-refusal-c4a.md`); a negative fixture with a foreign criteria change on the candidate, refused; positive fixtures for a member whose head ends in its completion commit and for a planning member; and refusal fixtures, each naming its cause, for a member head with no admitted pull request, a rewritten member head, a fabricated completion block or review reference, a closed or retargeted member pull request, an admission check green only on an older head, an unreadable forge, and a project with no forge admission check. A candidate branch push starts no hosted run; after this lands, the line push that lands a real candidate is the batch's hosted proof and TSK-1188 AC-7 route (b)'s evidence, not this criterion's.
