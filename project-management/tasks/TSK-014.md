# TSK-014 — recover the design method and utility directions

## Description

Correct the design failure exposed by the operator's real `cf-present` trial:
the current system safely republishes structured prose but does not reliably
produce subject-native visual explanation, and the built documentation portal
does not implement its settled prototypes. Strengthen the portable design
method, repeat the presentation and portal exploration at composition altitude,
and obtain operator-visible direction approval before reopening production
renderer or portal design work. Preserve the already qualified security,
lifecycle, source, portability, and cleanup foundations.

## Affected surfaces and interfaces

- `cf-design`, its conditional references, the shared quality contract, and
  rendered-design evaluation protocol.
- SPC-004/SPC-005 and the TSK-006/TSK-008 design settlements as design inputs;
  TSK-011/TSK-009 resume only from the recovered direction.
- Representative `cf-present` explanation/review cases and documentation-portal
  page families, rendered as disposable evidence rather than production code.
- Agent OS receives only the later runtime-neutral design protocol; CodeFlow
  runtime types, validators, themes, and visual primitives remain CodeFlow-owned.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] `cf-design` includes a proportionate composition stage beneath product
      direction: a governing idea, subject objects/relationships/states, a
      primary explanatory form, materially different encodings when that form
      remains open, post-selection system/component development, and rendered
      operator review before material implementation.
- [ ] Protocol gates are distinguished from contextual design judgment. The
      pre-registered review includes chat differential, five-second governing
      idea, thirty-second mechanics, form match, primary-form inventory,
      no-box where applicable, progressive depth, sibling distinctiveness,
      accessibility/craft, and an observer who did not author the artifact.
      These gates guide judgment without becoming a universal house style or
      banning containers that carry real product semantics.
- [ ] Under direct Claude design leadership, each representative `cf-present`
      case and portal page family explores materially different subject-native
      encodings before choosing a reusable shell, visual primitive, page type,
      or technical implementation. The same prose in different page furniture,
      palette swaps, generic Mermaid, card grids, and attractive text documents
      do not count as directions.
- [ ] Rendered evidence covers representative desktop/mobile or
      platform-appropriate viewports and coherent light/dark behavior. It uses
      real CodeFlow content, retains the authored source and comparison rubric,
      and includes the ordinary-chat and ordinary-Markdown baselines needed to
      show what the richer surface materially improves.
- [ ] The operator reviews the rendered board and settles the utility direction
      plus the balance between authored composition and any earned reusable
      visual primitives. Model agreement cannot substitute for this gate.
- [ ] The recovered boundary keeps session/authentication, immutable revision,
      anchored feedback, export, isolation, teardown, retention, source
      provenance, locked portal build, and deterministic evidence mechanics
      separate from authored visual composition. Existing accepted safety
      guarantees are not weakened to gain expressiveness.
- [ ] The invalid TSK-006/TSK-008 design-authority claims are explicitly
      superseded without rewriting historical evidence. SPC-004/SPC-005,
      implementation tasks, ADRs, skills, examples, manifests, and evals are
      amended only after the new rendered direction is approved.
- [ ] Claude and Codex review the same exact recovery inputs and rendered
      artifacts. Fable/high is attempted first for the bound Claude judgment
      verdict. When provider availability blocks Fable, Opus/high may supply
      operator-authorized Claude design input but is not relabeled as the bound
      judgment seat; the formal cross-lineage verdict remains open until the
      binding is available or separately qualified. Unavailable evidence never
      becomes a claimed pass.
