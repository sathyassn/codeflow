# TSK-1135: The pull request capability impact check reads trees and the catalog delta

## Description

The evaluation half of design unit U3. `codeflow ci` rule `git.pr_capability_impact` reads the policy, product paths and catalog presence at the target, the baseline catalog at the merge base and the head catalog from its own tree, computes the delta by `uid` in both directions, and checks the `## Capability impact` declaration against it (verbs `added`, `changed`, `improved`, and `none` with a reason). It applies the activation table and the scope classes. Basis: the Release impact rule it mirrors is about 120 lines (`pr_body.rs:1456-1557`) plus tree loading, delta comparison and fixtures; this is the larger part of the design's 8 to 10 hours. Security review owed: the rule parses declarations and trees the pull request controls and decides admission. Non-goals: the policy key defaults, template and wording (TSK-1136).

Design: `coordination/coordinators/capabilities/design-note.md` revision 5 (workspace repository) and its acceptance tests.

## Outcome

After this merges, `codeflow ci` judges a pull request's capability declaration against the catalog change it makes, from the base and head trees, under the target's policy.

## Deliverables

- the rule: `crates/codeflow-cli/src/cmd/ci/` (`pr_body.rs` and the rule wiring for a new `git.pr_capability_impact`)
- tree and merge-base readers that reuse TSK-1132's loader
- fixtures and journeys: `crates/codeflow-cli/tests/ci_cli/`

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-1 From a checkout with a different head, the rule shall read policy, product paths and catalog presence at the target, the baseline from the merge-base catalog and the head catalog from its own tree; evidence: a fixture with a differing head policy. (serves EPC-026 AC-3)
- AC-2 When the target gained CAP-019 and a new area after the branch forked, an unrelated PR shall pass with no removal reported and a PR adding a record in the new area at head shall pass, while `validate --docs` on the merge result shall refuse a duplicate id between the PR and the target's addition; a head that sets the key to `off` shall be judged by the target policy; evidence: fixtures. (serves EPC-026 AC-3)
- AC-3 The activation table shall hold row by row: key absent is not checked with a note; `off` with a catalog present is not checked and `doctor` notes it; `warn` with a catalog present warns; `block` with a catalog absent refuses a product PR declaring `none` with the bootstrap remedy; a minimal project that sets `block` and whose head deletes the folder is refused; a legacy-only catalog counts as present; evidence: one test per row. (serves EPC-026 AC-3)
- AC-4 Under `block` with an empty catalog at the authority, a product PR declaring `none` shall be refused with the bootstrap remedy, and a PR whose head supplies the first valid catalog with matching `added` declarations shall pass; with tracking disabled the rule shall still run; under `warn` an undeclared product change shall proceed with a warning and the summary line shall say so; evidence: fixtures. (serves EPC-026 AC-3)
- AC-5 A substantive frontmatter edit, a whitespace-only edit, a prose-only edit, a rename, a removed identity and an unrelated record edit shall each get the design's D5 verdict; a frontmatter change declared `improved` shall be refused and a prose-only change declared `improved` with its clause shall pass; two ids with two verbs, an unknown id, and a declared `added` absent from the delta shall be refused; evidence: one fixture each. (serves EPC-026 AC-3)
- AC-6 On an integration-line PR, a member that adds CAP-018 followed by a member that changes it shall yield one `added`; an edit reverted within the line shall yield no operation and a declaration naming it `changed` shall be refused; an improvement-only range with a fragment marker shall pass with its clause; evidence: range fixtures. (serves EPC-026 AC-3)
- AC-7 Watched Markdown, ordinary Markdown under `docs/` and a managed instruction file shall be classified separately with only the ordinary one exempt, and a 2,000-record catalog shall complete within the CI step budget; evidence: a classification test and a timing test. (serves EPC-026 AC-3)

- AC-8 Each declared capability line shall carry its id, its name and one line of context on what changed for its users, in the form `- Added CAP-018 <name>: <context>`; an entry with only an id, only a count, a missing name or a missing context shall be refused naming the line; evidence: fixtures for those four failures and a passing entry for each verb. (serves EPC-026 AC-3)

- AC-9 When the pull request body has no `## Capability impact` section on a range the rule covers, the rule shall refuse it under `block` and warn under `warn`, with tracking enabled or disabled; evidence: four fixtures. (serves EPC-026 AC-3)

- AC-10 When no pull request body is available at all (the `None` path, where `crates/codeflow-cli/src/cmd/ci.rs` evaluates the body only when one is supplied), the rule shall not be skipped: it shall refuse under `block` and warn under `warn`, with tracking enabled and with it disabled, and a supplied body that lacks the section shall be tested separately; evidence: eight fixtures (unavailable body and supplied body without the section, each under both levels and both tracking states), the unavailable-body ones failing before the rule reads the body. (serves EPC-026 AC-3)
