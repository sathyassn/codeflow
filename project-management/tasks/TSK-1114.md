# TSK-1114: The docs portal reads as a designed guide

## Description

The design audit of 2026-10-31 (Source, below) read the built docs portal
(Astro Starlight 0.41 with a custom frame; `docs-portal/astro.config.mjs:19-27`) and found, beyond the shared
type and palette causes: the page title is the file's H1 verbatim, dash
included; the provenance line (source path, two hashes, versions) sits in
mono between the title and the first heading (`portal.css:185`); the
sidebar lists raw source titles with 30 decisions under one group and no
landing page per layer (`astro.config.mjs:43`); the article measure is 68ch
inside a 960px column, leaving a third of it empty (`portal.css:177`); the
dark canvas is the same neutral family with no depth; the provenance wraps
to four mono lines at 390 px. TSK-446 (todo) makes the pages anchor, lead
and draw in colour and keeps that scope; this task carries the rest, after
TSK-446 and the token and frame tasks, and the shipped starter
(`assets/docs-portal/starter/`) ships the same. Non-goal: the content of
the pages, which TSK-446 and the docs tasks own.

## Outcome

After this merges, a reader of the docs portal, this repository's or an adopter's from the starter, finds a titled, grouped, figure-led guide whose pages fill their column and read well on a phone, in every skin and mode.

## Deliverables

- the portal frame: `docs-portal/src/components/PortalTitle.astro` (title from the frontmatter title or first words), the footer (provenance moves there), the sidebar groups and layer landing pages (`portal.config.json`, `astro.config.mjs`), `portal.css` (measure, figures full width, phone header)
- the starter: `assets/docs-portal/starter/` updated to the same frame, with its tests
- guidance: `assets/base/agents/skills/cf-docs-portal/SKILL.md` and its references (grouping, landing pages, the visual bar), the `.claude/skills` and `.agents/skills` mirrors byte-identical
- tests: `docs-portal/tests/` (title rule, provenance placement, sidebar groups, landing page per layer, figure gate unchanged), screenshots at 1200 and 400 px in light and dark at Default and Large for Graphite, Slate and Sage

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-1 Before any change, the builder shall research documentation portal design broadly: how well-designed documentation sites title pages, group navigation, place provenance and build landing pages with one orienting figure (Starlight's own options included), every custom portal component and stylesheet, the starter's differences from this repository's portal, TSK-446's criteria on the line so nothing is done twice, and adjacent concerns (search, breadcrumbs, the records layer, 30 decision pages under one group, the pre-paint theme script, print); and shall record the findings with sources and the chosen scope before anything else changes; evidence: the Findings section, committed before the first code change in the PR. Listed first because it runs first (the operator's rule of 2026-10-27).
- AC-2 A page title shall come from the frontmatter title or the first words of the page, never the file's H1 with its dash, and the provenance line shall render in the footer at the `micro` size; evidence: the portal tests and `orient.png` before and after. (serves EPC-022 AC-188)
- AC-3 The sidebar shall group pages by subject with short labels and each layer shall have a landing page with one orienting figure drawn by the grammar module; evidence: the config tests, the figure gate and the index screenshot. (serves EPC-022 AC-188)
- AC-4 The article shall fill its column (measure near 66 characters for prose, figures and tables full width), the dark canvas shall carry the TSK-1110 depth, and the phone header shall show title, menu and theme with no mono line above the content at 390 px; evidence: `narrow.png` before and after and the CSS tests. (serves EPC-022 AC-188)
- AC-5 The shipped starter shall build to the same frame in a fresh `codeflow init --standard` project with the `signal` theme, and its tests shall pass; evidence: the starter build and test output.
- AC-6 (journey) With the installed build, this repository's portal and a starter portal shall each build and be captured at 1200 and 400 px, light and dark, Default and Large, in Graphite, Slate and Sage, read by the design seat against the audit's portal table; evidence: the captures and the written verdict in the PR.
