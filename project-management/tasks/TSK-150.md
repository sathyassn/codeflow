# TSK-150: reading checked by structure, sizes reported

## Description

Operator direction of 2026-09-27: no hard caps on reading. Keep the
guidance, and make context loading progressive.

Today `artifact_budget_contract.rs` fails the build when the per-task
reading chain passes 151,552 bytes (`READING_CHAIN_CAP_BYTES`, TSK-129),
and a compile-time assertion keeps that cap at least 50,000 bytes below
the 214,816-byte baseline. Every shipped skill has its own byte ratchet
(`SKILL_BYTE_RATCHETS`, for example cf-consult 6,656), with class
ceilings for skills, agent definitions and the AGENTS and CLAUDE
templates, and `rule_map_contract.rs` caps each tier's managed block at
10,240 bytes and 12 always rules. The cost shows in the records: TSK-131
landed with 76 bytes of headroom after trading sentences, TSK-144 must
move cf-herdr detail because it sits at 8,184 of 8,192 bytes, and the
3.0.0 release merge failed on bytes alone at 175,166.

This task keeps the useful half of TSK-129, the progressive map and the
inventory of conditional reads, and drops bytes as a failure:

- The test enforces structure: a small always-read kernel, every other
  read reachable through an index entry or a reviewed trigger at the
  moment it is needed, nothing orphaned, and no conditional read without
  a trigger.
- Size becomes a reported measure with guideline numbers: the kernel, the
  chain total and each skill's size show in `codeflow doctor`, as
  information or a warning, never a failure. The former caps and
  ratchets become those guideline numbers.
- Duty maps stay as review evidence when content moves, not as a byte
  ledger.

One byte check stays a failure: the generated `AGENTS.md` with a
realistic adopter section must fit Codex's 32 KiB instruction limit
(`CODEX_INSTRUCTION_LIMIT_BYTES`; `init_e2e.rs` lines 640 to 648 at
`17a18529e`), because past it the host silently cuts shipped rules in
every adopter project. That is a host truncation point, not a reading
budget; doctor already warns an adopter whose own chain passes it. At
the release merge the other lines' copies of the budget test (their
larger root caps) resolve to this line's version.

The operator also asked that nothing shipped was shaved to meet a byte
budget and that nothing key was lost. A read-only audit on a Codex seat
(scratchpad `audit-byte-cuts.md`, 2026-09-27, pinned at `17a18529e` for
this line; committed by this task as
`docs/verification/tsk-150-byte-cut-audit.md`) classified 120 passage
groups: 41 MOVED, 43 DUPLICATE, 12 CONDENSED-OK, 9 LOST and 15
LOST-MINOR. The rows to restore:

|---|---|---|
| This line (restored by this task) | Q01, H27 | Q27, Q53, Q60, Q63, Q65, H04, H06, H31 |
| This line, delivered by TSK-106 (amended 2026-09-27) | Q52 | |

| Other lines only (see the disposition owners below) | R01, R02, H36 | X01, E04, E05, E06, H20, H37 |

Disposition owners for the nine other-line rows: R01 and R02 (the
rehearsal only) TSK-010; H36 and H37 (EPC-018) the EPC-018 lead; X01,
E04, E05 and E06 (EPC-016) the EPC-016 lead; H20 (EPC-016 and EPC-014)
both leads. Each row needs an accepted restoration or a justified policy
supersession, accepted by TSK-010, before the combined release is ready;
H36 needs a policy judgement and X01 is an editorial loss, so neither is
restored blindly.

The audit ranks Q01 (the Claude lifecycle adapter trigger narrowed to
Codex hosts, which drops Grok) and Q52 (the nonbreaking migration
exception dropped from the always-read release rule) first.

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-1 When a shipped skill, reference or resource is neither in the
  kernel nor reachable from an entry point through an index entry or a
  reviewed trigger, or a conditional read names no trigger, the reading
  test shall fail and name the file; evidence: `artifact_budget_contract`
  with fault fixtures for an orphaned file, a conditional read with no
  trigger and a reclassified trigger, each failing for its stated reason.
- AC-2 When any shipped artifact, the kernel, a tier's managed block or
  the reading chain exceeds its guideline number, no test shall fail on
  that size; the only byte failure left is the complete generated
  `AGENTS.md` with a realistic adopter section over Codex's 32 KiB
  instruction limit (the `init_e2e` full-document check), and an adopter's
  own oversize stays a doctor warning; evidence: a fixture that doubles a
  skill's size and passes, a fixture whose managed block is under 32 KiB
  but whose complete document with the adopter section crosses it and
  fails, and the diff removing the 50,000-byte assertion, the chain cap,
  the skill ratchets, the class ceilings and the rule-map block and
  rule-count caps as failures.
- AC-3 When `codeflow doctor` runs, it shall report the kernel size, the
  reading chain total and each skill's size against its guideline number,
  as information within the guideline and a warning above it that names
  moving detail behind a trigger as the step that clears it; evidence:
  doctor tests at and above a guideline.
- AC-4 When content moves between the kernel, an index and a triggered
  section, the pull request shall carry a duty map showing each duty's
  new home, and the pinned-duty tests shall keep every duty; no byte
  ledger is required; evidence: the duty-pin tests
  (`agents_byte_efficiency_cannot_delete_semantic_duties` and siblings)
  passing, and the charter section 4.4 text.
- AC-5 Every LOST and LOST-MINOR row the audit lists for this line, apart
  from Q52, shall be restored in its right progressive place, the kernel or
  a triggered section. Q52 is delivered by TSK-106 (its AC-11), because
  TSK-106 rewrites the `cf-ship` release guidance the row reconciles with
  (amended 2026-09-27). For H24, H26, H29 and X03, which the audit says may be
  superseded by later policy, the design seat shall record restore or
  superseded with the reason before the change. The nine rows that exist
  only on other lines shall be carried in the committed audit as a
  disposition table (row, line, owner, disposition), bound to TSK-010 and
  the line leads as listed in the Description. The restoring change shall
  land with or after the cap removal so no test is red in between;
  evidence: the committed audit and a before and after mapping (audit row,
  restored text, file and trigger) in the pull request.
- AC-6 Live shipped doctrine and repository docs shall no longer tell
  agents or builders to stay byte-neutral or within a byte budget, and
  shall state the guideline numbers as reported measures; frozen
  historical records (implemented specs such as SPC-007, complete tasks,
  `CHANGELOG.md` entries and `docs/verification/`) keep their text as
  history; evidence: a search of the shipped tree and `docs/` in the pull
  request showing no remaining live instruction of that kind.
- AC-7 (journey) In a freshly scaffolded standard-tier project driven by
  the installed CLI, the kernel shall be loaded at session start (the
  managed block and the session-start digest), a triggered section shall
  be reachable from its index entry in the installed tree, and `codeflow
  doctor` shall report sizes without failing when a skill is over its
  guideline; evidence: an `init_e2e` or `hooks_cli` journey. This proves
  structure and installation now. The live load of a triggered section at
  its moment is delivered later by TSK-128's re-injection and proved by
  TSK-128 and the epic close; TSK-128's prompt corpus does not prove every
  restored trigger.
