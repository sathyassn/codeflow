# TSK-1161: A push is judged against the line its task record targets

## Description

Judge a task branch's push against the line its record targets when the destination advertises that line, and treat an empty range whose head the line already contains as no change. Today the pre-push hook judges a task branch against the destination's default branch (`judged_by`, `crates/codeflow-cli/src/cmd/push_set.rs:1016`; `--policy-from` at `:334`; `ci_target` at `:366`), while `codeflow ci --branch` takes the target from the head's task record (`work_target_at`, `crates/codeflow-cli/src/cmd/ci.rs:1822-1830`; `work.stable_planning_anchor` at `ci.rs:1840-1870`). A push can therefore be refused, or judged against the wrong base, though ci on the same head passes. Found in TSK-1124's work; the issue 454 family, whose criteria half is TSK-350 AC-13. Non-goal: no change to criteria-authority rules beyond which line is the authority. Planned grade: Medium, 2 to 4 hours (2026-11-01). It follows TSK-350 because both edit the pre-push call in `push_set.rs` (one writer at a time).

- AC-1 When the destination advertises the line a task branch's head record targets, the pre-push hook shall judge the push against that line, the authority `codeflow ci --branch` takes from the record, and not against the destination's default branch alone. Evidence: a fixture where `codeflow ci --branch` passes on a head and a real pre-push of the same head is refused before the change and admitted after. (journey) (serves EPC-022 AC-47)
- AC-2 When a push's range is empty because its head is already contained in the target line, the hook shall treat it as no change and admit it. Evidence: a fixture for an empty range already on the line, admitted. (serves EPC-022 AC-47)
- AC-3 When the destination does not advertise the target line, the judging shall fail closed, and trust shall bind to the line's content and exact SHAs, never to a branch name. Evidence: a negative fixture refused both before and after, and one where a branch of the same name holds different content. (serves EPC-022 AC-47)
