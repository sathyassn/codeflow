# TSK-1159: capability migrate resumes after interruption and a repaired merge passes

## Description

Split from TSK-1137 when the page 2 rubric scored that task 10 (Very Hard). This task is the safe interruption and repair half of `codeflow capability migrate` (design unit U2): the rerun verifies each existing record's `uid` against its binding, refuses a record whose payload differs from its fence, completes the rest, and strips the fences only after every record verified. It also covers the judgement of a converted entry and the offline duplicate repair. Basis: interruption fixtures at each write step and the merge-result cases. Security review owed: the resume logic decides what is overwritten and what is stripped. Non-goal: the conversion itself (TSK-1137).

Design: `coordination/coordinators/capabilities/design-note.md` revision 5 and its acceptance tests.

Consolidated 2026-11-05 (third consolidation amendment, from the value audit ruled by coord/records): merged back into TSK-1137, which it was split from. It shares the command, the design unit (U2) and EPC-026 AC-5; its criteria AC-1 to AC-3 are kept there word for word as AC-4 to AC-6, and the edge to TSK-1135 moved with them.

## Outcome

After this merges, a catalog conversion that is interrupted can be rerun to completion without losing or overwriting a record, and two pull requests that allocated the same capability id offline can be repaired and merged.

## Deliverables

- resume and verification: `crates/codeflow-core/src/capability/migrate.rs` (the resume path) and `crates/codeflow-cli/src/cmd/capability.rs`
- the interrupted-state refusal in `validate --docs`: `crates/codeflow-core/src/validate/docs.rs`
- fixtures at each write step and for the merge cases: `crates/codeflow-cli/tests/`
