# TSK-385: The quick gate runs the cheap repository-wide contract checks

## Description

Source: landing candidates 12 and 14 on 2026-10-18 (workspace `RELEASE-PLAN.md`, 2026-10-18 morning and midday). Builders now cite targeted tests and the quick gate (AC-57, TSK-360), but quick does not run the cheap repository-wide contract tests. Three landing candidates failed on them alone: PR 206 and PR 222 pushed `docs/cli.md`, and PR 222 `docs/capabilities.md`, over the Technical word budget (`guide_altitude_budget`); PR 237 added a remedy catalogue row with no `remedy_clearing` entry; candidate 14 failed `name_decode_scan` on TSK-375's new `trim()`. Each runs in seconds. Today quick holds only `contract-scans` (`git_spawn_contract`, `artifact_budget_contract`) and `repository-update-noop` (`.codeflow/test-config.json`).

Outcome: **a unit that fails a cheap repository-wide contract check is stopped at the builder's quick gate and pre-push, not at the landing candidate.**

Scope: every integration test file under `crates/codeflow-core/tests/` that scans the whole repository or its records and runs in a few seconds (including `name_decode_scan`, `manifest_consistency`, `guide_altitude_budget`, `policy_key_placement`, `pipe_drain_rule`, `stdin_pipe_rule`, `history_read_scan`, `rule_map_contract`), the `remedy_clearing` catalogue check and the derived lookups (`docs/cli.md` command table and similar). The selection rule is recorded before any scan is excluded: a whole-repository scan joins quick when its median of three runs on the reference host is under 15 seconds; the builder measures each, lists the chosen set with its timings, and states each one left out with its reason. The three replays in AC-2 hold whatever the rule selects. The set is selected by a rule (a whole-repository scan under a time bound), so a new scan joins without a hand edit where the harness allows.

Not adopter-facing: this repository's gate config and contract only.

Added by the EPC-022 amendment of 2026-10-18 under the operator's release decision of that day (workspace `RELEASE-PLAN.md`, 2026-10-18 operator decision).
