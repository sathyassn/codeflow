# TSK-1137: capability migrate converts the legacy catalog into records and repairs offline id clashes

## Description

The tool half of design unit U2. `codeflow capability migrate` registers the legacy numbers with minted `uid`s, extracts each entry's fence fields and following prose into a record (with page and anchor merge for entries that own a page), and refuses an ambiguous prose span before any write. It also covers the verified resume after interruption: the rerun verifies each existing record's `uid` against its binding, refuses a record whose payload differs from its fence, completes the rest and strips the fences only after every record verified, with the judgement of a converted entry and the offline duplicate repair. These were split out as TSK-1159 when the page 2 rubric scored the whole task 10 (Very Hard) and were merged back on 2026-11-05 (third consolidation amendment), because the two halves share one command, one design unit and EPC-026 AC-5. Basis: the conversion verb and its byte-comparison fixtures. Security review owed: the verb writes repository files from parsed content, and the resume logic decides what is overwritten and what is stripped; the review covers both. Non-goal: running it on CodeFlow's own catalog (TSK-1138).

Design: `coordination/coordinators/capabilities/design-note.md` revision 5 (workspace repository) and its acceptance tests.

## Outcome

After this merges, a project with a legacy fence catalog can convert it to records with one command, an interrupted conversion reruns to completion without losing or overwriting a record, and an offline id clash can be repaired and merged.

## Deliverables

- the verb: `crates/codeflow-cli/src/cmd/capability.rs` and `crates/codeflow-core/src/capability/migrate.rs`
- legacy registration through the ids registry
- the resume and verification path (moved from TSK-1159): `crates/codeflow-core/src/capability/migrate.rs` and `crates/codeflow-cli/src/cmd/capability.rs`
- the interrupted-state refusal in `validate --docs`: `crates/codeflow-core/src/validate/docs.rs`
- fixtures for the conversion, each write step and the merge cases: `crates/codeflow-cli/tests/`

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-1 `migrate` on a 17-entry fixture shall first register 17 numbers with minted `uid`s, then produce 17 records whose fields equal the fence fields and whose prose equals the following prose byte for byte, with page and anchor entries keeping their pages; evidence: a byte comparison test. (serves EPC-026 AC-5)
- AC-2 An ambiguous prose span shall refuse before any write; evidence: a fixture and a no-write assertion. (serves EPC-026 AC-5)
- AC-3 (added 2026-11-02, journey amendment: the task had no journey criterion; this one drives its own change with the built binary) In a temporary copy of a repository with a 17-entry legacy fence catalog, the built binary's `capability migrate` shall exit 0 and write 17 records, and `validate --docs` shall then be green; evidence: the command transcript and the record count. (journey)
- AC-4 An interrupted run shall leave the fences in place; the rerun shall verify each existing record's `uid` against its binding, refuse a record whose payload differs from its fence, complete the rest and strip fences only after every record verified; a third run shall change nothing; committing the interrupted state shall be refused by `validate --docs`; evidence: an interruption test at each write step. (serves EPC-026 AC-5)
  - Moved 2026-11-05 from TSK-1159 AC-1 by the third consolidation amendment, which closed TSK-1159 into TSK-1137; the criterion text is unchanged.
- AC-5 A PR that converts one entry and edits its frontmatter shall be judged as one `changed` identity; two offline allocations of the same id shall be refused on the merge result naming both files, and `ids retarget` shall renumber one keeping its `uid` and writing `former_ids`, after which the repaired merge result shall pass `validate --docs` and the capability check; evidence: fixtures for the refusal and the passing repaired merge. (serves EPC-026 AC-5)
  - Moved 2026-11-05 from TSK-1159 AC-2 by the third consolidation amendment, which closed TSK-1159 into TSK-1137; the criterion text is unchanged.
- AC-6 (added 2026-11-02, journey amendment: the task had no journey criterion; this one drives its own change with the built binary) In a temporary repository, a `capability migrate` that ends after its first record shall leave a state that `validate --docs` refuses when committed, and a rerun of the built binary shall finish with the fences stripped and `validate --docs` green; evidence: the transcript of the interrupted run, the refusal, the rerun and the check. (journey)
  - Moved 2026-11-05 from TSK-1159 AC-3 by the third consolidation amendment, which closed TSK-1159 into TSK-1137; the criterion text is unchanged.
