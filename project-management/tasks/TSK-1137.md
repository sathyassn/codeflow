# TSK-1137: capability migrate converts the legacy catalog with a verified resume

## Description

The tool half of design unit U2. `codeflow capability migrate` registers the legacy numbers with minted `uid`s, extracts each entry's fence fields and following prose into a record (with page and anchor merge for entries that own a page), and resumes safely: the rerun verifies each existing record's `uid` against its binding, refuses a record whose payload differs from its fence, and strips the fences only after every record verified. Basis: about 400 lines plus interruption fixtures. Security review owed: the verb writes repository files from parsed content and its resume logic decides what is overwritten. Non-goal: running it on CodeFlow's own catalog (TSK-1138).

Design: `coordination/coordinators/capabilities/design-note.md` revision 5 (workspace repository) and its acceptance tests.

## Outcome

After this merges, a project with a legacy fence catalog can convert it to records with one command that is safe to interrupt and rerun.

## Deliverables

- the verb: `crates/codeflow-cli/src/cmd/capability.rs` and `crates/codeflow-core/src/capability/migrate.rs`
- legacy registration through the ids registry
- fixtures including interruption: `crates/codeflow-cli/tests/`

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-1 `migrate` on a 17-entry fixture shall first register 17 numbers with minted `uid`s, then produce 17 records whose fields equal the fence fields and whose prose equals the following prose byte for byte, with page and anchor entries keeping their pages; evidence: a byte comparison test. (serves EPC-026 AC-5)
- AC-2 An ambiguous prose span shall refuse before any write; evidence: a fixture and a no-write assertion. (serves EPC-026 AC-5)
- AC-3 An interrupted run shall leave the fences in place; the rerun shall verify each existing record's `uid` against its binding, refuse a record whose payload differs from its fence, complete the rest and strip fences only after every record verified; a third run shall change nothing; committing the interrupted state shall be refused by `validate --docs`; evidence: an interruption test at each write step. (serves EPC-026 AC-5)
- AC-4 A PR that converts one entry and edits its frontmatter shall be judged as one `changed` identity; two offline allocations of the same id shall be refused on the merge result naming both files, and `ids retarget` shall renumber one keeping its `uid` and writing `former_ids`; evidence: fixtures. (serves EPC-026 AC-5)
