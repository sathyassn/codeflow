# TSK-037: reconcile the utility presentation doctrine

## Description

The cf-docs-portal and cf-present skills each carry their own drifted account
of the utility presentation system, and both promise carriers the adapter does
not render, so authors cannot tell what a compliant page is. This task settles
one shared `utility-presentation-system.md` text for both skills with only the
profile-specific parts isolated in each skill's references, states the
composition rule and the page-class rule, truncates the carrier list to what
the adapter actually supports, and gives the doctrine a durable home in
`docs/architecture/utility-presentation.md` with a short ADR-0063 clarifying
precedence over ADR-0053. Non-goal: no new theme, no adapter feature is added
to make an unsupported carrier true; unsupported carriers are dropped or
marked, not implemented.

## Affected surfaces and interfaces

`.claude/skills/cf-docs-portal/**` and `.claude/skills/cf-present/**` with
their `.agents` and `assets/base` mirrors and the scaffold-manifest hashes;
new `docs/architecture/utility-presentation.md`; new `docs/decisions/ADR-0063`.
ADR-0053 is append-only and is not edited. CAP-015 and CAP-016 authoring
contract.
