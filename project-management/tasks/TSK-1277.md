# TSK-1277: A landing candidate's check judges each member at its own reviewed head

## Description

`codeflow ci --line-tip integration/EPC-022... --base origin/<line> --head <candidate>` on a multi-member landing candidate judges the whole range as one pull request. On candidate c2 (`8a32a233b`, eight reviewed members) it exited 1 with 167 blocks, none of them a member defect (evidence: `coordination/coordinators/planning/evidence/candidate-ci/c2-ci5.txt`, 742 lines, kept outside the repository):

- 151 `acceptance_binding` blocks, "outside its verified source and reviewed task heads", each naming another member's files
- 7 baseline-workflow ordering blocks, 6 `criteria_frozen` blocks across records, and 1 `branch_naming` block on `candidate/`
- one false "the archive must hold every completion this range made, in order" for TSK-350, whose record is identical at PR 501's head `bc2a4c2ad`, where hosted codeflow gates passed (run 41570636030)

Duplicate check (coord/planning): TSK-1268 changes the landing rules and `release.py check-pr`, not `codeflow ci`, and this task is its follow-up (cited here as context). TSK-1154 covers only one task's own criteria change through one candidate merge. TSK-1181 changes only a finding's message. TSK-1188 is hosted-run credit. None judges each member at its own head.

The dependency on TSK-1154 is stated because both change how `codeflow ci` judges a candidate's merges: this task generalizes TSK-1154's single-merge acceptance to every member, so the builder starts from TSK-1154's change rather than a parallel one.

Owner: coord/planning (builder to be staffed). Non-goal: hosted run credit (TSK-1188) and the landing rules (TSK-1268) do not change here.

## Outcome

After this merges, a primary can run one command on a multi-member landing candidate and get a verdict that blames only a real member defect, a non-merge commit or a hand-resolved hunk.

## Deliverables

- the candidate judging in `codeflow ci` and its fixture tests: the files under `crates/codeflow-cli/` and `crates/codeflow-core/` that the builder confirms
- the documentation of what the candidate check proves and the command a primary runs: `docs/delivery.md` and `.codeflow/rules/git-rules.md` "Bodies of work", with their mirrors
- the release note: `changelog.d/TSK-1277.md`

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-1 Before any code, the c2 shape shall be reproduced in a fixture (a line tip plus no-ff merges of three or more reviewed member heads, each with its own record and completion), and the record shall say which checks treat the candidate as one pull request and why. Evidence: the fixture test, failing before the change; the check list in the pull request.
- AC-2 `codeflow ci` on a landing candidate (selected by `--line-tip`, or a named mode the builder states) shall judge each member merge at its own reviewed head against the tip it merged onto. A clean multi-member batch shall pass, and the AC-1 fixture shall pass after the change. Evidence: the fixture test.
- AC-3 It shall still refuse, naming the member: a member head with a real defect (such as an unbound completion or a foreign criteria change); a non-merge commit on the candidate; and a merge whose result differs from the clean remerge of its parents (a hand-resolved hunk), which shall also name the hunk and say that the member's whole-unit review reopens. Evidence: one test per refusal.
- AC-4 Binding shall use exact SHAs and trusted line records, never branch names: the `candidate/` branch name shall raise no `branch_naming` finding, and a forged member, or one whose head is not a reviewed task head, shall be refused. Evidence: a test for each. (serves the security lens below)
- AC-5 `docs/delivery.md` and the git-rules "Bodies of work" section (and their mirrors) shall say what the candidate check proves and the command a primary runs. Evidence: the doc diff and the mirror check.
- AC-6 The real c2-shaped candidate shall pass with the built binary, and a doctored variant shall be refused. Evidence: the two runs with the built binary's path, version and source revision. (journey)
- AC-7 A changelog entry shall describe the change. Evidence: `changelog.d/TSK-1277.md`.
