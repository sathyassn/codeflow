# TSK-1510: The dependency audit fails when osv-scanner reports a file it could not extract

## Description

The security review job's dependency audit step treats some osv-scanner results as "nothing to audit". When osv-scanner reports that it could not extract a dependency file, the step shall fail, whatever the size of the scanner's output. On `main` 8aa375e4c the step's decision for that case does not always hold. The coordinators measured it on 2026-11-15 while reviewing TSK-365, and the details stay in the workspace until the fix is on `main`. The secret scan job's gitleaks step has the same kind of check: when gitleaks logs an ERR or FTL line, the step shall fail, whatever the size of its log.

Both checks live in three copies: the shipped template `assets/base/ci/codeflow-ci.yml` (the dependency audit step's check around :1176 and the gitleaks step's check around :909 on `main` 8aa375e4c), this repository's live workflow `.github/workflows/codeflow-ci.yml` (around :2032 and :1754), and its managed baseline copy `.codeflow/.baseline/.github/workflows/codeflow-ci.yml`. The builder changes the template. The live workflow and the baseline copy change only through an operator script that applies a reviewed patch, never by an agent edit.

Non-goals: no change to which scanners run, to their severity levels, suppressions or exemptions, or to any step other than these two checks.

Source: coord/ci, 2026-11-15, from the Grok review of TSK-365 (the step text is the line base's and main's, not TSK-365's); filed by coord/records at the Director's 17:28 agreement.

## Outcome

After this merges, an adopter whose dependency audit hits a scan or extraction error sees the security review job fail, rather than pass as "nothing to audit".

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-1 When osv-scanner exits with a scan or extraction error, or its output cannot be read, the dependency audit step in the shipped template shall fail the job at block level, whatever the size of the output; the step shall take the scanner's output once and decide from it without a construct whose result can depend on output size or timing. Evidence: a template test whose scanner output is larger than 64 KiB with the error reported early, failing before the change and passing after it, plus the existing "nothing to audit" case still passing. (security)
- AC-1b When gitleaks logs an ERR or FTL line, or its output cannot be read, the secret scan job's gitleaks step in the shipped template shall fail the job, whatever the size of the output; the step shall take gitleaks' output once and decide from it without a construct whose result can depend on output size or timing. Evidence: for both steps (the dependency audit and the secret scan), a fake scanner mode that prints the error plus output larger than 64 KiB, failing before the change and passing after it. Widened 2026-11-15 by the Director's ruling (18:45): takes TSK-450 AC-8, moved here. (security)
