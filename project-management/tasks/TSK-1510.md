# TSK-1510: The dependency audit and the secret scan fail when their scanner reports an error

## Description

The security review job's dependency audit step treats some osv-scanner results as "nothing to audit". When osv-scanner reports that it could not extract a dependency file, the step shall fail, whatever the size of the scanner's output. On `main` 8aa375e4c the step's decision for that case does not always hold. The coordinators measured it on 2026-11-15 while reviewing TSK-365, and the details stay in the workspace until the fix is on `main`. The secret scan job's gitleaks step has the same kind of check: when gitleaks logs an ERR or FTL line, the step shall fail, whatever the size of its log.

Both checks live in three copies: the shipped template `assets/base/ci/codeflow-ci.yml` (the dependency audit step's check around :1176 and the gitleaks step's check around :909 on `main` 8aa375e4c), this repository's live workflow `.github/workflows/codeflow-ci.yml` (around :2032 and :1754), and its managed baseline copy `.codeflow/.baseline/.github/workflows/codeflow-ci.yml`. The builder changes the template. The live workflow and the baseline copy change only through an operator script that applies a reviewed patch, never by an agent edit.

Non-goals: no change to which scanners run, or to their severity levels, suppressions or exemptions. Other steps in the shipped workflows change only as AC-6 requires.

Source: coord/ci, 2026-11-15, from the Grok review of TSK-365 (the step text is the line base's and main's, not TSK-365's); filed by coord/records at the Director's 17:28 agreement.

## Outcome

After this merges, an adopter whose dependency audit hits a scanner error sees it reported at the level the project's policy sets for the audit (the job fails at block, and at warn it reports the error as a warning rather than passing as clean), and an adopter whose secret scan does not complete sees the job fail.
