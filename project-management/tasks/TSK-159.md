# TSK-159: present composer opens after Add text under load

## Description

In the TSK-119 gates at `a7709c992`, the Firefox run of `check:browser`
(`CF_PRESENT_CHIP_ENGINES=firefox,webkit`) failed once: after the check
selected the fixture text and pressed the Add text tool, the composer never
opened (`saveComposerNote` waited 10 s for `composer-text`,
`browser-check.mjs` "Text excerpt check."). The load average was falling
from 99 at the time; a rerun at load 9 passed, and the web bundle was
byte-identical to `aa32da0e5`, where the run also passed. The last two
flakes on this surface were product defects (the TSK-071 keyboard walk and
the TSK-096 one-frame focus loss), so a pass on rerun is not a disposition.
Find the cause and fix it. Non-goal: changing the composer's design.

## Affected surfaces and interfaces

`crates/codeflow-present/web/src/chrome.tsx` and `selection.ts` on the
text-capture and composer-open path, or, if the cause is the check, the
Firefox path of `web/scripts/browser-check.mjs`; the present rebuild and its
checksum. No server, envelope or CLI change.

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-1 The cause is traced: the failure is reproduced under CPU load (CPU

      the text selection through the Add text tool to the composer, and the

## Closeout

The composer loss was a product race, not check timing. The chrome pins a
text selection 160 ms after `selectionchange`, from a timer local to the
comment-mode effect. Opening the composer reruns that effect, but Preact
clears effects only after the next paint. On a stalled main thread the
overdue pin ran after a tool press and pinned with the composer closed, or
landed between the press and its click, which then never arrived. The fix
is at `95c8aab29` on `task/TSK-159-firefox-composer`.

- Trace (AC-1): a scratch copy of the browser check with an event and
  MutationObserver log on the composer path ran the Firefox interactive
  surface 10 times under 16 CPU burners on the old bundle: 4 failed. Run 5
  logged the tool click, the composer attached, then the composer detached
  and a chip attached 18 ms later, at the TSK-119 failure point; runs 7 and
  10 logged the tool pointerdown, a chip 11 ms later and no click. Logs:
  `t159-repro-old.log`, harness `t159-diag.mjs` (scratchpad, not committed).
- Fix (AC-2): the pin timer lives in `selectionPinTimerRef`; a pointerdown
  on the Tools container cancels it for all four tools; the pin returns when
  a composer is open, which covers a keyboard activation.
- Test (AC-2): `selection-lifecycle-browser-check.mjs` stalls the page 250
  ms after the chrome armed its pin, then activates a tool: Add text by
  keyboard and by pointer, and Whole document by pointer. It asserts no chip
  mid-press and an open composer after. The Add text cases failed 12 of 12
  runs on the original bundle across Chromium, Firefox and WebKit; the Whole
  document case failed 4 of 4 in Firefox and in WebKit on the round-1 bundle
  and passed in Chromium, which does not always run the overdue pin first;
  the fixed bundle passed 12 of 12 (`t159-newtest-*.log`,
  `t159-r2-lifecycle.log`). Each case is an adversarial ordering, not a
  proof for every scheduler.
- Load (AC-3): under the same 16 burners the fixed bundle passed 10 of 10
  Firefox and 10 of 10 WebKit interactive runs at one-minute loads of 45 to
  360 (`t159-repro-new.log`); other work shared the host.
- Gates (AC-4) at `95c8aab29`: `npm run check` reproducible;
  `check:browser`, also with Firefox and WebKit chip engines,
  `check:matrix`, `check:entities` and `check:forms` pass;
  `cargo test -p codeflow-present` 176 passed; `present_cli` 16 passed and
  1 ignored; clippy and fmt clean (`t159-r2-*.log`).
- Behavior change: a tool press that is abandoned without a click also
  drops the pending pin; the selection stays, and a new selection or the
  tool itself pins it.
