# TSK-332: A docs portal workflow lease whose owner has exited is reclaimed at once

## Description

The docs portal's `check`, `build`, `dev` and `preview` scripts hold one
workflow lease, `.portal/workflow.lock`
(`docs-portal/scripts/publication.mjs:19`, `withWorkflowLease`). The lease
records only a token and two times (`publication.mjs:34`). A contender
treats it as live while its heartbeat is younger than
`WORKFLOW_LEASE_MAX_AGE_MS`, four hours (`publication.mjs:16`, `:50`),
although the owner writes a heartbeat every 30 seconds (`publication.mjs:66`
to `:73`). So a portal run that is killed (a timed-out gate target, a
closed terminal, `kill -9`) leaves a lease that refuses every `check` and
`build` with "portal build/check workflow already in progress" for up to
four hours, and nothing checks whether its owner still runs. The same file
ships to adopters as `assets/docs-portal/starter/scripts/publication.mjs`,
byte-identical today.

The lease owner is the workflow, not only its coordinating process. The
workflow holds the lease while it spawns the adapter, Astro and evidence
children (`docs-portal/scripts/workflow.mjs:12`, `:31` to `:36`). The
signal-aware lifecycle stops those children on `SIGINT` and `SIGTERM`
(`docs-portal/scripts/child-lifecycle.mjs`), but `SIGKILL` of the
coordinator runs no cleanup, so a child can outlive it and keep writing
`dist`, generated content or caches. A dead coordinator therefore does not
prove the workflow has stopped writing.

The outcome is that a lease whose whole workflow has stopped (the
coordinator and every writer it started) is reclaimed at once, with a
message naming the dead owner, while a live coordinator or a live writer
still blocks. The lease records its owner: the coordinator's process id
and host, plus whatever the builder needs against process id reuse (such as
a process start time), and either the writers it starts or a mechanism
that proves no writer can outlive the coordinator (for example a process
group the contender can check, or children that exit with their parent).
The builder chooses and records which. A contender on the same host checks
the coordinator and its writers before applying the age rule. Where
liveness of the coordinator or of any writer cannot be decided (another
host, a lease written by an older version without an owner, a permission
error on the check), the four-hour rule stays. A reused process id reads as
live, which blocks; that is the safe direction.

Non-goals: the publication lease (`PUBLICATION_LEASE_MAX_AGE_MS`,
`publication.mjs:15`) and changing the four-hour age for leases whose owner
cannot be checked.

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-1 A workflow lease shall record its owner (the coordinator's process
  id and host, any marker the builder adds against process id reuse, and
  the writers it starts or the mechanism that binds them to the
  coordinator) in the existing lease file, with older leases still read;
  evidence: a unit test of the written lease and of reading a lease
  without the new fields. (serves EPC-022 AC-29)
- AC-2 When the lease's coordinator on this host has exited and none of its
  writers is running, the next `check` or `build` shall reclaim the lease
  at once and print one line naming the dead owner and the lease age;
  evidence: a test whose workflow takes the lease with no child running
  and is killed with `SIGKILL`, after which a new workflow acquires the
  lease within one attempt. (serves EPC-022 AC-29)
- AC-3 While the lease's coordinator or any of its writers is alive, a
  second workflow shall still refuse with "portal build/check workflow
  already in progress", now naming the live process ids; evidence: a test
  with a live coordinator. (serves EPC-022 AC-29)
- AC-4 When the liveness of the coordinator or of any writer cannot be
  decided (a lease from another host, a lease with no owner fields, or a
  check that fails with a permission error), the contender shall not
  reclaim at once and shall keep the four-hour age rule; evidence: a test
  per case.
- AC-5 Two contenders that find the same dead owner with no live writer
  shall leave exactly one holder, and the existing lease and recovery tests shall pass unchanged;
  evidence: a race test in the style of the existing reclaim tests, and the
  `docs-portal` gate target.
- AC-6 Each changed file under `docs-portal/scripts/` and
  `docs-portal/tests/` shall stay byte-identical to its starter copy under
  `assets/docs-portal/starter/`; evidence: a byte comparison in the
  change's test run, and the starter manifest test in
  `crates/codeflow-core/tests/manifest_consistency.rs` passing.
- AC-7 (journey) During a real `npm run build` in `docs-portal/`, the run
  shall identify the lease-owning Node process (the `workflow.mjs`
  coordinator, not the `npm` wrapper) and its writer processes. With the
  coordinator and its writers killed with `SIGKILL`, a second
  `npm run build` shall start at once and print the reclaim line; the same
  shall hold in a scratch adopter portal made from the starter. Evidence:
  both runs' output with the process ids observed and killed.
- AC-8 When only the coordinator is killed with `SIGKILL` while a writer
  child is still running, a contender shall refuse to enter while that
  child runs, and shall reclaim the lease at once after the child exits;
  evidence: a test that keeps a child alive past the coordinator's death,
  asserts the refusal, ends the child and asserts the reclaim.
  (serves EPC-022 AC-29)
