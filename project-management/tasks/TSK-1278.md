# TSK-1278: Heavy targets continue while active and report stalls or ceiling timeouts

## Description

The full gate sizes a heavy target's workers from host load when the target starts (TSK-1145 AC-3: the job budget is shared, at least 1), but the target's timeout stays fixed. On candidate c2 at 01:00 (EPC-022), `rust-coverage` got `jobs=3, nextest=3` under load, against `jobs=9` on a quiet host, with `timeout_seconds: 1800` (`.codeflow/test-config.json:255`). It ran 1189 of 6827 tests, all passing, and was then killed. The log says only "completed target 'rust-coverage': FAILED", then "journey-gate: not run: prerequisite failed". A loaded host makes the gate size itself into a guaranteed timeout, and the result reads as a test failure.

Evidence (coordination/coordinators/planning/evidence/coverage-timeout/c2-gate.txt, kept outside the repository): line 224 `workers for 'rust-coverage': jobs=3, nextest=3, rust-tests=1, oracle=1`; line 227 `rust-coverage: Starting 6827 tests across 125 binaries (9 tests skipped)`; line 1416 the last pass, `(1189/6827)`; line 1417 `completed target 'rust-coverage': FAILED`.

Follow-up of TSK-1145 (complete), which added the shrinking job count but nothing about timeouts. Duplicate check (coord/planning): TSK-1147 is guidance text only, TSK-1263 is the Playwright apt cache, and nothing open covers this. The shipped test-config templates and schema under `assets/base/testing/` carry the same `timeout_seconds` key, so the change is adopter-facing; the builder confirms which shipped templates set a heavy target's timeout.

The Director's revised brief (`/private/tmp/claude/p4v-1278-design.md`, including the 04:00 addition) reports two further cases. Coverage ran about five times slower even at jobs=9 of 9, so worker-ratio scaling alone would not help. On a held host, c3a reached 6756 of 6757 passing tests at the 1800-second limit while one computing test was still running; that test took 413 seconds even on a quiet host. These are supplied evidence, not builder-reproduced measurements.

The selected design uses output or increasing process-tree CPU time as activity, a configurable 300-second stall window, and an absolute ceiling of four times the base timeout. Mere process existence does not count. Worker-ratio timeout scaling is dropped because it does not address contention at a full worker grant.

The proposed Unix own-CPU plus waited-child counter was evaluated and rejected as proof of complete accounting. The selected Linux design instead uses a fresh per-target cgroup v2 within existing writable delegation, with placement before workload execution or forking. Increasing `cpu.stat` `usage_usec` or new output counts as activity. Early stalls use validated pre-execution placement and valid cgroup accounting; missing delegation or observed membership/population anomalies select a stated ceiling-only fallback. Supervision stays outside the target cgroup. No privileged host reconfiguration or automatic delegation setup is included. macOS remains ceiling-only without a PID heuristic; the Director accepted that known limitation for 4.0.0, and the changelog shall state it.

Owner: coord/planning (builder assigned). Non-goal: the host-load sizing of workers (TSK-1145 AC-3) does not change.

## Outcome

A maintainer running the full gate on a loaded host can finish a slow, active heavy target beyond its base timeout. A silent computing test can continue beyond the stall window. A stalled target or one reaching the hard ceiling fails with a distinct reason in progress output, summaries and saved reports.

## Deliverables

- the timeout and worker handling, the summary and the report: the gate code under `crates/codeflow-cli/` and `crates/codeflow-core/` that the builder confirms, with its fixture tests

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-1 Before any code, the record shall say how the timeout and the workers are chosen today (file and line), and a test shall reproduce that a target given fewer workers than its timeout assumes is killed and reported as FAILED. Evidence: the file:line notes in the pull request, and the test failing before the change.
- AC-2 A heavy target shall continue beyond its base `timeout_seconds` while it shows activity, up to an absolute ceiling of `timeout_seconds * 4` (base default 600 seconds). Activity shall mean new stdout/stderr or increasing target-scoped CPU usage: Windows job accounting or Linux cgroup v2 `cpu.stat` `usage_usec`. A live process alone shall not count. Linux shall use a fresh per-target cgroup only within existing writable delegation, placing the workload there before it executes or forks through `CLONE_INTO_CGROUP` or a gated launch; supervision shall remain outside it. Early stalls require successful pre-execution placement and valid accounting, without a new containment mechanism. While the direct child remains live, an unpopulated group, an unexpected population notification or changed child membership shall report the anomaly and select ceiling-only for the rest of that run. The per-target `stall_timeout_seconds` defaults to 300 seconds from start or last activity. Worker-ratio scaling shall not change the ceiling. Evidence: slow output-producing work passes beyond the base timeout; silent computing work exceeds the stall window and passes; a delegated Linux cgroup with pre-execution placement and short-lived and auto-reaped children does not stall while CPU rises, then stalls once idle; uncertain accounting uses the explicit ceiling fallback.
- AC-3 A stall shall report `stalled: no progress for N s (workers W of M)`; a ceiling timeout shall report `timed out at the ceiling after S s`. Neither shall be labelled `FAILED` in target progress or the target summary. Both shall carry distinct structured statuses and nonzero target exits through the runner, gate, CLI summary and saved run report. Dependents shall say `not run: prerequisite stalled` or `not run: prerequisite timed out`, including transitive dependents. Ordinary test failures shall still report `FAILED`. A pass requires observing successful exit and completing required output collection before the ceiling; an exit first observed at or after the ceiling cannot pass. Termination, reaping, watcher shutdown and pipe draining shall have a fixed five-second cleanup grace after termination starts, never later than ceiling plus five seconds. Incomplete cleanup shall remain a non-pass with structured cleanup detail and any prior stall or ceiling cause preserved. Evidence: tests of messages, statuses, exits, dependency propagation, serialized report fields, late-observed successful exit, and a descendant that leaves the Unix process group and holds an output pipe beyond the drain deadline.
- AC-4 Fail-closed behaviour shall remain: a test failure, stall, ceiling timeout or incomplete cleanup shall fail the gate; a spinner, repeating output or CPU busy loop shall never defer the absolute ceiling. Both `timeout_seconds` and `stall_timeout_seconds` shall accept integers from 1 through 4611686018427386 seconds, inclusive, with omitted defaults 600 and 300 respectively. The multiplier is fixed at 4 with no config key. Unknown keys, tampered config, non-integers, values outside those bounds and arithmetic overflow shall be refused consistently by loader and schema. CPU accounting shall cover the target tree on Windows, macOS and Linux. An early stall requires complete accounting over the inactivity interval: Unix live-process snapshots alone shall not prove inactivity or account for unseen short-lived children and unobserved final CPU of exited children. Missing, unreadable or incomplete accounting shall report its uncertainty and select the ceiling fallback, never establish a pass. Host-process churn shall never count as target progress; uncertainty it exposes shall select the ceiling fallback. Windows job totals include terminated members; Unix observations shall not claim that guarantee without a complete source. Only successful completion and collection before the deadline plus existing gate checks can pass. Evidence: deterministic clock/accounting tests, ordinary failure and invalid-config fixtures, child CPU and PID-lifetime tests, short-lived children spanning the stall window, host-process churn, inclusive minimum/maximum and adjacent-invalid config values through loader and schema, explicit accounting-unavailable fallback tests for Windows, macOS and Linux, and native platform tests with unavailable platforms disclosed.
- AC-5 `docs/cli.md`, the troubleshooting page and the test config reference shall describe activity, `stall_timeout_seconds`, the four-times ceiling, accounting fallback, status messages and spinner limitations. The schema and shipped templates shall match; targets outside the heavy classification shall retain their current fixed timeout. Evidence: doc diff, schema/template checks and light-target regression tests.
- AC-6 With the built binary under a constrained job budget (`execution.host_jobs`), coverage shall either complete or report a stall or ceiling timeout distinctly. A real child computing without output beyond a shortened stall window shall pass. A sleeping child shall stall with complete accounting or explicitly use the ceiling fallback with incomplete accounting; a continuously active command shall reach the ceiling. Evidence: saved outputs and run reports with binary path, version and source revision; native platform results and any unexercised boundary shall be stated. (journey)
- AC-7 A changelog entry shall describe the change. Evidence: `changelog.d/TSK-1278.md`.
- AC-8 Every unfinished heavy target, including a ceiling-only target with no new output, shall emit a progress line at least every 300 seconds. It shall show tests done when available, otherwise explicitly unknown, and CPU state with source or unavailability reason. The gate's own line shall not count as target activity or extend the stall window or ceiling. Evidence: deterministic clock tests across successive intervals, with known and unknown test counts, CPU-accounting fallback, and no change to deadline behavior.

The ceiling multiplier shall be one named constant, `HEAVY_TIMEOUT_MULTIPLIER = 4`, with a comment recording the Director's basis: about four times the supplied quiet coverage runtime of 1713 seconds, with TSK-1279 removing the self-contention behind the observed fivefold slowdown. All deadline arithmetic and validation bounds shall derive from that constant. Coverage's 1800-second base therefore has a 7200-second ceiling. This basis is supplied evidence, not a builder measurement.

Linux fallback under AC-3 and AC-4 shall state `early stall unavailable: no delegated cgroup` when delegation is absent, and a specific accounting, population or membership reason when uncertain. macOS shall state `early stall unavailable: macOS complete descendant accounting unavailable` in progress, summary and run report, with no no-new-PID heuristic. No privileged host reconfiguration, controller reconfiguration or automatic delegation setup shall be attempted.

Accounting checks under AC-4 shall cover waited short-lived children, an orphaned child, children auto-reaped under `SIGCHLD=SIG_IGN` or `SA_NOCLDWAIT`, unrelated host churn and concurrent targets. Linux cgroup tests shall cover pre-execution placement, CPU growth followed by idle stall, no-delegation fallback, unexpected population transitions and live-child membership changes, and cleanup. Use `cgroup.kill` where available, confirm `cgroup.events` reports the owned subtree unpopulated before removal, and bound all cleanup by AC-3. Missing kill support or failed cleanup shall retain failure detail and the existing deadline; an unconfirmed target shall not be treated as terminated. macOS fallback shall have an explicit platform test. A Linux CI test shall exercise the no-delegation fallback even when the host cannot run a delegated-cgroup journey. Runtime and schema checks retain the AC-4 config boundaries.

The AC-3 result shall carry a typed `termination_unconfirmed` flag plus target identity and retained termination evidence. Related task TSK-1279 owns the allocation/lease response: when this flag is true, the job allocation and exclusive host lease remain held until termination is confirmed or the gate exits. This task produces and propagates the flag through runner, gate and saved report; it does not release those resources merely because bounded cleanup returned. Pipe-collection failure alone is distinct from unconfirmed process termination. Evidence: result/serialization tests and the TSK-1279 consumer contract, with integrated lease enforcement verified by that task.

## Design delta requested by coordination

Coord/planning revised the containment requirement after implementation at
`2c22179db`. CodeFlow has no existing containment launcher, and this task
shall not introduce one. Same-user migration can undercount activity and
cause a false early stall, which fails the gate. It cannot turn an unfinished
target into a pass: successful exit and output collection before the ceiling
remain required. A test can already fail directly. Coordination therefore
treats deliberate migration as a reliability concern and assumes ordinary
tests do not migrate cgroups.

This replaces AC-2's prevention/proof requirement with pre-execution placement,
valid accounting and anomaly fallback. A held `cgroup.events` descriptor
records unexpected populated-state transitions, including a transition out
and back between samples. The live launch child's `/proc/<pid>/cgroup`
membership is checked as well. Normal worker forks and exits do not change
populated state while that child remains in the group. These checks do not
claim to detect every individual descendant migration; an unobserved migration
can cause a false failure. No new namespace, launcher or host setup is added.

The delta needs Codex p3R confirmation before landing. The earlier requirement
was removed for the reliability reasoning above, not to permit a gate pass.

## Execution contract

Verification includes a table-driven property sweep of elapsed time, activity
time and stall windows. The deadline state machine's broad combinations and
independent invariants justify it: the ceiling always wins, and incomplete
accounting never permits an early stall. Native child-process and CLI journeys
remain required alongside that sweep. Platform module compilation does not
replace native Linux or Windows accounting evidence.
