# TSK-423: The managed rules and skills require the ledger and lanes

## Description

Issue #272, item 1 and its acceptance, under ADR-0086. With the store
(TSK-422), lanes (TSK-439) and hooks (TSK-440) in place, nothing yet
tells a session to use them: `workflow-discipline.md` "Sessions and
state" names no home for open commitments and no closure rule, and
`cf-model-orchestrator` names a task ledger with no shape.

Outcome: **every guidance home the operator named says how the ledger
and lanes are used: the managed `AGENTS.md` block (one always-rule and
one map row), `cf-model-orchestrator` (coordinator duties, lanes, the sweep),
`cf-herdr` and `cf-delegate` (seat-lane binding, resume), `cf-method`
(the concepts: session, coordinator, lane, seat), the per-harness notes
(`CLAUDE.md`, Codex and Grok), `docs/architecture.md` beside
ADR-0086, and the CLI help; and a fresh-context qualification shows a
coordinator reporting three handed-over items, one stalled, without being
asked.**

Rules the guidance states:

- The coordinator logs every operator-handed item and every unsettled or
  untasked item at once, with its fields; an item closes only on proof,
  never on "handed to a lane"; the coordinator reads the ledger at session
  start (the digest shows it), after compaction and before every
  status reply (the Stop hook flags a miss), and builds status from it.
- A holder is `coordinator` or a lane id; the coordinator claims the lease, sweeps
  all items, opens and closes lanes, owns operator asks and builds
  status from `follow list` with operator items under "needs you"; a
  lane sees and closes its own items and the operator items, and adds
  items for what it hands back or finds; a lone session is its own
  coordinator; a seat attaches to a lane, is labelled by its id and resumes it
  by its recorded resume id after a restart.
- The five enforcement layers of ADR-0086 are named, with the timer as
  an optional aid and the per-harness notes saying which hooks fire in
  each harness and giving the timer recipe; the guidance says the
  ledger is an aid, never a control: records, pull request bodies and
  `codeflow ci` never cite FU or LN ids, and an item that becomes real
  work becomes a task through normal planning citing its real origin.
- The managed map gains one item ("hand over, receive or find an open
  item: `codeflow follow`; delegate: `codeflow lane open`"), the
  "report status or hand off" row (which TSK-387 extends) and the
  "start a session" row name the ledger, and one always-rule states
  the duty: log every handed-over or untasked item, follow it up, close
  only on proof.

Non-goals: the commands and hooks (TSK-422, TSK-439, TSK-440); a
mandatory timer.
