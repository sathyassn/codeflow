# TSK-1306: A push gets its codeflow ci verdict inside the project's budget

## Description

A push runs `codeflow ci` before the gate selection, and on a records-only head it takes far longer than the 60 s push budget: 124 s of `ci` and 12 s of preflight at load 134 to 181 (coord/ci, 2026-11-03), and 26 minutes on a debug binary for the regroup head of 700 records (PR 562, 2026-11-03). Nothing reports where that time goes. This task makes the time visible inside `ci`, cuts what it spends on a push, and makes the budget a project setting. It is the remainder of issues 371 and 494 after TSK-1039 and TSK-1259, which handle gate target selection and not `ci` itself. Non-goal: weakening any check; every check still runs on what the push changes.

## Outcome

After this merges, a builder pushing a records-only or small head gets the `ci` verdict inside the project's push budget, and can see which check spent the time when it does not.

## Deliverables

- A per-check time breakdown in `codeflow ci`: provisional home in the ci command code; AC-1 decides.
- The fixes the breakdown names: the checks it blames.
- The push budget setting, in the limits registry (TSK-1299) once that lands, else in project policy and migrated by TSK-1299's route.
- the ordering in the pre-push run: `crates/codeflow-core/src/hooks/` and `crates/codeflow-cli/src/cmd/push_set.rs` (from TSK-1250)
- fixtures: a bad commit message, a bad record, and a clean push (from TSK-1250)
- docs where the pre-push order is described (from TSK-1250)

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-1 `codeflow ci` shall report the time each check took, on a flag and in the pre-push hook's report when the budget is exceeded; evidence: output tests, and a measurement of the records-only head and of the PR 562 range on the release binary, in the PR body.
- AC-2 Before any cut, the builder shall name from that measurement the checks that spend the most, and the cause in each (for example repeated record parsing or a per-commit walk), with file:line; evidence: the findings in the PR body.
- AC-3 On a records-only head of a repository the size of this one, on the release binary and an idle host, the `ci` step of a push shall finish inside the default budget of 60 s, with every finding it reported before; evidence: before and after timings, and a test over a fixture range showing identical findings before and after the change.
- AC-4 The push budget shall be a project setting with a default, and a push over budget shall report the overrun without refusing the push; evidence: tests for the default, an override and an overrun.
- AC-5 (journey) A real records-only push on the EPC-022 line shall report its `ci` time and finish inside the budget, or name the check that did not; evidence: the push output.
- AC-6 (moved from TSK-1250 AC-1) Pre-push shall run the cheap checks first and stop on the first failure, before taking the lock or running any suite; evidence: a fixture with a bad commit message that fails before the change after the suites and passes the ordering test after.
- AC-7 (moved from TSK-1250 AC-2) When the cheap checks pass, the gate shall run as it does today and its recorded evidence shall be unchanged; evidence: a before and after comparison of the gate record on a clean push.
- AC-8 (moved from TSK-1250 AC-3) The refusal shall name the check that failed; evidence: the output of the bad-message and bad-record fixtures.
- AC-9 (moved from TSK-1250 AC-4) A push with a bad record shall be refused in seconds and shall name the check that failed, and a clean push shall still run the full gate; evidence: both runs with timings. (journey)
- AC-10 On a landing-candidate range the size of EPC-022 candidate 79ae7ed52 (77 non-merge commits and 18 merges from ae4a89312), on the release binary and an idle host, `codeflow ci --base <base> --into <line>` shall finish within 5 minutes, with every finding it reported before; evidence: before and after timings on that range, a profile naming the cause with file:line, and a test over a fixture range with several tasks and merges showing identical findings before and after.
