# TSK-1172: The clock ends at the bound approval or the verification, and deferred proof stays owed

## Description

Issue #334 item C. Today the clock ends at the completion commit, which can
be written long after review approved the work. This task gives the clock
its end: the later of a review approval bound to the final reviewed commit
and the verification point. A deferred criterion (TSK-436) is reported as
owed, not hidden. TSK-1008 (EPC-024, structured evidence) is a design input,
pinned, not an edge.

Outcome: **after this merges, a task's clock ends at the later of a review
approval bound to the final reviewed commit and the task's verification
point, with the landing standing in when the completion was written later; an
approval on an earlier commit is ignored with a note; a deferred criterion is
reported as owed against the task with its later result linked; and a failed
deferred proof places the task in the `reopened` category with its follow-up
task linked, while the task's status stays `complete` as TSK-436 defines.**

Design to settle in the PR (recommendation): both points ride in the
acceptance block beside `reviewed` (the block is already bound to the
reviewed commit and written by `task status complete`), not in the ledger:
git holds them, ADR-0079's "derive from git" stands, and squash landings
keep them. When `verified_at` is absent the completion commit's time stands
in. The landing stands in by ancestry only (a merged landing whose
completion was written by a later records change), never by comparing
clocks (`outcomes/mod.rs:271-277`; `ADR-0079:45-47`).

Non-goals: structured evidence entries (TSK-1008); a queue-time clock;
changes to TSK-436's status rules.

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-1 With `approved_at` bound to the final reviewed commit and `verified_at` present, the clock shall end at the later of the two; a completion commit written later shall not move the end, except that when the task landed with a merge and its completion was written by a later records change, the landing is the end, decided by ancestry and never by comparing clocks (D2); with `verified_at` absent the completion time shall stand in; an approval bound to an earlier commit shall be ignored with a note; evidence: a fixture per case, including a delayed completion, a later in-scope edit and the landing case. (old TSK-1013 AC-3 and AC-8) (serves EPC-022 AC-201)
- AC-2 A `deferred` criterion in TSK-436's form shall be reported as owed against the task with its event and owner, its resolution line linked, and a `--failed --task` resolution shall place the task in the `reopened` category with the follow-up task linked, the task's own clock unchanged and the denominator counting it once; evidence: a fixture with the original and the follow-up task together. (old TSK-1013 AC-3, second half)
- AC-3 The commit-to-approval elapsed time, from the last commit before `approved_at` to `approved_at`, shall be reported as a labelled span inside the task clock, with the note that it holds queue, review work, verification and uncommitted work together; queue time and spans between verdicts shall read `not recorded`; evidence: a fixture.
- AC-4 With neither point present, the report shall read as TSK-1171 leaves it; evidence: TSK-1171's fixtures green.
