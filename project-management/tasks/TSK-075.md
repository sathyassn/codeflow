# TSK-075: autonomy reference and its decision record

## Description

Write the one reference an agent cites to know whether a step is its own to
take, and reduce the operator-owned lists to that one list plus pointers.

`cf-method/references/autonomy.md` carries three parts, taken from section 4
of Fable's autonomy audit:

- the short statement: a task runs from its settled outcome to its finish
  line (build, verify, review, open the pull request, follow it, report
  readiness); the agent settles discoverable facts and reversible choices
  from evidence and says what it chose; it stops only at a listed gate; it
  never stops to offer, to confirm a safe step it already has authority
  for, to report progress, or because a peer, tool or job is red; a stop
  halts only that action, keeps the rest moving, and brings evidence,
  options and a recommendation; a missing seat, tool or credit is reduced
  assurance to record, not a question;
- the ladder figure, four rows: act; notify and act (one line in the
  running report, not a pause); ask (one question, options, a
  recommendation, other work continues); hard gate (wait for a human);
- the decision table, one row per situation with its source, covering the
  audit's rows and naming seats by role, plus these rows: a reversible seat
  dissent after two rounds is settled by the Claude judgment primary and
  recorded as `SETTLED_DISSENT`, never as approval, while a dissent on an
  operator-owned, safety, security or evidence-adequacy axis is not
  settleable; a pull request into an `integration/` branch that policy does
  not protect is merged by the primary, and any protected target, including
  a protected `integration/` glob, is a human merge; "ready for your merge on
  local evidence" needs a completed green result of every owed check and
  names the hosted gap, and a check with no completed result anywhere is a
  missing gate; a trusted installed tool's one classified unsandboxed retry
  is taken without asking; a launch with `bypassPermissions`,
  `danger-full-access` or `--always-approve` is not a sandbox, and the
  boundaries then rest on hooks, guards and this reference (ADR-0055).

The reference owns the only full operator-owned list: intent or public
behavior the brief does not fix, taste, authority or scope beyond the
brief, spend, credentials, anything sent outside the conversation, a
protected-branch merge, production, a delete nothing can restore, and a
step with no way back. It owns the trust prompt rule in the workspace's
words, with foreign defined by authorization and path identity, not by who
created the folder: the agent answers a trust prompt for a path inside its
task's own authorized project or worktree, or a disposable sample its own
harness created in this run; any other path goes to the operator. The
hard-gate list in the contract's "Match the gate" bullet stays where it is
and the reference cites it.

Reduce the lists:

- standard contract: the blocker bullet swaps "Escalate only an external
  dependency or operator-owned choice, with a recommendation." (85 bytes) for
  "Escalate only a gate in `cf-method/references/autonomy.md`, with a
  recommendation." (82 bytes); the planning bullet keeps its compact list;
- minimal contract (no skill tree at that tier, so no pointer target): remove
  the Git rules paragraph that repeats the tier sentence already at the top
  of the file (222 bytes, pinned by no test), and extend "Work to the
  outcome" with a compact clause of about 230 bytes: a task runs to its
  finish line without check-ins, and stops only for the listed gates;
- `CLAUDE.md.tmpl`: one pointer sentence;
- `workflow-lifecycle.md`, as the only EPC-018 writer of that file: one
  pointer in "Establish the route" (a request for a change selects
  implementation to the readiness report), the "Settle before
  implementation" approval sentence gains "or record settled dissent per
  `autonomy.md`", and one pointer in the closeout section. The hard-gate
  paragraph stays: `artifact_budget_contract.rs` pins its "explicit
  authenticated human approval" clause;
- `cf-plan/SKILL.md`: the clarity gate keeps its structure; its list of
  operator-owned axes becomes the pointer alone.

ADR-0070, "autonomous operation with judgment", records the reference as the
owner, the statement, the ladder, the list, the trust prompt rule and the
settled-dissent rule. It amends ADR-0035's joint-approval clause by a dated
Note (settled dissent on a reversible item and approval by the available
seats after a mid-run seat loss) and cites ADR-0034, ADR-0038 and ADR-0066.
Add `autonomy.md` to the scaffold manifest for the standard and full tiers,
and add a test that asserts, per file, the pointer in the standard contract,
`CLAUDE.md.tmpl`, `workflow-lifecycle.md` and `cf-plan/SKILL.md`, plus the
absence of an axis list in `cf-plan/SKILL.md`. TSK-076 extends the test to
the files it owns.

Non-goal: a new gate, a change to the hard-gate list, `quality-contract.md`
(TSK-081 and TSK-076 own it), and skill behaviour beyond pointers.
