# TSK-1304: Work records can name areas that are checked against the project's areas

## Description

Issue 522: `areas` are written to `.codeflow/project.toml` at init and never used afterwards. This task adds an optional `areas` field to task and epic records, validated against the project's areas, so `codeflow ci` and a review brief can name a task's areas.

## Outcome

After this merges, a maintainer can tag a task or epic with the project's areas and see them named in `codeflow ci` and review briefs.

## Deliverables

- the field and its validation: `crates/codeflow-core/src/workgraph/` and `crates/codeflow-core/src/validate/`, with the task and epic templates in `assets/base/`
- the consumers: `codeflow ci` output and the review brief template
- release note: `changelog.d/TSK-1304.md`

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-1 Task and epic records may carry an optional `areas` list, and `codeflow validate --docs` shall refuse an area the project's `project.toml` does not declare, naming the record and the area; evidence: validation tests.
- AC-2 `codeflow ci` and the review brief shall name a task's areas when it has them, and say nothing when it has none; evidence: a CLI test and the brief template.
- AC-3 A changelog entry shall describe the field; evidence: `changelog.d/TSK-1304.md`.
