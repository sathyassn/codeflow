# TSK-255: Builders and reviewers ask whether a change fits the repository

## Description

Builders and reviewers in every adopting repository, and the operator, get
two questions asked in the steps that already run: does this change fit how
the repository already does things, and does the design survive a failure
that composes two of its mechanisms. PR 112 (TSK-249) is the case: a policy
key went into the shipped template while the convention eight entries above
it said not to, and the same-family review cited a precedent commit instead
of the rule. This task sharpens the plan challenge, the build step, the
reviewer rules and the review brief contract, and pays for the per-task
reading chain with cuts of restated text. It adds no step, record, round or
check. Non-goals: a challenge or record before `task new` (item 2 of the
audit), convention lines in this repository's contract (6(a), 6(c)), the
quick-mode replay test (another task) and the planning-PR Reviews section
(an operator decision).

- build step finds the neighbour and the last change of the same kind: `assets/base/agents/skills/cf-develop/SKILL.md`

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-1 When the orchestrator's plan challenge runs, it shall ask for fit to the existing code (the mechanism extended, what is reused, each departure with its reason), one failure case composing two mechanisms the design names, and whether a criterion can pass while the outcome is missed, with the pinned challenge substring intact. Evidence: `orchestration_contract` pins, three new signals on `design-options-and-challenge` with `eval_kit.py validate-suite`, and a focused native trial of that case or a stated reason it was not run.
- AC-2 When a builder follows `cf-develop` step 4a, it shall be told to find the neighbour that already does the job and the last change of the same kind, and to extend them or say why not, in words a later cut cannot drop silently. Evidence: a CF-QA-003 marker on `cf-develop/SKILL.md`, a pin in `artifact_budget_contract`, and a focused trial or a stated reason it was not run.
- AC-3 When `cf-reviewer` raises a finding that asks for a change to policy, hooks, CI, templates, schema or managed instructions, its rules shall require the finding to cite the rule's home with file:line and shall say a precedent commit is not the rule; its UI step shall point at the quality-contract sections that hold the removed procedure, and the file shall be under 10496 bytes. Evidence: `catalog_craft_contract` pin, byte count, and a replay of the TSK-249 brief at ecd77d23a or a stated reason it was not run.
- AC-4 When a review brief is written, the brief contract shall name the change's fit: the neighbour it should match, what it reuses and each departure with its reason. Evidence: an `orchestration_contract` pin.
- AC-5 When the cuts land, the lifecycle shall keep its assignment sentence and point at "Write only what earns its keep" in at most 110 bytes, issue-handling intake notes shall go in the PR Summary or the design note with the critical table's interim-guidance line unchanged, and cut D shall land only if measured byte-neutral on both files. Evidence: the diff, byte counts and the `issue_handling` pin test.
- AC-6 When `codeflow doctor --check reading` runs on the result, the per-task chain shall be at or under 131072 bytes and the kernel unchanged at 12283; every installed copy shall match its asset and baseline so `codeflow update` is a no-op. Evidence: doctor output, `repository_update_noop`, `manifest_consistency`, `rule_map_contract` and the skill mirror tests.
- AC-7 When a reader opens this repository's project section, the areas line shall not name stack profiles, which ADR-0003 dropped. Evidence: the diff.
