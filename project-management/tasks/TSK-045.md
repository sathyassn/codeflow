# TSK-045: give the three themes their own identity in light and dark

## Description

The three utility skins read as one skin today: a reader who switches from
instrument to editorial to ink sees the same grey page with almost the same
accent, so the theme control looks decorative rather than functional. Plan v6
settles a distinct hue family per skin in both modes, with the text and muted
roles held at or above 4.5:1 on canvas and on surface for all six pairs, and
with the palette pills carrying a canvas swatch and an accent swatch drawn from
the live tokens. This task applies the settled values across the live portal,
the starter mirror and the embedded present distribution so the three surfaces
stay identical, and keeps the present CLI contract intact by documenting
`--theme technical` as an alias of instrument. Non-goals: no new skin name is
introduced, no role beyond text and muted is claimed to meet 4.5:1, and this
task does not edit `browser-verify.mjs` or anything under `docs-portal/tests`,
where the swatch assertion belongs to TSK-042.

## Affected surfaces and interfaces

The three token sheets: `docs-portal/src/styles/utility-tokens.css`,
`assets/docs-portal/starter/src/styles/utility-tokens.css` and
`crates/codeflow-present/web/src/styles.css`. Both `PORTAL_ACCENT_BACKGROUNDS`
definitions in the live and starter `docs-portal/scripts/lib.mjs`. The present
chrome and theme plumbing: `chrome.tsx`, `contracts.ts`, `theme.ts` and
`prepaint.ts`. `PortalDisplay.astro`. The present rebuild and its embedded
distribution hashes. The token contract test in
`crates/codeflow-core/tests/manifest_consistency.rs`. The present export theme
alias in `crates/codeflow-present`. CAP-015 and CAP-016. This task does not
touch `docs-portal/scripts/browser-verify.mjs` or `docs-portal/tests/**`.
