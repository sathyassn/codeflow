# TSK-1443: Planning records the three landing answers, the size rule and the marker

## Description

Under ADR-0102 every landing leaves `main` valid, and planning is where that is decided. This task makes planning record it: the task and epic templates carry a Landing section with three answers (what becomes reachable; what stays inert and how; what every other line must do, expected "nothing"); cf-plan and cf-method "Planning an epic" state the cutting rule, the size rule and when an epic is marked; `codeflow epic new` marks new epics `whole-epic` by default; an epic planned with checkpoint pull requests states why; and `codeflow validate --docs` warns on a new record without the answers. It documents TSK-1437's marker, so it lands after EPC-055. Non-goal: the body-of-work sections, which TSK-1439 owns.

Landing on its target (`main`, alone, after EPC-055 lands):

- Reachable: the templates, the planning text, the `whole-epic` default and the warning.
- Inert: the missing-answers check is a warning only.
- Other lines: nothing.

Waits for TSK-419's work on `main`: its record is on `main`, its work is on the EPC-022 line. Its AC-1 and AC-2 move here (below); TSK-419's own record is amended in the EPC-022 records pull request.

## Outcome

After this merges, a planner gets templates and guidance that ask for the three landing answers, new epics marked to reach `main` once and complete by default, and a warning when a new record leaves the answers out.
