# TSK-368: Native workflow capabilities are qualified with current evidence

## Description

Source: both test integrity audits of 2026-10-17 at
`eaf2ed04e932186cdd26dc4a7e5740cc553acee3`. Five shipped capabilities are
workflows that agents run: CAP-009 cross-vendor delegation, CAP-010 duo
orchestration, CAP-011 security review, CAP-012 customization and CAP-013
model-binding evaluation. Their tests are structural (packaging,
instruction markers, the doctor's placeholder check, graders against
scripted outcomes) or protocol doubles. Their live behaviour rests on
historical canaries that do not qualify this revision:
`docs/verification/host-neutral-duo-canary-2026-07-15.md` asks for a local
repeat, `herdr-primary-consult-canary-2026-08-30.md` names unexercised
lifecycle and cleanup, and `whole-flow-ui-isolation-canary-2026-07-26.md`
ran no app or browser.

Outcome: each of the five capabilities has current native evidence on a
controlled consuming repository with real interactive seats, bound to the
4.1.0 candidate revision, or the unproved part is declared in the registry
as a gap.

Folded rather than duplicated:

- PR 224's meaning-taken pack qualification (it replaces TSK-273) owns
  that pack on native seats; its runs count as CAP-013 evidence here and
  are not repeated.
- TSK-194 (EPC-018, designated seats qualified once on the suite) owns
  seat qualification; the "live pack graded with zero, one and two
  primaries" journey is remapped by TSK-362. This task records whether
  that portion is proven and does not re-plan TSK-194.
- PR 224's Codex startup task owns the Codex launch boundary.

None of these is an input this task waits for: it runs its own trials with
the Codex launch as it stands at dispatch, and reuses another task's
evidence only when that evidence exists and exercises the same portion.
Evidence from a seat or pack qualifies only the portions it exercised.
- The sample adopter repository of TSK-303 can serve as the controlled
  consuming repository.

Native trials follow `cf-evaluate-model` (disposable fixture repositories,
traces, environment metadata, cleanup of its own marked roots only) and
the transport rules: interactive seats, never headless.

Product surface: evidence and registry only; a defect a trial finds is a
follow-up task, not fixed here.

## Deliverables

- one verification record per capability (CAP-009 to CAP-013) under `docs/verification/`, each with the revision, seats and versions, the scenario, the expected failure cases, traces and the cleanup evidence
- registry updates in `docs/capabilities.md`: each entry's native promise pointing at its record, or a declared gap (using the evidence kinds TSK-362 adds)
- follow-up tasks for defects found, filed with `codeflow task new --follow-up-of`

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-1 For CAP-012, a native agent shall customize a controlled consuming
  repository from its own evidence, with the reconciled documents and the
  doctor check as the observed result, including one case it must refuse
  or ask about; evidence: the record with traces. (serves EPC-023 AC-25)
- AC-2 For CAP-009 and CAP-010, one cross-vendor consult and one delegate
  through interactive seats shall complete with evidence harvest and
  cleanup, and one duo run shall show independent discovery, a plan
  challenge and an author-relative review; a consult or delegate whose
  seat fails or times out shall end with its failure recorded and its tabs,
  worktrees and fixtures cleaned up, never reported as a completed trial;
  evidence: the records with seat provenance and cleanup listings.
  (serves EPC-023 AC-25)
- AC-3 For CAP-011, the security reviewer shall find and name the planted
  vulnerability at its file and line with a blocking disposition, and shall
  give the clean control a nonblocking result with no fabricated finding;
  the grading path shall reject a deliberately broken reviewer result
  (always approving, always rejecting, empty, or naming an irrelevant
  location); evidence: the record with both verdicts and each listed
  broken-reviewer case rejected. (serves EPC-023 AC-25)
- AC-6 A trial that misses its expected result shall leave that capability
  portion recorded as unqualified in the registry, with an owner and a
  revisit event, never satisfied by recording the wrong verdict; evidence:
  the registry entries for any missed trial.
- AC-4 For CAP-013, the evaluation shall run on native seats at the
  candidate revision, reusing the meaning-taken pack runs where they
  apply, and the zero, one and two primary cases shall be proven or
  declared as a gap; evidence: the record and the registry entry.
- AC-5 Each record shall name the candidate revision it qualifies, and a
  record older than the candidate shall not be cited as current; evidence:
  `validate --docs` resolving each cited record, and the native trials
  themselves. (journey)
