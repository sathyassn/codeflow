# TSK-012 — refine design exploration, sourcing, and revision contracts

## Description

Close the bounded gaps found after TSK-005: guide meaningful in-direction
variation after an operator selects a direction, make inspiration and asset
sourcing trustworthy, and retain which revision the operator reviewed. Extend
the existing product-design contract without creating a second design method,
a provider catalog, a tweak-panel ritual, or a design database.

## Affected surfaces and interfaces

- `cf-design` and one on-demand sourcing reference across managed mirrors.
- The existing orchestrator quality contract and model-evaluation kit, only
  where the new invariants require a shared verification statement.
- SPC-006, a new append-only ADR extending ADR-0043, capability/architecture
  references where materially affected, manifests, baseline, changelog, and
  bounded verification evidence.

Not in scope: `cf-present` or `cf-docs-portal` implementation; edits to
SPC-003/004/005 or ADR-0043; a theme, tool, provider, asset, or component
catalog; restating existing design-system, accessibility, or fidelity doctrine.

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-1 After a direction is selected, the design flow offers further variants
      only when an unresolved choice materially affects the outcome; variants
      are meaningfully different, bounded, and contextual rather than palette
      swaps, a generic tweak panel, or endless generation.
- AC-2 Inspiration is labeled as inspiration rather than user-need evidence;
      material references and assets retain source, rights/consent, generation,
      and transformation provenance needed for safe use and review.
- AC-3 Private or sensitive material is not uploaded to an external asset or
      generation service without explicit authority, and sourced/generated
      assets are verified in the actual product context before acceptance.
- AC-4 The settled handoff records only the smallest justified foundations for
      the product and its expected lifetime: applicable tokens, modes,
      primitives, recurring application components, states, responsive or
      platform behavior, accessibility, and internationalization/localization.
      Existing canonical guidance is linked rather than duplicated.
- AC-5 A material feedback cycle identifies the exact reviewed version and
      preserves accepted/rejected rationale through Plan vN+1 without creating
      a parallel design database or mandatory ceremony for bounded work.
- AC-6 Deterministic mirror/manifest/source-marker checks and fresh native
      Fable 5/high plus GPT-5.6 Sol/high semantic trials cover positive,
      collapse, provenance, licensing/consent, private-data refusal, in-product
      verification, and revision-loop cases without hard-coded test theater.
- AC-7 The retro-verification obligation for TSK-009/011 utility assets is
      recorded in TSK-010 acceptance; this task does not silently add a new
      block or runtime surface to either utility when their settled contracts
      cannot express a design comparison.

Criteria format converted to `- AC-n` at release cleanup (TSK-109,
2026-09-27). No checkbox was ticked before conversion.
