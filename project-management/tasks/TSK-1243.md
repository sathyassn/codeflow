# TSK-1243: work.records does not judge a base-line merge as the branch's own reopen

## Description

Source: the CI throughput study of 2026-11-02 (`coordination/coordinators/parallel/throughput/`: RECOMMENDATIONS.md, FINDINGS.md, CHECK-codex.md, CHECK-fable.md), cross-checked by Codex GPT-6 Astra and Claude Fable 5.1, and the operator's priority on CI throughput.

Issue [#481](https://github.com/sathyassn/codeflow/issues/481) (CodeFlow 3.1.1): task X is reopened on `main` and then completed again, in two separate merges. A task branch for an unrelated task Y merged `main` after the reopen and again after the completion. The pre-push check then refuses Y's push on X with `work.records` ("reopening needs the old acceptance block kept under `acceptance_superseded:`") and `work.acceptance_binding` ("a reopened task's reviewed commit ... must lie inside the fix range"), although X's record on the branch is byte-identical to X's record on `main`.

Checked on the EPC-022 line on 2026-11-02: a search of the task and epic records finds #481 only in TSK-035's evidence note, so no open record owns it. TSK-1149 and TSK-1066 are the sync-merge tasks the study names beside it. TSK-1036 (issues #400 and #424) is complete and covers legal status transitions judged one commit at a time. The study found that the `work.records` half has no owner. This task owns both halves, `work.records` and `work.acceptance_binding`, so the issue is closed by one pull request. TSK-1124, TSK-1129 and TSK-1181 share the same judgment code and are not predecessors; the builder checks them live only to avoid editing the same lines at once.

Expected behaviour from the issue: a record that is identical at the base and at the head, and that changed within the range only through merges of the base line, is not judged as the branch's own reopen.

## Outcome

After this merges, a task branch that has merged its base line more than once is no longer refused for another task's reopen that the base line carries.

## Deliverables

- the record judgment: `crates/codeflow-core/src/workgraph/` and `crates/codeflow-cli/src/cmd/ci/` (the `work.records` and `work.acceptance_binding` rules)
- tests: a `ci_cli` fixture of the issue's shape, and unit tests for each judged case
- docs: the rule's text in `docs/policy-reference.md` where it describes reopen

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-1 Before any change, the builder shall reproduce the issue on a fixture (task X reopened on the base line, completed in a later merge, a task branch for Y that merged the base line after each) with `codeflow ci --base <main> --head <head> --policy-from <main> --commits-from <main>`, record the `work.records` and `work.acceptance_binding` output, and check TSK-1124, TSK-1181 and TSK-1129 live for overlapping edits; evidence: a `## Findings` section, committed before the first code change.
- AC-2 When a record is identical at the base and at the head and changed within the range only through merges of the base line, `work.records` shall not judge it as the branch's own reopen; evidence: the fixture of AC-1, refused before the change with the issue's message and accepted after. (serves EPC-022 AC-233)
- AC-3 A record the branch itself reopens or edits shall be judged as before, as shall a record that differs between the base and the head, a merge whose result is not the clean merge of its parents, and a range whose history cannot be read, which shall be refused; evidence: one test per case, passing before and after.
- AC-4 `work.acceptance_binding` shall not judge a record that is identical at the base and at the head, and changed within the range only through merges of the base line, as a reopened task whose reviewed commit lies outside the fix range, and it shall still refuse a branch's own reopen with a reviewed commit outside the range; evidence: the same fixture passing that check after the change and refused before, and a case for the own-reopen refusal. (serves EPC-022 AC-233)
- AC-5 In a scratch repository with the installed build, a task branch that merged its base line after a reopen and after the later completion shall push without the branch being rebuilt, and a branch that reopens a record itself shall still be refused; evidence: the journey output. (journey)
