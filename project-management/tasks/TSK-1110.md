# TSK-1110: The utility palette has depth and hue in every skin and mode

## Description

The design audit of 2026-10-31 (Source, below) found that the utility
palette has no depth and no hue: the
Graphite canvas `#f6f6f6` sits two percent from the white surface,
`--cf-grad-a` is set transparent and the background gradient is painted
with transparent stops (`styles.css:15-30`, `:342`, `:348-352`), one teal
accent carries everything, and the three skins are hard to tell apart. The
portal shares the tokens and so the problem (`portal.css`, `utility-tokens.css`).

This task rewrites `tokens.json`, the value source of both design kits, to
the audit's palette roles: every
neutral carries 2 to 4 percent chroma toward the skin's accent hue (Graphite
cool, Slate blue, Sage green, both modes), canvas and surface differ by at
least 4 percent lightness, a card gets a hairline and a 1px shadow at 4
percent ink, new roles `surface-sunken`, `ink-secondary` and a semantic ink
and wash per state (positive, warning, danger, accent) measured at 4.5:1 on
the wash, and six data hues validated with the dataviz skill's validator
with the non-hue cue rule kept. Both design kits and the portal sheet stay
byte-identical, the contrast, CIEDE2000 and drift tests extend to the new
roles, and ADR-0053 and ADR-0068 get dated notes. The design seat checks the
six renders (three skins, two modes). Non-goal: the frame chrome and block
vocabulary (TSK-1111, TSK-1113).

## Outcome

After this merges, a cf-present or portal page has a canvas with depth, neutrals with the skin's hue, state colours with a readable wash and six data hues, in every skin and both modes.

## Deliverables

- the tokens: `assets/base/agents/skills/cf-present/resources/design-system/tokens.json` (the value source) and `tokens.css` derived from it, the same files in `assets/base/agents/skills/cf-docs-portal/resources/design-system/`, each installed to both harness destinations per `scaffold-manifest.toml` (`:790`, `:1586`) and kept byte-identical; the portal's `docs-portal/src/styles/utility-tokens.css` and the starter's `assets/docs-portal/starter/src/styles/utility-tokens.css`. There is no generator today: `crates/codeflow-core/tests/design_kit.rs` and `scripts/design-kit-check.mjs` are the checks that hold the kits to the source
- the runtime use: `crates/codeflow-present/web/src/styles.css` (canvas, surface, card shadow, gradient in the skin's hue)
- tests: the kit drift test in `design_kit.rs`, the contrast and CIEDE2000 floors there extended to the new roles, the dataviz validator run on the six data hues, six renders (three skins, two modes) at 1200 and 400 px
- decisions: dated notes on `docs/decisions/ADR-0053-*.md` and `docs/decisions/ADR-0068-*.md`
