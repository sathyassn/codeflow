# TSK-002-004: delegate PTY stress suite

## Description

Build the fake-TUI/PTY stress harness for host-side prompt delivery and durable
event observation. The suite proves transport behavior under delayed,
duplicated, interrupted, malformed, and concurrent conditions rather than
inferring correctness from a stable-looking pane.

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-1 Tests cover delayed ready and accepted events, duplicate Enter, Unicode,
  canonical internal-LF multiline and large inputs, early CRLF/terminal-LF
  rejection, spinner and silent-pane timeouts, dual sessions, and
  kill-at-each-stage.
- AC-2 Every run ends with the expected valid record or an explicit timeout or
  poison; no test permits false completion, lost input, duplicate delivery, or
  cross-session misdirection.
- AC-3 The UI-answer interaction is exercised through the fake TUI; the
  operator-owned sibling Stop-hook preflight is pinned by a deterministic
  decision-table fixture rather than claimed as product or live-hook behavior.
- AC-4 Fable independently reviews the suite and reruns its gates.
