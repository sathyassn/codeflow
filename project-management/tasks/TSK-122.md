# TSK-122: present review parity qualification and distribution

## Description

Node F of the present review parity package (Plan v5). An agent cannot
check its own page today: the service refuses the in-app browser and
`export` needs a browser, so figures have been checked with an unsupported
headless screenshot (proposal section 1). This task adds
`present check <id>` and qualifies the package as a whole: cross-browser,
phone width, the blind evaluation cases that grade the failure modes the
package exists to prevent, and the distribution to consumers through the
skill mirrors, `init` and `update` (proposal sections 2, 3.6, 3.7 and 4.2
row F).

Plan v5.2 adds four phone-width and rail defects that the design primary's
rendered review of TSK-118 found (TSK-118 AC-13 review, 2026-09-27) and
routed here, because this task qualifies present at phone width: the notes
sheet covers half the page in comment mode wherever the notes dock is a
bottom sheet (today below 880 px); a hand-written
`html` stage scales with no floor and its labels shrink to about 4 px at
390 px; untitled blocks show their ids in the review rail; and the
selection chip covers the neighbouring entities below the selected part.

## Affected surfaces and interfaces

- `crates/codeflow-cli/src/cmd/present.rs` (`present check`),
  `crates/codeflow-present` validation reused for framing, anchors and
  forms; the supported Chromium lookup the QA suite uses under
  `crates/codeflow-present/web/scripts`.
- The evaluation kit resources (`requirements.json`, `cases.json`,
  `fixtures.json`, `packs.json`) and `model_eval_contract.rs`.
- The phone-width chrome in `crates/codeflow-present/web/src/`: the notes
  sheet in `chrome.tsx` and `styles.css`, the `html` stage scale and pan,
  the rail labels (`chrome.tsx`, `Block::review_label` in `document.rs`),
  chip placement in `selection.ts`; the browser checks under
  `web/scripts/`, including `checkEntityCrops` and `check:entities`; the
  present rebuild.
- Skill mirrors, managed baselines, `assets/` manifests, the scaffold
  delivery through `init` and `update`, and the release notes entry that
  describes the contract (3.7). CAP-016, CAP-013 for the cases.

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-1 `present check <id>` validates framing, anchors and forms without a
      browser and reports each failure by block; with `--screenshot <png>`,
      when a supported Chromium is found it writes the screenshot there, and
      otherwise reports the screenshot as not available (SPC-014 B11);
      evidence: `present_cli` tests for each failure kind, the written
      screenshot path and the not-available path (3.6).
- AC-2 The package passes the browser suites on the engines of the present
      toolchain targets (Chrome, Firefox, Safari) that the qualification
      host can run, with any engine it cannot run recorded as not qualified
      with the reason, and at phone width with touch targets, safe areas and
      print of the export qualified; evidence: the qualification record per
      engine and width (4.2 row F, section 2 themes row).
- AC-3 The evaluation kit carries blind cases, each with a faulty control
      that must fail and a positive control, for: whole-stage selection,
      missing framing, clipboard instead of submit, queued reported as
      received, stale answer consumed, answer treated as permission, and
      forged anchor; evidence: the grading inventory test and
      `validate-suite` (4.2 row F).
- AC-4 The skill mirrors, managed baselines and manifests are regenerated, a
      scaffolded consumer receives the contract through `init` and through
      `update` from 3.0.x, and the release notes entry describes the v2
      contract and its compatibility; evidence: the manifest test, harness
      `init` and `update` rows, and the changelog entry (3.7).
- AC-5 The full gate, both validators and the present checksum are green on
      the task head; evidence: recorded output with the head SHA.
- AC-6 At every width where the notes dock is a bottom sheet (today below
      880 px), comment mode opens it as a peek bar (Notes, the note count,
      Close) and expands it when a target is pinned, a note exists or the
      reader pulls the bar up, so an empty sheet no longer covers the lower
      half of the page; evidence: a browser case at 390 and 800 px, in one
      skin and mode since the sheet's height is layout, asserting that the
      empty sheet is one bar row (at most 56 px), that a drag of the bar
      expands it, and that the sheet expanded after a pin, a note or that
      drag is at most today's dock height (`min(46vh, 440px)`).
- AC-7 A hand-written `html` stage keeps a minimum scale of 0.75 px per
      unit, the value the crop padding cap uses, and pans horizontally
      inside its frame when the column is narrower; evidence: a browser
      case at 390 px asserting the scale floor and that every entity is
      reachable by panning, and `checkEntityCrops` and `check:entities`
      rerun green on a floored stage. The floor bounds the scale a stage
      gets from fitting its column; the T118-3 stretch case keeps its
      below-floor scale, since it tests the crop pad, not the floor.
- AC-8 The review rail never shows a block id as its label: a block whose
      label would be its id is skipped or labelled by its kind ("Code",
      "List"); evidence: a rail label assertion over a document with
      untitled list and code blocks, and the framing browser case.
- AC-9 The selection chip is placed beside or above the selected part when
      the space below it holds another entity, and it never covers the
      selected part; evidence: a browser case at 1280 and 390 px asserting
      that neither another entity's box nor the selected part's own box
      intersects the chip, on the figure and
      `html` stage fixtures of the TSK-118 review.
