# TSK-049: retitle decisions and architecture pages without dashes

## Description

The portal sidebar and the decisions map show every ADR by its frontmatter
title, and most of those titles carry an em dash, as do two architecture page
titles. The writing rule for the public docs forbids em and en dashes. A title
is record metadata, not the append-only decision text, so it can be rewritten
without touching the decision. This task rewrites every ADR and architecture
frontmatter title into a plain form, keeps the body text unchanged, updates the
generated decisions map and every test that pins a title literal, and records
the mapping. Non-goals: no change to any ADR body, status or supersession; no
new ids.

## Affected surfaces and interfaces

`docs/decisions/ADR-*.md` frontmatter `title` only; `docs/decisions/README.md`
rows; `docs/architecture/*.md` frontmatter titles; tests under `crates/` that
pin a title literal; the portal source snapshot for those routes. CAP-015.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [x] No ADR or architecture frontmatter title shall contain an em or en dash;
      evidence: a grep over the frontmatter of those files returns zero and the
      closeout lists every old and new title.
- [x] No ADR body line other than the leading title heading shall change;
      evidence: the diff of every ADR touches only the frontmatter title line
      and the `# ADR-NNNN` heading line.
- [x] The decisions map and every pinned test literal shall follow; evidence:
      `codeflow validate --docs` and `cargo test --workspace` pass at the head.
- [x] The portal shall build and validate with the new titles; evidence:
      `npm run build` and `codeflow validate --portal docs-portal` pass.
