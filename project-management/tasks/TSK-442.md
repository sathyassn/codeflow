# TSK-442: cf-present figures carry category colour per skin and mode

## Description

Operator report of 2026-10-22: cf-present figures look monochrome and
do not seem to use the design system's colours. Cause, checked in the
shipped tokens: `tokens.json`
(`assets/base/agents/skills/cf-present/resources/design-system/tokens.json`)
gives each of the six skins (graphite, slate and sage, light and dark)
grey ink (`fig-line`, `fig-line-mid`, `fig-line-soft`, `fig-fill`,
`fig-hatch`) plus only three colour roles (`fig-accent`, `fig-warn`,
`fig-stop`), and the figure grammar (`resources/figure-grammar.md`,
`figure.css`) maps almost every mark (regions, states, boxes, edges,
nodes) to the grey roles. There are no category colours, so a
structure or sequence figure with no governing path renders nearly
grey.

Outcome: **the utility design system has a small set of category
colour roles per skin and mode (4 to 6 `--cf-fig-cat-*` hues that hold
contrast in light and dark), the figure grammar says when a figure uses
category colour and when it uses accent, rule 3 (never hue alone)
holds, and every home of the system changes together; the visual
direction is settled with `cf-design` from options the operator
chooses, and the change lands only with the operator's visual
sign-off.**

Rules:

- **Direction first.** `cf-design` presents options (at least two
  palettes per skin, each shown on the structure, sequence and flow
  specimens in light and dark) and the operator's taste decides; the
  accepted direction is recorded as the task's DESIGN_INTENT.
- **Roles.** 4 to 6 category roles per skin and mode, each with a
  soft fill and an ink that meet the contrast the design system
  states; the accent keeps the governing path, warn and stop keep
  their meaning; category colour marks membership (lanes, layers,
  kinds, participants), never emphasis.
- **Rule 3.** Never hue alone: every category mark also carries a
  shape, label, pattern or position cue, checked by the grammar and the
  browser check.
- **Homes change together**: `tokens.json`, `tokens.css`, `figure.css`
  and the grammar module in `cf-present` and `cf-docs-portal`
  resources (byte-identical), the present web build
  (`crates/codeflow-present/web/src/figure.css`), the portal starter
  token sheet (`assets/docs-portal/starter/src/styles/`), the
  specimens, and the palette drift test
  (`crates/codeflow-core/tests/design_kit.rs`), which must keep the
  palette anchored to `tokens.json`.
- The change is recorded under the utility presentation ADRs
  (ADR-0053, ADR-0068) as a dated note or a successor ADR, whichever
  their append-only rule allows.

Non-goals: new figure families; the docs portal's prose styles; the
explanation-surface default (TSK-441).

Added by the EPC-022 amendment of 2026-10-22 at the operator's
report; needs the operator's visual sign-off before it lands.
