# TSK-445: The cf-present skill teaches anchored, figure-led documents

## Description

The operator's words of 2026-10-22: "make sure the cf-present skill is
properly updated to ensure it presents things visually rich, explaining
things visually in a nicer way, with supporting text and preferably
short prose and bullet points and not walls of text; any concept or
such should be explained with diagrams and flows, not text." The
failure shown: one paragraph defining follow-up, lane and work stream
where a figure belongs; and documents that make the reader connect the
dots. The worked example is the coordination ledger presentation (the
cf-present session for the ledger the primary rebuilt; five figures,
one line of prose, tables in a disclosure). The primary began the
method text on `task/TSK-441-cf-present-explains-visually` (commits
`f4197d1fe` and `db057edb8`) at the operator's ask; those commits are
this task's starting input and are judged with its whole unit, and the
task is claimed from this id when the amendment lands.

Outcome: **the `cf-present` skill and its resources teach one coherent
method for anchored, figure-led documents, rewritten as a method rather
than patches: the anchor (what this is, why it matters, where it
stands) and a picture of the whole; titled sections with one or two
lead lines wherever the topic changes; every concept, mechanism,
lifecycle, ownership model or comparison carried by a figure in the
right grammar family, with text limited to a one-line lead, the caption
and short bullets; terms drawn before they are used and named in words
before identifiers; a logical order (the familiar problem, the new
model, how it behaves, the decision); the cold-reader walk; visual
weight (a narrative block of a few sentences, tables in a disclosure);
the governing path in accent-bearing marks; the lessons of Anthropic's
artifact-design and artifact-diagramming guidance; a rendered look
before handoff; with the example document and figure specimens rebuilt
and rendered and the coordination ledger presentation cited as the
worked example.**

Non-goals: the runtime and check (TSK-443); the design direction and
tokens (TSK-444); the docs portal (TSK-446); the default-surface rows
(TSK-441).

Added by the EPC-022 amendment of 2026-10-22, restructured the same day
from TSK-441 so each outcome is one task. The primary (Opus 5.5)
writes the method text itself, since this is design of the method.

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-1 The skill and its resources shall state that every concept, mechanism, lifecycle, ownership model or comparison is carried by a figure in the right grammar family, with text limited to a one-line lead, the caption and short bullets, a narrative block bounded to a few sentences, and the several-terms paragraph named as an anti-pattern with its figure rewrite shown; evidence: the text diff with the before and after. (serves EPC-022 AC-123)
- AC-2 The skill shall require every document to open with an anchor before any detail (what this is, why it matters to the reader, where it stands, and one orienting figure that names the parts later figures zoom into), each part to open with a titled section (a narrative block whose Markdown starts with a `## ` heading, rendered as a heading and used as the section-list label) and one or two lead lines tying it to the whole, each block to connect to the one before it in an order a reader can follow, titles to name the question in words with identifiers only after the thing is named, and a term to be introduced in a figure before it is used; evidence: the text and the cold-reader check in `visual-craft.md` ("can a reader who has seen only the blocks above this one follow it?"). (serves EPC-022 AC-123)
- AC-3 Tables shall be placed inside a disclosure as reference detail, never as the main carrier of a concept, and figures shall use the accent-bearing marks (`agent`, `done`, `human`, `merge`, `return`, `stop`) for the governing path as `present.reference.html` does, with accent against category colour used as TSK-444 decided; evidence: the text and the specimens. (serves EPC-022 AC-123)
- AC-4 The figure guidance shall carry the lessons of Anthropic's artifact-design and artifact-diagramming guidance as they apply here: draw the mechanism, not its name; draw the difference when comparing; match the drawing to the stakes; label arrows with verbs; one figure, one claim, with a caption that states it; ground it in the subject's real names and numbers; a title that is a name with the purpose in `summary`; evidence: the text and the example document. (serves EPC-022 AC-123)
- AC-5 `visual-craft.md` shall give the author the pre-handoff check (figures against narrative and table blocks, a warning when a concept-shaped document has no figure or narrative dominates) matching the `present check` warning, and shall require a look at the rendered page before handoff with the reason stated (the check passed documents whose figures did not draw); evidence: the text. (serves EPC-022 AC-123)
- AC-6 The example document and the figure specimens shall be rebuilt to the method and rendered, the coordination ledger presentation shall be cited as the worked example with what it does right named, and before-and-after renders of both shall be in the evidence folder; the three skill homes shall be byte-identical and the `manifest_consistency` test shall pass; evidence: the renders and the test output. (serves EPC-022 AC-123)
- AC-7 The method text shall pass `cf-editorial-review`, and a native qualification under `/cf-evaluate-model` shall show a session asked to explain a mechanism producing a document that opens with an anchor and an orienting figure and carries the mechanism in a figure; evidence: the review record and the qualification record. (serves EPC-022 AC-123)
- AC-8 (journey) A fresh `codeflow init --standard` project and a project updated from 3.1.1 shall carry the new skill text and example, every pointer shall resolve, and the example shall render clean under `present check`; evidence: the journey output. (journey)
