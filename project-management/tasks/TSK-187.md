# TSK-187: the guide states the delivery process once and derives its CLI reference

## Description

The guide describes the delivery process TSK-184 retired: planning per task
on a `plan/` branch, tasks landing one at a time with gates after each, and
the `Task: none` direct-change route. The process is restated on the
adoption page, the CAP-010 page, the capabilities registry and the glossary,
which is why they drifted apart. This task gives the process one home for
human readers, a Guides-layer page "How work moves to main" composed with
figures from TSK-184's `delivery-process.md` (sections 1, 4 and 5) and
ADR-0076, and rewrites the process sections of the other pages as short
pointers at their own altitude. It also carries TSK-184's guide edits into
the altitude-form pages that the merge cannot place, registers
`docs/workspace-mode.md` in the portal with a companion figure, and proves
the portal green. A second task owns `docs/cli.md`, the derived command
reference and the one-line stale fixes; this task does not edit those.

## Affected surfaces and interfaces

- `docs/delivery.md` (new), `docs/adoption.md`, `docs/glossary.md`,
  `docs/capabilities.md`, `docs/capabilities/CAP-010-duo-model-orchestration.md`,
  `docs/architecture.md`, `docs/releasing.md`, `docs/release-checklist.md`:
  guide content only, no product code.
- `docs-portal/portal.config.json` layers, page classes and figure bindings;
  figure declarations under `docs-portal/figures/delivery/`,
  `docs-portal/figures/workspace-mode/` and `docs-portal/figures/adoption/`.
- Portal gates: `docs-portal` check, build and browser-verify, and
  `codeflow validate --docs` and `--portal`.

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-1 When a reader opens the Guides layer, the portal shall publish one
  page, "How work moves to main", whose Concept, Architecture and Technical
  panels each lead with a figure and whose Technical panel carries a table of
  the checks by stage, all derived from TSK-184's delivery process (sections
  1, 4 and 5) and ADR-0076; evidence: the built route `guides/delivery`, the
  figure declarations with facts pinned to the page, and browser-verify green
  for the route.
- AC-2 When a reader follows the adoption page, the CAP-010 page, the
  capabilities registry or the glossary, each shall state the 3.0.0 process
  (plan once at the breakdown, one PR per task with a `Task:` line, one
  holistic review, batch candidates with one full gate, the operator merges
  `main`) at its own altitude and point to the delivery page for the rest,
  with no remaining claim of per-task planning PRs, tasks landing one at a
  time or the `Task: none` route; evidence: a grep over `docs/` for the
  retired phrases and a read of each rewritten section.
- AC-3 When the figure declarations that pin adoption prose are checked
  against the rewritten page, every fact shall still derive from the source
  and the `howto-body` idea shall no longer say tasks land one at a time;
  evidence: the portal build, which re-derives every fact.
- AC-4 When TSK-184's guide hunks are compared with the altitude-form pages,
  each shall be present at its target: the bot `task` field and paragraph,
  the `codeflow-policy` registry line, the gate scheduling and `work start`
  rows, the CAP-004 and CAP-010 passages, the `Breaking` and nextest lines,
  and the Rust job removal; evidence: the diff of this task against the
  TSK-184 head it was taken from, named in the Closeout.
- AC-5 When the portal is built, `docs/workspace-mode.md` shall be a Guides
  page classed by `page-classes.mjs` with the carrier its class demands;
  evidence: the `page_classes` entry, the companion figure and browser-verify
