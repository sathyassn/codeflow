# TSK-109: retroactive cleanup of ids, statuses and closeouts

## Description

Bring every existing record on every line to the new rules from evidence,
without renumbering anything (design v4 section 9). A scan of every ref found
no canonical id naming two different landed records; the deviations are of
format and status. This task normalises the eleven legacy `TSK-002-*`,
`TSK-003-*` and `TSK-004-*` records where the mapping is one to one, seeds
the registry once with a `uid` for every record, and reconciles statuses
and Closeouts after the status verbs and acceptance rules exist. Where
evidence is gone, the record says "historical evidence unavailable" and
names the merge that landed the work. Nothing is invented.

Landing (SPC-013 R-102): this task lands the cleanup of the records that
`main` holds, with their `uid` backfill, through the EPC-020 line. The
cleanup of each other line is its own follow-up record, allocated in the
Plan v6 planning change: TSK-124 (the EPC-016 line), TSK-125 (the EPC-018
line) and TSK-126 (the backlog plan branch), each with an owner, that line
as its target, and the line's next sync from `main` as its trigger. No
criterion here waits on their pull requests.

## Affected surfaces and interfaces

- `project-management/` records from `main`, landed through the EPC-020
  line; the `codeflow/registry` seed. The other lines are
  TSK-124, TSK-125 and TSK-126.
- No product code.
