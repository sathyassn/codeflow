# TSK-1488: The explanatory guide pages get room under the prose budget

## Description

`guide_altitude_budget` (crates/codeflow-core/tests/guide_altitude_budget.rs) keeps each explanatory page's Technical prose at or under 1500 words. Several pages sit within 30 words of that limit, so two branches that each pass can merge to a page that fails. That happened twice on 2026-11-12 on `docs/capabilities.md` (TSK-1348 at 1503; PR 555 at 1511), and each time an unrelated pull request trimmed the page by hand to get through its quick gate.

coord/ci's counts at PR 555's head d895aeb3d: `docs/architecture.md` 1500, `docs/adoption.md` 1497, `docs/release-checklist.md` 1494, `docs/cli.md` 1489, `docs/releasing.md` 1488, `docs/troubleshooting.md` 1479, and `docs/capabilities.md` at the limit after its trim.

The repository rule (AGENTS.md, "This repo builds the product and is its own first consumer") is to move detail behind a trigger and never cut a duty to meet a number. TSK-374 did that for two pages on 2026-10-18, and both have filled again.

## Outcome

After this merges, the crowded explanatory pages have room under the budget, with reference detail moved behind triggers (reference tables, collapsed sections or linked reference pages) and no fact lost, so a typical docs change from an unrelated pull request merges without a hand trim.

## Deliverables

- Technical prose moved behind triggers on the pages listed in AC-1.
- A fact-destination table in the pull request: each moved fact and where it now lives.
- No change to the test, its limit or how it counts.

## Estimate

- Grade: Medium; band: Medium; moved: none (new, from coord/ci's report of 2026-11-12)

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-1 When `altitude_words` counts the pages at this task's head, `docs/capabilities.md`, `docs/architecture.md`, `docs/adoption.md`, `docs/release-checklist.md`, `docs/cli.md`, `docs/releasing.md` and `docs/troubleshooting.md` shall each have at most 1350 Technical prose words; evidence: a before-and-after count per page from the shared counter, with the starting revision named.
- AC-2 Every moved fact shall have a recorded destination, with no information loss, and generated blocks shall stay byte-identical to the starting revision; evidence: the fact-destination table and a diff of the generated blocks.
- AC-3 Moved detail shall sit behind a trigger the reader can find from the place it left (a table, a collapsed section or a link with a stated purpose); no duty a page states shall be cut to meet the count; evidence: the reviewer's check against the table.
- AC-4 `guide_altitude_budget`, `codeflow validate --docs` and the docs-portal gate shall pass at the head; evidence: their outputs.
- AC-5 (journey) The docs portal built from the head shall render each changed page, with every trigger opening or linking to its moved detail; evidence: the portal run and a note per page.

Whichever lands second merges the other's changes in, keeps both outcomes intact and measures the Technical word counts again before its review.
