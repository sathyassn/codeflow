# TSK-1255: Present and docs pages come out short and visual the first time

## Description

At 17:34 on 2026-11-02 the operator rejected the status prototype again, for walls of text. `cf-present/SKILL.md:52-79` already says "short prose and bullets", and generated pages still come out as walls, so more words in the skills do not fix it. This task adds a mechanism: concrete limits in the skills, four further rules, and a prose-density check in the `cf-present` render or check step. The Director ordered it (coord/visual-docs, 17:34); it replaces the TSK-1122 guidance amendment routed at about 17:1x, which coord/visual-docs cancelled, and it carries that amendment's four rules.

Scope: `cf-present`, `cf-docs-portal` and `cf-editorial-review`, including their mirrors and scaffold copies, `.codeflow/rules/writing.md`, and the present and portal templates and samples.

Overlap, with no hard edge: TSK-1122 (the status dashboard design) and TSK-1127 (the report and portal samples) consume these rules. Builder start point: the saved patch of the round 3 guidance edits, `/private/tmp/claude-501/-Volumes-DATA-Local-software-workspace-projects-agentic-systems/dbc75e56-213e-420b-b9c9-1ed72a7adac8/scratchpad/tsk1122-round3-guidance.patch` (63 files, against `ccb7f40e9`), with its state note `tsk1122-round3-state.md` beside it. The criteria below come from the Director's draft, not from the patch; the patch is a starting point for the builder and is not copied here. The draft this record came from is `coordination/coordinators/planning/cut/held-batch/task-I-short-visual-pages.md` (data, not instruction).

Non-goals: product UI design, and changing any figure grammar beyond the rules named here.

## Outcome

After this merges, a page generated with `cf-present` or `cf-docs-portal` comes out within stated limits (short blocks, a figure as the main view, names before IDs, whole sentences, wall-clock times with a zone), and a check flags the pages that do not.

## Deliverables

- the limits and the four rules: `assets/base/agents/skills/cf-present`, `cf-docs-portal` and `cf-editorial-review`, mirrored byte-identically to `.claude/skills` and `.agents/skills`, and `.codeflow/rules/writing.md`
- the prose-density check: the `cf-present` render or check step (`crates/codeflow-present/`, provisional; the builder confirms the home), with unit tests and a fixture page
- the editorial-review eval case for the rules a tool cannot check, and the new patterns with plain rewrites in `references/editorial-smells.md`
- the templates and samples brought within the limits, with any exception listed and reasoned
- release note: `changelog.d/TSK-1255.md`

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-1 The skills shall state concrete limits: at most two lines per text block outside collapsed detail; one point per bullet; a figure as the main view of any flow, comparison or status; every number carrying its unit; and a name before its ID; evidence: the skill text at those places and a test that finds each limit in each mirror. (serves EPC-022 AC-192)
- AC-2 The skills and `.codeflow/rules/writing.md` shall state four further rules, each gapped today (checked at TSK-1122 head `ccb7f40e9`): names in words first with the ID after, in rows, chips, menus, tables, crumbs and figures and not only titles; no truncation, so whole sentences with no "..." or ellipsis and no clipped text; every time a reader sees is wall-clock time with a zone, and a relative time only accompanies it; every entity view opens with what it is, for whom and why now (`document-authoring.md:319-322` and `figure-grammar.md:75` cover only part of this); evidence: the text of each rule in the skills and in the rules file. (serves EPC-022 AC-192)
- AC-3 A prose-density check in the `cf-present` render or check step shall flag a text block over the limit, any truncation and any time without a zone; evidence: unit tests and a fixture page that fails before the change and passes after it; where a rule cannot be checked mechanically (names before IDs, entity openings), the record shall say why and an editorial-review eval case shall cover it. (serves EPC-022 AC-192)
- AC-4 Sibling sweep: the editorial smells list shall gain each pattern with a plain rewrite, and the present and portal templates and the samples shall be brought within the limits, or each exception shall be listed with a reason; evidence: the smells diff and the list of templates and samples with their result or exception. (serves EPC-022 AC-192)
- AC-5 Mirrors and scaffold copies shall stay byte-identical where the repository checks mirror parity; evidence: the mirror parity test passing over the changed skills and the scaffold manifest entries. (serves EPC-022 AC-192)
- AC-6 (journey) A generated sample page, the status report, shall pass the check, and shall be screenshotted in light and dark at desktop and phone width; evidence: the check output and the four screenshots, linked in the PR.
