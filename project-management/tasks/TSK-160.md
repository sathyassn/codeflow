# TSK-160: present keeps a held drag from pinning mid-gesture

## Description

In the TSK-120 gates at `553346acf`, `check:matrix` failed with "diff
whitespace: a plain drag pinned Element". The trace found a product defect
that predates TSK-120 and reproduces the same way at the line tip
`7dfd35129`. While the button is still down, the selection pin timer in
`chrome.tsx` fires once the selection has not changed for 160 ms, so a
reader who pauses mid-drag gets a Text pin of the partial selection and the
float opens under the pointer. If the drag continues, Chromium extends the
selection into the float, `captureSelection` rejects the cross-root range,
and pointerup pins the line the drag began on as an Element, with the
selection gone. Slow machines make the check hit it more often, but a person
hits it on any machine by pausing. This task makes a drag pin only on
release. Keyboard selection and a selectionchange after pointerup keep their
current timing. The trace is in the TSK-120 scratch record
`t120-matrix-repro/`.

## Affected surfaces and interfaces

- `crates/codeflow-present/web/src/chrome.tsx`: `onSelection` and
  `onPointerUp` in the comment-mode gesture handlers.
- `crates/codeflow-present/web/scripts/matrix-browser-check.mjs` and the
  selection lifecycle check; the present rebuild and checksum.

## Closeout

A drag that paused mid-gesture pinned before release. The selection pin
timer armed on every `selectionchange`, so a hold of 160 ms or more opened
the float under the pointer; a continued drag then carried the selection
into the float, and release pinned the first line as an Element. The fix
keeps every pin while the primary button is down and pins on release. It
is at `fcea692b3` on `task/TSK-160-held-drag-pin`.

- Mechanism: held-button state lives in window capture listeners outside
  the comment-mode gesture effect, so a press cancels an armed pin, no pin
  is armed or fires while the button is down, and window blur ends a lost
  gesture. Release pins any selection the review text can hold, one
  character included. A press the gesture did not track pins on release
  too when it began inside the review text (a prose link) or outside the
  chrome's controls (the page margin, or a press made before the listeners
  mounted just after the composer closed); a press on the chrome's own
  controls outside the review text never pins.
- Tests first, each failing before its fix: the held prose drag
  (`prefix-browser.log`, "opened a chip while the drag was held") and the
  held diff drag (`t160-g1-matrix-prefix.log`, the pre-fix binary built at
  the line tip); a one-character drag and a press held after a fresh
  selection (`r1-prefix-browser.log`, `r1-prefix-matrix.log`); a selection
  begun on a prose link (`c1-prefix-matrix.log`). Each hold asserts its
  partial selection first. Logs are in the scratchpad `t160/`.
- A regression in review round 1 was caught by the gate: at `ea48eb41c` a
  press made just after the composer closed never pinned
  (`t160-g2-matrix.log`); `6cc9cffa2` fixed it (`t160/matrix-r1b.log`).
- Gates (AC-4) at `fcea692b3`: `npm run check` passes with a reproducible
  tree; `check:browser` with and without the Firefox and WebKit chip
  engines, `check:matrix`, `check:entities`, `check:forms` and
  `check:figures` pass; `cargo test -p codeflow-present` 176 and 2 passed;
  `present_cli` 16 passed and 1 ignored; `init_e2e` 2 passed; workspace
  clippy, rustdoc and fmt are clean; `validate --docs` and `codeflow ci` are
  clean (`t160-g4-*.log`).
- Behavior change: a text selection now pins on release rather than 160 ms
  after the last selection change during a drag; keyboard selection keeps
  the 160 ms settle.
