# TSK-108: one lifecycle guidance section across the skills

## Description

Once the verbs, classification, acceptance, release and adopter changes
exist, the guidance must describe them once and correctly. This task writes
one lifecycle section in cf-method that replaces the scattered passages,
and updates cf-plan, cf-develop, cf-ship, cf-customize (policy edits need a
human, Plan v4 section 7) and the orchestrator task-graph contract (guarded
plans write only the selected branch into `depends_on`, section 3). It adds
the research folder rule of section 3.1 row 1 (`docs/research/`, one file
per question with its sources, cited in an inputs list) and states the
section 3.1 rules for specs, epics and standalone tasks once. The AGENTS
templates stay within their byte budget. Plan v4 says this task merges with
TSK-113's orchestrator edits in one reviewed pass.

## Affected surfaces and interfaces

- Skills in `assets/base`, `.claude/skills`, `.agents/skills` and the
  baseline: cf-method, cf-plan, cf-develop, cf-ship, cf-customize,
  cf-model-orchestrator `resources/task-graph.md` (CAP-010, CAP-012).
- `assets/base` AGENTS templates; `.codeflow/manifest.json` hashes.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] AC-1 cf-method shall hold one lifecycle section that matches the
      shipped verbs and rules, and the passages it replaces shall be removed
      or point to it; every command it names shall exist in the CLI;
      evidence: the section, a removed-passage list and a command-name check.
- [ ] AC-2 cf-plan, cf-develop, cf-ship and cf-customize shall follow that
      section, cf-customize shall state that policy edits need a human, and
      `task-graph.md` shall state the guarded-plan rule and the "awaiting
      selection" block; evidence: skill text and `orchestration_contract`.
- [ ] AC-3 The guidance shall state the `docs/research/` rule, the many to
      many spec and epic relationship, and the one standalone test (one
      reviewable pull request) in one place each; evidence: skill text.
- [ ] AC-4 Skills shall be byte identical across mirrors with matching
      manifest hashes, and the AGENTS templates and skills shall stay within
      their byte budgets; evidence: `manifest_consistency` and
      `artifact_budget_contract`.
- [ ] AC-5 The orchestrator edits shall be reviewed in one pass together with
      TSK-113's; evidence: the review reference.
