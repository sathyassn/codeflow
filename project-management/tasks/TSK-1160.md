# TSK-1160: One shared navigation component with a spec and a browser test that every page build passes

## Description

Split from TSK-1097 on 2026-11-01 (planning amendment of the same day): the
operator decided at 19:25 on 2026-11-01 that navigation behaves like
ChatGPT, Linear or Notion and that its regressions are stopped by a
mechanism, never by care. Source:
`coordination/coordinators/visual-docs/DESIGN-DIRECTION.md`, "Navigation and
text (operator, 19:25 on 2026-11-01, binding)". The nav rail and peek had
regressed more than once; this task makes one component, written down as a
spec and held by a browser test that every page build must pass.

TSK-1097 was graded Very Hard once it carried this component beside the bar,
the token system and the checks, so the component is its own task (the token
system later became TSK-1162). This task
takes the former TSK-1097 AC-22 and AC-23 (they are AC-1 and AC-2 here, with
their behaviour list removed on 2026-11-01; see their Amended notes). It builds on the token source of TSK-1162 (motion,
breakpoint and floor tokens) and the kit sheets, and so depends on it. TSK-1120
(the `cf-present` shell), TSK-446 (the portal pages) and TSK-1127 (the
shipped samples) consume the component and depend on this task. The portal
and present shells keep their own other chrome; only the navigation is shared.

Navigation definition of done (the Director, 20:32 on 2026-11-01, binding;
`coordination/coordinators/visual-docs/DESIGN-DIRECTION.md`, "Navigation
definition of done"): the behaviour is judged against a real reference app,
never against our own spec. AC-3 to AC-6 carry it.

The navigation behaviour is settled (the operator, 21:00 on 2026-11-01, from
screenshots, relayed by the Director at 21:04): a two-level sidebar. Its source
of truth is `coordination/coordinators/visual-docs/DESIGN-DIRECTION.md`, section "Required", item "Left menu, two levels" (workspace commit 3953e4c). AC-1 lists the behaviour from that item and AC-3 records the pick. The behaviours first written from
the 19:25 rule were a guess the operator rejected (the Director, 20:42 on
2026-11-01), so they are kept only as history in the Amended notes.

Non-goals: the shared top bar with the panel toggle, title and crumb and the display gear (TSK-1168, added 2026-11-01 by the Director, 20:50; its toggle controls this component); the dashboard shell and its other parts (TSK-1120); the portal
frame (TSK-1114); the text rule and its checks (TSK-1097).

## Outcome

After this merges, an author of a `cf-present` document or a portal page gets
the same sidebar navigation, and a regression of its behaviour fails the
build instead of reaching the operator.

## Deliverables

- the component: one navigation component in the shared kit script and stylesheet both surfaces load (`cf.js` and `cf.css` in the design direction), with no second copy in the present web sources, the starter or this repository's portal
- the spec: the navigation behaviour written in the design system
- the test: the Playwright interaction test, run by every page build on both surfaces
- release note: `changelog.d/TSK-1160.md`
- managed wiring, mirrors and baseline: `assets/base/scaffold-manifest.toml`, installed managed files and `.codeflow/` baseline, synchronized when changed

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-1 (moved 2026-11-01 from TSK-1097 AC-22; the operator, 19:25; amended 2026-11-01, the Director, 20:42, and again for the operator's pick, 21:00, relayed by the Director at 21:04) The design system shall have one navigation component, in the shared kit script and stylesheet both surfaces load; every page of `cf-present` and `cf-docs-portal` shall use this one component, with no second copy; its behaviour is the two-level sidebar of `coordination/coordinators/visual-docs/DESIGN-DIRECTION.md`, section "Required", item "Left menu, two levels" (workspace commit 3953e4c):
    - a rail of section icons, always visible, one per top-level area, with the utility areas at the bottom;
    - a panel beside the rail that lists the selected area's items (a title, rows with icons, small group labels);
    - pinned: the rail and panel sit side by side and the panel pushes the content;
    - collapsed (a toggle, Cmd or Ctrl plus backslash): the panel slides away, the rail stays and the content moves left;
    - hover peek: pointing at a rail icon for about 150 ms shows that area's panel as a floating card over the content (rounded, a small gap from the rail and from the top and bottom edges, a slightly lighter surface, a soft shadow); the content does not move; pointing at another rail icon swaps the card; leaving both the icon and the card closes it after about 250 ms;
    - clicking a rail icon selects the area (pinned: the panel shows it; collapsed: it goes to the area's first item); the selected icon has a filled rounded background;
    - the panel collapse and expand animate its width and its content together, 200 ms ease-out; the card fades and slides in a few pixels over 150 ms and out over 120 ms; there is no jump and no layout shift; reduced motion makes all of it instant;
    - the panel is resizable and its state persists per viewer; under 768 px the navigation is an off-canvas drawer; scroll bars show only when needed;
    evidence: the component, and a test that finds one navigation component per page and no copy in either surface's sources. (serves EPC-022 AC-192)
