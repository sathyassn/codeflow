# TSK-1160: One shared navigation component with a spec and a browser test that every page build passes

## Description

Split from TSK-1097 on 2026-11-01 (planning amendment of the same day): the
operator decided at 19:25 on 2026-11-01 that navigation behaves like
ChatGPT, Linear or Notion and that its regressions are stopped by a
mechanism, never by care. Source:
`coordination/coordinators/visual-docs/DESIGN-DIRECTION.md`, "Navigation and
text (operator, 19:25 on 2026-11-01, binding)". The nav rail and peek had
regressed more than once; this task makes one component, written down as a
spec and held by a browser test that every page build must pass.

TSK-1097 was graded Very Hard once it carried this component beside the bar,
the token system and the checks, so the component is its own task (the token
system later became TSK-1162). This task
takes the former TSK-1097 AC-22 and AC-23 unchanged in meaning (they are
AC-1 and AC-2 here). It builds on the token source of TSK-1162 (motion,
breakpoint and floor tokens) and the kit sheets, and so depends on it. TSK-1120
(the `cf-present` shell), TSK-446 (the portal pages) and TSK-1127 (the
shipped samples) consume the component and depend on this task. The portal
and present shells keep their own other chrome; only the navigation is shared.

Navigation definition of done (the Director, 20:32 on 2026-11-01, binding;
`coordination/coordinators/visual-docs/DESIGN-DIRECTION.md`, "Navigation
definition of done"): the behaviour is judged against a real reference app,
never against our own spec. AC-3 to AC-6 carry it. The behaviour of AC-1 is
the operator's rule of 19:25 until the real-app study now under way (due to
the Director by 21:00 on 2026-11-01) is done and the operator picks from it;
the picked behaviour then replaces it (AC-3). This record does not guess that
result.

Non-goals: the dashboard shell and its other parts (TSK-1120); the portal
frame (TSK-1114); the text rule and its checks (TSK-1097).

## Outcome

After this merges, an author of a `cf-present` document or a portal page gets
the same sidebar navigation, and a regression of its behaviour fails the
build instead of reaching the operator.

## Deliverables

- the component: one navigation component in the shared kit script and stylesheet both surfaces load (`cf.js` and `cf.css` in the design direction), with no second copy in the present web sources, the starter or this repository's portal
- the spec: the navigation behaviour written in the design system
- the test: the Playwright interaction test, run by every page build on both surfaces
- release note: `changelog.d/TSK-1160.md`
- managed wiring, mirrors and baseline: `assets/base/scaffold-manifest.toml`, installed managed files and `.codeflow/` baseline, synchronized when changed

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-1 (moved 2026-11-01 from TSK-1097 AC-22; the operator, 19:25) The design system shall have one navigation component, in the shared kit script and stylesheet both surfaces load, with this behaviour: expanded, the sidebar pushes the content and never overlays it; collapsed, a slim rail shows one distinct icon per top-level group only (never one repeated icon per item), each with a tooltip on hover; hovering the rail or the left edge, or keyboard focus on it, slides the full sidebar in flush to the edge (no gap, no floating card) over the content with a soft shadow, eased about 200 ms, and closes it on leave after a short delay; the toggle state persists per viewer and Cmd or Ctrl plus backslash toggles it; under 768 px it is an off-canvas drawer with a scrim, closed by swipe or Escape; no jump anywhere and reduced motion respected; every page of `cf-present` and `cf-docs-portal` shall use this one component, with no second copy; evidence: the component, a test that finds one navigation component per page and no copy in either surface's sources, and the behaviour proven by AC-2. (serves EPC-022 AC-192)
- AC-2 (moved 2026-11-01 from TSK-1097 AC-23; the operator, 19:25: regressions "are stopped by a mechanism, never by care") The navigation behaviour shall be written as a spec in the design system and proven by a Playwright interaction test that every page build on both surfaces must pass, covering collapse and expand with the content pushed and not overlaid, the rail's group icons and tooltips, peek open and close by rail hover, left-edge hover and keyboard focus with the panel flush to the edge and no content shift, the persisted state after a reload, the Cmd or Ctrl plus backslash toggle, the 390 px drawer with its scrim, swipe and Escape, the animation running about 200 ms and not jumping, and reduced motion settling at once; the test shall fail on a planted gap at the edge, a planted content shift on peek, a planted repeated icon and a planted missing tooltip; evidence: the spec, the test run over this repository's portal, the starter and a present document, and the planted failures. (serves EPC-022 AC-192)
- AC-3 (added 2026-11-01, the Director and the operator: the Director's 20:32 definition of done, and the operator's pick after the real-app study due to the Director by 21:00) The behaviour AC-1 states shall be the one the operator picks from the real-app study; until the pick is recorded in this record, it is the operator's 19:25 rule as AC-1 words it; when the pick differs, AC-1 and AC-2 are amended in the same pull request that builds it, with the pick, its date and the study's source cited; evidence: the recorded pick, or the record's statement that none was made at build time. (serves EPC-022 AC-192)
- AC-4 (added 2026-11-01, the Director, 20:32) The written spec shall cite the observed reference app, as a screenshot or the app's own documentation, for every navigation behaviour it states, and shall name the reference; evidence: the spec with one citation per behaviour and the reference named. (serves EPC-022 AC-192)
- AC-5 (added 2026-11-01, the Director, 20:32: Fable and Grok review our page "side by side with the reference app, never against our own spec") A Claude Fable 5.1 reviewer and a Grok 4.7 reviewer shall each review the built navigation page side by side with the reference app, not against this task's spec, and each shall record a verdict naming the reference and the captures compared; evidence: both verdicts, each naming the reference and the captures. (serves EPC-022 AC-192)
- AC-6 (added 2026-11-01, the Director, 20:32) The Playwright interaction test of AC-2 shall add checks for the chosen reference's behaviour, each failing on a planted departure from it, and TSK-1160 shall not be marked ready until the Director has looked at the built navigation next to the reference; evidence: the added checks with their planted failures, and the Director's recorded check. (serves EPC-022 AC-192)
