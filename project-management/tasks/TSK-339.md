# TSK-339: Completed work keeps its verified origin when lines merge

## Description

Issues: #181, #197 and #218. These share loss of the completion's home authority during import. They have distinct manifestations which need one composed regression set.

- `workgraph/acceptance.rs:2992` adds own-line stacking authority only when the task target matches the currently judged line (`:3004`). That explains #197 and #181's target-history form.
- `acceptance.rs:2940` assigns a task range all its changes. Its `origin` becomes the range head at `:2953` unless the amendable-line path applies. `task_landing` at `:1058` and the refusal at `:1152` can therefore treat a sync merge as the landing of imported main completions and blame later product work on them. This covers #181's 2026-10-17 comment as well as its earlier differing-shared-file comment.
- `recovered_completion` at `:1427` handles `Landed::Unjudged` at `:1475` by retaining earlier criteria. This explains #218 after a valid pre-landing reopen on the task's home.
- TSK-275 explicitly excludes attribution after a merge (`project-management/tasks/TSK-275.md:31`). Its AC-5 (`:72`) overlaps the simple sync case, but PR 206 at `1b2c9664e` still has the core routines and decisions above unchanged. TSK-306 settles inherited held-version conflict (`TSK-306.md:36`); it does not authorize a previously unjudged home. TSK-308 AC-1 covers a landed direct adoption (`TSK-308.md:52`). TSK-318 explicitly excludes merges (`TSK-318.md:53`) and its AC-1 rejects them (`:67`). None closes this group as written.

Outcome: **A completion imported unchanged from its proven home is judged at its accepted home landing, while changes introduced by the receiving line remain accountable to that line.**

Source inspection is from the accepted triage at `c5c6f6f671d2bf67a77113c656b3be446fb0bea1`; runtime reproductions remain implementation evidence.
