# TSK-234: Unlanded work is judged as unlanded

## Description

Two checks judged work that had not landed as if it had. Issue #67: a task
the target records adds a criterion on its own branch, completes, and is
reopened to take a merge of its line; the second completion was refused
with "a reopened task keeps its criteria" though the task never landed.
Issue #69: `work claim --on <pred>@<sha>` accepts a reviewed pin, but the
claim's own push ran `codeflow ci` over the predecessor's commits as the
successor's and refused on the predecessor's criteria change and its
anchored status. This task makes the reopen freeze follow a landed
completion, has a push of a stacked branch judge the commits up to each
reviewed pin as that predecessor's pull request, accepts a pin that adds
only the predecessor's status and Closeout to its reviewed commit, and lets
an acceptance block quote `follow_ups` so it loads as YAML. Protections
kept: a landed task's criteria change only through a reviewed planning
change; criteria come only from the run's own bases, and a movable ref can
only make the check stricter; a pin is honoured only when a review row
names it. After two review rounds found further holes of one class, the
work was rebuilt on PR 61 (TSK-229) under one design: one judgement
answers whether a completion landed (read from the judged history, never
the tip, with a proof-of-absence contract) and which criteria the target
holds (the newest judged version), for the reopen freeze, the criteria
freeze and the planning amendment's report; readiness keeps reading the
predecessor's current status; and one reading of a merge, against the
automatic remerge of its parents, serves the binding and the journey
rule. Non-goals: the review binding of a reopening range (no stacking, no
own-range waiver) and the R-42 rule that a stacked task's pull request
lands only after its predecessor.

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-1 When a task the target records but has not completed adds a criterion on its own branch, completes, is reopened to take a merge of its line and completes again after a review of the merge, `codeflow task status <id> complete` and `codeflow ci` shall both accept it and CI shall print the criteria delta as a note, on the installed CLI; evidence: `a_reopen_to_take_a_line_merge_keeps_an_unlanded_criterion` in `acceptance_cli`, which fails on `main` and passes after. (journey)
- AC-2 When a completion of the task has landed (some commit reachable from a judged point holds a version of its record that is complete, keeps an acceptance block, active or superseded, or records a reopen, however the target changed or deleted the record later), a reopen that changes its criteria shall still be refused by the verb and by CI, the criteria kept shall be those of the newest judged version, compared with the record whatever one judged point holds, and a local branch or remote-tracking ref shall still only make that check stricter; evidence: `a_landed_task_keeps_its_criteria_on_reopen`, `a_cleared_closeout_does_not_hide_a_landed_completion`, `a_deleted_landed_record_keeps_its_newest_judged_criteria` (fails before the redesign), `a_newer_run_base_holds_the_criteria_to_keep` (fails before the round 6 fix) and the TSK-217 and TSK-220 tests rebased on a landed record in `acceptance_cli`.
- AC-3 When `codeflow work claim <id> --on <pred>@<sha>` names a reviewed predecessor head whose pull request corrected its criteria and recorded its completion, the claim's push through the installed pre-push hook shall pass, and so shall a later push of the successor's own work, with the predecessor's commits judged as its reviewed pull request; evidence: `a_claim_on_a_reviewed_head_passes_its_own_push_check` in `work_cli`, which fails on `main` with the two blocks the issue names and passes after. (journey)
- AC-4 When the stacked head is not named by a review row, when the successor changes the predecessor's criteria or any other part of its record itself, when the successor changes or deletes an adopter-facing path itself without a journey criterion, in a commit or inside a merge, when a merge in the range is an octopus, or when the successor's pull request into the target is checked before the predecessor lands, `codeflow ci` shall refuse, and the journey rule shall hold the successor neither to the predecessor's own paths nor to target work a clean merge brought in; evidence: `an_unreviewed_predecessor_head_is_not_honoured_by_the_push_check`, `a_stacked_branch_answers_the_journey_rule_for_its_own_paths` (its merge-time deletion case fails before the redesign), `a_merge_edit_is_not_hidden_by_a_path_that_reads_alike` (fails before the round 5 fix), `a_stacked_branch_keeps_its_predecessors_record` and the later steps of `a_claim_on_a_reviewed_head_passes_its_own_push_check`.
- AC-5 When the predecessor's branch tip follows the commit its review names only by commits that change the predecessor's own status and Closeout, `codeflow work claim --on` shall accept that tip as the pin, and when the tip adds anything else, a file whose name only reads like the record's included, it shall refuse with "no review names"; evidence: `a_pin_may_add_only_the_predecessors_status_and_closeout_to_its_review` and `a_pin_cannot_add_a_path_that_reads_like_the_record` (fails before the round 6 fix) in `work_cli`.
- AC-6 When an acceptance block writes `follow_ups` quoted, as `"none: <reason>"` or `'none: <reason>'`, the verb and CI shall accept it as they accept the plain form, the block shall load with a YAML parser, and plain blocks shall stay valid; evidence: the unit test `follow_ups_may_be_quoted_so_the_block_loads_as_yaml` and `a_quoted_follow_ups_completes_by_the_verb_and_in_ci` in `acceptance_cli`.
- AC-7 When the rule text changes, SPC-013 R-42, R-52, R-53, R-60, R-119 and the acceptance block interface shall carry dated amendments that name their bound consumers, the cf-method reference shall say the same, and the 3.1.0 CHANGELOG shall describe both fixes under Fixed; evidence: the diff and `codeflow validate --docs`.
- AC-8 When the judged history cannot prove that no completion landed (a shallow clone, a graft file or replace refs, a record, tree or object of the task's records that does not read, a task file that does not parse and may name the task (its own file name, its id or uid, or an escape in its frontmatter), one id carried with two uids anywhere in that history, or two records of the task in one commit), the landing answer shall be unknown, never planned, and only a criteria change shall be refused for it; when the target no longer holds the record, its criteria shall come from the newest version on every line of the history, and lines that disagree shall give no answer, a line's version counting as older only when that line inherited it unchanged from the other; evidence: the unit tests `a_complete_history_proves_a_planned_task`, `a_shallow_clone_gives_no_planned_answer`, `one_id_with_two_uids_gives_no_planned_answer` and `a_stale_line_does_not_compete_with_the_newest_version` in `workgraph::landing`, and `an_unreadable_historical_record_tree_never_proves_a_planned_task`, `a_dropped_uid_does_not_hide_an_ambiguous_history`, `identical_duplicate_records_are_ambiguous` and `deleted_histories_that_disagree_give_no_authority` in `acceptance_cli` (each fails before the round 5 fix), and `crossed_versions_on_two_lines_give_no_authority` and `an_escaped_record_that_does_not_parse_gives_no_planned_answer` (each fails before the round 6 fix).
- AC-9 When a planning amendment changes the criteria of a task whose completion landed, or adds again a task record the target deleted, CI shall flag the landed task, compare the record with its newest judged version and own it by that version's epic, and the reopen freeze shall still refuse an amendment that reopens a landed task and changes its criteria, a deleted record added back reopened included; evidence: `an_amendment_that_readds_a_deleted_landed_record_keeps_its_epic` (fails before the redesign), `an_amendment_flags_a_landed_task_reopened_on_the_target`, `an_amendment_cannot_reopen_a_deleted_landed_task_with_new_criteria` (fails before the round 5 fix) and the reopen case of `planning_amendment`.
- AC-10 When a predecessor that landed complete is reopened on the target, its successor shall not be claimed without a reviewed pin, since readiness reads the current status and a historical landing satisfies no dependency; evidence: `a_reopened_predecessor_is_not_ready_without_a_pin` in `work_cli`.
