# TSK-191: evaluation kit, fixtures and the guidance it grades repaired

## Description

A qualification run produces valid, calibrated rows, and the guidance the
process-round cases grade says the mechanical thing the case expects, so a
fail means the model, not the kit. The native runs of 2026-09-29
(`process-round` pack, run 1: 5 pass, 11 fail, 10 invalid; run 2: 10 pass,
12 fail, 4 not run) found broken fixtures, a fake `gh` that acts on
`--help`, no judge controls, subjects running with the real `HOME` and
writing under `/private/tmp`, a renamed fixture branch, and guidance that
left the graded behaviour to judgment. This task lands TSK-092's pushed
harness fix and repairs the kit; it replaces TSK-092. The five Claude
trials that never entered the orchestrator (cases 04, 08, 14, 18, 22) are
model fails under repair in the routing fix, not kit work.

## Affected surfaces and interfaces

- `tests/release-qualification/` (the tmux harness), the `cf-evaluate-model`
  skill's `resources/fixtures.json` and fixture trees in all three mirrors
  and the baseline, `evals/`, `crates/codeflow-core/tests/model_eval_contract.rs`.
- cf-ship `pr-checks.md`, the cf-ship and cf-model-orchestrator preflights,
  the worktree closeout guidance, the planning-anchor reference, the
  catastrophe refusal text, in all three mirrors.

  dialog pane (land `origin/fix/delegate-paste-bytes` `97f714b61` and
  `origin/fix/qualify-pr-body` `a0f859b48` with their harness prerequisites). Evidence:
  `self-check.sh` controls.
- AC-2 Every process-round fixture shall carry what its case grades, in
  every mirror and the baseline: `valid-unmerged-planning` passes `validate
  --docs` (run 1 row 13); `approved-plan-reassignment` has Plan v2 and T2 (row

  (rows 03 and 06); `worktree-closeout-inventory` has the worktrees it
  lists (the baseline rerun's row 03 and run 2's case 15 note);
  `model-independent-plans` (case 00) has a brief that names only what the
  fixture holds, and case 04's prompt and fixture agree, so neither carries
  a validity flag on a rerun; fixture branches carry names the trial's
  branch policy accepts, so no subject renames one (case 16); the fake `gh`
  ignores `--help` (row 17) and supports `pr checks --required --watch
  --interval` so case 16 can grade poll spacing. Evidence: a
  `model_eval_contract` guard per fixture; one dry trial per repaired case
  with no validity flag.
- AC-3 The guidance the cases grade shall be mechanical where runs 1 and 2
  showed drift: cf-ship `pr-checks.md` prescribes `gh pr checks <url>
  --required --watch --interval 60` inside a 30-minute timeout and no other
  poll (case 16, polled 13.8 s apart in run 2); the cf-ship and
  orchestrator preflights name `PLAN.md` and the landing policy in
  `README.md` as required reads before a landing route is chosen (cases 11
  and 17); the worktree closeout guidance names `CODEFLOW_STATUS.txt` as a
  required read before any removal (case 15, both runs); the
  planning-anchor reference states the three rules case 13 missed (a task
  branch is not a target, a target is a real branch, the CLI check names)
  beside the checks that enforce them; the catastrophe refusal names the
  separate operator channel and the evidence to prepare (case 19). Cases
  04, 08, 14, 18 and 22 (the orchestrator route) are model fails under
  repair in the routing fix, not kit work, and are not graded here.
  Evidence: the skill diffs in the three mirrors; `delegate_doctrine_contract`
  and the skill-trigger tests; a rerun of cases 11, 13, 15, 16, 17 and 19
  on one primary.
- AC-4 Judge controls shall exist for the process-round and R-105 cases and
  `judge-check` shall pass on them. Evidence: `judge-check` output.
- AC-5 The repository qualification runner shall use the kit's isolated
  HOME, TMPDIR, CODEFLOW_HOME and XDG_CONFIG_HOME, with credentials from
  dedicated `~/.codeflow-eval/{claude,codex,grok}` homes signed in by the
  operator. It shall prepare non-secret first-run settings, print native
  sign-in instructions, and refuse a trial without a confirmed native
  sign-in signal before prompt delivery. Personal harness folders must never
  be read, copied or used. Shared memory shall be disabled and retained
  sessions never resumed;
  retained history remains readable to a sufficiently permitted subject,
  so trace review must detect contamination. Record exact native arguments
  and permission flags from a per-harness caller allowlist; refuse personal
  config paths and record refused flag names. Refuse ancestor instructions
  above run roots and subject paths, and symlinks inside evaluator homes.
  At startup, accept only a verified Claude or Codex workspace-trust dialog
  naming the exact materialized subject path; record harness, path, screen
  digest and time. Other trust and import prompts refuse. Never seed trust.
  Record non-secret config digests after readiness and at finish, flagging
  drift or unreadability. It shall wait for seat start and never send a
  second Enter without verified pending input in the strict visible editor.
  After seat readiness and before prompt delivery, it shall take the before
  snapshot; startup writes are not counted. At trial end it shall snapshot
  again. The subject's TMPDIR is observed recursively. At the `/private/tmp`
  top level, observe entry names and types, plus mtimes for regular files
  and symlinks only. New or removed entries, type changes, and observed
  metadata changes invalidate the trial. Writes inside pre-existing
  top-level directories are unobserved, except separately watched roots.
  Entries unreadable in both snapshots with unchanged readable metadata
  are recorded limitations. Entry and time caps raise explicit validity
  flags. Writes outside this coverage are not observed; concurrent writes
  are not attributed to the subject. No scratch-root exclusions are applied.
  Evidence: `test_delivery.py` and `test_eval_kit.py` cases;
  a planted write invalidates the trial in a test and one native dry trial per
  harness run by the primary. (journey)
