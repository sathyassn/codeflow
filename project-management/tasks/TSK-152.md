# TSK-152: restore byte-budget cuts in the EPC-016 guide and skills

## Description

The byte-cut audit (2026-09-27) found passages on this line that were cut
to meet a byte budget rather than because they stopped being true. The
operator wants nothing key lost, and byte budgets are now guidelines
(TSK-150 removes the caps on EPC-020). This task restores each audit row on
this line by meaning, in its original wording or better, at the place a
reader reaches when they need it: X01 (the product's founding rationale,
restated without v1 history, which the guide keeps out of public prose),
E04, E05 and E06 (the present method's five-second picture examples, the
attention cost of a block, and the bad and good page-walk pair) and H20
(the harness labels of the minimal tier's guard inventory). In the same
paragraphs it removes the retired `diagram` block as a carrier from the
present method, since present refuses that block. H20's cut also exists on
the EPC-014 line; the release merge carries this fix there. Non-goal:
trimming anything to fit a byte test; a byte test that fails because of a
restoration is reported, not satisfied by a cut.
