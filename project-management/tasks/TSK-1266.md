# TSK-1266: Portal and present generators and validators share one parity check

## Description

Consolidated 2026-11-04 into TSK-1096 by the consolidation planning amendment (operator ruling of 2026-11-04). Every criterion below moved there word for word, with a `Moved` note naming its old id: AC-1 is TSK-1096 AC-12, AC-2 is TSK-1096 AC-13, AC-3 is TSK-1096 AC-14. This record stays as history; build work happens under TSK-1096.

Source: TSK-1162's sweep (`scratchpad/tsk1162-validator-fix.md` and its sweep table, held by coord/visual-docs), reported by coord/visual-docs at 20:0x on 2026-11-02 as a material test gap that nothing open covers.

A generator and the validator that checks its output were changed without a test tying the two together. The same pattern hid a validator defect, the pre-paint hash fixed in TSK-1162, that would have failed every adopter portal. This task adds three checks where the sweep found the pattern still open.

- The portal companion wrapper markup: the generator and the validator are not tied by a parity test.
- `FIGURE_FAMILIES` in present (`crates/codeflow-present/src/document.rs`): no test ties it to the validator's list.
- The real-build test in `docs-portal/tests/figure-gate.test.mjs:472-473` skips with "the codeflow binary is not built in this checkout", so a CI run without a built binary passes without running it.

Non-goals: new figure rules, and any change to the generators or validators beyond what a parity test needs to fail before and pass after.

## Outcome

After this merges, a change to a portal or present generator without the matching validator change fails a test instead of reaching an adopter, and the real-build figure check cannot pass by skipping in CI.

## Deliverables

- the portal companion wrapper parity test: `docs-portal/tests/` and the matching `assets/docs-portal/starter/tests/` copy (provisional; the builder confirms the homes)
- the `FIGURE_FAMILIES` parity test: `crates/codeflow-present/` tests (provisional)
- the CI-strict real-build test: `docs-portal/tests/figure-gate.test.mjs` and its starter copy

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-1 A parity test shall tie the generator and the validator for the portal companion wrapper markup, failing when either changes without the other; evidence: the test failing before the change on a mutated generator and passing after.
- AC-2 A parity test shall tie `FIGURE_FAMILIES` in present to the validator's list, failing when one gains or loses a family the other lacks; evidence: the test failing before the change on a mutated list and passing after.
- AC-3 The real-build test in `figure-gate.test.mjs` shall fail, not skip, when it runs in CI without a built codeflow binary, and shall still skip on a developer checkout; evidence: a run in CI mode without the binary failing, and a local run skipping with its message.
