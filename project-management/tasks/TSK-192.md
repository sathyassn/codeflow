# TSK-192: present and portal shipped defects fixed

## Description

The shipped present and portal journeys stop misbehaving: verifier
keep-alive stalls, the false "newer revision" notice, present at phone
width, the sequence, specimen and vertical-bar figure defects. One rebuild
and one pin bump cover all of them, since they share the three
`figure-grammar.mjs` copies, the starter pins, `validate --portal`, the
manifest tests and the present distribution checksum. The branch
`task/TSK-161-verifier-keepalive` (`d71beff9a`, 2 ahead and 51 behind the
line) is the base. This replaces TSK-161, 162, 167 and 168, TSK-122's
phone-width criteria (AC-6 to AC-9) and TSK-064's specimen and end-cap
work (AC-4, AC-11) with one bounded reading in place of its AC-5; TSK-064's
gate, PR and remaining review rows are TSK-010's. It also carries the three
findings of the Linux check of 2026-09-29 (`linux-check/`): the public
`codeflow gates` failure is a stale-toast race in the present browser
check, the same pattern sits in `browser-check.mjs`, and the renderer's
`.js.gz` differs between macOS and Linux builds only in the gzip header's
OS byte, so the export checksums depend on the build OS.

## Affected surfaces and interfaces

- `docs-portal/scripts/` (verifier, site server, adapter, figure gate), the
  portal starter copies and pins, `assets/docs-portal/manifest.json`,
  `crates/codeflow-present/web/src/` (forms, rail, stage, grammar), the
  present distribution and its checksum, the specimen copies and fixtures.

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-1 The portal verifier and its fixture tests shall serve pages with no
  idle close during a run; 495 navigations against both servers shall not
  stall. Evidence: the stall probe log; `figure-gate` and `site-server`
  tests.
- AC-2 A present form shall show "a newer revision exists" only for a
  revision newer than its own. Evidence: a deterministic browser case,
  failing before and passing after; `check:forms` stable across three runs.
- AC-3 At 375 px, the notes sheet shall open as a peek bar, an `html` stage
  shall keep a 0.75 scale floor and pan, the rail shall show no block ids,
  the selection chip shall cover nothing, and sequence lane labels shall
  stay inside the SVG. Evidence: browser cases at 375 px; the grammar
  specimen test.
- AC-4 Explanatory pages shall carry `data-cf-page-class` and the verifier
  shall fail a built route without one; the figure gate shall refuse a
  vertical bar whose end cap sits down its side; the flow, derivation,
  graph and layering specimens shall be recomposed as TSK-064 AC-4 states,
  identical across their ten copies and the fixtures. Evidence: adapter,
  verifier and gate tests with the marker or cap removed; the specimen
  copy test.
- AC-5 One rebuild: the three `figure-grammar.mjs` copies byte-identical,
  starter pins and manifest updated, the present distribution checksum
  equal across two clean rebuilds, portal and present suites green (the
  suites are the rendering proof; TSK-064's skins-by-modes-by-widths
  reading is not repeated). Evidence: `validate --portal`, manifest tests,
  suite logs. (journey)
- AC-6 In `selection-lifecycle-browser-check.mjs` (`saveAndSubmit`), after
  `submit-all` the check shall wait for
  `.cf-chrome-frame[data-commenting="false"]` before reading the "Review
  received" toast, so the previous submit's toast (still shown for 3.4 s)
  cannot satisfy the wait while the Add text button is disabled; the full
  check shall pass on Linux amd64 and arm64 Chrome. Evidence: the
  slow-response probe failing before and passing after
  (`linux-check/fix.patch`, `delay-repro-*.png`); three green runs per
  architecture (`amd64-chrome-run*.log`, `arm64-run*.log`); the public
  `codeflow-ci` `codeflow gates` job green on Linux at the landed head.
  (journey)
- AC-7 `browser-check.mjs` `submitCapturedReview` (line 797 at
  `e6831ff36`) shall wait for the same commenting-off state instead of the
  toast text, with the same probe. Evidence: the probe before and after;
  the full check green on macOS and Linux.
- AC-8 The renderer's `.js.gz` and every gzip artifact of the present
  export shall be byte-identical across macOS and Linux builds: the gzip
  header's OS byte (byte 10) is written as a fixed value or an equivalent
  deterministic setting, and `npm run check` fails when a build's gzip
  header carries an OS-dependent byte. Evidence: sha256 of the export and
  `corpus_sha256` equal from a macOS and a Linux build of the same source;
  the new check failing on a header with the OS byte set.
