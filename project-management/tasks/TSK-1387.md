# TSK-1387: Main's coverage shard plan matches measured test time

## Description

Main's `codeflow gates (coverage report)` job fails on push run 42399622467 at
`7b76f5c4e` (job 128490304943), and on PR 710 (job 128603584008): coverage
shard 2 took 18.3 test minutes, past the 18 minute `check` bound (24 job
minutes with about 6 of setup). Every pull request into main fails the same
way until the plan matches the suite again.

Cause, from the JUnit reports of 34 hosted runs between 2026-11-06 and
2026-11-08 (main pushes and pull requests):

- The data was stale. `scripts/coverage-test-times.json` came from six runs
  of 2026-10-29 and summed 87.7 test minutes, 11.0 planned per shard. The
  suite now sums about 122 minutes at the median (range 73 to 129).
- Runners vary by about 1.4 times. Each shard's wall time is its summed test
  time over 4 threads (red run: shard 2 summed 72.8 minutes, 18.2 over 4,
  recorded 18.3), and a slow runner puts that shard 1.4 times above a fast
  one. Shard 2 already reached 17.0 to 17.6 test minutes on slow runners
  before TSK-1374 (runs 42334257062, 42313827668, 42436100485).
- From the last green main run (42388031686, `acf0be5b6`) to the red one,
  every binary slowed by 23 to 36 percent at once, unrelated ones included
  (hooks_cli 749 to 992 s, codeflow-core 688 to 910 s, remedy_clearing 619 to
  760 s): the runner, not a code change.
- TSK-1374 added 22 tests worth 175 summed seconds, about 0.7 wall minutes
  (the largest, `deep_structure_is_refused_without_exhausting_the_stack`,
  92 s), which took a slow-runner shard 2 past 18.
- No regression: no test failed or timed out, no existing test became slow
  on its own, and no test changed shard.

The fix refreshes the data with `scripts/coverage-shards.py measure` from
nine runs (main pushes 42246011344, 42251645560, 42293946164, 42313278075,
42388031686; TSK-1374's 42394276012; PR 710's 42436242070; 42439481615; and
the red run 42399622467 last, so the test set is main's). The balanced plan
is then 15.3 planned minutes per shard, past the plan bound of 14, so
`PLAN_MINUTES` in `scripts/coverage-shards.py` moves from 14 to 16.

Why the bound may move: `PLAN_MINUTES` is a sizing target for the planner,
not a correctness check. Every test still runs in exactly one shard, and
`check` still fails a shard past 18 test minutes. Margin: 16 planned minutes
plus about 6 of setup is 22 of the 24 minute margin of the 30 minute job
limit. The job margin, `SETUP_MINUTES`, the 18 minute `check` bound, every
check, and the Windows planner's own `PLAN_MINUTES` (12) are unchanged.

Decision: the Director, 2026-11-07, option B; reversible; the operator is
informed and may override.

Siblings: TSK-1157 AC-5 to AC-10 own catching coverage drift in CI before a
shard overruns (they replaced TSK-1071). TSK-1321 (PR 620) moves coverage to
three hash shards with no measured data; when it lands, `PLAN_MINUTES`
returns to 14 or lower, or that record says why not (follow-up below).

Non-goals: the workflow, the number of shards, and drift detection.

## Outcome

After this merges, every pull request into main and every main push gets a
coverage report verdict that passes on a balanced plan of today's suite
instead of failing on October's measurements.

## Deliverables

- the refreshed measurements: `scripts/coverage-test-times.json`
- the plan bound and its reason: `scripts/coverage-shards.py`
- the evidence: the PR body
