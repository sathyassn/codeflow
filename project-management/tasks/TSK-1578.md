# TSK-1578: Fix the gates job timeout line in the ci README

## Description

Owner: to be assigned by the Director. Staged by coord/records on 2026-11-23 from the EPC-057 completion amendment, at the Director's direction.

The adopter CI guide says the gates job has no timeout, but the shipped
workflow gives it one. At TSK-1444's reviewed head `29433e3a47`,
`assets/base/ci/README.md:330-332` ("Timeouts and superseded runs") reads:
"The `gates` job in `codeflow-gates.yml` has no cap, because it runs your
own suite, whose length only you know; add one to your copy when you know
it". `assets/base/ci/codeflow-gates.yml:269` sets `timeout-minutes: 45` on
the `gates` job, and `:99` sets `timeout-minutes: 5` on the `decide` job. An
adopter reading the guide would not know a long suite is cut off at 45
minutes and fails.

The change is one paragraph of the guide. It rides with the next change to
that file on EPC-057's line, or with the docs pass of EPC-057's pull request
into `main`, whichever comes first.

Non-goals: changing either timeout value.

## Outcome

After this lands, an adopter reading the CI guide sees the timeout each
shipped gates workflow job really has, and how to change it in their copy.

## Deliverables

- the guide paragraph: `assets/base/ci/README.md`, "Timeouts and superseded runs"

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-1 The guide's "Timeouts and superseded runs" section shall state the timeout of each job in `codeflow-gates.yml` as the workflow sets it (45 minutes for `gates` and 5 for `decide` at `29433e3a47`), and that a project can change the `gates` value in its copy, which `codeflow update` keeps; evidence: the diff.
- AC-2 A test shall fail when the guide's stated timeout for a `codeflow-gates.yml` job differs from the workflow's `timeout-minutes`; evidence: the test, failing on the guide text at `29433e3a47` and passing at the head.
