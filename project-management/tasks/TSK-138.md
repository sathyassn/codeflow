# TSK-138: shipped docs and artifacts right-sized with duty maps

## Description

CodeFlow ships 413,152 bytes of skill markdown (measured at `b2b14569c`)
plus templates, docs and portal text, and much of it restates rules held
elsewhere or enforced by a tool. The operator asked for every shipped or
maintained doc and artifact to be right-sized without losing its expected
outcome. This task covers what TSK-127 (root map) and TSK-129 (reading
chain) do not: for each artifact it names the reader and the job, lists the
duties, removes a rule's second statement where one statement and a pointer
serve, and moves detail behind an index entry or trigger on its merits.
This task leaves SPC-013 unchanged.

Amended 2026-09-28 for the operator direction of 2026-09-27 afternoon
(workspace `RELEASE-PLAN.md`; SPC-013 planning resolution 21): size budgets
are guidelines with progressive loading, never hard caps, and shipped
content is never trimmed to fit a number. Right-sized here means each duty
is stated once, where its reader needs it, at the moment it is needed; the
byte totals are reported, and a total may rise where a duty needs the
words. A rule a tool enforces keeps its statement where the agent reads it
before acting, beside the named check; the check is the backstop, not the
only home. The 24 passages TSK-150 restored after earlier byte cuts
(`docs/verification/tsk-150-byte-cut-audit.md`) stay, with their text and
the moment they are read.

## Affected surfaces and interfaces

- Every shipped skill, reference and resource not already split by
  TSK-129; record and PR templates; managed CLAUDE.md; `docs/` (product,
  capabilities, architecture, adoption, releasing, the ADR index); the
  adopter-facing portal text; mirrors and the manifest.
- `artifact_budget_contract.rs` (reading structure; sizes are reported
  by `codeflow doctor` against guideline numbers, per TSK-150).

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] AC-1 Each changed artifact shall carry a reader and job line in its
      duty map, and a duty map shall show every duty's new home (kept, or
      moved with a pointer from where it was read), reviewed before
      landing; a duty a named check enforces stays stated where the agent
      reads it before acting and names that check; no duty may silently
      disappear (amended 2026-09-28: "trimmed" read as a size goal, and a
      named check alone is no longer a duty's home); evidence: the duty
      maps in the pull request and the review record.
- [ ] AC-2 Each artifact in scope shall be reachable from its index entry
      or trigger, and the total shipped skill markdown shall be measured
      before and after against the 413,152-byte baseline as a reported
      figure, not a gate (amended 2026-09-27, planning resolution 21,
      TSK-150); no artifact shall be shortened to meet a size, and a rise
      is acceptable where a duty needs it (amended 2026-09-28); evidence:
      `artifact_budget_contract` and the measured totals in the Closeout.
- [ ] AC-3 The pinned-duty tests shall pass with pins re-pointed, none
      deleted, and TSK-130's retention pack shall not regress on the changed
      candidate. Each passage TSK-150 restored keeps its text and the moment
      it is read (the kernel, or the same trigger), so
      `restored_audit_passages_stay_where_they_are_read` passes unchanged
      or with a pin re-pointed to a file read at that same moment, its
      pinned text intact (amended 2026-09-28); evidence:
      `orchestration_contract`, `artifact_budget_contract` and a retention
      run report.
- [ ] AC-4 (serves EPC-020 AC-13) In a freshly scaffolded project at every
      tier (minimal, standard, full) the right-sized artifacts shall be
      installed and valid, and `codeflow update` shall bring them to an
      existing project without leaving stale files; evidence: `init_e2e`,
      `tier_floor_e2e` and an update test.
