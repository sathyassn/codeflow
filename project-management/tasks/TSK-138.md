# TSK-138: shipped docs and artifacts right-sized with duty maps

## Description

CodeFlow ships 413,152 bytes of skill markdown (measured at `b2b14569c`)
plus templates, docs and portal text, and much of it restates rules held
elsewhere or enforced by a tool. The operator asked for every shipped or
maintained doc and artifact to be right-sized without losing its expected
outcome. This task covers what TSK-127 (root map) and TSK-129 (reading
chain) do not: for each artifact it names the reader and the job, lists the
duties, cuts duplication, restatement and prose a tool enforces, and moves
detail one hop away. SPC-013 stays unchanged while EPC-020 is in flight.

## Affected surfaces and interfaces

- Every shipped skill, reference and resource not already split by
  TSK-129; record and PR templates; managed CLAUDE.md; `docs/` (product,
  capabilities, architecture, adoption, releasing, the ADR index); the
  adopter-facing portal text; mirrors and the manifest.
- `artifact_budget_contract.rs` (a budget per artifact).

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] AC-1 Each trimmed artifact shall carry a reader and job line in its
      duty map, and a duty map shall show every duty's new home (kept, moved
      with pointer, or enforced by a named check), reviewed before landing;
      no duty may silently disappear; evidence: the duty maps in the pull
      request and the review record.
- [ ] AC-2 Each artifact in scope shall have a byte budget in the artifact
      budget test, and the total shipped skill markdown shall be below the
      413,152-byte baseline; evidence: `artifact_budget_contract` and a
      measured total in the Closeout.
- [ ] AC-3 The pinned-duty tests shall pass with pins re-pointed, none
      deleted, and TSK-130's retention pack shall not regress on the trimmed
      candidate; evidence: `orchestration_contract` and a retention run
      report.
- [ ] AC-4 (serves EPC-020 AC-13) In a freshly scaffolded project at every
      tier (minimal, standard, full) the trimmed artifacts shall be installed
      and valid, and `codeflow update` shall bring them to an existing project
      without leaving stale files; evidence: `init_e2e`, `tier_floor_e2e` and
      an update test.
