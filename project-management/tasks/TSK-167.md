# TSK-167: sequence lane labels fit the narrow figure

## Description

TSK-063's rendered design review of the Agent OS guide found that in a
four-lane `sequence` figure the rightmost lane label ends a few pixels past
the right edge of the narrow (375 px) SVG. It happens in all three skins, in
light and dark, in the figures doc-set-updates, deploy-steps,
memory-seed-gate and instance-update, whose last lane is "Hermes home". The
label stays legible only because the SVG overflow is visible and the figure
padding absorbs it; the figure probe reports it as text past the SVG edge.
This task makes the starter's sequence renderer keep every lane label inside
the narrow figure, by wrapping, aligning or reserving a gutter, without
shortening authored labels. A guide-side rename is a non-goal. The evidence
is the TSK-063 round 1 design review, which the Agent OS record
`docs/verification/guide-companion-figures-2026-09-28.md` on
`docs/guide-visual-doctrine` cites.

## Affected surfaces and interfaces

- The figure grammar's `sequence` layout in
  `docs-portal/scripts/figure-grammar.mjs`, its byte copies in
  `assets/docs-portal/starter/scripts/` and
  `crates/codeflow-present/web/src/`, the specimens, and the starter pins in
  the scaffold manifest.

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-1 When a `sequence` figure renders in its narrow variant, every lane
      label lies inside the SVG's view box; evidence: a grammar test with a
      four-lane specimen whose last label is at least as long as "Hermes
      home", failing before the fix, and the figure probe reporting no text
      past the SVG edge for it at 375 px.
- AC-2 The wide variant and every existing specimen keep their rule
      outcomes; evidence: the specimen table check and `check:figures` in
      cf-present, which reads the same grammar.
- AC-3 The byte copies of the grammar stay identical and the starter pins
      are updated; evidence: the copy check, `npm run check` in
      `docs-portal`, `codeflow validate --portal` on a scaffolded guide and
      the scaffold manifest tests, recorded with the head SHA.
