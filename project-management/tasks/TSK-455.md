# TSK-455: Agents check changeable state live before stating or acting on it

## Description

Agents stated, routed on and acted on changeable state from stale
information. A belief that Codex was out until 2026-10-30, carried from an
earlier usage-limit message, sent about a dozen units to Claude-only review
with reduced assurance while Codex was in fact available. The guidance let
that happen: cf-model-orchestrator's "Preflight" runs once per session per
lane and is rechecked only when a tool, binding, permission or selector
visibly changed, and a limit reset is not a change an agent sees; "Degrade
legibly" records a lost seat unavailable with no re-probe before later
assignments. Operator direction of 2026-10-24, which is the operator's
approval of this process.

Scope: one home for the rule in `.codeflow/rules/workflow-discipline.md`
("Check changeable state live"): what counts as changeable state (seat and
model availability and usage limits, tools, MCP servers and credentials, CI
runs, required checks and rulesets, PR, branch and line state, task record
status, host load and locks, other agents' progress), a live check at each
claim, routing decision or action, "unverified, last checked at <time>" when
a check is impossible, and that this holds after a resume, a compaction or
a summary. The managed always rule "Evidence, never assumption" gains one
short clause; cf-model-orchestrator's preflight expiry and degrade rule,
`resources/routing/transport.md`, `cf-delegate`'s preflight, the writing
rule "Replies and status" and the map's status row point to the one home.

Non-goals: no hook, guard, CI or `codeflow doctor` change; the rule is
guidance, pinned by contract tests.

## Deliverables

- The full rule: `.codeflow/rules/workflow-discipline.md` and `assets/base/rules/workflow-discipline.md`
- The always rule and status row: `assets/base/rule-map.toml`, rendered into `AGENTS.md` and `assets/base/AGENTS*.md.tmpl`
- Seat re-probe: `cf-model-orchestrator` `SKILL.md` and `resources/routing/transport.md`, `cf-delegate` `SKILL.md`, in `.claude/skills`, `.agents/skills` and `assets/base/agents/skills`
- Status names live-checked facts: `.codeflow/rules/writing.md` and `assets/base/rules/writing.md`
- Contract tests: `crates/codeflow-cli/tests/orchestration_contract.rs`

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-1 When an agent states, routes on or acts on state that can change, the guidance shall require a live check at that moment, or the statement "unverified" with the time of the last check, and shall say that an earlier result is a lead and never a fact, including after a resume, a compaction or a summary; the full rule, with its list of changeable state, shall live once in `workflow-discipline.md` "Check changeable state live", and the managed always rule "Evidence, never assumption" shall carry one short clause pointing to it. Evidence: a contract test in `crates/codeflow-cli/tests/orchestration_contract.rs` pinning each sentence of the rule once, the always-rule clause in `rule-map.toml` and the rendered `AGENTS.md`, plus `rule_map_contract`.
- AC-2 When the orchestrator routes a unit or a review, seat availability shall be re-probed live before that decision: preflight availability shall expire, a seat recorded unavailable shall be re-probed before every later assignment and review routing, and reduced assurance shall be recorded only after a live probe fails, naming the probe and its time; `transport.md` and `cf-delegate` shall agree and point to the one home rather than restate it. Evidence: the same contract test pinning the SKILL.md, transport.md and cf-delegate sentences and refusing the retired "once per session per lane" wording, and `skill_mirrors`.
- AC-3 When an agent reports status or hands off, each changeable fact it names shall be live-checked for that report or marked unverified with its last check time, stated in `writing.md` "Replies and status" and the map's status row, both pointing to the one home. Evidence: the contract test and the rendered `AGENTS.md` status row.
- AC-4 When the managed files change, the skills shall be mirrored byte-identically, the templates regenerated, the baseline and manifest resynced with the worktree binary's `codeflow update`, and `AGENTS.md` shall stay under 32 KiB. Evidence: `rule_map_contract`, `manifest_consistency`, `orchestration_contract`, `skill_mirrors`, `artifact_budget_contract` and `project_baselines_resolve` pass, and `wc -c AGENTS.md`.
- AC-5 When an adopter installs the scaffold with the worktree binary, a fresh minimal and a fresh full install shall carry the evidence-rule clause and the status row in `AGENTS.md`, the "Check changeable state live" section and the status bullet in `.codeflow/rules/`, and, in the full tier, the orchestrator, transport and cf-delegate sentences in both skill trees, with the retired preflight wording absent. Evidence: `codeflow init --minimal --yes` and `codeflow init --full --yes` in fresh scratch repositories, with the greps and their output in the PR. (journey)
