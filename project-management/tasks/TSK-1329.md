# TSK-1329: A task's landing credit follows the judged policy and its recorded limits

## Description

Source: the reviews of PR 582 (TSK-1309): `coordination/coordinators/planning/reviews/pr582-delta-review-fable.md` nit 1 and `pr582-ac5-review-fable.md` nits 1, 2 and 4. All three sit on the receiving-merge credit path TSK-1309 builds (`crates/codeflow-core/src/workgraph/acceptance/receiving_merges.rs`), so they are one task by the operator's sizing rule of 2026-11-04.

- Prefixes: `completions_received` reads the branch's task through `task_id_from_branch_at` (`acceptance.rs:4121`), whose prefixes come from the checkout's policy (`work_start.rs:1536-1548`), while the CLI's own-task judgment uses `judged_task_id` with the judged policy (`ci/acceptance.rs:175`). The two can disagree. Credit stays bounded by the reviewed chain, and a missing prefix refuses.
- Doc line: `receiving_merges.rs:121`, "Task commits previously landed through a side parent retain their credit", reads two ways. It describes the recorded limit: any unlanded history on the bound head's first-parent chain is credited.
- Sub-branch: a task branch that merges its own sub-branch (not the line) has that sub-branch's product work refused at landing, though the task's review covered it. This fails closed; none is on the current line (the AC-5 run found only six cases).

Line numbers are at PR 582's head 34945dc43.

## Outcome

After this merges, a maintainer can rely on landing credit being judged with the same branch prefixes as the rest of `codeflow ci`, and can read in the docs exactly which commits a reviewed head credits and which it refuses.

## Deliverables

- the prefix change and its test: `crates/codeflow-core/src/workgraph/acceptance.rs`, `work_start.rs`
- the recorded limits: the doc comment at `receiving_merges.rs:121` and the limits list beside the stacked-predecessor limit in TSK-1309's Findings or `docs/delivery.md` (the builder names which)
