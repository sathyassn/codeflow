# TSK-1049: The compaction default is revisited from the adopter's measurement

## Description

TSK-1029 changes the shipped Claude compaction point from 50 to 35 percent
of the 1M window, by the operator's decision of 2026-10-27. Its criterion
AC-4 asks that the value be revisited once the adopter of issue 358 posts
a measurement of 50, 40, 35 and 30 percent: cost per session, and whether
a session still knows its task, branch and next step after compaction. On
2026-10-27 the adopter posted only their own decision to run at 35 percent
and said they would report how it behaves; no measurement exists yet. This
task is that revisit. It is an evaluation: it reads the measurement first,
then the operator decides, and only a decided change touches the presets.

Revisit event: the adopter's measurement posted on issue 358. Until it is
posted this task waits, and 35 percent stands.

It belongs to EPC-023 (4.1.0), where the 3.2.0 plan moved its evaluation
work, so EPC-022 can close without waiting on an adopter's report.

Non-goal: running CodeFlow's own measurement of compaction points. If the
adopter's data is too thin to decide, this task records that and what more
would be needed, and the operator decides whether to measure.

## Deliverables

- the evaluation record: `docs/verification/tsk-1049-compaction-point-revisit.md`
- the operator's decision, recorded in the evaluation record and linked from a comment on issue 358
- when the decision changes the value: the presets in `assets/base/settings/`, `settings_presets.rs`, `cf-customize/references/claude-context-policy.md` and its mirrors, this repository's settings and baseline, and `changelog.d/TSK-1049.md`

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-1 When the adopter's measurement is posted on issue 358, the investigation shall come first: the evaluation record shall summarise the measured cost per session and the post-compaction recall for each tested percentage, state what the data does and does not show (sample size, models, session shapes), and compare it with the tradeoff stated in `claude-context-policy.md`; evidence: the evaluation record with a link to the measurement.
- AC-2 From that record the operator shall decide to keep 35 percent or move to another value, and the decision shall be recorded with its date and reason; evidence: the decision in the record and its link on issue 358. (serves EPC-023 AC-37)
- AC-3 If the decision changes the value, a fresh `codeflow init` shall write it with every preset, `codeflow update` shall move a project still at the recorded shipped value and keep any value the project chose, and the context policy shall state the new thresholds; evidence: preset and settings-merge regression tests failing before the change and passing after, the doc diff, and a scratch adopter run on the published previous release that shows a fresh init writing the new value, update moving the recorded shipped value, and a project-chosen value and a personal local-settings value each surviving a later update. If the operator keeps 35, this criterion records "no change" with the decision's link and no preset changes. (journey)
