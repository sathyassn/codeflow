# TSK-1282: A planted process group in a gate lock record has a sanctioned recovery

## Description

Source: the TSK-1269 Fable security review round 1, approved (`coordination/coordinators/parallel/reviews/tsk1269-security-fable-r1.md`, Low 1), a track-once item. A writer in `.git/codeflow/` can plant a `group=<live foreign pgid>` line in the per-worktree gate lock record. The gate then refuses, and the only way out is ending that process group. It is the same trust model as the slot records (TSK-1128, complete; its follow-ups say "none", so nothing covers this yet).

Severity: low. Revisit event: when the slot-record trust model is revisited. Until then the task is ready but low priority. Related: TSK-1269 and TSK-1128. The change shall not weaken the refusal: a live gate that really holds the lock still blocks every other caller.

Owner: coord/planning (builder to be staffed). Adopter-facing: the refusal message and the recovery are what an adopter sees.

## Outcome

After this merges, a maintainer whose gate refuses because of a planted or stale process group in the lock record is told which record and group, and the way to clear it without killing a process they do not own.

## Deliverables

- the Findings (the fixture, the shared trust model): this record
- the refusal message and the sanctioned recovery, with tests: the gate lock code under `crates/codeflow-core/` that the builder confirms
- the release note: `changelog.d/TSK-1282.md`

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-1 Before any change, the builder shall confirm the refusal with a fixture that plants a live foreign process group in the lock record, and record the trust model shared with the slot records; evidence: a `## Findings` section committed before the first code change.
- AC-2 With a planted or stale group line, the refusal shall name the lock record, the group it names and the sanctioned recovery, and that recovery shall need no killing of a process the caller does not own; evidence: a test, failing before the change and passing after.
- AC-3 No recovery path shall let a caller take a lock that a live gate it does not own really holds; evidence: a test with a real holder.
- AC-4 A changelog entry shall describe the change; evidence: `changelog.d/TSK-1282.md`.
