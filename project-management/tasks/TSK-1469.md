# TSK-1469: A whole-epic line judges builder lineage from a source the builder cannot write

## Description

TSK-1433 reads who built a task from `built_by` in its acceptance block and compares it with the reviewer named first in `review`. Agents under review write both values, so that check catches mistakes and drift but not a forged claim, as TSK-1433 states. This task gives a whole-epic line a lineage judgment that rests on a source the builder does not control. If no such source exists, the builder stops and the Director decides the scope before a weaker source is accepted. Unmarked lines keep TSK-1433's judgment unchanged.

This task follows TSK-1433 and becomes buildable once TSK-1433 lands on `main`. It targets `main` until EPC-055's line is cut, as TSK-1433 does.

## Outcome

After this merges, an operator can trust that a whole-epic line refuses a task whose recorded builder disagrees with a source the builder cannot write, and refuses when that source is missing. If AC-1 finds no such source, the Director's scope decision sets the outcome before any weaker source is accepted.

## Deliverables

- the lineage source and its check in `codeflow ci`: `crates/codeflow-core`
- fixtures for a disagreeing claim, a missing source and an unmarked line
- `## Findings`: each candidate source, who can write it and whether a builder can forge it

## Estimate

- Grade: Hard; band: Hard (4 to 6 h); moved: none
- Split: not needed; one outcome (lineage on a marked line rests on a source the builder cannot write); if AC-1 finds none, the Director's scope decision sets the outcome and the builder re-scores
- Basis: a source survey with a security answer, one check in `codeflow ci` with three fixtures, and a cross-family security review; graded by coord/records under issue 334's bands. The builder re-scores at claim.

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-1 Before any change, the builder shall record each candidate source of builder lineage (the commit identity the harness sets, a signed or seat-recorded provenance record, the forge's account data, and any other found), who can write it, and whether a builder can forge it, including against an agent with the operator's local credentials. If none is unforgeable, the builder stops before any change and the Director decides the scope before a weaker source is accepted; the accepted source and its limit are then recorded. Evidence: `## Findings` with file:line or documentation links, and the Director's scope decision when one was needed.
- AC-2 On a marked epic's line, `codeflow ci` shall judge builder lineage from the chosen source and refuse when it disagrees with `built_by`, naming both values. Evidence: a fixture where `built_by` claims another family than the source, refused.
- AC-3 When the source is missing or unreadable on a marked line, the check shall refuse and shall not fall back to `built_by` alone. Evidence: a fixture.
- AC-4 Unmarked lines shall be judged exactly as before. Evidence: TSK-1433's EPC-022-shaped fixture passing unchanged.
- AC-5 The change shall have a cross-family review with the `cf-security-reviewer` checklist, and the pull request shall state the threat each rule closes. Evidence: the review row.
