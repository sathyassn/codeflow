# TSK-1083: A landed task whose record was added on the line binds at its reviewed head

## Description

Source: issue #413, triaged 2026-10-30. No open task covers it: TSK-1066
is the sync-merge case and TSK-1042 only reports.

A task can land on a line through a clean landing merge M of its
reviewed head H while its record exists only on the line: a planning
amendment P added the record after the task branch was cut, so H has no
record. Once the line moves past M (L1, L2), neither binding is accepted:

```text
line:   ... P (adds TSK-A.md) -- M (landing merge of H) -- L1 -- L2
task:   B (no TSK-A.md) -- H (reviewed head, no TSK-A.md)
```

- Bound at H, `binding_source` finds the landing merge through
  `task_landing` and then calls `reviewed_span_problem` at H, which
  refuses with "is missing at completion"
  (`crates/codeflow-core/src/workgraph/acceptance.rs:1295-1350`,
  `:1392`).
- Bound at M, the direct path judges every non-record change after M
  (`later_change_judged`, `:2054`) and refuses for L1's changes.

The task stays `todo` for good, so `codeflow` lists it as ready and every
edge that depends on it looks unmet.

Design (fix A, the narrower of the two the issue offers): when the
record is absent at H and present at M, the landing path may take the
record from M, and only when all of these hold:

- `task_landing` already found M, M is on the first-parent line of a
  trusted receiving line (`untrusted_landing`, `:1714`), and M is the
  clean re-merge of its parents (`is_clean_remerge`, `:1814`);
- the record at M equals the record at M's first parent, so the task
  side added or changed nothing in it and its scope is the one the line
  planned;
- the completed record differs from the record at M only in status and
  Closeout (SPC-013 R-60), as the existing `reviewed_part` comparison
  states;
- no path other than the record differs between H and M's second
  parent. `task_landing` returns that second parent as the span head,
  and it may be a descendant of H (`acceptance.rs:1677-1702`; the
  `AdoptionFixture` at `:5528` builds that shape with a status-only
  tail); today the span check that
  would judge it sits after the missing-record return at `:1392`, so
  the new path must run that check itself, also when the record is
  absent at both H and the second parent.

A record absent at M keeps today's "is missing at completion" refusal.
The completed record is compared with the blob at M (on a worktree
completion, `task.content`), never with the blob at the second parent.
The reviewed commit stays H, and the review span stays H's. Nothing else
in the binding changes: a record present at H follows today's path, and
the direct path at M still judges later changes.

This binds trust, so the design gets a Fable review and the build a
cross-lineage review with the `cf-security-reviewer` checklist.

Non-goals: fix B of the issue (treating a binding at M as a landed
head); the sync-merge check (TSK-1066).

## Outcome

After this merges, a maintainer can complete a task that landed through a clean landing merge on a trusted line against its reviewed head, even when its record was added on the line after that head and the line has since moved.

## Deliverables

- the binding: `crates/codeflow-core/src/workgraph/acceptance.rs` (`binding_source`, `reviewed_span_problem` and the landing path)
- tests: the acceptance binding tests beside the existing task-landing cases
- release note: `changelog.d/TSK-1083.md`

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-1 Given a line P (adds the record), M (clean landing merge of the reviewed head H, which has no record), L1 and L2 (later landings that change other paths), when `codeflow task status <id> complete` binds at H from a branch at L2, the binding shall be accepted with H as the reviewed commit and M as the landing merge; evidence: a fixture test of that shape, failing before the change with "is missing at completion". (serves EPC-022 AC-169)
- AC-2 The same fixture shall still refuse, naming the cause, when the record at M differs from the record at M's first parent, when M is not the clean re-merge of its parents, when M is not on the first-parent line of a trusted receiving line, when the completed record differs from the record at M outside status and Closeout, when M's second parent is a descendant of H that changes a path other than the record (the record absent at both), and when the record is absent at M; evidence: one negative test per case, the descendant-span case failing on a patch that only takes the record from M and checks the first four conditions, with H still the reviewed commit. (serves EPC-022 AC-169)
- AC-3 A completion bound directly at M, with later non-record changes on the line, shall still refuse as today; evidence: the existing direct-path test or a new one, passing before and after. (serves EPC-022 AC-169)
