# TSK-1148: CodeFlow's initial catalog is evidenced, reviewed and complete and the check blocks

## Description

The last two bootstrap stages for CodeFlow itself. Evidence is resolved for each record (typed `test:`, `journey:`, `record:` or a declared `gap:`, with `stage` set honestly), an independent review of the whole catalog is recorded, `inventory: complete` is set, and CodeFlow's policy sets the capability check to `block` (the severity the operator approved on 2026-11-01). The pull request carries a matching `## Capability impact` section in the new format. Basis: evidence resolution for 20 to 60 records through TSK-362's resolver, one review, one policy edit.

Design: `coordination/coordinators/capabilities/design-note.md` revision 5 and the operator's comments of 2026-11-01 (about 12:47 EST) in the same folder's `DIRECTION.md`.

## Outcome

After this merges, CodeFlow's catalog is evidenced, reviewed and marked complete, and every product pull request to CodeFlow must declare its capability impact or be refused.

## Deliverables

- evidence on the records: `docs/capabilities/`
- inventory marker: the catalog's project file
- policy: `.codeflow/policy.json`, the capability check set to `block`
- the recorded review: the bootstrap epic's review task

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-1 Every record shall resolve its evidence to a test identity, a verification record or a declared gap, and `codeflow validate --docs` shall be green; evidence: the run and the gap count in the PR. (serves EPC-026 AC-12)
- AC-2 The catalog shall carry `inventory: complete` after a recorded independent review of the whole catalog by the other lineage; evidence: the review verdict. (serves EPC-026 AC-12)
- AC-3 The policy shall set the check to `block`; evidence: the policy diff. (serves EPC-026 AC-6)
- AC-4 With the installed candidate, a product PR on the line without a declaration shall be refused and one with a declaration that names each capability with a context line shall pass; evidence: both `codeflow ci` runs. (journey) (serves EPC-026 AC-6)

- AC-5 The capability verdict shall run in CodeFlow's hosted CI and be a required check on `main` and on the line this epic lands on; evidence: the hosted run for the final head showing the verdict, and the repository's live protection rules read at that time (`codeflow doctor --check remote-perimeter`). Any change to protection stays the operator's, and the task records the gap and asks if it is not required. (serves EPC-026 AC-6)
