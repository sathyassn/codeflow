# TSK-125: reconcile the EPC-018 line's records after it syncs from main

## Description

SPC-013 R-102 moves the cleanup of every line other than `main` out of the
3.0.0 release: this record is the EPC-018 line's cleanup, allocated in the EPC-020
planning change as a follow-up of TSK-109. It applies the rules TSK-109
applies to `main`'s records (design v4 section 9): statuses and Closeouts
reconciled from evidence, the line's `uid` backfill, and "historical
evidence unavailable" with the landing merge where evidence is gone.
Nothing is renumbered or invented.

- Owner: the responsible primary that runs the EPC-018 line's next sync from
  `main`, assigned at dispatch under
  `cf-model-orchestrator/resources/capability-routing.md`.
- Target: `integration/EPC-018-autonomy-roster`.
- Trigger: the EPC-018 line's next sync from `main` after EPC-020 has landed
  there. The dependency on TSK-109 is a code dependency, so readiness is
  met only when TSK-109's change is in this line's base (SPC-013 R-42).

## Affected surfaces and interfaces

- `project-management/` records on `integration/EPC-018-autonomy-roster` only; no product code.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] AC-1 The line's records shall carry their registered `uid`s through
      `ids backfill`, with no id renumbered; evidence: `ids check` on the
      line.
      Spec: R-3, R-25, R-100.
- [ ] AC-2 TSK-065's "Pending review" Closeout and the TSK-066 to TSK-068
      placeholder Closeouts shall be replaced from evidence, and EPC-017 and
      TSK-069, which are behind `main` on this line, shall match their
      landed state; evidence: the pull request.
      Spec: R-101.
- [ ] AC-3 Every reconciled record whose evidence is gone shall say
      "historical evidence unavailable" and name the landing merge;
      evidence: review of each such record.
      Spec: R-101.
- [ ] AC-4 On the line after this change, `validate --docs` shall be clean
      apart from the recorded baseline, `status` shall show no stale flag,
      and `doctor` shall reconcile the registry with the inventory;
      evidence: the three outputs.
      Spec: R-102.
