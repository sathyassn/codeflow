# TSK-177: every writing and reply surface states the plain-writing rule

## Description

The operator's direction of 2026-09-28: every place where an agent writes or
replies says that it writes plainly, and adopters inherit that through the
managed files. The patterns are already described in `cf-editorial-review`
(`references/editorial-smells.md`, "Mannered prose"), but the managed
instruction block says only "plain words", and most skills that produce text
do not say it at all. This task places one rule, in the operator's wording,
once per surface, and rewrites the existing equivalent lines into it rather
than adding second copies. The operator's addendum adds two gaps to the same
places: a default of short prose and bullets, and figure forms that name
Markdown files explicitly. It adds no new doctrine.

The rule, full form, for instruction files:

> **Write plainly.** Everything you write, replies and status updates
> included, is simple, straightforward and clear, with the detail the reader
> needs and no more. Avoid mannered prose, writing that performs for effect:
> slogans, "not X but Y" turns, rhetorical triplets, dramatic fragments,
> stacked hedges, colon reveals, self-narration, ceremonial framing and walls
> of text. State the fact directly.

The short form, for skills and agents, one line where they tell the agent to
write: "Write it plainly: simple, straightforward and clear, no mannered
prose (see `.codeflow/rules/writing.md`)", with the noun adapted.

The addendum, for `rules/writing.md` and the doc-writing skills: default to
short prose and bullets, and write long prose only when the reader asks for
it or the artifact is prose by nature. Figures take the form the surface
shows: inline HTML where the harness renders it, a `cf-present` page when a
figure needs a full page, the portal's figure grammar on docs-portal pages,
and fenced ASCII in other Markdown files (READMEs, docs, records, PR bodies)
and in terminal output; never Mermaid.

Non-goals: no new pattern list and no change to the em and en dash rule.
`docs/` and the docs portal describe no writing conventions for adopters, so
neither changes.

- Managed instructions: `assets/base/rule-map.toml` and the three AGENTS
  templates it renders; this repository's `AGENTS.md` managed block. The
  CLAUDE templates open with `@AGENTS.md` and Codex reads `AGENTS.md`
  natively; the Grok starter carries only hooks, so none of them gains a copy.
- Writing references: `assets/base/rules/writing.md`, the PR bodies bullet of
  `assets/base/rules/git-rules.md`, and the PR template
  (`assets/base/ci/pull_request_template.md`, this repository's
  `.github/pull_request_template.md`).
- Skills and agents: `cf-editorial-review`, `cf-consult`,
  `cf-model-orchestrator`, `cf-delegate`, `cf-plan`, `cf-ship`, `cf-develop`,
  `cf-present`, `cf-docs-portal`, `cf-design`, `cf-estimate`,
  `cf-evaluate-model`, the `cf-method` lifecycle reply rule and the
  `cf-reviewer` agent, in `assets/base/` and their mirrors; the CF-OUT-003
  marker in `cf-evaluate-model/resources/requirements.json`.
- Reported size guidelines: the rule count and rule line in `rule_map.rs`
  and the skill and agent numbers in `reading.rs`, raised where the new
  text passes them so a fresh install reports clean.
- Installed copies: `.claude/`, `.agents/`, `.codeflow/rules/`,
  `.codeflow/.baseline/` and `.codeflow/manifest.json` hashes; `CHANGELOG.md`.
- Consumers: every adopter at every tier through `codeflow update`. The
  change is additive text in managed files: a minor change, not a breaking
  one, since no command, flag, config key, format or default changes and a
  project's own section is untouched.

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-1 When a project is scaffolded or updated at any tier, its `AGENTS.md`
      managed block shall carry a "Write plainly." always rule with the full
      form above, pointing at
      `.agents/skills/cf-editorial-review/references/editorial-smells.md` at
      the standard and full tiers and at `.codeflow/rules/writing.md` at the
      minimal tier, and its "Show complex things" rule shall name fenced
      ASCII for Markdown files and a terminal (and the portal's figure grammar
      on docs-portal pages at the standard and full tiers); evidence:
      `rule_map_contract` pins in every tier's rendered block, with
      `assets/base/AGENTS.md.tmpl`, `AGENTS.full.md.tmpl` and
      `AGENTS.minimal.md.tmpl` regenerated from `rule-map.toml`.
- AC-2 `assets/base/rules/writing.md` shall open, after its one-paragraph
      lead, with the full-form rule, its pointer to the pattern list, and the
      short-prose default; the former "Write plainly: no slogans" sentence
      and "Write in a plain, calm voice" shall be folded into it and no
      longer appear; "Shape the deliverable" shall say short prose for the
      rest instead of "prose that earns its place"; and "Figures by surface"
      shall list the addendum's forms, naming READMEs, docs, records and PR
      bodies for fenced ASCII and the portal's figure grammar on docs-portal
      pages; evidence: a contract test that pins the lead, the figure list
      and the absence of the folded lines in the asset, the installed copy
      and the baseline, with the reply-duty test's figure clause for the
      writing reference updated to the new wording.
- AC-3 Each of these files shall state the short form once, where it tells
      the agent to write: `cf-consult` (the synthesis),
      `cf-model-orchestrator/SKILL.md` (briefs, status and reports),
      `cf-delegate/SKILL.md` (briefs), `cf-plan` (records), `cf-ship` (PR
      body and release notes), `cf-develop` (the completion report),
      `cf-present` (pages), `cf-docs-portal` (pages), `cf-design` (copy),
      `cf-estimate` (reports), `cf-evaluate-model` (grader notes),
      `cf-method/references/workflow-lifecycle.md` (replies), the PR bodies
      bullet of `rules/git-rules.md`, the PR template header, and
      `cf-reviewer.md`, where the reviewer also treats mannered prose in
      changed text as a finding; `cf-editorial-review/SKILL.md` shall open
      with the rule and name "Mannered prose" in its smells reference as the
      pattern list; evidence: the same contract test, one pinned line per file.
- AC-4 The doc-writing skills shall carry the addendum where they tell the
      agent to write: `cf-plan`, `cf-ship`, `cf-present` and `cf-docs-portal`
      the short-prose default, `cf-plan` and `cf-editorial-review` fenced
      ASCII for Markdown files, and `cf-docs-portal` the portal's figure
      grammar (a `cf-stage` fence), each by rewriting the existing line where
      one exists; and the lifecycle reply rule's fenced ASCII bullet shall
      read "Use fenced ASCII on a terminal or other plain-text surface, in a
      Markdown file (a README, doc, record or PR body), or when unsure what
      the surface renders", replies becoming records and PR bodies; evidence:
      the same contract test and the reply-duty test's lifecycle column.
- AC-5 Every changed managed file shall match byte for byte across
      `assets/base/`, its `.claude/` and `.agents/` mirrors, its installed
      copy and `.codeflow/.baseline/`, with `.codeflow/manifest.json` hashes
      updated, and a fresh scaffold at each tier shall install the new text;
      evidence: `manifest_consistency` and `init_e2e` green.
- AC-6 The cf-evaluate-model requirement CF-OUT-002 shall cover replies and
      status updates; it already names "every operator-facing reply" and "a
      reply, report, or pull request body" with mannered prose, so it stays
      unchanged. The CF-OUT-003 marker on the lifecycle's fenced ASCII bullet
      shall follow its rewritten text, and no other marker changes; evidence:
      the `model_eval_contract` suite green.
- AC-7 `CHANGELOG.md` shall carry one pending 3.0.0 entry under Changed,
      marked `minor`, that says what adopters get from `codeflow update`;
      evidence: `codeflow ci` with the PR body clean.
- AC-8 (journey) On a fresh `codeflow init` project at each tier, and on
      an existing project after `codeflow update`, the installed
      `AGENTS.md` shall carry the "Write plainly." rule,
      `.codeflow/rules/writing.md` shall lead with it, and
      `codeflow doctor --check reading` shall report a fresh install within
      its guidelines; evidence: CLI and scaffold tests that drive the built
      binary through init, update and doctor.
