# TSK-081: instructions that name roles and workloads only

## Description

Rewrite the instruction surfaces so duties are assigned to seats, standing
roles and catalog duties, and so every launcher gets its selector and effort
from `codeflow models resolve`. After this task a model added or removed
changes no instruction. Today no skill or contract carries a concrete
selector (checked at `d092295ea`; the only managed hit outside the catalog is
the `sonnet` example in `pipeline.workflow.js`), but duties are written by
family and by the old binding: "Claude leads design", "the Claude design
owner ... through its primary", "load the concrete primary selectors", and a
same-Claude fallback described in prose.

Rules for every managed text: it may name seat ids, standing role ids, duty
ids, the catalog path and the command, and a family (`Claude`, `Codex`,
`Grok`) only where a lineage invariant is the point (the design owner is
Claude lineage; review is opposite the author). It never names a line,
version, selector or alias.

Surfaces:

- orchestrator `SKILL.md` "Claude leads design" bullet (the pinned heading
  stays): the design owner is seat `claude-primary`; workers design only
  through scoped tuples; fallback is the seat's next eligible alternative;
  and the preflight step that selects a lane calls `codeflow models resolve`
  with the duty, host, author, fresh exclusions and observed ids, and
  applies the drift response; net zero or negative bytes;
- `capability-routing.md` design owner paragraph and "Review and
  degradation": seat and duty words, `unit-review`, `body-review`,
  `computer-use-qa` and `design-and-editorial-review` by name, the
  same-lineage second-opinion label, the drift rule by pointer to ADR-0069;
- `quality-contract.md` design paragraph (near line 139): the same rule in
  the same words;
- `CLAUDE.md.tmpl` and `AGENTS.md.tmpl`: "load the concrete primary
  selectors" becomes "resolve duties with `codeflow models resolve`";
  `AGENTS.md.tmpl` changes at net zero bytes;
- `cf-delegate`, `cf-herdr`, `grok-host.md`: the `<selector>` and `<effort>`
  placeholders are the values the command returned; the pinned placeholder
  strings stay;
- `pipeline.workflow.js` comment: a seat-neutral placeholder;
- the evaluation markers and the pins in `artifact_budget_contract.rs` and
  `orchestration_contract.rs` that quote the rewritten lines.

`cf-design/SKILL.md` needs no change: it names the role
`claude-judgment-primary`, which resolves to Opus through seat
`claude-primary`; TSK-085's design variants prove that.

Non-goal: the autonomy lines of the same files (TSK-076), the catalog data
(TSK-085), the workspace contract, Agent OS (TSK-082).
