# TSK-081: instructions that name roles and workloads only

## Description

Rewrite the instruction surfaces so duties are assigned to seats, standing
roles and workloads that the catalog resolves, and a model added or removed
changes no instruction. Today no skill or contract carries a concrete
selector (checked at `d092295ea`; the only managed hit outside the catalog is
the `sonnet` example in `pipeline.workflow.js`), but duties are written by
family and by the old binding: "Claude leads design", "the Claude design
owner ... through its primary", "load the concrete primary selectors", and a
same-Claude fallback described in prose.

Rules for every managed text:

- it may name seat ids, standing role ids, workload ids, the catalog path,
  and a family (`Claude`, `Codex`, `Grok`) only where a lineage invariant is
  the point (the design owner is Claude lineage; review is opposite the
  author);
- it never names a line, version, selector or alias.

Surfaces:

- orchestrator `SKILL.md` "Claude leads design" bullet (the pinned heading
  stays): the design owner is seat `claude-primary`; workers design only
  through scoped-qualified tuples; fallback is the seat's next eligible line;
  net zero or negative bytes;
- `capability-routing.md` design owner paragraph and the "Review and
  degradation" UI paragraph: seat and workload words, `computer-use-qa` and
  `design-and-editorial-review` by name, the same-lineage second-opinion
  label;
- `quality-contract.md` design paragraph (near line 139): the same rule in
  the same words;
- `CLAUDE.md.tmpl` and `AGENTS.md.tmpl`: "load the concrete primary
  selectors" becomes "resolve seats and workloads from the catalog";
  `AGENTS.md.tmpl` changes at net zero bytes;
- `cf-delegate`, `cf-herdr`, `grok-host.md`: the `<selector>` placeholder is
  "the selector the catalog resolved for the workload";
- `pipeline.workflow.js` comment: a seat-neutral placeholder instead of a
  model name;
- evaluation kit: the design routing case (near `cases.json` line 2786, with
  `opus_as_fable_high_substitute`) and the Terra case (near line 5068) are
  re-scoped to seat and workload signals with fictional model names in
  fixtures, and the design case gains three variants: both Claude lines
  available (design stays with the primary seat, the review line gives a
  labeled second opinion), the review line absent (design proceeds, the
  missing second opinion is recorded), and a candidate worker offered
  fidelity approval (refused). Each keeps a faulty control that fails.

`cf-design/SKILL.md` needs no change: it names the role
`claude-judgment-primary`, which now resolves to Opus through seat
`claude-primary`; the design variants above prove that.

Non-goal: the autonomy lines of the same files (TSK-076), new autonomy cases
(TSK-077), the workspace contract, Agent OS (TSK-082).
