# TSK-194: designated seats qualified once on the suite

## Description

The catalog says which designated primary seats passed the evaluation
suite, `doctor --check model-bindings` stops warning for them, and the
release notes' "not run" rows close. One run of the full suite per
designated primary seat on the released binary replaces TSK-139's per
version and per profile cohorts and TSK-116's three trials and retention
pack; the R-105 pack is included for the two primaries. It runs after the
kit repair (TSK-191 on the delivery line) and the Codex profile fix
(TSK-190), so the fails of the 2026-09-29 native runs are re-judged
against repaired fixtures and guidance, not carried as they are. Those two

  (`claude-code`, `codex-cli`, `grok-cli`) on the released binary, graded
  by the other lineage, with the R-105 pack included for the two primaries.
  Evidence: `result-*.scored.json` per harness, `validate-result` clean,
  the suite digest pinned.

  Evidence: catalog diff; doctor output.
- AC-3 Every hard failure shall be read by a human once and its disposition

- AC-4 The `gpt-5.6-sol`, `gpt-6-luna` and Grok naming questions shall be
  settled by one native launch each. Evidence: launch logs.

  TSK-191's repaired fixture cases (rows 13, 02, 03, 06 and 15, cases 00, 04
  and 16) and its guidance cases 11, 13, 15, 16, 17 and 19. A validity flag
  on any of them stops TSK-194 and returns that fixture or guidance to its
  owner before the full suite runs. Evidence: the first batch's launch and
  observation records and graded results, which are also TSK-191's native
  AC-2 and AC-3 evidence. Reason: the full suite runs every case natively
  on every seat, so running the repaired cases first proves the fixtures

- AC-6 A Claude trial shall start with no plugin, synced skill or claude.ai
  connector from the dedicated Claude evaluator home, and a trial whose

  runner and kit tests, failing before and passing after; a live Claude
  trial's `observation.json` `claude_extensions`. Reason: added 2026-10-04;
  every Claude trial of the 2026-10-01 first batch loaded about 100
  account skills and up to 148 account MCP tools.
- AC-7 The runner shall recognise Codex 0.160.0's idle and pending screens.
  Evidence: the frames pinned under `evals/qualification/frames/` and their
