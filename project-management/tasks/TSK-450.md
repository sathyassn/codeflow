# TSK-450: Commit and PR style rules warn and the audit weighs severity

## Description

Style rules and the dependency audit cost retries and stalls without
protecting a boundary. The description limit, body shape and count, unlisted
trailers, en and em dashes and the PR Summary shape refuse commits and PR
edits (`crates/codeflow-core/src/hooks/standards.rs:172-200, 266-350, 546-590`;
`crates/codeflow-cli/src/cmd/ci/pr_body.rs:1160-1270`); only the type, the
breaking marker and the subject length feed a machine. The dependency audit
fails the security review job on any advisory with no severity threshold
(`.github/workflows/codeflow-ci.yml:1372-1485`); a medium advisory in a
dev lockfile and the tokio advisory each stopped every PR. Seven policy keys
(`security.script_bypass`, `interpreter_scan`, `enforcement_baseline`,
`workflow_pushes`, `sandbox_retry`, `sandbox_retry_allow` and
`git.clean_regenerable`) have no consumer in the crates and
`docs/policy-reference.md:891` says the sandbox keys are not read yet.
Source: the operator approved process audit of 2026-10-23, including the
operator's decision of that day on the dependency audit (line numbers at
`c9993bcf3`).

Scope: (1) Move to warn: description over 50, body shape and count, unlisted
trailers, `policy_characters` and the PR Summary shape (a level for the
description limit may need a new key; place it per `policy_key_placement`).
Stay at block: commit type, the breaking marker, subject 72, `ai_attribution`
(an AI trailer is still refused, though other unlisted trailers only warn),
emoji, secrets, branch prefix, the `Task:` line, required PR sections and the
release-impact job. The `ai_attribution` refusal message names the Claude
setting that turns the host's trailer off (`includeCoAuthoredBy` and
`attribution`), and the shipped settings keep both off. (2) The dependency
audit becomes severity-aware: critical and high advisories block; any advisory
a PR newly introduces blocks; a medium advisory passes only with a triage
entry naming owner, rationale and expiry; low and informational advisories are
reported only; dev-only lockfiles get no exemption; `security_review` and
`dep_audit` share the one rule; suppressions and triage entries are read from
the trusted commit only; a failed scan stays missing evidence that fails the
job. The builder states how "newly introduced" is decided (advisory sets of
base and head) and how medium and "newly introduced" combine, and discloses
the choice. (3) The seven unread keys leave the shipped policy, the schema
and `docs/policy-reference.md`; a policy file that still sets one keeps
loading. (4) `codeflow policy` prints a report of effective values.

Non-goals: no hook, guard, secret scan or remote rule changes. Protection
lost by (2): a medium advisory without a triage entry, and a low one, no
longer stops a merge; both are still printed on every run and the weekly scan.

Effect on adopting projects: shipped style defaults match the intent
(advisory style, blocking semantics); adopters that set the seven removed keys
see them ignored with a notice, and the shipped template's audit follows the
same severity rule when `dep_audit` is `block`.

Owned files: `assets/base/policy.json` and `.codeflow/policy.json` (all lines
but `test_gate_on_push`, which TSK-449 owns), `hooks/standards.rs`,
`hooks/policy.rs`, `hooks/policy_schema.rs`, `security/privilege.rs` where it
names a removed key, `crates/codeflow-cli/src/cmd/policy.rs`, the Summary
shape and `policy_characters` hunks of `ci/pr_body.rs` and `ci.rs`,
`docs/policy-reference.md` (but the `test_gate_on_push` row), the dependency
audit step of the security review job in `codeflow-ci.yml`, `osv-scanner.toml`,
a new audit script under `scripts/` with its test, the shipped Claude settings
under `assets/base/settings/` and `.claude/settings.json`. The last-release
fixtures are not edited. The Reviews and release-impact hunks of `pr_body.rs`
are TSK-451.

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-1 When a commit has a description over 50, a body that is not 3 short bullets, an unlisted non-AI trailer, an en or em dash, or a PR has a Summary of the wrong shape, the hook and `codeflow ci` shall warn and not refuse; a wrong type, a missing breaking marker, a subject over 72, an AI attribution trailer, an emoji, a staged secret, a bad branch prefix, a missing `Task:` line or a missing required section shall still refuse. Evidence: hook and `codeflow ci` tests in `crates/codeflow-cli/tests/hooks_cli.rs` and `crates/codeflow-core/tests/`, one fail-before and pass-after per lowered rule and one refusal per kept rule. (serves EPC-022 AC-126)
- AC-2 When the `ai_attribution` rule refuses a trailer, its message shall name the Claude setting that turns the trailer off, and the shipped Claude settings shall keep `includeCoAuthoredBy` false and `attribution` empty. Evidence: a hook message test and a settings contract test. (serves EPC-022 AC-126)
- AC-3 When the dependency audit runs, a critical or high advisory shall fail the security review job, an advisory newly introduced by the PR shall fail it, a medium advisory shall pass only with a trusted-commit triage entry naming owner, rationale and expiry (an expired or incomplete entry fails), a low or informational advisory shall be printed and pass, a dev-only lockfile shall follow the same rule, and a failed scan shall fail the job. Evidence: the audit script's tests over osv-scanner JSON fixtures for each case, and the hosted run on this PR. (serves EPC-022 AC-126)
- AC-4 When `security_review` or `dep_audit` is `block` or `warn`, both shall apply the one severity rule and a PR shall not be able to edit the suppressions or triage entries that judge it. Evidence: a workflow contract test and a test that the entries are read from the base commit. (serves EPC-022 AC-126)
- AC-5 When the seven unread keys are removed, the shipped policy, schema and `docs/policy-reference.md` shall not name them and a policy file that still sets one shall load unchanged with a notice. Evidence: `policy_key_placement`, `crates/codeflow-core/tests/policy_last_release_journey` style compatibility test against the last-release fixtures, and a grep of the crates for the seven names. (serves EPC-022 AC-126)
- AC-6 When `codeflow policy` is run, it shall print each effective policy value and where it came from. Evidence: a case in `crates/codeflow-cli/tests/policy_cli.rs`. (serves EPC-022 AC-126)
- AC-7 When a builder commits a body with a prose line and a PR with a long Summary (journey), both shall land with warnings while an attribution trailer is still refused. Evidence: the commits and PR of this task and the hook output pasted in the PR. (journey) (serves EPC-022 AC-126)
