# TSK-450: Commit and PR style rules warn and the audit weighs severity

## Description

Style rules and the dependency audit cost retries and stalls without
protecting a boundary. The description limit, body shape and count, unlisted
trailers, en and em dashes and the PR Summary shape refuse commits and PR
edits (`crates/codeflow-core/src/hooks/standards.rs:172-200, 266-350, 546-590`;
`crates/codeflow-cli/src/cmd/ci/pr_body.rs:1160-1270`); only the type, the
breaking marker and the subject length feed a machine. The dependency audit
fails the security review job on any advisory with no severity threshold
(`.github/workflows/codeflow-ci.yml:1372-1485`); a medium advisory in a
dev lockfile and the tokio advisory each stopped every PR. Seven policy keys
(`security.script_bypass`, `interpreter_scan`, `enforcement_baseline`,
`workflow_pushes`, `sandbox_retry`, `sandbox_retry_allow` and
`git.clean_regenerable`) have no consumer in the crates and
`docs/policy-reference.md:891` says the sandbox keys are not read yet.
Source: the operator approved process audit of 2026-10-23, including the
operator's decision of that day on the dependency audit (line numbers at
`c9993bcf3`).

Scope: (1) Move to warn: description over 50, body shape and count, unlisted
trailers, `policy_characters` and the PR Summary shape (a level for the
description limit may need a new key; place it per `policy_key_placement`).
Stay at block: commit type, the breaking marker, subject 72, `ai_attribution`
(an AI trailer is still refused, though other unlisted trailers only warn),
emoji, secrets, branch prefix, the `Task:` line, required PR sections and the
release-impact job. The `ai_attribution` refusal message names the Claude
setting that turns the host's trailer off (`includeCoAuthoredBy` and
`attribution`), and the shipped settings keep both off. (2) The dependency audit follows one precedence table, used here, in the
criteria and in EPC-022 AC-126. An advisory is "new" when its identity
(advisory id, package, ecosystem) is in the head scan and not in the base
scan. Rows apply in order: (a) a scan that fails or is incomplete is missing
evidence and fails the job at both `block` and `warn`; (b) an advisory matched by a trusted
suppression that has not expired passes and is reported; (c) a new advisory
of any severity, low and informational included, fails; (d) a critical or high
advisory fails unless a trusted triage entry (owner, rationale, expiry) states
that it is not reachable, and unknown or missing reachability evidence counts
as reachable; (e) a medium advisory fails unless a trusted triage entry
(owner, rationale, expiry) exists and has not expired; (f) a low or
informational advisory that is not new is printed and passes. There is no
dev-only exemption. Composition: `security_review` and `dep_audit` share the
one table and the effective level is the stricter of the two; at `block` a
failing advisory row fails the job; at `warn` the same advisory rows print as
warnings and the job passes; a failed or incomplete scan fails the job at both
`block` and `warn`; at `off` the audit does not run. Suppressions and triage entries are read from the base commit
only. The same table is mirrored by the shipped template step (this task owns
`assets/base/ci/codeflow-ci.yml`'s audit step). (3) The seven unread keys leave the shipped policy, the schema
and `docs/policy-reference.md`; a policy file that still sets one keeps
loading. (4) `codeflow policy` prints a report of effective values.

Non-goals: no hook, guard, secret scan or remote rule changes. Protection
lost by (2): a low or informational advisory that is not new, a medium advisory
with a valid trusted triage entry, and a critical or high advisory with a
trusted not-reachable triage entry no longer stop a merge; all are still
printed on every run and the weekly scan. A medium advisory without a triage
entry still fails, and so does any new advisory.

Effect on adopting projects: shipped style defaults match the intent
(advisory style, blocking semantics); adopters that set the seven removed keys
see them ignored with a notice, and the shipped template's audit follows the
same severity rule when `dep_audit` is `block`.

Owned files: `assets/base/policy.json` and `.codeflow/policy.json` (all lines
but `test_gate_on_push`, which TSK-449 owns), `hooks/standards.rs`,
`hooks/policy.rs`, `hooks/policy_schema.rs`, `security/privilege.rs` where it
names a removed key, `crates/codeflow-cli/src/cmd/policy.rs`, the Summary
shape and `policy_characters` hunks of `ci/pr_body.rs` and `ci.rs`,
`docs/policy-reference.md` (but the `test_gate_on_push` row), the dependency
audit step of the security review job in `codeflow-ci.yml`, `osv-scanner.toml`,
the shipped audit step in `assets/base/ci/codeflow-ci.yml`, a new audit script under `scripts/` with its test, the shipped Claude settings
under `assets/base/settings/` and `.claude/settings.json`. The last-release
fixtures are not edited. The Reviews and release-impact hunks of `pr_body.rs`
are TSK-451.

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-1 When a commit has a description over 50, a body that is not 3 short bullets, an unlisted non-AI trailer, an en or em dash, or a PR has a Summary of the wrong shape, the hook and `codeflow ci` shall warn and not refuse; a wrong type, a missing breaking marker, a subject over 72, an AI attribution trailer, an emoji, a staged secret, a bad branch prefix, a missing `Task:` line or a missing required section shall still refuse. Evidence: hook and `codeflow ci` tests in `crates/codeflow-cli/tests/hooks_cli.rs` and `crates/codeflow-core/tests/`, one fail-before and pass-after per lowered rule and one refusal per kept rule. (serves EPC-022 AC-126)
- AC-2 When the `ai_attribution` rule refuses a trailer, its message shall name the Claude setting that turns the trailer off, and the shipped Claude settings shall keep `includeCoAuthoredBy` false and `attribution` empty. Evidence: a hook message test and a settings contract test. (serves EPC-022 AC-126)
- AC-3 When the dependency audit runs, the verdict shall follow the precedence table in the Description exactly. Evidence: audit script tests over osv-scanner JSON base and head fixtures, one per row and one per overlap: new low (fails), new medium with triage (fails because new), old low (passes), old medium without triage (fails), old medium with triage (passes), expired triage (fails), old high with no reachability evidence (fails), old high with a not-reachable triage entry (passes), suppressed advisory (passes), a dev-only lockfile advisory (judged by the same table), and a failed scan and an incomplete scan (each fails at both `block` and `warn`). (serves EPC-022 AC-126)
- AC-4 When `security_review` and `dep_audit` differ or are `block`, `warn` or `off`, the effective level shall be the stricter of the two, `block` shall fail the job on a failing row, `warn` shall print the advisory rows as warnings and pass, a failed or incomplete scan shall fail the job at both `block` and `warn`, `off` shall skip the audit, and a PR shall not be able to edit the suppressions or triage entries that judge it (they are read from the base commit). Evidence: a workflow contract test of the three levels over the fixtures of AC-3, with failed-scan and incomplete-scan fixtures run at both `block` and `warn` (each failing) and at `off` (not run), a test that entries are read from the base commit, and the shipped template's audit step passing the same contract test (`assets/base/ci/codeflow-ci.yml`). (serves EPC-022 AC-126)
- AC-5 When the seven unread keys are removed, the shipped policy, schema and `docs/policy-reference.md` shall not name them and a policy file that still sets one shall load unchanged with a notice. Evidence: `policy_key_placement`, `crates/codeflow-core/tests/policy_last_release_journey` style compatibility test against the last-release fixtures, and a grep of the crates for the seven names. (serves EPC-022 AC-126)
- AC-6 When `codeflow policy` is run, it shall print each effective policy value and where it came from. Evidence: a case in `crates/codeflow-cli/tests/policy_cli.rs`. (serves EPC-022 AC-126)
- AC-7 (journey) When a builder commits a body with a prose line and a PR with a long Summary, both shall land with warnings while an attribution trailer is still refused. Evidence: the commits and PR of this task and the hook output pasted in the PR. (serves EPC-022 AC-126)
  - Amended 2026-11-02: the journey tag moved to open the criterion, where the check counts it; the wording and the serves tag are unchanged.
- AC-8 The dependency audit's "error during extraction" check and the secret scan's "gitleaks logged ERR/FTL" check shall capture the scanner output first and then match it, so neither result depends on a pipeline's exit status. Today they run `! printf ... | grep -q ...` (`assets/base/ci/codeflow-ci.yml:1151` and `:885`; this repository's `.github/workflows/codeflow-ci.yml`, including `:1545`). Under pipefail, an early grep exit can SIGPIPE printf; the pipeline returns 141, and `!` turns that into success, passing a failed scan. Risk: latent, not live. Hosted steps set no `shell:`, so they run `bash -e` without pipefail, but adding `shell: bash` or `set -o pipefail` would make the check fail open, and `ci_pin` already runs it that way. Evidence: a `ci_pin` fake scanner mode that prints the error plus about 2 MB (so the SIGPIPE is deterministic) fails before the change and passes after, for both checks. The template, managed baseline and manifest are updated. This repository's own workflow gets an operator-ready script. Every other negated pipeline under pipefail in the shipped workflows is fixed the same way, or recorded with the reason it cannot misfire.
  - Amended 2026-11-02: added; reason: coord/parallel at 23:44 and 23:5x (the TSK-336 builder found the fail-open pattern in the shipped workflow checks); this task already owns "a failed or incomplete scan fails the job" for the dependency audit (AC-3, AC-4), and the mechanism and its secret-scan sibling are not covered there.
  - Security note: this criterion changes a security admission check, so the review is cross-lineage with the `cf-security-reviewer` checklist, and the PR states the threat it closes and why the design is the standard one.
