# TSK-1136: The capability check ships with its policy key, template, remedy and guide

## Description

The shipping half of design unit U3. The policy key, the tier-aware defaults (`off` at minimal, `warn` at standard and full: the operator's option B decision of 2026-11-01), the remedy text, the `## Capability impact` section in the PR template, `pr-evidence.md`, the rule-map, skill and reviewer wording, and the harness-neutral guide `.codeflow/rules/capabilities.md` on declaring impact. The bootstrap part of the guide is TSK-1144. It replaces the "not a blocking gate" wording named in design section D8. Basis: seven instruction files plus init and update defaults and their tests. Security review owed: the update path rewrites policy for existing repositories and must preserve an explicit `block`.

Design: `coordination/coordinators/capabilities/design-note.md` revision 5 (workspace repository) and its acceptance tests.

## Outcome

After this merges, a new or updated project at standard or full tier gets the capability check at `warn`, a minimal project can opt in with one key, and the agent instructions say how to declare impact.

## Deliverables

- policy key and tier defaults: `crates/codeflow-core/src/hooks/policy.rs`, and the init and update writers
- PR template and evidence reference: the `assets/base/` pull request template, `.agents/skills/cf-ship/references/pr-evidence.md`
- instruction wording and the guide: `assets/base/rules/capabilities.md`, `cf-method`, `cf-ship`, the rule-map and the reviewer agents (mirrored across `.claude` and `.agents`)
- scaffold manifest entries: `assets/base/scaffold-manifest.toml`

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-1 A fresh minimal init shall carry `off` and a fresh standard init `warn`; update of a 3.x minimal project shall add `off` and of a 3.x standard project `warn` once its recorded pin reads the key (the update that raises the pin adds nothing and says so); an init or update whose reader is older than the key shall not write it; a repeated update shall preserve an explicit `block`, a minimal project's opt-in `warn` and a standard project's explicit `off`; evidence: init and update tests per tier. (serves EPC-026 AC-4)
  - Amended 2026-11-04 with coord/catalog's approval: a binary older than a policy key rejects the whole policy file and stops every commit, so the key is written only when the release that reads the file knows it (`docs/policy-reference.md` "Where a new key goes"; `crates/codeflow-core/tests/policy_key_placement.rs`). The tier levels are unchanged.
- AC-2 The PR template, `pr-evidence.md`, the rule-map, the skills and the reviewer agents shall describe the `## Capability impact` section and replace the "not a blocking gate" wording; evidence: the manifest consistency test and a grep check of the named files. (serves EPC-026 AC-4)
- AC-3 The template shall show the shape of the section with a worked example of an added, a changed and an improved capability, each as `- <Verb> CAP-NNN <name>: <what changed for its users>`, and the guide shall explain what the author writes, what the check compares and what passes and fails, with one worked example of each outcome, without naming one harness; evidence: review of the template and guide against the check's fixtures. (serves EPC-026 AC-4)
- AC-4 (journey) A product PR shall pass or fail the same way through scaffolded CI on GitHub, GitLab and the generic wrapper; evidence: the three scaffold tests. (serves EPC-026 AC-4)
  - Amended 2026-11-02: the journey tag moved to open the criterion, where the check counts it; the wording and the serves tag are unchanged.
