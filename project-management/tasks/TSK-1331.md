# TSK-1331: Evaluation runs check the estimation rubric, the sizing test and the qualification runner's peer

## Description

The estimation method TSK-1013 ships needs evidence that its rubric grades
consistently and that the plan flow behaves as written. This task holds the
evaluation criteria the operator moved out of TSK-1013 on 2026-11-04, word
for word: the independent grading that produces the rubric's anchored
examples, and the native qualification cases for the sizing test. It also
holds the two native dry runs with a live Codex peer that the Director moved
out of TSK-1102 (the qualification runner) on 2026-11-04, so every
evaluation run moved out of the build tasks is in one place.

Recorded work it starts from: the predeclared cases, both graders' files
and the reconciliation in `project-management/estimates/rubric-check-1/`
(committed on TSK-1013's branch; one model family, reduced assurance). The
clarifications that check produced are in `rubric.md` as version
`codeflow-agentic-2`; the anchored examples are not, and this task adds
them once a Codex seat has graded.

Non-goals: changing the rubric's points or cuts; the time-box profile
itself (TSK-1013).

## Outcome

after this merges, a planner grading a package can trust the `codeflow-agentic-2` rubric's anchored examples, because two lineages graded them under a predeclared rule, the sizing test is qualified by native cases, and the qualification runner is shown to open a live Codex peer and to refuse one at the hook screen.

## Deliverables

- grading record: `project-management/estimates/rubric-check-1/` (a Codex grade beside the existing files) or a successor check folder
- anchored examples: `assets/base/agents/skills/cf-estimate/references/rubric.md` and its mirrors
- qualification cases and record: `assets/base/agents/skills/cf-evaluate-model/resources/` and its mirrors, with the qualification record
- dry-run evidence: the two runs' `peers.json` and `observation.json`, kept with the qualification record

## Estimate

- Band: Hard (4 to 6 h), profile v1; moved: one step for proof volume (two lineages grading about 20 cases, the qualification cases and two dry runs)
- Split: not needed
- H: none; B: none
