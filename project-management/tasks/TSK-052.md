# TSK-052: compose the guide pages from the sources

## Description

The source pages are walls: adoption at 4687 words, capabilities at 5280 with
17 raw YAML fences, harness posture, releasing and the checklist as prose
essays. The adapter renders what the sources structure, so this task authors
the guide pages as layered documents: for each page a Concept panel with one
governing claim and one figure, an Architecture panel with the structure as a
labeled stage or table and the sentences an engineer needs, and a Technical
panel with the tables, commands, files and evidence. Prose frames every figure
above and tells the reader what to do below. Every fact in the current text is
kept or moved; nothing is invented. Pages: product, capabilities (with the four
graduated capability pages folded back unless one earns its own trio),
adoption, enforcement planes (from adoption and harness posture), harness
posture, model bindings (from model upgrades), architecture, present, utility
presentation, releasing, release checklist, a CLI reference of the 22
subcommands, and the layer descriptions and home sentences in the portal
configuration. Non-goals: no new facts; no change to record files.

## Affected surfaces and interfaces

`docs/product.md`, `docs/capabilities.md`, `docs/capabilities/CAP-*.md`,
`docs/adoption.md`, `docs/harness-posture.md`, `docs/model-upgrades.md`,
`docs/architecture.md`, `docs/architecture/*.md`, `docs/releasing.md`,
`docs/release-checklist.md`, new `docs/reference/cli.md`, the layer entries in
`docs-portal/portal.config.json`; tests that pin doc literals in `crates/`.
CAP-015.

- [ ] Every explanatory page shall carry the altitude trio with one figure in
      Concept, a labeled stage or table in Architecture and lookup tables in
      Technical, each framed by prose; evidence: `validate --portal` and the
      page-class gate pass, and the rendered review lists every page with its
      reader question per altitude and a pass verdict.
- [ ] No fact shall be lost; evidence: a mapping in the closeout from every
      removed paragraph or table to its new location, reviewed by Grok.
- [ ] Titles shall be plain and descriptive with no em or en dashes, no ids
      as titles and no slogans; evidence: grep and the rendered review.
- [ ] The reading order across layers shall progress from what it is to what
      it does to adopting it to how it is built to operating it; evidence: the
      layer configuration order and the home figure.
- [ ] Editorial review shall pass on the substantive copy; evidence: the
      cf-editorial-review record in the closeout.
- [ ] The full local gate shall pass at the head, including the contract tests
      that pin doc literals; evidence: the gate output in the closeout.
