# TSK-389: A lookup helper that keeps stderr open costs one timeout, not three

## Description

Source: TSK-305 review finding L-3 (2026-10-16). A process the local configuration starts during the default-branch lookup (an ssh command, a credential helper) that keeps stderr open turns a good lookup into three timeouts and leaves orphan processes. It fails closed, so it is a delay and a leak, not a gap.

Outcome: **a lookup whose answer has arrived is not held by a helper that keeps stderr open, and no helper outlives the lookup.**

The builder chooses between not piping stderr past the main process's exit and a short bounded read after exit, and records why.

Added to EPC-023 (4.1.0) by the amendment of 2026-10-18: the operator's release decision of that day puts lower-severity review follow-ups in 4.1.0 (workspace `RELEASE-PLAN.md`, 2026-10-18 operator decision).

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-1 When a helper keeps stderr open after the lookup's answer, the lookup shall return within one bounded wait and leave no child process running; evidence: a test with a stub helper, failing before the change. (serves EPC-023 AC-29)
- AC-2 A lookup that really fails shall still refuse under its own rule; evidence: tests.
- AC-3 (journey) With the installed build, a push whose ssh command is a stub that keeps stderr open shall finish its destination check in one wait; evidence: the journey output with timings.
