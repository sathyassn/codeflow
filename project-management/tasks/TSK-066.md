# TSK-066: written content policy and reply rule

## Description

Decide and write the policy the other tasks cite. A new decision record,
numbered at task start from the highest record across `main`,
`integration/EPC-015-engineering-bar` and `integration/EPC-016-visual-guide`,
extends project policy in the family of the existing no-emoji rule: em and en
dashes are absent from tracked documents, planning records, commit messages,
pull request bodies and operator-facing replies; titles, headings, navigation
labels and pull request titles name the subject in words and never consist of
a stable identifier or an acronym alone (the identifier stays in the body or
link); a summary is two to four sentences of plain prose followed by bullets
or a table only where they carry facts better; mannered prose (slogans,
rhetorical setups, contrast crutches, self-narration, ceremonial framing,
paragraph walls) is a defect in any operator-facing text, not only in
substantial documents. The record narrows ADR-0032: policy characters and
identifier-only titles may be checked mechanically because they are policy,
while word choice, punctuation in general and style stay contextual and on
demand, and the over-correction canary stays. Write the one contract
sentence, amend the editorial smells reference (new rows for identifier-only
titles and paragraph walls in replies; the false-positive sentence no longer
protects the em dash where policy forbids it), add the reply rule to the
lifecycle reference (when the point is a flow or relationship the reply
carries a figure, fenced ASCII in chat and an HTML or SVG stage in present or
the portal; a substantial comparison or review opens or offers `cf-present`
with the reason; a simple answer stays simple), add the policy-character
check to `codeflow ci` and the commit-msg hook with tests, and sweep the
tracked tree so the check is green at this head. Non-goal: a word list, a
style linter or an acronym glossary.
