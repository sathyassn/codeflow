# TSK-1550: The reference-transaction hook judges local protected-ref moves at the landed policy

## Description

Source: finding L2 of the cross-cutting security review of TSK-1078 (coordination/coordinators/capabilities/reviews/tsk1078-security-cross-family-opus.md, lines 180-211, reviewer family claude), ruled for filing by the Director on 2026-11-19 (16:2x). Filed by coord/records. Severity low (cvss about 2.0), confirmed in source, not executed.

As reported by the review, not re-checked here: `run_reference_transaction_with_reader` in `crates/codeflow-cli/src/cmd/git_hook.rs` (lines 269 and 300) loads the policy with `Policy::load_effective(root)`, the checked-out working copy, and judges `protected_branches`, `delete_protected` and `local_ref_protection` with it (core `git_hook.rs` lines 1178-1215). TSK-1078 moved the four classic stages (pre-commit, commit-msg, pre-merge-commit, pre-push) to the landed policy through `load_for_hooks`; the reference-transaction hook was left out by the build brief (TSK-1078 record line 138, "Leave unchanged per build brief").

The review reports that an uncommitted local change to the policy can weaken what this hook enforces for a protected local branch, because the hook reads the checked-out working copy of the policy and not the landed one. What still holds: the in-session guard, pre-push and remote protection, so the damage stays local. The concrete scenario is kept in a coordination note that does not reach GitHub, and goes to the builder with the brief.

Premise: before any change, open `git_hook.rs:240-320` and core `git_hook.rs:1178-1215`, `load_for_hooks` and its callers (to see its cost and fast path), the `git.local_ref_protection`, `git.delete_protected` and `git.protected_branches` notes in the policy reference, the pre-merge-commit comment at `git_hook.rs:988-992` that names this hook as what closes the fast-forward merge gap, and the review's L2 section. The hook fires on every fetch, so measure the cost of the landed-policy load behind the existing fast path.

Non-goals:

- Changing the pre-push, commit-msg, pre-commit or pre-merge-commit stages, or the landed-policy design.
- Weakening any refusal; a landed-policy load that cannot be sure fails closed.
- Slowing every fetch beyond the measured budget.

## Outcome

After this merges, the reference-transaction hook decides the three protected-ref rules from the same landed policy as the other four hooks, so an uncommitted local policy edit cannot let local `main` be moved or deleted, and the policy notes say which hooks read which policy.

## Deliverables

- the hook's policy source: `run_reference_transaction_with_reader` reads the landed policy through `load_for_hooks` for `protected_branches`, `delete_protected` and `local_ref_protection`, keeping the existing fast path and failing closed when the landed policy cannot be read
- tests for the review's scenario: a protected `main` with an uncommitted policy that turns the rule off or drops `main`, where a move and a delete are refused; the same tests fail before the change
- a measured cost note for a fetch-shaped transaction before and after
- the policy reference notes for the three keys: which hooks read the landed policy
- the release note: `changelog.d/TSK-1550.md`

## Estimate

- Grade: Medium; band: Medium; moved: none
- Split: not needed; one hook, its policy source, tests and notes.
- Basis: read the hook and `load_for_hooks`, one change, the tests with a fail-before, a cost measurement, one cross-family whole-unit read (hooks and guards, attacker lens). Graded by coord/records; the builder re-scores at claim.

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-1 When local `main` is protected by the landed policy and the working copy holds an uncommitted policy that turns `local_ref_protection` off or removes `main` from `protected_branches`, the reference-transaction hook shall refuse a move of local `main`; evidence: a test of that scenario that fails before the change and passes after.
- AC-2 When the same uncommitted edit sets `delete_protected` off, the hook shall refuse a deletion of local `main`; evidence: a test that fails before the change and passes after.
- AC-3 When the landed policy cannot be read, the hook shall fail closed as the other hooks do; evidence: a test with an unreadable landed policy, asserting the refusal and its message.
- AC-4 When a fetch-shaped transaction updates only non-protected refs, the hook shall stay on its fast path and add no measurable cost beyond the budget the record names in the premise note; evidence: the before and after measurement.
- AC-5 The policy reference notes for `git.local_ref_protection`, `git.delete_protected` and `git.protected_branches` shall say which hooks read the landed policy; evidence: the changed doc lines.
