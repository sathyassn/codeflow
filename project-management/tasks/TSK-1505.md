# TSK-1505: Reopen and archive checks agree on a completion edited in place

## Description

When a task's completion block was edited in place after it was made, two checks disagree on which completion is "the previous completion". The reopen check takes one version of the block and the history (archive) check takes another. A reopened task whose completion had been edited in place can then never be completed again, because whichever block it archives, one of the two checks refuses it. TSK-416's re-completion on the EPC-022 line hit this.

Overlap: TSK-1185 (import authority ordering record versions) and TSK-1316 (folded into TSK-1185) judge record versions across landings; neither covers which completion a reopen archives. No open record holds this.

Source: the Director, 2026-11-15 04:00, from coord/ci's TSK-416 re-completion report; the fix was built by a Claude Worker of coord/ci in `acceptance-history-fix` on `main` at d59f1688d before this record existed.

## Outcome

After this merges, the reopen check and the history check name the same previous completion for a completion that was edited in place, so a task reopened after such an edit can be completed again through `codeflow task status`, and a completion never edited in place is judged as before.

## Deliverables

- one reading of "the previous completion" shared by the reopen check and the history check
- tests with fail-before evidence
- `## Findings`: each reader of the previous completion, swept, with its disposition

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-1 When a task's completion block was edited in place and the task is then reopened, the reopen check and the history check shall name the same completion as the previous one, and the archived block shall show the completion as made and then as edited; evidence: `a_completion_edited_in_place_is_archived_as_made_then_as_edited` failing before the change and passing after it.
- AC-2 A completion that was never edited in place shall be archived and judged exactly as before; evidence: the existing reopen and acceptance-history tests passing unchanged at the head.
- AC-3 Every other reader of a task's previous completion shall be listed with its disposition (uses the shared reading, or why it cannot meet an edited completion); evidence: the sweep table in this record's Findings with file:line for each reader.
- AC-4 (journey) When an adopter reopens a task whose completion was edited in place and completes it again with `codeflow task status <id> complete --acceptance <file>`, the built binary shall accept the re-completion and `codeflow ci` shall pass the range; evidence: the built binary on a scratch repository fixture of that history, refused before and accepted after.
