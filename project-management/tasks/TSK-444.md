# TSK-444: The presentation and portal design direction is settled and tokened

## Description

Operator direction of 2026-10-22. The operator reports that cf-present
figures look monochrome: `tokens.json` gives each of the six skins
(Graphite, Slate and Sage, light and dark) grey ink plus accent, warn
and stop only, and the grammar maps most marks to grey, so a figure
with no governing path renders nearly grey. The operator's renders
also show an inverted type scale: `comparison` column titles and the
`decision` title render larger than a `### ` section heading and about
as large as `## `, so options outrank the section that contains them;
and the document `summary` is shown only by `present list`, never on
the page. Figures may use the existing colours or added ones that
harmonise with them; there is no hard restriction, but the result must
show strong aesthetic judgment, elegant and coherent across the three
skins in light and dark, and taste is the operator's.

Outcome: **a `cf-design` pass led by the Claude design primary settles,
from options shown to the operator as rendered pages, the page anatomy
(document title and summary at the top, the anchor, a section heading
with its lead, the figure sheet), one type scale in which the document
title is larger than a section, a section than a subsection, and a
subsection than a block title, and the figure colour (the governing
path in accent-bearing marks, and whether harmonious category hues are
added per skin and mode), across Graphite, Slate and Sage in light and
dark; the operator signs off on the direction and on the result; and
the tokens, the present styles (header, hierarchy) and the portal token
sheet change together with the palette drift test.**

Rules: options are shown as rendered pages in `cf-present` (the
reference figures and a reference document redrawn per skin and mode);
rule 3 (never hue alone) and the design system's contrast floors hold
for any added hue; the change is recorded under ADR-0053 and ADR-0068
as their append-only rule allows; where no hue is added the tokens
stay byte-identical; whether a dedicated `section` block (title, lead)
is worth adding beyond the `## ` heading pattern is decided from
evidence and recorded.

Non-goals: the skill method text (TSK-445); the runtime defects
(TSK-443); new figure families.

Added by the EPC-022 amendment of 2026-10-22, restructured the same day
from TSK-441 so each outcome is one task.

## Settled direction (operator, 2026-10-23)

The operator chose from the rendered options. These are settled and are
not reopened by the builder:

- Anatomy A1, with the body text as short prose paragraphs plus points
  where they fit. The rendering the operator chose is pinned in the
  option pages committed on `task/TSK-444-the-presentation-and-portal-design-direction-is`
  at `aab5b9089` ("docs(verification): settle the TSK-444 design direction
  options"), under `docs/verification/evidence/tsk-444/`; fidelity is
  judged against that commit. The document title and summary sit at the
  top of the document column, then the anchor, then titled sections with
  a lead line, then blocks.
- Type scale S2: wider steps of 34, 24, 18 and 15 px at the default size.
- Figure colour C2 with palette A, "Cobalt and plum" (present session
  `73fc1f7e`, revision 3, holds the chosen palette render): category 1
  and category 2 colours fill and ink figure marks beside the accent,
  each mark keeping a non-hue cue, the governing path staying in the
  accent-bearing marks:

  | Token | Light | Dark |
  |---|---|---|
  | fig-cat1 | #3860ac | #9bbeff |
  | fig-cat1-soft | #e4edfe | #2a3852 |
  | fig-cat2 | #722756 | #cf82ae |
  | fig-cat2-soft | #f0d5e3 | #3d2131 |

  The same values apply in Graphite, Slate and Sage. Accents and greys
  are unchanged.
- No `section` block; the `## ` heading pattern stays.
- The result must look elegant and refined, not childish.

- AC-2 The runtime shall render the document title and `summary` at the top of the document column, and the `section` block question shall be decided from evidence and recorded; evidence: render and web tests and the decision. (serves EPC-022 AC-122)
- AC-3 One type scale shall hold in which the document title is larger than a `## ` section heading, which is larger than a `### ` subsection heading, which is larger than any block title (`comparison` column titles, the `decision` title); evidence: a render test measuring computed sizes, failing before the change, and captures per skin and mode. (serves EPC-022 AC-122)
- AC-4 Figures shall draw their governing path with the accent-bearing marks as `present.reference.html` does, and where the pass adds category hues, each of the six skin and mode combinations shall define them with a fill and an ink meeting the contrast floors, measured, with every category mark carrying a non-hue cue (rule 3) checked by the figure browser check; where no hue is added the tokens shall be byte-identical to today's; evidence: a contrast table, the browser check and a byte comparison. (serves EPC-022 AC-122)
- AC-5 `tokens.json`, `tokens.css`, `figure.css`, the present styles, both skills' byte-identical resources, the portal token sheet and the palette drift test shall change together and agree, the drift test keeping the palette anchored to `tokens.json`; evidence: the `manifest_consistency` and drift tests passing and a byte comparison of the mirrors. (serves EPC-022 AC-122)
- AC-6 The change shall be recorded under ADR-0053 and ADR-0068 as their append-only rule allows, `codeflow validate --docs` shall pass, and the operator shall give visual sign-off on the rendered result in each skin and mode, recorded in this task before it lands; evidence: the notes, the validate output and the sign-off note. (serves EPC-022 AC-122)
- AC-7 (journey) With the installed build, `codeflow present` on the reference document shall show the title and summary at the top, the type scale in order and the governing path in colour in a light and a dark skin; evidence: the journey captures. (journey)
- AC-8 The settled direction recorded in "Settled direction" shall be implemented as written (anatomy A1, type scale S2, figure colour C2 with palette A, no section block) and the result shall look elegant and refined, not childish; evidence: the DESIGN_INTENT record citing each decision and the operator's sign-off on the result. (serves EPC-022 AC-122)
- AC-9 Every companion pair (each category colour with its soft fill, and the ink on each) shall meet WCAG 4.5:1 on its surfaces in all six skin and mode pairs (Graphite, Slate and Sage, light and dark); evidence: a token test in `crates/codeflow-core/tests/design_kit.rs` that computes the ratios from `tokens.json` and fails on any pair under 4.5:1, passing after the change. (serves EPC-022 AC-122)
