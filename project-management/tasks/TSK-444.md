# TSK-444: The presentation and portal design direction is settled and tokened

## Description

Operator direction of 2026-10-22. The operator reports that cf-present
figures look monochrome: `tokens.json` gives each of the six skins
(Graphite, Slate and Sage, light and dark) grey ink plus accent, warn
and stop only, and the grammar maps most marks to grey, so a figure
with no governing path renders nearly grey. The operator's renders
also show an inverted type scale: `comparison` column titles and the
`decision` title render larger than a `### ` section heading and about
as large as `## `, so options outrank the section that contains them;
and the document `summary` is shown only by `present list`, never on
the page. Figures may use the existing colours or added ones that
harmonise with them; there is no hard restriction, but the result must
show strong aesthetic judgment, elegant and coherent across the three
skins in light and dark, and taste is the operator's.

Outcome: **a `cf-design` pass led by the Claude design primary settles,
from options shown to the operator as rendered pages, the page anatomy
(document title and summary at the top, the anchor, a section heading
with its lead, the figure sheet), one type scale in which the document
title is larger than a section, a section than a subsection, and a
subsection than a block title, and the figure colour (the governing
path in accent-bearing marks, and whether harmonious category hues are
added per skin and mode), across Graphite, Slate and Sage in light and
dark; the operator signs off on the direction and a fresh-context
design reviewer checks the result against it (operator decision,
2026-10-24); and
the tokens, the present styles (header, hierarchy) and the portal token
sheet change together with the palette drift test.**

Rules: options are shown as rendered pages in `cf-present` (the
reference figures and a reference document redrawn per skin and mode);
rule 3 (never hue alone) and the design system's contrast floors hold
for any added hue; the change is recorded under ADR-0053 and ADR-0068
as their append-only rule allows; where no hue is added the tokens
stay byte-identical; whether a dedicated `section` block (title, lead)
is worth adding beyond the `## ` heading pattern is decided from
evidence and recorded.

Non-goals: the skill method text (TSK-445); the runtime defects
(TSK-443); new figure families.

Added by the EPC-022 amendment of 2026-10-22, restructured the same day
from TSK-441 so each outcome is one task.

Amended 2026-10-24 by the operator's decision of 2026-10-24: AC-6 no
longer requires the operator's visual sign-off on the built result. A
fresh-context Claude design reviewer confirms the rendered result matches
the accepted direction instead. Reason: the operator settled the taste
question at the direction (AC-1), so the result check is fidelity to that
direction, which a reviewer can verify without the operator. The same
decision covers AC-8, AC-11 and the sign-off clause of AC-1, amended
2026-10-25: their result check is the reviewer's too. The fresh-context
design reviewer's fidelity review of 2026-10-24 at da8b561eb
(`target/review-TSK-444-fidelity-01.md`, verdict matches) is that check.
