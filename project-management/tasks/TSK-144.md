# TSK-144: Herdr delivery confirms a started turn

## Description

On 2026-09-27 a prompt pasted into a Codex seat in Herdr and followed by
Enter was left unsubmitted twice (08:09, two seats), and one review sat
idle for about 40 minutes before anyone noticed. The Claude lane already
waits for the lifecycle's `accepted` state and sends Enter once more
(`cf-delegate` lane-lifecycle), but a seat without the lifecycle hook, a
Codex or Grok seat, gets no check that its turn started. Separately, a
worktree cleanup removed the working folders of three Codex seats (08:43),
and a seat whose folder was gone could not start turns, with nothing
saying why. This task makes Herdr delivery confirm that the turn started
and fail legibly when the seat's folder is gone.

## Affected surfaces and interfaces

- Skill `cf-herdr` (delivery, resume and cleanup) in `assets/base` and the
  installed copies (8,184 bytes today against an 8 KiB guideline; detail
  that serves one moment moves behind a trigger, per TSK-150); the Herdr
  sentence of `cf-delegate` `resources/lane-lifecycle.md` only if it must
  match.
- `evals/herdr-delivery/test_delivery.py` and
  `crates/codeflow-core/tests/herdr_host_contract.rs` (CAP-009).
- Shared files, per SPC-013 R-118: `cf-herdr` (owner, after TSK-150's
  restorations) and `cf-delegate` after TSK-129 and TSK-150.

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-1 When a prompt is delivered to a Herdr seat without the lifecycle
  hook (a Codex or Grok seat), the delivery recipe shall confirm within a
  stated bound that the seat started working, send Enter at most once
  more if the prompt still waits in the input, and otherwise stop and
  report that the prompt was not submitted, naming the pane; the Claude
  lane shall keep its `accepted` wait; evidence: delivery tests against a
  stub `herdr` for a seat that starts, one that starts after one more
  Enter, and one that never starts.
- AC-2 When the seat's working folder no longer exists, delivery and
  resume shall stop before sending anything and report the missing folder
  and the relaunch step, and cf-herdr's cleanup shall keep a worktree that
  a live seat uses as its folder until that seat is closed; evidence: a
  stub test with a deleted folder and the cleanup text.
- AC-3 When `HERDR_ENV=1`, the live canary shall deliver to a real seat
  and observe the confirmed start; evidence: the live test in
  `evals/herdr-delivery`, or its recorded skip with the reason when Herdr
  is unavailable.
- AC-4 cf-herdr's mirrors shall be byte identical with manifest entries
  current, and any detail moved behind a trigger shall stay reachable
  from the skill's index; its size is reported against the 8 KiB
  guideline, not gated (TSK-150); evidence: `artifact_budget_contract`
  and `manifest_consistency`.
- AC-5 (journey) In freshly scaffolded standard and full-tier projects
  driven by the installed CLI, the installed cf-herdr shall carry the
  confirmed delivery, the recipe as installed shall pass the AC-1 and
  AC-2 stub cases, and `codeflow update` shall bring it to an existing
  project; evidence: `init_e2e` and the delivery tests pointed at the
  scaffolded copy.

a Codex or Grok turn started and stops by name when it did not, and sends
nothing to a busy seat or one whose folder is gone. Grok approved
`8e6ac818a` in round 2, after a round 1 with its live canary and two
findings, and confirmed the merge of the EPC-020 line at `55f67d494`,
whose only change beyond its parents is the CHANGELOG resolution.

- AC-1: the stub cases cover a seat that starts (one Enter, exit 0), one
  that would start only after one more Enter, one that never starts, and
  one whose first Enter submitted while its status had not moved yet. The
  last three get one Enter and exit 4, naming the pane. The criterion allows
  at most one more Enter "if the prompt still waits in the input"; Grok's
  round 1 finding showed that Herdr 0.9.0 cannot tell the input from the
  same words already in the scrollback (its read sources are `visible`,
  `recent`, `recent_unwrapped` and `detection`, with no input line or
  cursor), so the script never sends it and reports the turn not confirmed
  instead. A `working`, `blocked` or `unknown` seat gets nothing (exit 5).
  The Claude lane sends one Enter and keeps its `accepted` wait.
- AC-2: a deleted folder stops both lanes before any send, naming the
  folder and the relaunch step; the cleanup text keeps a live seat's
  worktree until its tab is closed.
- AC-3: Grok ran `test_live_delivery_confirms_a_started_turn`'s path on a
  real Grok seat at `f77729c78` in a disposable folder: tab `w2:t6P`, pane
  `w2:p6P`, agent `cf-tmp-t144-gk01`. The seat went from `idle` at
  `state_change_seq` 2131 to `working` at 2132 on the first Enter, exit 0,
  and the tab was closed. The builder's session was not inside Herdr, so
  its live cases were skipped with that reason.
- AC-4: the four mirrors are byte identical to `assets/base`, the manifest
  hashes are current, and `cf-herdr/SKILL.md` is 9,216 bytes against the
