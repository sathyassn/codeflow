# TSK-1076: A qualified usage reading stops an away session's new admissions at a threshold

## Description

Source: the session system design, revision 12.3 of 2026-10-29: decision
3 (usage advisory only) is unchanged, and its records list keeps usage
as a stop trigger tracked. In 4.0.0 a configured usage, token or spend
limit is refused at `away start` on every harness (TSK-1045 AC-12(e)),
and the limits an activation honours are the ones CodeFlow counts
itself: an end, units claimed, seats admitted and admitted ticks.

Why it waits: no harness reports usage to the session in a form whose
source, scope and freshness are defined, and a reading can reset or
move to another account (the workspace notes record usage-limit stops
and account switches as recurring), so a threshold on it could stop too
late or for the wrong scope.

## Outcome

After this merges, a user can give an away activation a usage threshold
that stops new admissions when a qualified reading reaches it, on a
harness whose reading meets the measurement contract.

## Deliverables

- investigation: a `## Findings` section in this record before any change
- the trigger: `crates/codeflow-core/src/coord/away.rs` (limits and admission) and `cf-away`'s skill text, mirrored and resynced
- tests: CLI tests with recorded readings
- docs: the away guide page and `docs/cli.md`
- release note: `changelog.d/TSK-1076.md`

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-1 Before any change, the builder shall record per harness, with sources, whether usage is reported to the session, its source, its scope (session, account, family), its freshness and how it behaves on a reset or an account switch, starting from TSK-1045 AC-1(e)'s findings; evidence: the Findings section.
- AC-2 A measurement contract shall state, per supported harness, the source, scope, freshness bound, takeover and reset handling, and an unsupported harness keeps refusing a usage limit at start; evidence: the contract in the record and a refusal test per unsupported harness.
- AC-3 A qualified reading at or past the threshold shall stop new admissions (claims, seats, landings, ticks) while existing work progresses, and the report shall name the limit and the reading with its observation time; evidence: tests with recorded readings below, at and past the threshold.
- AC-4 A missing, stale or reset reading under a configured threshold shall refuse new admissions and say why, never treat the limit as unmet; evidence: tests for each case.
