# TSK-305: An unanswered default-branch lookup is reported as a network failure

## Description

`codeflow ci` and the pre-push hook ask the destination for its default
branch with one `git ls-remote --symref` call and a fixed 10-second
deadline (`crates/codeflow-core/src/git/remote_query.rs:15`, `ls_remote` at
`:28`, no retry). When the destination answers slowly, the push is refused
(https://github.com/sathyassn/codeflow/issues/161). The failure is wrapped
at `crates/codeflow-core/src/workgraph/release_line.rs:182-187` and
reported by `scope_refusal` in
`crates/codeflow-cli/src/cmd/ci/acceptance.rs:223` and by the record check
in `crates/codeflow-cli/src/cmd/ci/work_records.rs:142`.

Two parts of the report differ in age. The message the issue quotes, with
a `sanctioned` line that points at a planning PR, is the older text: at
`main` 4226a9408, `scope_refusal` already says "repair the destination or
policy named above, then rerun codeflow ci" (`acceptance.rs:227`). The
defect that remains in the current source is the classification: a
destination failure is still reported under a lifecycle rule
(`work.criteria_frozen` through `FROZEN_RULE`, and `work.records`) rather
than under a rule of its own, and its remedy does not name the network.

Failing closed is right and stays: an unanswered destination never lets a
range through unjudged, and a stale local default is never trusted. This
task distinguishes the causes (a timeout, an authentication failure, a
malformed advertisement, and a destination with no usable default branch)
and reports each once under its own rule with a remedy that names the
network or the remote. One shared lookup owns the query and its bounded
retry sequence: a first-attempt success makes one network attempt; a
timeout gets a bounded number of extra attempts with a short backoff,
whether they end in success or exhaustion; the other causes are not
retried. The lookup's final answer, or its final error, is cached and
shared: pre-push passes it to its child checks
(`crates/codeflow-cli/src/cmd/push_set.rs:354-360`, `--advertisement-stdin`),
and no child check or additional pushed ref queries the destination again.
Scope per the Codex triage of 2026-10-13 (`triage-codex-01.md`, the
paragraph on issue 161). Non-goal: a configurable deadline.

## Deliverables

- cause classification and the bounded retry: `crates/codeflow-core/src/git/remote_query.rs`, `crates/codeflow-core/src/workgraph/release_line.rs`
- reporting under one rule: `crates/codeflow-cli/src/cmd/ci/acceptance.rs`, `crates/codeflow-cli/src/cmd/ci/work_records.rs`, the remedy in `crates/codeflow-core/src/remedy.rs`
- tests: `crates/codeflow-cli/tests/ci_cli/` and the unit tests of `remote_query.rs`
- docs: the new rule in `docs/policy-reference.md`
- release note: `changelog.d/TSK-305.md`

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-1 When the default-branch query times out, fails authentication,
  returns a malformed advertisement, or reaches a destination with no
  usable default branch, the run shall still refuse, and the refusal shall
  name that cause under one destination rule, once per run, with a remedy
  that names the network or the remote and not a planning PR; evidence: a
  test per cause with a stub remote. (serves EPC-022 AC-15)
- AC-2 No refusal under `work.criteria_frozen` or `work.records` shall
  carry a destination failure as its reason; evidence: the issue 161
  reproduction run against a stub that never answers, showing one
  destination refusal and no lifecycle refusal.
- AC-3 One shared lookup shall own the retry sequence: a timeout shall be
  retried a bounded number of times with a short backoff, and an
  authentication failure, a malformed advertisement and a missing default
  shall not be retried; evidence: a unit test of the lookup that counts
  the attempts per cause.
- AC-4 A stale local default branch shall never be used in place of the
  destination's answer; evidence: a test with a local `origin/HEAD` and a
  stub that never answers.
- AC-5 One pre-push run shall make exactly the lookup's attempts and no
  more: one network attempt when the first attempt succeeds; one plus the
  retries used when a timeout is followed by success; one plus the full
  retry bound when every attempt times out, ending in the fail-closed
  destination refusal. No child check and no additional pushed ref shall
  query the destination again, and the cached final answer or error shall
  be what they read; evidence: three pre-push fixtures (success, timeout
  then success, exhausted timeout), each with two pushed refs, that count
  the `ls-remote` calls against a stub.
- AC-6 When the installed pre-push hook pushes a task branch while the
  destination stub never answers, the push shall be refused under the
  destination rule, and the same push shall pass once the stub answers
  (journey); evidence: the journey output of both runs.
