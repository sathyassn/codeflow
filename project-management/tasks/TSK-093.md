# TSK-093: sweep em and en dashes from the managed skills

## Description

ADR-0067 checks em and en dashes only on lines a change adds, so the skill
files CodeFlow ships still carry them: at `e49726807`, 42 files under
`assets/base/agents/skills` and `assets/base/claude/skills` hold 378 lines
with U+2013 or U+2014 (the release follow-up list counted 41). Every adopter
receives those files, and after the 3.0.0 change the scan skips unmodified
managed files, so the dashes are no longer a CI blocker; they remain prose
that contradicts CodeFlow's own writing guideline. This task rewrites them to
the guideline (a comma, colon, full stop or hyphen; a dash kept only where
really needed, such as a numeric range or a quoted title).

It also makes the managed contract agree with the policy. The contract line
"no em or en dash in new text" in `AGENTS.md.tmpl` (and CodeFlow's own
`AGENTS.md`) reads as absolute, while `git.policy_characters` now defaults to
`warn` for adopters and stays `block` only in CodeFlow's own policy (operator,
2026-09-26). TSK-088 on the EPC-018 line already rewrites that sentence as a
prose guideline for 4.0.0; if 3.1 ships first, this task carries the same
sentence so 3.1 does not ship the absolute rule, and TSK-088 then finds it
done.

Target release: 3.1. Sequencing: the sweep lands on the guide line,
`integration/EPC-016-visual-guide`, and ships with it (operator direction,
2026-09-27). Files that TSK-061 and TSK-062 change on that line are left
alone here, because those tasks edit some of the same lines; they are swept
in a follow-up after both land. This is not a `depends_on` edge.
Non-goal: dashes outside the skill trees (templates, hooks, CI files) and any
change of meaning.

## Affected surfaces and interfaces

- The 42 skill files under `assets/base/{agents,claude}/skills`, their
  mirrors in `.agents/skills` and `.claude/skills`, `.codeflow/.baseline`,
  and `.codeflow/manifest.json` hashes.
- `assets/base/AGENTS.md.tmpl` and `AGENTS.md` (one managed sentence).
- Adopters: every swept file changes for them. `codeflow update` replaces
  unmodified copies and 3-way merges modified ones, so an adopter who edited
  a swept line gets a merge or a `.new` sidecar; the CHANGELOG says so.

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-1 After the sweep, no file under the two skill trees contains U+2013 or
  U+2014 except where a dash is really needed (a numeric range, a quoted
  title) or is itself test input; each kept occurrence is listed with its
  reason in the closeout; evidence: a `git grep` count before and after.
- AC-2 Evaluation resources whose dashes are the subject of a case
  (`cf-evaluate-model/resources/cases.json`, `fixtures.json`,
  `requirements.json`) keep those dashes, and the dash cases still pass
  and fail as before; evidence: the evaluation kit's validate step and
  the grading inventory.
- AC-3 No skill changes meaning: every rewritten line keeps its facts,
  conditions and references; evidence: editorial review of the diff by a
  second model family with no open finding.
- AC-4 Mirrors are byte identical, the baseline and manifest hashes match,
  and no skill exceeds its byte ratchet; evidence: `manifest_consistency`
  and `artifact_budget_contract`.
- AC-5 The managed contract states the dash rule as a prose guideline checked
  mechanically at the project's policy level (`warn` by default), using
  TSK-088's settled wording, unless TSK-088 has already landed it;
  evidence: the diff or the landed TSK-088 commit.
- AC-6 The CHANGELOG entry names the sweep and the merge effect on adopters
  who edited a swept file; evidence: the entry.
