# TSK-061: codeflow guide pages composed with figures

## Description

Compose every explanatory page of the CodeFlow guide with a figure at each
altitude under the grammar: the design primary chooses the family and
authors each figure's declaration (the relationship, the marks, the legend,
the narrow variant), and the page prose is rewritten to frame the figure
rather than restate it. Thicken the Technical altitudes that are under 250
words of prose and cut the two that are over 3000 by moving reference detail
into tables and new pages. Close the guide's coverage gaps with new pages: a
skills catalog generated from the skill frontmatter, a policy and hooks
reference generated from the policy schema and the five hook stages, a
troubleshooting page for the fifteen doctor checks, a glossary, the decision
map as a page, the governance documents (contributing, security, conduct,
license) reachable from the portal, a present user guide and a delegation and
Herdr page. Remove the seven charter citations, the v1 and legacy internals
and the fourteen bare acronyms from public prose; expand ADR, CAP, EPC, SPC
and TSK on first use. Non-goal: records as pages (ADR-0064 stands).

## Affected surfaces and interfaces

`docs/**` guide sources, new pages under `docs/`, `docs-portal/figures/**`
declaration files, the layer entries and page carriers of
`docs-portal/portal.config.json`, `README.md` links, pinned doc literals in
crate tests. A source root addition or generated pages for the skills catalog
and policy reference, decided with TSK-059's declaration format. CAP-015.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Every explanatory page carries one figure per altitude in a named
      family, and a reader with the titles masked can name the relationship
      and answer the page's question from the figure; evidence: the figure
      gate and the answer-blind observation in TSK-064.
- [ ] No Technical altitude has under 250 words of prose and none has over
      1500 outside its tables; evidence: the altitude word count in the
      adapter's evidence manifest.
- [ ] The nine new pages exist, are reachable from the section indexes, and
      the generated ones (skills catalog, policy reference) are derived from
      their sources with a fidelity check; evidence: the built routes and the
      validator.
- [ ] The public prose carries no charter section citation, no v1, V2 or
      legacy schema history, no bare acronym on first use, and no dash
      characters; evidence: grep sweeps recorded in the closeout.
- [ ] `validate --docs`, `validate --portal` and `browser:verify` are green;
      evidence: recorded output with the head SHA.
- [ ] Editorial review of every rewritten page, Grok adversarial review of
      the figure choices against the grammar, Codex review where the seat
      allows; evidence: the review records.
