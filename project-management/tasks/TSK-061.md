# TSK-061: codeflow guide pages composed with figures

## Description

Rebuild the CodeFlow guide fully on the doctrine and the rebuilt shell:
compose every explanatory page with a figure at each altitude and every
how-to section with a figure in the sequence, state or extent family, under
the grammar: the design primary chooses the family and authors each figure's
declaration (the relationship, the marks, the legend, the narrow variant),
and the page prose is rewritten to frame the figure rather than restate it,
short and plain, with bullets where they help and no em or en dash. Runs
after TSK-060 so each page is composed and checked once against the final
chrome, and after TSK-072 so each page is composed with the explanation
method, naming the reader and question per altitude. Move what Concept owns (what the subject is, who it
is for, what it is not) out of Technical headings into Concept, and cut the
two Technical altitudes that are over 3000 words by moving reference detail
into tables and new pages; a short Technical altitude whose figure and table
carry the relationship stays short. Close the guide's coverage gaps with new pages: a
skills catalog generated from the skill frontmatter, a policy and hooks
reference generated from the policy schema and the five hook stages, a
troubleshooting page for the fifteen doctor checks, a glossary, the decision
map as a page, the governance documents (contributing, security, conduct,
license) reachable from the portal, a present user guide and a delegation and
Herdr page. Classify each new route in the portal configuration with the
page classes TSK-059 defines: explanatory (figure per altitude), derived
lookup (a generated table with a fidelity check) or pass-through with its
reason (governance text for the governance documents), so the figure gate
asks the right thing of each. Remove the seven charter
citations, the v1 and legacy internals and the fourteen bare acronyms from
public prose; expand ADR, CAP, EPC, SPC and TSK on first use. Non-goal:
records as pages (ADR-0064 stands).

## Affected surfaces and interfaces

`docs/**` guide sources, new pages under `docs/`, `docs-portal/figures/**`
declaration files, the layer entries and page-class entries of
`docs-portal/portal.config.json`, `README.md` links, pinned doc literals in
crate tests. A source root addition or generated pages for the skills catalog
and policy reference, decided with TSK-059's declaration format. CAP-015.
