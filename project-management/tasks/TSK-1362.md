# TSK-1362: Task and epic records can name areas that are checked against the project

## Description

A task or epic record gains an optional `areas` list, and `codeflow validate
--docs` refuses an area that the project's `project.toml` does not declare.
`codeflow ci` and the review brief name a task's areas when it has them. This
is TSK-1304's outcome. The 2026-11-04 consolidation pass merged it into
TSK-1186; the 2026-11-05 records follow-up (Director ruling, relayed by
coord/records) splits it back out, because TSK-1186's own outcome is strict
parsing of serves tags and record markers, and the areas field is a separate
contract with its own consumers and proof.

Non-goals: the serves-tag parser and the record-marker sweep (TSK-1186), and
the task graph coverage check (TSK-1363).

## Outcome

After this merges, a person writing a task or epic record can name its areas and get a refusal for an area the project does not declare, and a reviewer sees those areas in `codeflow ci` and the review brief.

## Deliverables

- the field and its validation: `crates/codeflow-core/src/workgraph/` and `crates/codeflow-core/src/validate/`, with the task and epic templates in `assets/base/`
- the consumers: `codeflow ci` output and the review brief template
- release note: `changelog.d/TSK-1304.md`
