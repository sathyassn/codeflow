# TSK-446: The docs portal pages anchor, lead and draw in colour

## Description

The cf-docs-portal audit of 2026-10-22 (`docs-portal-audit.md` in the
planning session's scratchpad) found that the portal gives the reader
none of the anchoring the method asks for:

- `assets/docs-portal/starter/scripts/lib.mjs:1103-1108` places the
  Concept figure before the governing lead sentence, against the
  reference page and `figure-grammar.md:75-78`, so `portal.css:459`'s
  `h2:first-child + p` never matches;
- no page-head anchor: `adapter.mjs:488` and `PortalTitle.astro:27`
  never render the description or any text before the altitude tabs;
- the shared `explanation-method.md` must stay byte-identical across
  both skills (`manifest_consistency.rs:3088-3089`);
- 29 of 70 repository figure declarations draw no accent mark;
- no visual-weight report for portal pages (the audit proposes a
  three-sentence cap; a numeric cap is the operator's decision, so this
  task reports and never refuses), and fenced ASCII kept in
  sources where a drawn companion exists (`docs/product.md:83-112`,
  `docs/delivery.md:49-70`);
- sub-sections get no lead (`.cf-lead` exists in `portal.css:34` and
  the adapter never applies it).

The audit also holds three page rewrites (`docs/product.md`,
`docs/delivery.md`, `docs/adoption.md`) usable as the portal's worked
examples.

Outcome: **every finding of the portal audit is fixed, the portal's
guidance carries the method of TSK-445 and the direction of TSK-444,
the shared `explanation-method.md` stays identical, and this
repository's portal pages are rewritten to it, product, delivery and
adoption first.**

Non-goals: the cf-present runtime (TSK-443); the method text itself
(TSK-445); the tokens (TSK-444).

Added by the EPC-022 amendment of 2026-10-22, restructured the same day
from TSK-441 so each outcome is one task.

## Deliverables

- the starter: `assets/docs-portal/starter/scripts/lib.mjs` (lead before the Concept figure), `adapter.mjs` and `PortalTitle.astro` (the page-head lede from the description; sub-section leads through `.cf-lead`), with a page-shapes test for the order and render tests
- the checks: the governing-path rule in the grammar self-check and the portal gate, and an advisory visual-weight report (narrative blocks longer than a few sentences, plain tables outside a figure twin; encoded tables raise nothing), reported and never refusing; any enforced cap is the operator's decision and is not added here
- the guidance: `cf-docs-portal/SKILL.md`, `references/visual-craft.md` and the shared `explanation-method.md` (byte-identical with `cf-present`) in `assets/base/`, `.claude/skills` and `.agents/skills`, with the Anchor rule
- the repository figures: the 29 declarations without an accent mark repaired or their exception stated, and the fenced ASCII dropped from `docs/product.md` and `docs/delivery.md` where a drawn companion exists
- the pages: `docs/product.md`, `docs/delivery.md` and `docs/adoption.md` rewritten from the audit's drafts as the worked examples, then the remaining portal pages
- release note: `changelog.d/TSK-446.md`
- managed wiring, mirrors and baseline: `assets/base/scaffold-manifest.toml`, installed managed files and `.codeflow/` baseline, synchronized when changed

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-1 The portal starter shall place a page's governing lead sentence before its Concept figure, with a page-shapes test for the order; evidence: the test, failing before the change. (serves EPC-022 AC-124)
- AC-2 The portal shall render a page-head anchor from the pre-trio Markdown (the description and any text before the altitude tabs) as a lede, and every sub-section shall get a lead through `.cf-lead`, with the Anchor rule in the portal guidance; evidence: render tests, failing before the change, and the guidance text. (serves EPC-022 AC-124)
- AC-3 `explanation-method.md` shall stay byte-identical across `cf-present` and `cf-docs-portal`, and every figure shall carry a governing path drawn with an accent mark, checked by the grammar self-check and the portal gate, with each of the 29 repository figures repaired or its exception stated; evidence: the mirror test and the checks' output before and after. (serves EPC-022 AC-124)
- AC-4 The portal gate shall report visual weight on each page as an advisory (narrative blocks longer than a few sentences, plain tables outside a figure twin; an encoded table of TSK-1096 is a visual and raises nothing, amended 2026-10-31), never refusing, with the note that an enforced cap is the operator's decision under "Only the operator adds process"; and fenced ASCII shall be dropped from sources where a drawn companion exists; evidence: the report test and the source diffs. (serves EPC-022 AC-124)
- AC-5 `docs/product.md`, `docs/delivery.md` and `docs/adoption.md` shall be rewritten to the method as the worked examples, then the remaining portal pages, each passing the portal gate; evidence: the page diffs, the gate output and the rendered pages. (serves EPC-022 AC-124)
- AC-6 (journey) The portal built from this repository shall show, on the product page, the lede above the tabs, the lead before the Concept figure, a sub-section lead and a figure with its governing path in colour, in a light and a dark skin; evidence: the journey captures. (journey)
- AC-7 Portal pages shall follow the same writing and heading rules as present documents: plain topic titles and never question-form headings, no mannered prose, short paragraphs with points where they fit, and heading order title, section, subsection, block title on the S2 scale of TSK-444; evidence: portal build checks that fail on a question-form heading and on a heading out of order, and portal-side tests for the known mannered patterns of TSK-445 AC-10 (each pattern flagged as a warning, with a clean-page control raising none), tests for each heading rule with a clean control, an editorial check (`cf-editorial-review`) of the portal pages for broader prose judgment, and rendered screenshots of portal pages in light and dark. (serves EPC-022 AC-124)
- AC-8 Portal figures shall use the palette A companions of TSK-444 and shall behave as fixed in TSK-443 wherever the portal shares those blocks (tabs, figure checks); evidence: the shared token tests (WCAG 4.5:1 on all six skin and mode pairs, mirror and drift) passing for the portal token sheet, a portal test that a `tabs` block switches panels, and rendered screenshots of portal figures in light and dark. (serves EPC-022 AC-124)
- AC-9 (amended 2026-10-31, the operator's clarification of 2026-10-30) Every rewritten portal page shall meet the immediately obvious bar TSK-1097 states: the first screen carries the anchor and an orienting figure, each section opens with a lead line and carries a figure or an encoded table that answers it (or its lead says why none applies), every table is an encoded table (TSK-1096), restrained, with a plain table only as a figure twin, every details panel opens to a lead and a figure or an encoded table, the sections follow the reader's questions in order with each lead naming its question, every state differs on two non-hue channels with an accessible name, a cold reader names the page's subject and its main relationship within a few seconds, and every summarised value keeps its exact value and source reachable; evidence: the portal gate's advisories of TSK-1097 clean on each rewritten page, and the recorded cold-reader answers for the three worked-example pages in light and dark. (serves EPC-022 AC-177)
