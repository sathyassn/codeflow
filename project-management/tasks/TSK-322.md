# TSK-322: Blocked names and protected prefixes are refused at any depth

## Description

pre-commit's only name rule is `is_env_file`
(`crates/codeflow-core/src/hooks/scan.rs:190-203`), and nothing refuses a
secret-by-nature name deep in the tree or a change under a path a project
protects (https://github.com/sathyassn/codeflow/issues/174). This task adds
the path gate (design foundation F5) under decisions 7 and 12:

- Keys: `git.path_policy` (a level, `block` by default);
  `git.blocked_names` (a string, or `{pattern, except_under, reason}`);
  `git.protected_paths` (`{prefix, allow: [{path, reason}]}`).
- Patterns: without `/`, a glob on the final component; with `/`, a
  component sequence at any depth; a `protected_paths` prefix is
  root-anchored on component boundaries; `except_under` and `allow` are
  root-anchored and normalized; a root entry is a whole-rule waiver that
  `doctor` names.
- Comparison: patterns, paths and the built-in exemption list are all
  NFC-normalized and folded with one fixed ASCII fold; a trailing dot or
  space, `.`, `..`, an empty or non-UTF-8 component, or a backslash
  refuses; git objects only.
- Links and gitlinks: a symlink is a hit when its path is a hit or its
  normalized target is absolute, holds `..`, or lands in a blocked name or
  protected prefix; a gitlink is an addition at its path; neither is
  followed.
- Walk: the commits `base..head` introduces; a non-merge commit by its
  parent diff; a merge by its remerge diff, so inherited parent content is
  not introduced; an octopus or unreadable merge refuses; add-then-delete
  refuses.
- Change kinds: blocked names refuse additions, renames into, content and
  type changes, and allow deletion with a history note; protected prefixes
  refuse every kind; pre-commit judges the same kinds over staged changes.
- Where: pre-commit reading landed policy (refuse when unreadable; the
  stricter of landed and worktree when both read; the no-remote and unborn
  cases of `landed_policy.rs:58-72` kept); `ci` from the target's policy.
  Exceptions come only from the target's policy; no actor, branch, slug or
  body text discharges a path. Denials union; `except_under` and `allow`
  intersect, through TSK-320's composition API.
- The gate never judges its own inputs: `.codeflow/policy.json`,
  `.codeflow/project.toml`, `.codeflow/bootstrap.sh`,
  `.codeflow/bootstrap.ps1`, `.codeflow/ci-setup.sh`,
  `.codeflow/git-hooks/*`, `.github/workflows/codeflow-policy.yml`,
  `.github/workflows/codeflow-ci.yml` and every `.gitignore` are outside the
  four new rules whatever the policy says, compared with the same
  normalization and fold. Other CodeFlow or host rules may still refuse
  them (decision 12).
- `is_env_file` and the content secret scan stay on `git.secret_scan`;
  `git.path_policy: off` removes only the new gate.
- Shipped default names (decision 7): the `.env` family; `*.pem`, `*.key`,
  `*.p12`, `*.pfx`, `*.jks`, `*.keystore`; `id_rsa*`, `id_dsa*`,
  `id_ecdsa*`, `id_ed25519*`; `.netrc`, `.pgpass`; `*.tfstate`,
  `*.tfstate.*`; `kubeconfig`; the sequences `.kube/config` and
  `.aws/credentials`; `application_default_credentials.json`.

The ignore-file and size rules are TSK-323.

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-1 The matcher shall pass a case table for both pattern kinds,
  root-anchored prefixes, `except_under` and `allow`, NFC on both sides
  (an NFD path matched through NFC), the ASCII fold, and a refusal for each
  refused component form; evidence: unit tests. (serves EPC-023 AC-8)
- AC-2 A symlink whose path is a hit, whose target is absolute or holds
  `..`, or whose target lands in a blocked name or protected prefix shall
  be a hit; a gitlink shall be an addition at its path; neither shall be
  followed; evidence: tests.
- AC-3 The walk shall judge non-merge commits by their parent diff and
  merges by their remerge diff, shall not refuse content a merge inherited
  unchanged, and shall refuse an octopus merge, an unreadable merge and an
  add-then-delete; evidence: fixtures. (serves EPC-023 AC-8)
- AC-4 Each change kind shall get its stated result in `ci` and in
  pre-commit over staged changes, and deleting a blocked name shall pass
  with a history note; evidence: tests per kind.
- AC-5 pre-commit shall read landed policy (refusing when unreadable,
  taking the stricter of landed and worktree, keeping the no-remote and
  unborn cases), `ci` the target's, and an exception present only on the
  branch shall discharge nothing; evidence: tests.
- AC-6 The built-in exemption shall keep each listed input outside the
  four rules in any spelling, a case variant included, while
  `is_env_file` and the secret scan still apply; evidence: a journey that
  tries to lock each input through `blocked_names` and `protected_paths`.
  (serves EPC-023 AC-11)
- AC-7 The shipped default names shall be exactly decision 7's list, and
  the release note shall name them and the `except_under` remedy;
  evidence: a test of the defaults and the fragment.
- AC-8 When the installed hooks and `codeflow ci` judge a scratch adopter
  project, `config/prod/.env.local`, `deploy/id_rsa`, a symlink into a
  protected prefix and a merge that introduces a blocked name shall be
  refused, and a merge that only inherits one shall pass (journey);
  evidence: the journey output.
