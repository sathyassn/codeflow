# TSK-1109: The display scale is a root scale and nothing renders under the floor

## Description

The operator said on 2026-10-31 11:10 that `cf-present` and `cf-docs-portal`
pages are hard to read and have no aesthetics, and asked for both to reach
the standard of a well-designed website (type, palette, spacing, hierarchy,
charts, chips), with the artifact-design guidance as reference and the 4.0.0
status report page as the reference look (queue item 36). the design audit of 2026-10-31 (Fable 5.1, design seat; the artifact "Present and Portal Redesign" and its scratch notes `present-redesign/` and `portal-audit/`) found
the first cause: Size is a token multiplier on a fixed 16px root
(`styles.css:50-58`, `:113-115`, `:344`). Compact 0.94, Default 1 and Large
1.12 scale only the `--cf-fs-*` tokens, so every rem or px an author writes
in an html stage ignores the setting; the status report's stages stayed at
11.5 to 13px at every Size (10 rules at 0.82rem, 9 at 0.78rem, 7 at 0.72rem,
4 at 12px in `export-light.html`), and the runtime's own kickers sit at
0.7rem (`styles.css:421`, `:436`, `:449`, `:479`), under its declared 12.5px
floor (`:58`). `document-authoring.md` gives authors no type guidance.

This task makes the root the scale: `html { font-size }` per Size step
(Compact 15px, Default 17px, Large 19px), every runtime token in rem, the
stage host inheriting the `ui` size, nothing under the floor, and the type
scale the audit sets (display 2.35rem, section 1.65, subsection 1.25, lede
1.18, body 1, ui 0.88, caption 0.82, micro 0.76; labels that name structure
leave mono for the body face). The Display panel gains a Headings choice
(body face or a bundled display face) and a live preview that shows a figure
label and a stage line, so Size is seen to scale them. It is first in the
redesign because every later mockup and screenshot depends on it. Non-goal:
palette (TSK-1110), frame chrome (TSK-1111), stage at-rules (TSK-1112).

## Outcome

After this merges, a reader who sets Size in cf-present sees every line on the page scale, stage content included, and nothing on the page renders under the readable floor.

## Deliverables

- the root scale and rem tokens: `crates/codeflow-present/web/src/styles.css` (tokens, scale steps, root, chrome kickers), `figure.css` shared with the portal
- the Headings choice, the bundled display face and the live preview: `crates/codeflow-present/web/src/chrome.tsx` (the Display panel), `fonts.ts`, the font asset beside Inter, Archivo and Plex
- the `present check` warning on px under 13 in a stage sheet: `crates/codeflow-present/src/` (the check) and its tests
- authoring guidance: `assets/base/skills/cf-present/references/document-authoring.md` (the rem rule and the scale names), both mirrors byte-identical
- tests: a scale test that measures a stage line at each Size step (browser test under `crates/codeflow-present/web/tests/`), the token drift test, screenshots at 1200 and 400 px in light and dark at Default and Large
