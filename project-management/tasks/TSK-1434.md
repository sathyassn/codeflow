# TSK-1434: A clean merge of main is judged by git's own merge and main's first-parent history

## Description

When `main` moves, a line merges it in. That merge should keep every task review already given on the line when it is exactly git's own merge of its parents, and judge anything else as today. Today equality is decided by a libgit2 reconstruction (`crates/codeflow-core/src/workgraph/acceptance.rs:1889-1967` on `main` at `f8aabc62c`), and the second parent is accepted when it is any ancestor of the default branch (`main_sync.rs:116-118`), not a commit on its first-parent history. This task decides equality by `git merge-tree`, binds the parents, and keeps one clean-merge helper for every caller. Non-goal: a new `git.work_records` switch; the clean case is fixed instead.

Landing on its target (`main`, alone, early):

- Reachable: reviews kept across a clean merge of `main` into a line.
- Inert: any merge that fails a condition is judged as today.
- Other lines: nothing.

Waits for TSK-1123's work on `main`: its record is on `main`, its work is on the EPC-022 line, and both change `acceptance.rs`.

## Outcome

After this merges, a maintainer can merge `main` into a line and keep every task review on the line when the merge equals git's own merge of its parents.
