# TSK-248: An epic line with a direct commit can be repaired and lands

## Description

Fixes #85. Let adopters repair and land an epic integration line that already
contains a direct commit by recording its adoption in reviewed, landed work.
Refuse new unadopted direct commits at pre-push while they can still be moved
into a task or planning pull request. Scope includes epic record validation,
first-parent history checks, CI reporting, push checks, regression tests and
the documented rule. Changing protected-branch policy, rewriting shared
history and changing workspace root-branch behavior are out of scope.

## Deliverables

- Epic-line adoption and provenance checks: `crates/codeflow-core/src/workgraph/work_start.rs`.
- Adoption shape validation: `crates/codeflow-core/src/validate/mod.rs`.
- Adoption reporting on the CI class line: `crates/codeflow-cli/src/cmd/ci/classification.rs`.
- Epic-line pre-push refusal and recovery remedy: `crates/codeflow-core/src/hooks/git_hook.rs`.
- CI, hook and repair-journey regression coverage: `crates/codeflow-cli/tests/`.
- Core and rule contract regression coverage: `crates/codeflow-core/src/` and `crates/codeflow-core/tests/rule_map_contract.rs`.
- Managed rule and synchronized installed baseline: `assets/base/rules/git-rules.md`, `.codeflow/rules/git-rules.md` and `.codeflow/.baseline/`.
- Dated R-52 amendment and release note: `project-management/specs/SPC-013.md` and `CHANGELOG.md`.

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-1 When an epic line's first-parent range holds a non-merge commit that
  the epic record at the head lists under `line_adoptions` with a full id,
  a reason and a review, and that entry arrived by a merge commit or is
  already at the target, `codeflow ci` classes the head as the epic line and
  prints the adoption on the class line; `work.criteria_frozen` and
  `work.acceptance_binding` do not fire for tasks of the line. Control: the
  same line without the entry, or with the entry added by a second direct
  commit, is refused naming the commit (fails on main today).
  `crates/codeflow-cli/tests/ci_cli.rs`, unit tests in
  `crates/codeflow-core/src/workgraph/work_start.rs`.
- AC-2 An entry with a short id, an empty reason or an empty review is a
  record validation error naming the field; an entry naming a commit outside
  the range is a note, never a refusal.
  `crates/codeflow-core/src/validate/mod.rs` tests.
- AC-3 A push to `refs/heads/integration/EPC-*` whose pushed range carries
  a non-merge first-parent commit without a landed adoption is refused with
  a `git reset --keep` remedy naming the line and the adoption route; a push of merges only, a first push of a fresh line, and
  a push to a line already carrying an older direct commit outside the
  pushed range all pass. `crates/codeflow-cli/tests/hooks_cli.rs`.
- AC-4 The reporter's scenario end to end: a line with one direct commit,
  a planning PR adding the adoption lands on the line by merge, the line's
  PR to `main` passes `codeflow ci`. `crates/codeflow-cli/tests/refusal_journey/`. (journey)
- AC-5 git-rules.md and SPC-013 carry the rule; contract tests pin the
  sentences. `crates/codeflow-core/tests/rule_map_contract.rs` or the
  existing rule contract tests.
