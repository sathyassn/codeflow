# TSK-381: A pinned research input stays satisfied after its producer's line is deleted

## Description

Issue: #236. A task with a pinned research input cannot be claimed once the producer's integration branch is deleted after landing. `work claim` and `work next` report "depends on TSK-104 on 'integration/EPC-002-carry', which is not fetched here: unknown, not satisfied" (`crates/codeflow-core/src/workgraph/work_start.rs:109`), although the producer is complete and the pin is an ancestor of both the consumer's target and `origin/main`. The adopter's workaround repoints the producer records' `integration_target` to `main`. The issue's comment adds a second defect: readiness reads the local `main`, so a pin on `origin/main` fails until the local branch is fast-forwarded (`work_start.rs:2591`, "not on 'main'").

Outcome: **a pinned research or decision input stays satisfied after its producer's line is deleted, when the pin holds the producer's completed record and is an ancestor of a verified target that still exists, and readiness reads remote-tracking refs, never a local branch that may be behind.**

Identity, completion and the other claim checks stay. A missing object or an unproven landing still fails closed. TSK-341 owns stacked predecessor pins (`kind` absent); this task owns research and decision pins.

Added by the EPC-022 amendment of 2026-10-18 under the operator's release decision of that day (workspace `RELEASE-PLAN.md`, 2026-10-18 operator decision).

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-1 When a research or decision pin holds the producer's completed record and is an ancestor of the consumer's target or of the remote-tracking protected branch, `work next`, `work claim` and `work start` shall treat the edge as satisfied after the producer's integration branch is deleted; evidence: a fixture that lands the producer, deletes its line and claims the consumer, failing before the change. (serves EPC-022 AC-77)
- AC-2 Readiness shall read `origin/<branch>` (the trusted remote's tracking ref), so a local `main` behind `origin/main` changes nothing; evidence: the issue comment's reproduction as a test.
- AC-3 A pin whose object is missing, whose record at the pin is not the producer's completed record, or that is an ancestor of no existing verified target shall still refuse, and the refusal shall name the ref it read; evidence: negative tests per case.
- AC-4 (journey) With the installed build, a scratch adopter project shall land a producer epic, delete its line, and claim and start the consumer with no record edit; evidence: the journey output.
