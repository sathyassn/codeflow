# TSK-260: Review and research briefs are holistic

## Description

Briefs for review and research, in every file an agent reads, ask for the
whole unit at one head in a stated manner and treat a named scope as where
the work starts, never a fence. On 2026-10-08 several review briefs asked
the other-lineage seat for a fix delta or a merge only, and the verdicts
carried an earlier approval forward to a head they did not cover (TSK-254
rounds 2 and 3, TSK-242 rounds 8 and 9, the PR 114 Reviews row). The rule
was right as written; the brief contract was restated in six drifting
files, two sentences licensed a narrower pass, the batch landing doctrine
said "review the resolved hunks", the verdict never named its head, and
nothing mechanical read a brief or a Reviews row. This task gives each rule
one home (the workflow discipline rules for what a review reads, how review
and research look and how a fix is confirmed; `findings.md` for the brief;
`cf-reviewer` for the verdict; `cf-method` "Land in batches" for landing),
turns the restatements into pointers, and adds two checks where the words
failed: `deliver.py --review <base>...<head>` refuses a narrower brief, and
`codeflow ci` requires the Reviews row to approve the bound `reviewed`
commit. Closes sathyassn/codeflow issue 121. Design: Fable, revision 3,
after two Grok challenge rounds; Grok round 3's five findings are applied
(the kernel's land row keeps `red: diagnose first` and the dependents rule
moves to the git rules' "Bodies of work", which the minimal tier's land row
opens; the orchestrator keeps `Self-review is never independent.`; a path
anywhere in one `git diff` or `git log` is refused; a light `None:` Reviews
section counts as absent). Non-goals: no new eval case, policy key, template
section or record; no raised guideline number.

Reading measurements (`codeflow doctor --check reading` with the branch
binary after `codeflow update`): kernel 12283 to 12286 of 12288; per-task
chain 131069 to 131037 of 131072. The chain is the orchestrator skill,
workflow-lifecycle, current-ensemble.json, cf-delegate, cf-plan,
cf-develop, cf-ship, capability-routing, routing-policy.json, the quality
contract, quality evidence, routing evidence, transport, pr-evidence, plan,
design-implementation, materiality, authority, verification, coverage,
review and completion. The on-chain files this task changes, before and
after in bytes:

- `cf-model-orchestrator/SKILL.md`: 26009 to 25995
- `cf-delegate/SKILL.md`: 7637 to 7636
