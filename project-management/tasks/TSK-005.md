# TSK-005 — strengthen editorial and product design contracts

## Description

Consolidate the contextual language baseline and extend product design intent
with language/voice and applicable appearance-mode reasoning. Preserve the
consuming project's authority and collapse paths; do not add product theme
presets, a second editorial skill, or mechanical style enforcement.

## Affected surfaces and interfaces

- `cf-editorial-review`, `cf-design`, AGENTS/CLAUDE routing, orchestrator quality
  contract, evaluation requirements/cases, scaffold manifests and mirrors.
- CAP-010/CAP-013 design and review behavior; SPC-003.
