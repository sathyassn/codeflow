# TSK-1133: Capability ids come from the registry and coverage shows in the status commands

## Description

The second half of design unit U1. Capability ids come from the shared registry: `Kind::Cap` is added, `codeflow capability new` allocates through it, and the coverage lines (how many capabilities, how many with resolved evidence, how many unassessed) appear in `init`, `update`, `orient` and `doctor`. Basis: about 600 lines across `ids/`, a new command, `recall.rs`, `status.rs`, `orient.rs` and `doctor`, plus fixtures. Non-goal: the check that reads these records (TSK-1135).

Design: `coordination/coordinators/capabilities/design-note.md` revision 5 (workspace repository) and its acceptance tests.

## Outcome

After this merges, an agent can create a capability record with a registry-allocated id and see catalog coverage in the session digest and in `doctor`.

## Deliverables

- the `Kind::Cap` registry kind: `crates/codeflow-core/src/ids/`
- the `capability new` command: `crates/codeflow-cli/src/cmd/capability.rs`
- coverage lines: `crates/codeflow-core/src/status.rs`, `orient.rs`, `recall.rs`, the doctor module, and the init and update reports
- tests for each surface: `crates/codeflow-cli/tests/`

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-1 `capability new` with tracking on shall allocate through the registry and write `ids/CAP/<N>.toml`; with tracking off it shall allocate locally, and the next registry-backed allocation shall not reuse the number; evidence: tests for both modes. (serves EPC-026 AC-2)
- AC-2 An existing CAP-017 fence followed by a new allocation shall yield CAP-018, not CAP-001; evidence: a test on a fence fixture. (serves EPC-026 AC-2)
- AC-3 `init`, `update`, `orient` and `doctor` shall each show the coverage line, with coverage kept separate from evidence; evidence: one test per command. (serves EPC-026 AC-2)
