# TSK-154: operator decisions restored to the reserved list

## Description

The byte-cut audit (2026-09-27) found that TSK-076's commit `c477d3f7d`,
while pointing the orchestrator's ask rules at `autonomy.md`, dropped two
operator-owned decisions from them (row H36: risk tolerance and a material
security boundary) and the rationale for staged reads (row H37). The
operator ruled that both decisions stay operator-owned and that nothing key
is cut for bytes. This task restores H36 and H37 by meaning, in their
original wording or better, in the one full operator-owned list in
`cf-method/references/autonomy.md`, and makes the orchestrator read that list
at the moment it decides whether to ask, escalate or stop. The
security-weakening hard gate stays as it is; it is narrower than the
boundary decision. Rows R01 and R02 need no change on this line, since both
losses exist only on the release rehearsal. Non-goal: trimming anything to
fit a byte test; the orchestrator skill's byte ratchet is raised to hold the
restored text, with the reason recorded in the test.
