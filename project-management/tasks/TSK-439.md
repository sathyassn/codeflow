# TSK-439: Lanes have ids, a lifecycle and a current seat

## Description

ADR-0086, the lanes part, decided by the operator on 2026-10-22. A
lane is a durable unit of delegated work; a seat is the harness session
attached to it, replaceable; a session is a process; the host is the
coordinating role. Today a lane exists only as a Herdr tab and a
conversation, so a deleted session loses which seats were running and
which reviews were in flight, and a follow-up row has no stable holder
to name.

Outcome: **a lane has an id `LN-NNN-<slug>`, allocated per project,
monotonic and never reused, with the slug derived from its scope; the
host role is held by one session at a time, by a lease and heartbeat;
a lane records its kind (build, review, research, consult), scope
(task, tasks, epic or topic, with refs), model, current seat (harness,
pane, resume id, worktree, head), heartbeat and its follow-up rows; moves through open,
active, idle, closed (reason done, abandoned or superseded, with proof)
and archived, never deleted; and `codeflow lane open|attach|close|list`
drives it, with a follow-up row's holder being `host` or a lane id,
Herdr tab labels using lane ids, and a standalone session being its
own host.**

Rules:

- Lane records are events in the same clone-local store as the ledger
  (`.git/codeflow/follow/`, TSK-422), with the id allocated through the
  shared id registry's rules (`codeflow ids`) so numbers are monotonic
  per project and never reused.
- **Scope.** A lane often carries more than one task, sometimes a
  whole epic or a body of work on a topic. `lane open` records kind,
  model and a scope with a kind (`task`, `tasks`, `epic` or `topic`)
  and refs (task ids, an epic id or a topic phrase); `codeflow lane
  scope` changes it over time, each change an event, so the history
  shows which work the lane carried. The slug is derived from the
  scope: lowercase, short, unique among open lanes and renamable; the
  number is the identity and never repeats. Readiness per kind: a
  `task` lane builds when `codeflow work next` reports the task ready;
  a `tasks` lane starts each task when it is ready; an `epic` lane takes
  tasks in the epic's order as each becomes ready; a `topic` lane builds
  nothing until it creates or links tasks. `work claim` opens or links
  the task's lane. A replaced lane closes as superseded and names its
  successor. `attach` binds or replaces the current seat
  and starts its heartbeat; a replaced seat's resume id stays in the
  lane's history. `close` needs a reason (done, abandoned, superseded
  naming the replacing lane) and a proof; `list` shows lanes with
  state, seat and heartbeat age. A lane whose seat heartbeat is older
  than the seat timeout shows as idle, and its rows as orphaned until a
  new seat attaches (TSK-440 surfaces them).
- The host role is a lease (`.git/codeflow/roles/host.lease`) one
  session holds at a time with a heartbeat; a lone session is its own
  host; a stale lease is reclaimed by the next session claiming `host`,
  which adopts the orphaned rows.
- `cf-herdr` refuses to open a seat without a lane id, and tab labels
  and seat names carry the id.
- Whether a lane may open sub-lanes is open for the operator (proposed:
  only the host, so one session sees the whole tree); until decided,
  `lane open` from a lane records the parent and warns.
- **Timing.** Two settings in the project configuration, placed by
  TSK-280's rule: `check interval`, how often the host surfaces due
  rows (default 10 minutes), and `heartbeat timeout`, how long without
  a sign of life before a host or seat counts as gone (default 30
  minutes). The timeout must exceed the interval, because one long
  command (a 25 to 45 minute full gate) refreshes no heartbeat while it
  runs; heartbeats are refreshed by every hook firing and every
  `codeflow` command.
- **Takeover is never automatic.** The start hook reports a stale host
  or seat; a session runs an explicit `codeflow lane adopt <id>` or
  `codeflow follow adopt host`; a returning session that finds its role
  or lane adopted stops and says so.

Non-goals: the ledger rows themselves (TSK-422); the hooks (TSK-440);
Herdr's own tab lifecycle beyond the label.

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-1 `codeflow lane open` shall allocate `LN-NNN-<slug>` monotonically per project, never reusing a number even after a close or archive, derive the slug from the scope (lowercase, short, unique among open lanes) and record kind, model and scope (kind `task`, `tasks`, `epic` or `topic` with its refs); `lane scope` shall change the scope and rename the slug as an event, so `list --history` shows which work the lane carried; evidence: CLI tests including a reopen after close and a scope change. (serves EPC-025 AC-2)
- AC-2 `lane attach` shall bind or replace the current seat (harness, pane, resume id, worktree, head), keep the replaced seat's resume id in the lane's history and start the heartbeat, `lane close` shall refuse without a reason (done, abandoned, superseded naming the replacing lane) and a proof, and no command shall delete a lane; evidence: CLI tests. (serves EPC-025 AC-2)
- AC-7 `work claim` shall open or link the task's lane, and `cf-herdr` shall refuse to open a seat without a lane id and label the tab with it; evidence: CLI tests and a native Herdr canary. (serves EPC-025 AC-2)
- AC-3 A lane shall move only through open, active, idle, closed and archived, a lane or host whose heartbeat is older than `heartbeat timeout` shall be reported as stale with its rows orphaned and never taken over automatically, an explicit `adopt` shall attach the new session or seat and adopt the rows, and a returning session that finds its role or lane adopted shall stop and say so; evidence: lifecycle tests with expired seat and host heartbeats, an adopt, and a return after adoption. (serves EPC-025 AC-2)
- AC-4 A follow-up row's holder shall be `host` or an existing lane id, refusing any other value, and a standalone session claiming `host` shall work with no lane; evidence: CLI tests.
- AC-5 `check interval` (default 10 minutes) and `heartbeat timeout` (default 30 minutes) shall be project settings placed by TSK-280's rule, a configuration whose timeout does not exceed the interval shall be refused naming both, every hook firing and every `codeflow` command shall refresh the caller's heartbeat, and `lane open` from inside a lane shall record the parent and warn until the operator decides sub-lanes (proposed: host only); evidence: settings tests, a refresh test and the warn test.
- AC-8 Readiness shall follow the scope kind: a `task` lane shall build only when `work next` reports the task ready, a `tasks` lane shall start each task when it is ready, an `epic` lane shall take the epic's tasks in its order as each becomes ready, and a `topic` lane shall refuse to build until it creates or links a task; a replaced lane shall close as superseded naming its successor; evidence: one CLI test per kind and the supersede test. (serves EPC-025 AC-2)
- AC-6 (journey) With the installed build, a host session shall open an `epic` build lane, a Herdr seat shall attach with its resume id and the tab label shall carry the lane id, the lane shall take the epic's tasks in order, the seat shall go stale, the start hook of a new session shall report it, an explicit `adopt` shall attach a new seat and adopt the rows, and the old seat on return shall stop and say so; evidence: the journey output.
