# TSK-059: portal figure runtime and figure gate

## Description

Give the portal and the present review surface one figure runtime that
draws the grammar: one grammar module (`docs-portal/scripts/figure-grammar.mjs`,
the `shared/svg.js` lineage from the W5 board rewritten on the utility
tokens) with scales, the closed mark vocabulary drawn with the mark classes
of the kit's `figure.css` from TSK-070, legend keying from the drawn set, the
accessible description, the table twin and per-width recomposition; the
portal and present each carry the kit's `figure.css` byte for byte as their
figure sheet, and present carries a byte-identical copy of the module at
`web/src/figure-grammar.mjs`, all pinned by tests, since its esbuild entry
reaches only `web/src/`. The portal
adapter emits a figure block from a figure declaration file, bound to its
page in `portal.config.json` by route and by altitude panel or section
anchor, never by a marker inside a source; present gains a
`figure` block variant in its closed Rust enum and document schema, rendered
as a placeholder carrying the declaration and drawn on the client by
`enhance.ts`, the way its diagram block already works. Both take the same
declaration, in two binding modes: authored (positions declared in the
file) and derived (the adapter binds a data model from a named source such as
the capability registry, the policy schema, the ADR graph or the CLI help, and
records the derived values). Extend `browser-verify` with the figure gate,
using the probes the W5 board needed: every explanatory page has one figure
per altitude; every figure has a legend that keys every drawn state and
nothing more, a description, a table twin and no literal colour; every pair
of drawn states differs on at least two channels measured off the render
(interior, edge, geometry, overlay) in both modes, with light and dark token
identity as the non-hue proof; every drawn inner mark is at or above the
declared floor at both widths, measured by following each state group down
to its marks; no text box intersects another text box or a mark; the narrow
render draws the declared narrow variant, whose mark set is not a permutation
of the wide set; derived figures pass a fidelity check against their source.
Rewrite the page classes so every explanatory altitude demands a figure and
a table is an additional Technical carrier, never a substitute. Add the
classes a guide needs beside explanatory and the record pointer:

- derived lookup: a generated table with a fidelity check;
- illustrated source: the source rendered as it is, plus the companion
  figures bound to it in configuration, each drawn above the source or at
  the head of its anchored section. The gate demands at least one figure
  at the page head, verifies every bound figure against all twelve figure
  rules (the fidelity rule re-derives each of its `facts[]` from the
  source anchor it names), and does not demand the Concept, Architecture,
  Technical trio. This is how a guide illustrates a source it must not
  edit, such as an instruction an agent loads;
- pass-through: the source rendered as it is, with no figure demanded, for
  one reason from a closed set: accepted record (append-only, such as an
  ADR), governance text, or no relationship to draw (the design primary's
  judgment, with the reason recorded).

A page is illustrated or pass-through only by a declaration in
`portal.config.json` naming its source path or prefix. Neither class drops
a route or changes source bytes; the evidence manifest lists every route
with its class, reason and bound figures, and the gate prints the counts.
Every source without a declaration stays explanatory. Prove that
`validate --portal` accepts the content the adapter inserts on a route (a
companion figure above an unchanged source) and still fails a tampered
source (a rendered route whose source body no longer equals the committed
source) or a figure bound to a missing route or anchor. Mirror the
runtime into the managed starter and teach the Rust validator the figure
block's evidence. Once the block renders, make the skills' default form
true in the files that still teach the stage: rewrite
`cf-docs-portal/resources/portal-page-shape.example.md` under the grammar on
a subject other than the evidence board's own case, with a declaration and
the figure block in every panel; route `how-presentation-works.md` section 2
and the block catalog in `document-authoring.md` to the figure block with
the family named, the stage kept only as the flow interim; replace the
`cf-stage` default and the one-figure-in-Concept gate in the portal
`visual-craft.md` with a figure in every explanatory panel; make the portal
`SKILL.md` line "figures lead every altitude panel" true as written; and
replace the doctrine's "Not yet a rendered carrier" interim sentence in both
`utility-presentation-system.md` copies. Non-goal: authoring the guide's
figures (TSK-061); the explanation method, the dedupe and the rest of
`how-presentation-works.md` (TSK-072).

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] When a page declares a figure in a family, the runtime draws it from the
      declaration with the grammar module and no page-local SVG; the nine
      specimen figures from TSK-058 render through it unchanged in the portal
      and in a present document; evidence: `node --test` cases per family,
      the portal fixture page and a present fixture reviewed by the design
      primary in light and dark.
- [ ] When a figure is derived, the adapter records the source path, the
      derived values and the drawn values in the evidence manifest, and
      `validate --portal` compares them; a hand-edited drawn value fails;
      evidence: the validator test with a tampered fixture.
- [ ] When any explanatory page lacks a figure at an altitude, or a figure
      has an unkeyed mark, a literal colour, a missing description or table
      twin, two states that differ on fewer than two rendered channels in
      either mode, an inner mark under the floor at either width, or a text
      box that intersects another text box or a mark, `browser:verify` fails
      and names the page, the altitude and the rule; evidence: one failing
      fixture per rule beside one compliant page that cannot mask it.
- [ ] Every figure recomposes at 390 px by the declaration's narrow variant
      or the family's default recomposition, and the gate fails a narrow
      render whose mark set is a permutation of the wide set; evidence: the
      structural check with a reflowed fixture that fails, and the design
      primary's review of the specimens at 390.
- [ ] An Architecture table without a figure and a Technical figure without
      a table both fail the composition gate, and a Technical table beside a
      figure passes; evidence: the page-class tests.
- [ ] On a mixed fixture guide: an illustrated source (an unchanged agent
      instruction with one companion figure bound in configuration) passes
      with its source bytes unchanged; the same route with a bound figure
      that breaks a figure rule fails, and with no bound figure fails; an
      accepted ADR declared pass-through passes without a figure; an
      unillustrated explanatory page fails; a pass-through declaration with
      no reason or a reason outside the closed set fails the build, and so
      does a figure bound to a route or anchor that does not exist; the
      validator accepts the inserted figure and fails a tampered source;
      evidence: the page-class and validator tests on that fixture and the
      evidence manifest listing each route's class and bound figures.
- [ ] On the same fixture, a companion whose anchor exists but whose
      asserted fact is wrong fails the fidelity rule, so the check
      re-derives the fact and does not stop at the anchor; the companion
      declarations and their bindings are committed inputs under the
      adapter's input pins and tamper checks, and a hand-edited companion
      declaration or binding fails as a tampered source does; generated
      pages and Markdown twins attribute companion content to its
      declaration, never to the source; evidence: the validator tests with
      both tampered fixtures.
- [ ] The starter carries the runtime byte for byte and `portal setup` on a
      sample renders the specimen fixture; the present copy of the grammar
      module is byte identical to the portal source, and the portal, starter
      and present figure sheets are byte identical to the kit's `figure.css`;
      evidence: the starter equality test, the copy tests and a harness row.
- [ ] The page shape example declares a family per panel and draws each
      with the figure block; `how-presentation-works.md` section 2, the
      `document-authoring.md` catalog and the portal `visual-craft.md` name
      the figure block as the default and `cf-stage` only as the flow
      interim; the doctrine no longer calls the block unrendered; the portal
      `SKILL.md` stays within its 9216-byte ratchet; evidence: pins on those
      markers, a grep that finds no "Prefer a `cf-stage` fence" in the
      skills, and the budget test output.
- [ ] Editorial review of the declaration file format documentation, Grok
      adversarial review of the gate, Codex review where the seat allows;
      evidence: the review records.
