# TSK-1334: Present and portal checks enforce the immediately obvious bar

## Description

Split from TSK-1097 on 2026-11-04 by the second consolidation amendment (the operator's planning direction of 2026-11-04 21:5x, relayed by coord/catalog: a task is one noticeable outcome with its own proof). TSK-1097 states the immediately obvious bar in both skills and the figure guidance; this task makes the checks hold authors to it. It takes TSK-1097's check criteria word for word: the `codeflow present check` warnings with a clean control each, the 390 px render checks of the reference pages, the left-border style test, the text fit test, the inline SVG, accessibility and Mermaid checks, the visual-coverage check, the pill line test, and the card and panel text checks (former TSK-1097 AC-3, AC-6, AC-11, AC-13, AC-15, AC-16, AC-17 and AC-21, now AC-1 to AC-8). Where their text cites a sibling criterion it keeps TSK-1097's numbering, and their `Moved` notes say so. TSK-1097's Description holds the bar (items (a) to (j)) these checks test.

Non-goals: the bar's text, the figure guidance and the reference pages' content (TSK-1097); the token source and its line-limit token (TSK-1162); the missing-`topic` and registry-carrier warnings (TSK-1121 AC-8, TSK-1120 AC-4); the shipped sample set (TSK-1127).

## Outcome

After this merges, `codeflow present check` and the portal gate warn or fail, by name, on every part of the immediately obvious bar a machine can measure, on both surfaces.

## Deliverables

- the checks: `codeflow present check` warnings in `crates/codeflow-present/src/document.rs` (a stage over 600 units, a table over six columns, a plain table anywhere outside a figure twin, a section with no lead line, a disclosure whose content is only narrative or a plain table, a disclosure with no lead); the portal gate's matching advisories in `assets/docs-portal/starter/scripts/` (from TSK-1097)
- checks: the left-border style test, the fit test, the Mermaid refusal and the visual-coverage test on both surfaces (from TSK-1097)
- tests: present check tests per warning with a clean control, and 390 px render tests on both reference pages (from TSK-1097)
- the card and panel text checks in `present check`, the portal gate and the editorial check (from TSK-1097)
- release note: `changelog.d/TSK-1334.md`
- managed wiring, mirrors and baseline: `assets/base/scaffold-manifest.toml`, installed managed files and `.codeflow/` baseline, synchronized when changed
