# TSK-1324: Quick's target caps fit the documented nice-10 build

## Description

Source: coord/ci, 2026-11-04 12:3x (coord/records tracker). Under `coordination/scripts/nice10.sh`, the documented way to run heavy work on the shared host (nice 10, two cargo jobs), quick's `contract-scans` target hits its 600 s cap (the default `timeout_seconds`; `.codeflow/test-config.json:182-193` sets none) on the builds of TSK-1085, TSK-1278, TSK-1318, TSK-1151 and TSK-1058 on 2026-11-04, while it takes 5 to 6 minutes run directly. `docs-portal` hits its 300 s cap (`.codeflow/test-config.json:499`) on PR 571, at 346 s direct. Every test inside passed each time, so quick reads red for time alone.

This task sizes those caps from measurement. No existing record covers it: TSK-1306, TSK-1299, TSK-1241 and TSK-1253 were checked on the line at 12:34. The caps are this repository's own gate settings; an adopter's caps stay its own, set through the same keys.

Non-goal: changing what either target tests.

## Outcome

After this merges, a builder who runs quick through the documented nice-10 wrapper gets a verdict on the tests, never a timeout on a target whose tests all pass, and a target that really hangs still fails.

## Deliverables

- the measurement and the chosen caps: a `## Findings` section in this record
- the caps: `.codeflow/test-config.json`, declared through the keys of TSK-1299's registry and TSK-1278's per-target keys
- release note: `changelog.d/TSK-1324.md`

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-1 Before any change, the builder shall record in a `## Findings` section, per quick target, the wall time and CPU time under `nice10.sh` and run directly on the same host, with run ids and the host load, and the failing runs above as fail-before (contract-scans at its 600 s cap on the five builds named; docs-portal at its 300 s cap on PR 571, 346 s direct); evidence: the Findings table.
- AC-2 Each quick target's cap shall fit its measured nice-10 time with a stated headroom, or the target shall use a CPU-time or stall cap instead of a wall deadline; the builder chooses with the evidence of AC-1 against the operator's bands in codeflow issue 334 and states why; evidence: the Findings and the config diff.
