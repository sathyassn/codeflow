# TSK-432: Pending work moves to a seat with capacity and a stopped seat resumes

## Description

Issue #234, items 3 and 4. With the limit state readable (TSK-431),
nothing yet acts on it: a run stops when a family hits its limit and
the operator restarts seats by hand from a cold start.

Outcome: **at a configurable threshold (default 90 %) the orchestrator
first moves pending work to the other family's seat under the existing
routing rules, then offers the capped API-key fallback from the
profile, and prompts for a manual profile switch only between
separate accounts the profile names; each switch is recorded in the
run's evidence; and a seat stopped by a limit resumes on the new route
with its brief and state instead of a cold restart.**

Rules: the threshold and the order of routes are a project setting
(provisional `seats.capacity` in `.codeflow/model-selection.json`);
a move to the other family keeps the review-lineage rules (the
reviewer never becomes the builder's lineage); the API-key fallback is
used only within its spend cap and recorded; the resume reuses the
delegate lifecycle's resume id (`cf-herdr`) and the task's recorded
brief; the operator is prompted, never switched automatically, between
separate accounts; and the terms boundary of the issue is restated in
the docs.

Non-goals: automatic rotation between one person's subscriptions;
reading or storing tokens.

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-1 With a family at or past the threshold, `codeflow models resolve` shall route pending work to the other family's seat where the routing rules allow it, name the API-key fallback where they do not, and name the manual switch only when the profile lists a separate account; evidence: CLI tests per route with fixture limit states. (serves EPC-024 AC-9)
- AC-2 Each switch shall be recorded in the run's evidence with family, route, limit state and time, and a reviewer lineage shall never be moved onto the builder's lineage; evidence: the evidence record in the tests and a negative test. (serves EPC-024 AC-9)
- AC-3 A seat stopped by a limit shall resume on the new route with its brief and its native resume id, and the resumed turn shall continue the task's state; evidence: a qualification run on a native seat.
- AC-4 No route shall read, copy or store a credential, and the docs shall state the terms boundary; evidence: a search of the change and the doc text.
- AC-5 (journey) With the installed build, a fixture that drives one family to 95 % shall move the next build to the other family's seat with the switch recorded, and the stopped seat shall resume; evidence: the journey output.
