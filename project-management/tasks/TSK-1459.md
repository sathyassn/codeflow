# TSK-1459: The release-route and terminal-carrier guards read assignments through the shared reader

## Description

TSK-1457 gives the guards on `main` one shared reader for every way a shell line sets a variable. Two readers on the EPC-022 line read assignments on their own: the release-route reader added by TSK-1346 and its fix forward, TSK-1431, and the terminal-carrier reader added by TSK-1344 (PR 706). Each was hardened separately, and keeping three readers means a later gap is fixed in one and missed in the others. After the main sync brings TSK-1457 to the line, both move to the shared reader.
