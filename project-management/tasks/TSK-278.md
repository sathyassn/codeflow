# TSK-278: A closed record can be redacted without losing its results

## Description

A project that must remove personal or customer names from a completed
record has no sanctioned route when a non-planning commit followed the
record's landing on its line (https://github.com/sathyassn/codeflow/issues/134). PR 111 (TSK-253, ADR-0080) lets a
completed standalone task's criteria text be corrected, but the acceptance
block and an `acceptance_superseded` block cannot be redacted, and the
waiver that the `work.acceptance_binding` message names has no documented
field or format (a grep of `docs/` and cf-method finds none). This task
adds a record-only redaction route that keeps every result, the reviewed
commit and the review provenance and records a digest of the replaced
text, and documents the waiver. Non-goal: changing a criterion's meaning or
result, which stays a reopen.

## Deliverables

- the redaction route: `crates/codeflow-core/src/workgraph/acceptance.rs`, `crates/codeflow-core/src/workgraph/status_verb.rs`
- the message pointer: `crates/codeflow-core/src/remedy.rs`
- tests: `crates/codeflow-cli/tests/ci_cli/` and the acceptance unit tests
- docs: the acceptance section of `assets/base/claude/skills/cf-method/references/project-organization.md` (mirrors and baseline), `docs/delivery.md`; an ADR under `docs/decisions/` if the route changes ADR-0080's rule (provisional: the builder decides with the reviewer)
- release note: `changelog.d/TSK-278.md`

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-1 When a record-only change replaces text in a completed record's
  acceptance block or criteria wording and records a digest of the original
  text and the review of the change, `codeflow ci` shall accept it, with
  each criterion result, `reviewed` and the review provenance unchanged;
  evidence: ci fixtures for issue 134 reproductions 1 and 2. (serves
  EPC-022 AC-2)
- AC-2 When the change also alters a result, the reviewed commit, the review
  provenance or a file outside the record, it shall be refused, naming what
  changed; evidence: one refusal fixture per field.
- AC-3 The route shall work on a line where a non-merge, non-planning commit
  followed the record's landing merge, and for a reopened record's
  `acceptance_superseded` block; evidence: the reproduction 1 fixture with
  that commit, and a superseded-block fixture.
- AC-4 The waiver named in the `work.acceptance_binding` message shall be
  documented (its field, its format, and what "a record-only amendment
  strictly before the reviewed revision" means), and the message shall
  point at that text; evidence: the doc diff and a message test. (serves
  EPC-022 AC-2)
- AC-5 (journey) When the installed build runs on an adopter-shaped line (a completed
  standalone task, its landing merge, then a direct commit), a redaction
  pull request shall pass `codeflow ci` and pre-push; evidence: a
  CLI journey on a scratch repository.
