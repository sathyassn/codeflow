# TSK-1359: New task and epic records state the work they overlap, their value class and who they serve

## Description

The planning-rules review of the Director (2026-11-05) found that new task
and epic records duplicate open or recently landed work unseen, and that
some name an internal role (a coordinator, a seat, an agent) as the one they
serve. The operator approved enforcing new-record checks for overlap, value
and user on 2026-11-05 17:24 (workspace `RELEASE-PLAN.md`). This task builds
those checks: `codeflow task new` and `epic new` print the overlap candidates
and write an `overlaps:` field with a disposition per candidate; a record
carries a `value_class:` and an Outcome subject that names an adopter, a
user, the operator or a maintainer; and `codeflow ci` refuses a record the
range adds that fails either check. `codeflow validate --docs` only reports.

Split 2026-11-05 from TSK-419 by the Director's decision, in the value audit
planning pull request (PR 638): TSK-419 keeps the instruction and template
text, and this task holds the record checks with their security review. It
depends on TSK-419 for the template form these checks read.

Non-goals: refusing or warning per record on records created before the
project adopts the checks, or on records a sync or checkpoint carries; a
semantic duplicate search beyond shared key words.

## Outcome

After this merges, a maintainer can file a task or epic only after recording, for each open or recently closed record that shares its key words, whether it extends that record or why it is distinct, and only with a value class and an adopter, user, operator or maintainer named as the one it serves.

## Deliverables

- the overlap print and the `overlaps:` field: `codeflow task new` and `epic new` in `crates/codeflow-cli/src/cmd/new.rs` (provisional; the builder confirms the path at claim)
- the record checks: the Outcome validator beside TSK-435's check in `crates/codeflow-core/src/validate/` and the `codeflow ci` refusal (provisional)
- the planning amendment report's disposition print: `crates/codeflow-core/src/workgraph/amendment.rs`
- the adoption uid set: the project config beside `outcome_present_uids`, written by `codeflow update` (provisional)
- docs: `docs/policy-reference.md` or the record format reference the builder finds owns record fields (provisional)
- release note: `changelog.d/TSK-1359.md`

## Acceptance Criteria

- AC-1 `codeflow task new` and `codeflow epic new` shall print the overlap candidates for the new record and write them to an `overlaps:` field, one entry per candidate, each with a one-line disposition (`extends`, `distinct: <reason>` or `duplicate`), or `none` when there is no candidate. Matching rule: the key words of a record are the words of its title and its `## Outcome` line, lowercased, with words under four letters and a fixed stop list shipped in the binary removed; a candidate is a task or epic record that is open, or whose closeout date (the date of its `- cancelled:` line, or the date in its acceptance block) falls within the 30 days before the committer date of the range's base, and that shares at least three key words with the new record, or at least half of them when the new record has fewer than six; candidates are ranked by shared key words, then by id, and at most ten are listed. `codeflow ci` shall recompute the candidates from the records at the range's base and the other records the range adds, and shall refuse a new record whose `overlaps:` leaves out a candidate, says `none` when a candidate exists, gives an entry no disposition, or marks one `duplicate`; the refusal shall print each missing candidate with its shared key words, so an author whose list went stale (the line moved, or the range adds two records that match each other) can add the entries; the planning amendment report shall print each disposition for the reviewer; evidence: CLI tests of the print and the field with an exact expected list, and ci fixtures that refuse a missing field, a blind `none`, an omitted candidate, an entry with no disposition and a `duplicate`, a fixture where one range adds two matching records, and a fixture that passes a complete list.
