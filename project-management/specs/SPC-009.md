# SPC-009 — typed worker routes and accountable delegation

## Summary

Pin the smallest typed worker-route extension to the managed ensemble and the
accountability/evidence contract that consumes it. Candidate routes are usable
for bounded non-design work under owning-primary review; scoped-qualified routes
add outcome-bearing evidence only for declared workloads. Neither state changes
standing primary promotion, implies lower cost, or proves a later dispatch.

## Delta against current capability

ADDED — CAP-010/CAP-013 gain ensemble schema v4 typed internal routes, a
`design_execution_owner`, scoped route evidence and honest doctor reporting.

MODIFIED — descriptive model-class/compound-effort worker hints become exact
selectors, discrete efforts, typed workloads and explicit status. Orchestration
records responsibility separately from actual execution and resource context.

REMOVED — no existing public binding or project-selection field. Vague
`medium-or-high` and unsupported economy/default claims are not retained.

## Behavior

The managed ensemble is the only CodeFlow worker-route catalog. A `candidate`
route may execute bounded `reasoning`, `implementation`, `evidence`, or
`review-support` work when the owning primary launches it, reviews the returned
artifact/evidence and accepts the outcome. Candidate use is an experiment under
the existing quality floor, not a scoped qualification or economical-default
claim. A `scoped-qualified` route additionally carries human-landed,
outcome-bearing native evidence for each workload it claims; status is explicit
and is never inferred from a primary binding or a doctor probe.

Substantial well-specified routine execution should use a suitable route when
available. Direct primary execution remains valid for a tiny warm-context
change, inseparable unresolved reasoning, material risk, unavailable route or
bounded recovery; substantial retention records the concrete reason. Routine
commands use tools directly. The primary remains accountable, inspects and
integrates worker output, and does not secretly duplicate the implementation.
Producer-relative cross-family review and all existing quality/safety gates
remain required.

`design_execution_owner` names the standing Claude judgment role. That primary
settles direction and `DESIGN_INTENT`, owns fidelity and integrated judgment,
and directly executes product, UX, UI, interaction and visual-design work while
no Claude route is scoped-qualified for `design-implementation`. A candidate
under the owner binding may declare that workload so controlled disposable
qualification fixtures can exercise it, but it may not execute real product
design work or claim qualification. Once scoped-qualified with observed
application, repeated accepted fidelity evidence and independent cross-family
QA, it may execute settled design without acquiring authority to change
direction or approve fidelity. On recorded Fable unavailability, the qualified
Opus fallback executes at the same Claude authority. Other families provide
planning challenge, feasibility review and QA unless an explicit operator
instruction names a task override in Plan vN; Claude absence alone is not that
override.

Resource snapshots guide dispatch and protection of later design/review
capacity, but missing or stale telemetry remains unknown and does not invent a
separate bucket or block ordinary authorized work unless an explicit hard limit
depends on it. Same-selector worker routes are valid and can add isolated
reasoning or specialization without being cheaper; separation is established by
native same-family-worker dispatch, bounded scope and accountable provenance,
not by a different model name.
Reasoning and review-support workers advise; they never own plan, design,
acceptance or independent-review approval.

## Interfaces and formats

`EnsembleCatalog` schema version 4 adds
`design_execution_owner: String`, which must resolve to a standing role whose
lineage is `claude`.

Every `InternalRoute` contains only these fields:

| Field | Contract |
|---|---|
| `route_id` | Safe atom, unique within its parent binding |
| `model_class` | Non-empty descriptive class; not an economy assertion |
| `native_selectors` | Harness ID to exact selector; keys are a subset of the parent binding's harness keys |
| `efforts` | Non-empty unique values from `low`, `medium`, `high`, `xhigh` |
| `default_effort` | One member of `efforts` |
| `workloads` | Non-empty unique values from `reasoning`, `implementation`, `evidence`, `review-support`, `design-implementation` |
| `status` | Exactly `candidate` or `scoped-qualified` |
| `evidence` | Repository-relative evidence paths; may be empty for a candidate and must be non-empty for scoped-qualified |
| `use` | Non-empty policy nuance; never a cost, savings or automatic-default claim |

`required_capabilities` is not added: the existing harness contract and native
preflight remain authoritative. A selector/default equal to its parent primary
is valid when the complete typed route is present.

For `scoped-qualified`, deterministic validation requires evidence references;
the contract test requires each referenced path to exist, name the exact route
and record the observed applied selector and effort. Capability qualification
requires three fresh accepted trials for every pre-registered case/arm, primary
inspection/integration and cross-family review, with every attempt and its
coordination/rework overhead retained. `design-implementation` additionally
requires a fidelity result against settled `DESIGN_INTENT` and independent
cross-family QA. These structural checks do not assess trial quality; human
source/evidence review does.

The model-bindings doctor report lists each route, status and harness probe
result separately and retains the warning that model availability and applied
selection require native observation. It never changes status or describes a
candidate as qualified.

The existing assignment row gains `EXEC_MODE` (`primary-retained`,
`same-family-worker`, or `cross-family-primary`), `EXECUTION`
(`binding_id@effort` for primary execution or `route_id@effort` for worker
execution), `ROUTING_REASON`, `ROUTING_EVIDENCE`
(`requested=...; observed=...|unobserved; provenance=...`) and `USAGE`
(source, observation time, scope/bucket, remaining/reset when exposed, and
freshness). Requested-only evidence cannot support an applied-selection,
qualification or economy claim.

## Edge cases

- Reject unknown schema versions, fields, workload/status/effort values,
  duplicate route IDs/list values, unsafe IDs, empty required values, unsupported
  harness keys, missing defaults and invalid evidence paths.
- Reject `design-implementation` on a non-owner/non-Claude binding regardless of
  status. A candidate may exercise only controlled disposable qualification
  fixtures and must not claim or perform real product design; scoped evidence
  lacking observed application, repeated accepted fidelity results or independent
  cross-family QA is invalid. Deterministic design-policy cases are not rendered
  fidelity qualification.
- Do not reject a same-selector/same-effort route or imply it is cheaper. Do not
  infer that a configured or probed route was applied at runtime.
- Any failed acceptance criterion, missing exact route/selector/effort trace, or
  unresolved validity threat leaves the route candidate. Three fresh accepted
  trials per pre-registered case/arm can qualify only that workload; evidence and
  any status change land atomically in the same reviewed PR. A small successful
  cohort supports only this narrow scoped-quality claim. A separate economical-
  default or savings recommendation additionally requires measured all-in cost,
  capacity or time benefit, including every attempt and coordination/rework
  overhead; scoped qualification alone supplies no such claim.
- Scoped qualification covers only the declared harness/selector/effort/workload
  tuples actually evidenced. Before a status flip, narrow the route to tested
  tuples or evidence every claimed tuple; one harness/effort never qualifies
  another. Candidates may list multiple discrete efforts without claiming any
  tuple is qualified. This remains a prose/review constraint, not a new evidence
  database.
- Missing optional workers do not invalidate otherwise qualified standing
  primaries. Existing customized managed files retain the established update
  ownership behavior.
