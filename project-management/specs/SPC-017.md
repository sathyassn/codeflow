# SPC-017: The binding of a task whose record was planned on the line

## Summary

This spec pins one addition to the delivery system's structural binding rule (SPC-013 R-60): how a landed task binds when its record was added on the integration line after its reviewed head. TSK-1083 built it on the EPC-022 line (commit 8b5a8d220, 2026-11-03). That commit wrote the addition into SPC-013 itself; this spec carries it instead, and SPC-013's text is as `main` holds it.

### Why this spec does not supersede SPC-013

SPC-013 is implemented, so its text is frozen. Superseding it would reopen every completed SPC-013 consumer, because a completed task may not cite a superseded spec (SPC-013 R-32). That is right only when the contract they were built to changes, and here it does not: the rule gains one case and every earlier case is unchanged. SPC-016 adds to SPC-013 for the same reason. A reader of SPC-013 R-60 takes the text below as part of that requirement.

## Behavior

SPC-013 R-60, in the rule for a landing merge, says:

> A merge elsewhere is no landing, and the review must cover the combined result (TSK-340). Its second parent is the reviewed head or follows it only by status and Closeout changes to that task; its tree is the clean re-merge of its parents.

After that sentence it now adds:

> When the record is absent at the reviewed head and present at the landing merge, it was planned on the line: the record at the merge equals the record at the merge's first parent, the completed record differs from it only in status and Closeout, no path other than the record differs between the reviewed head and the second parent, and the reviewed commit stays the reviewed head (TSK-1083).

The rest of R-60 is as SPC-013 states it.

## Consumers

- TSK-1083 built this addition and is complete on the EPC-022 line. Its record is not changed to name this spec, so it names no consumer.
- SPC-013's consumers are unaffected: their contract is SPC-013, which is unchanged.
