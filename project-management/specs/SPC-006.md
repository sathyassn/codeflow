# SPC-006 — bounded design exploration, sourcing, and revision contract

## Summary

Pin the missing product-design behavior for exploration after direction
selection, trustworthy inspiration and asset sourcing, and traceable revision
feedback. This is a delta to the implemented SPC-003 and accepted ADR-0043,
not a replacement design method.

## Delta against current capability

**MODIFIED — CAP-013 product-design judgment.** Materially open work may
continue with bounded variants inside a selected direction, but only while a
named choice remains unresolved. Inspiration and assets carry enough provenance
and authority evidence to be safely used, and material feedback retains the
reviewed design version.

## Behavior

1. After an operator selects a direction, further variation is optional and
   bounded. It occurs only when an unresolved typography, color, imagery,
   interaction, composition, motion, or comparable choice would materially
   change the accepted outcome. The flow presents meaningful alternatives and
   a recommendation; it does not manufacture a universal tweak panel or keep
   generating after the choice is settled.
2. Inspiration reduces design uncertainty but is not evidence of audience
   needs, preferences, or product facts. Material references record their
   source and the qualities being learned from without copying protected
   expression or treating public availability as permission to reuse.
3. A sourced, commissioned, or generated asset records provenance proportionate
   to its risk: origin/provider, rights or consent basis, generation or material
   transformations, applicable restrictions, and the product use it was
   verified in. Unknown rights or sensitive subjects are surfaced, not assumed.
4. Private material, personal data, secrets, or confidential product content is
   never sent to an external inspiration, asset, or generation service without
   explicit authority for that service and use. A provider name does not imply
   availability, safety, approval, or a stable recommendation.
5. The handoff identifies the smallest product foundations justified by the
   settled direction and expected lifetime. It reuses established systems and
   names only applicable tokens/modes, primitives, recurring components,
   states, responsive/platform behavior, accessibility, and
   internationalization/localization obligations. It does not create an
   abstraction merely to make the handoff look complete.
6. A material revision records the exact version reviewed, the feedback and its
   authority, accepted and rejected rationale, and the new approved Plan vN+1.
   Normal project work records remain authoritative; no design database or
   parallel decision log is introduced.

## Interfaces and formats

- Add short generation-time behavior to `cf-design` and route detailed sourcing
  judgment to one progressive-disclosure reference.
- Reuse `DESIGN_INTENT.RESEARCH_OR_REFERENCES` for provenance. At most one
  optional, collapsible asset-sourcing line may be added when it materially
  steers implementation or verification; no new mandatory field set is created.
- Concrete services and model releases, when a consuming project chooses to
  record them, belong to project-owned qualified capability/configuration data
  rather than durable doctrine.
- Extend existing quality/evaluation contracts only for shared verification
  invariants. Existing design-system, accessibility, proportionality, and
  fidelity text remains in its canonical home.

## Edge cases

- A local conformance change collapses without variations, sourcing research,
  or a revision ledger.
- A reference board cannot be cited as proof of user demand or preference.
- An asset with an unknown license or sensitive subject is not silently shipped.
- Private input is refused for external generation until the operator grants
  explicit authority; a local or already-approved path may be proposed.
- A project with an established component system extends it rather than
  generating a parallel foundation.
- If the settled `cf-present` block catalog cannot express a comparison, changing
  that utility is a separately approved plan mutation, not TSK-012 scope.
