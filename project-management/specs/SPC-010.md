# SPC-010: composed portal views from frontmatter and the doc graph

## Summary

Withdrawn to draft on 2026-09-20 by Plan v7 and ADR-0064: records are pointed to as
folders, not composed into portal views. Kept for the record.

Pin the data contract and the rendering contract for the composed portal views
that replace re-rendered Markdown on the decisions landing, the records
landing, epic pages, task pages, ADR pages and the layer landings. The
authoritative living sources are the page-class table and the altitude grammar
in `.claude/skills/cf-docs-portal/resources/utility-presentation-system.md`
section 3 and the stale and twin rules in
`.claude/skills/cf-docs-portal/references/content-contract.md`; this spec does
not restate them, it states what the adapter may read and what it must emit.
The governing constraint is that the portal is a presentation of the
repository, never a second authority: every rendered field traces to
frontmatter or to one of the two bounded extracts named below.

## Delta against current capability

ADDED. The adapter gains two bounded body extracts and a composed view per
record class, each following the existing altitude trio grammar.

MODIFIED. The records landing, the system landing and the layer landings move
from a flat link list and a crude table to composed figures with declared
limits and table fallbacks. Epic, task and ADR pages gain a composed opening at
each altitude; ADR body text is unchanged.

REMOVED. Nothing. No source loses a route, an id or its Markdown twin.

## Behavior

### Inputs

The adapter reads only what it already parses today:

- Frontmatter scalars: `id`, `title`, `status`, `date`, `epic_id`.
- Frontmatter relationship lists: `specs`, `depends_on`, `capabilities`,
  `adrs`, `related`, `superseded_by`.
- The `capabilities` YAML fences, for the generated capabilities table.
- The derived index: `ownerById` and the backlinks the adapter already builds
  (`adapter.mjs` around lines 299 to 316).

Two new bounded extracts are added, and nothing else:

1. Named H2 first sentence. Given a heading name, return the first sentence of
   the first paragraph under that depth-2 heading, neutralized, truncated at
   240 characters on a word boundary, empty when the heading is absent.
2. Acceptance criteria checklist. Under a depth-2 heading named exactly
   `Acceptance Criteria`, return each top-level checkbox item as a pair of
   checked state and neutralized single-line text.

The Rust `validate --docs` workgraph is not imported. Extract helpers live in
`adapter.mjs`; no second parser is introduced.

### Altitude grammar for every composed view

Every composed page follows the doctrine's altitude grammar rather than one
figure per page. Where the adapter already turns the depth-2 headings Concept,
Architecture and Technical into a real tablist (`lib.mjs` around lines 258 to
268), the composed pages emit the same tablist, so the reader navigates one way
everywhere. Supporting text is allowed at each level as framing sentences only;
no prose goes inside a box.

Each view states, per altitude, who is reading, the question they bring, the
smallest carrier that answers it, and what stays reachable one level down.

Decisions landing.

| Altitude | Reader and question | Carrier | One click below |
|---|---|---|---|
| Concept | A maintainer or newcomer asking what has been decided recently and what it replaced | A timeline of decisions with supersession edges | The status groups and the table |
| Architecture | A maintainer asking which decisions still stand and what they change | Decisions grouped by status with supersession chains and impact edges | The table |
| Technical | A reader looking up one decision | A sortable table of id, title, date, status, superseded by and impact, with links | The ADR page |

Records landing and epic page.

| Altitude | Reader and question | Carrier | One click below |
|---|---|---|---|
| Concept | A contributor asking what the body of work is and what is blocked | The task dependency graph with status in node style | The per task detail |
| Architecture | A planner asking how one task sits in the graph | The same graph expanded per task with in edges, out edges and integration target | The criteria tables |
| Technical | A reviewer looking up criteria and evidence | The criteria and evidence tables | The task page |

Task page.

| Altitude | Reader and question | Carrier | One click below |
|---|---|---|---|
| Concept | A contributor asking where this task sits and what it waits on | The task's node and its edges | The execution contract |
| Architecture | A reviewer asking who is accountable and who reviews | The execution contract as a small flow of responsible primary, executor and reviewer | The criteria table |
| Technical | A reviewer checking acceptance | The criteria checklist table and the closeout evidence | The linked evidence |

ADR page.

| Altitude | Reader and question | Carrier | One click below |
|---|---|---|---|
| Concept | A newcomer asking what was decided and why | The derived header panels with the first sentences of Context, Decision and Consequences | The edges figure |
| Architecture | A maintainer asking what this decision replaced and what it changes | The supersession and impact edges as a small figure | The full text |
| Technical | A reader needing the decision of record | The unchanged append-only text | The linked sources |

Layer landing and home.

| Altitude | Reader and question | Carrier | One click below |
|---|---|---|---|
| Concept | A newcomer asking what this layer covers and where to start | The layer description and its reading-order figure | The page structure |
| Architecture | A reader asking how much is here and how it is grouped | The pages of the layer as a structure with counts | The index |
| Technical | A reader looking for one page | The searchable index | The page |

The altitude tablist is not reused as a reinterpretation of an ADR's own
section names: Context, Decision and Consequences supply panel contents inside
the Concept level, and the ADR's append-only text is reproduced unchanged at
the Technical level.

### Outputs and figure grammar

Four figure kinds carry all views, and no other syntax is emitted:

- Timeline. Decisions ordered by `date`, grouped by `status`, with a
  supersession edge drawn from the superseded record to its successor.
- Dependency graph. Nodes from records, edges from `depends_on`, status carried
  in node style rather than in a label, laid out in stages.
- Layer figure. The pages of a layer in reading order with counts.
- Decision flow. A short left to right flow of named roles, used for the
  execution contract.

Meaning sits in geometry, time, edges and node state; text is labels only.

### Limits and fallbacks

- A dependency graph renders as a graph only while it has at most six stages
  and at most five nodes per stage. Beyond either limit the same data renders
  as a table with the identical rows, and the page says which form it is
  showing. EPC-014 itself exceeds the limits and is the fixture for the
  fallback.
- A timeline with no supersession edge renders as the plain ordered list of
  decisions.
- A layer with no pages renders the empty statement the adapter already emits.

### Stale sources, twins and llms.txt

Stale behaviour is unchanged and follows `content-contract.md`: a stale source
renders its error stub, its ids and relationships are cleared, and it is
excluded from search, previews, `llms.txt`, maps and boards, while its siblings
continue and no ancestor recovery is attempted. A stale record therefore
contributes no node, no edge and no table row to any composed view. The
Markdown twin remains the rendered page with the source body preserved
(`adapter.mjs` around line 162) and carries no claim that is not on the page.
`llms.txt` lists only non-stale source routes. The evidence schema stays at
version 1 and gains no field.

### Negative case

A record page that ships as Markdown re-rendered under the portal shell, with
no composed carrier, fails the TSK-042 gate and is named by the failure.

## Interfaces and formats

Two helper signatures in `adapter.mjs`:

- `firstSentenceOfSection(body, headingName)` returns a string, empty when the
  heading is absent.
- `acceptanceChecklist(body)` returns an array of `{ checked, text }`.

Composed views are emitted as the carriers the doctrine already lists: the
`cf-stage` grammar, tables, and full-width `text` fences. No tree syntax and no
arbitrary diagram syntax is introduced on either surface. The generated
capabilities table is built from the `capabilities` YAML fences and from
`docs/capabilities/CAP-*.md` once TSK-039 lands; TSK-039's hand written table
is the fallback until then.

## Edge cases

- A `depends_on` target that does not resolve in `ownerById` is dropped from
  the graph and reported by the adapter, never rendered as a dangling node.
- A dependency cycle renders as the table fallback rather than as a graph.
- An ADR with no Context, Decision or Consequences heading renders the header
  panels it can fill and omits the rest; it never invents a sentence.
- An acceptance criteria list with nested items keeps only the top level.
- A record with `status` absent renders the neutral node style, not a guessed
  status.
- An epic with a single task still renders the graph rather than a special
  case.
- Every extracted string is neutralized before it reaches the page, so a source
  cannot inject markup through a first sentence or a criterion.
