Bands that failed, and cannot be observed

An independent Claude design-primary audit of the rendered board recorded these. Each entry says two things. First, the render is inadmissible as comparison evidence — it does not carry what its page contains, so no observation gate may be recorded from it. Second, the failure that made it inadmissible, with its severity. Inadmissible is not neutral and does not mean unjudged: every band here is inadmissible because something failed. What none of them claims is a rubric gate result, because the audit read the answer key — but a measured clip and two marks in one position do not need a blind observer to be true. Nothing was redrawn: the seat that judges a board does not repair the drawings it is judging. See registry.invalidated_bands and rubric.v3.md.
