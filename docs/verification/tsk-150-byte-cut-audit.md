## The audit, as returned

Many cuts are sound progressive loading or duplication removal. Historical losses, intentional policy amendments, and repaired losses are identified separately; a historical LOST row is not a claim that the same defect still exists at every branch tip. No blanket restoration of retired permission defaults or vestigial metadata is recommended.
