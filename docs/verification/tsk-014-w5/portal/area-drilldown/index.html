Monorepo — enter an area by what it owns

In a monorepo, a reader needs the whole system's shared boundaries before an area, and then needs to enter an area by what it owns rather than by which folder it happens to sit in.

This one is a declared fixture, and that is stated here rather than buried. CodeFlow is a single project, so a monorepo family cannot be demonstrated against its real content graph without inventing one — and inventing one quietly is the exact failure this task exists to correct. The fixture below is named as a fixture on the page, in registry.json, and inside the figure's own accessible description. Every other family on this board renders from real repository facts.

The family

Areas are rows; shared contracts are columns. An area that owns a contract, an area that consumes one, and an area consuming an old version mid-migration are three different marks. A contract column with no owner is drawn with a broken head — the one thing a folder-per-package navigation dump can never show, because an unowned contract has no folder to put it in.

Emitted by portal:area-drilldown. The repository-wide band is drawn as the span it is, above every area, because the information-architecture contract already says global orientation sits above area drill-down and a sidebar cannot express "above".
