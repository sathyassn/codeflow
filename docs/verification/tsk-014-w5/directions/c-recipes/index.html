TSK-014 W5 · contract altitude · direction C

Project-neutral recipe library

The runtime executes the same primitives as direction A. What moves is where composition authority sits: a versioned library of named, parameterised recipes ships as a managed skill asset. A document names a recipe and binds real data to its declared slots.

Adding a new subject form becomes a reviewed managed asset — no binary release, no schema version, no renderer change — but still a review, not a document. Every figure below is emitted at load by recipes.js resolving a named recipe into a primitive call.

What it cannot express

The record-authority subject has no recipe. The refusal names the library, its versions, and the path a new form actually takes — which is the difference this direction is asking the operator to buy.

A managed asset review is materially cheaper than a binary release and materially slower than writing a document. Whether that is the right place for the boundary is the decision.
