One policy document, five planes — and only one of them is a boundary here

Rows are the rules that can refuse you. Columns are the planes that read the same .codeflow/policy.json. The heavy division is not decoration: everything left of it is fast feedback you could work around; everything right of it is the perimeter. On this repository one of the two perimeter planes is not armed at all, which is why the column is struck out rather than omitted.
■ the plane enforces that rule. · it does not. A mark means this plane refuses it — not that the rule is optional elsewhere.
The division shows where a refusal can be avoided and where it cannot. It says nothing about severity: every rule here blocks in the plane that owns it.
Hatched means the plane exists in the design and is not armed on this repository. It is drawn so its absence is visible rather than silently missing.
refuses n of 9 counts the preconditions of starting a task that this rule alone would stop. The gate of record is the largest.
