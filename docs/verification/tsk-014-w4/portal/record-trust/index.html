Record — a trust surface, not a rendered file

A record page has to let a reader trust or distrust the record in front of them: which accepted decision still governs it, how much of it that decision actually reaches, and which of its claims has nothing behind it.

What the shipped portal does today

The built portal renders SPC-004 as its Markdown, a source-and-commit provenance line, and a "Record context" section listing referenced records and inverse links. Every link is correct. Neither question above is answerable from the page: partial reach collapses to a link, and a claim with no evidence looks exactly like a claim whose evidence is somewhere else.

The family

Claims in reading order. Each governing decision is a span down the left covering exactly the claims it reaches, thick where it governs and thin where it replaces only that claim's mechanism. Each claim keeps an evidence position at its own eye level, and a claim whose evidence does not exist keeps that position — drawn, at full height, and visibly unfilled.

Emitted by portal:record-trust from shared/record-authority.js, whose every frontmatter value, heading, numbered behaviour and quotation is checked against the real record. Resize: the evidence margin becomes a reserved band beneath each claim and keeps its full height when empty.
