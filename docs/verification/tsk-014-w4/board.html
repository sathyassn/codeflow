Two decisions, and the evidence for each

Nothing on this board is a selection. It exists so the direction can be settled by someone who is not a model, from artefacts that were rendered rather than described.

What this board is not. No composition here has been shown to communicate. The observation gates in rubric.v4.md need someone who authored nothing in this directory and has read no answer, and no such session has looked. Every green result below is a deterministic one: internally consistent, accessible to the automated checks, contained in its own frame. That is admissibility, not approval.

Decision 1 — what should carry a drawn explanation at all

W3 qualified eleven candidate compositions against the product's real technical contract and ten came back negative. That is not a result about compositions. The closed cf-present block catalogue cannot express the relationships this product's own subjects have: diagram cannot place a mark at a computed position or size anything to a quantity, and the html escape renders in a sandboxed frame whose inline CSS may not contain @ — so no media query, no prefers-color-scheme, no self-sizing.

Five content models are demonstrated below. Each renders real CodeFlow content through its own mechanism, at three widths in both modes, and each ships one subject it genuinely cannot carry. They differ on one axis: where composition authority sits, and therefore what a new subject form costs.

Decision 2 — what the documentation portal's pages should be

The shipped portal does not implement its accepted prototypes. It is a correct Starlight index over the repository's Markdown: a link list at /orient/, every ADR title in the sidebar in file order, and each record rendered as its own file with a provenance line. Three page families are demonstrated against the real content graph; three more are specified and not built, and say so.

The composition board, corrected

Fourteen W3 render bands failed. Every one carries a disposition, the W3 record is untouched, and each repaired band was re-rendered under one browser build. The two that needed a design decision rather than a repair had their narrow composition declared in registry.json before it was drawn — which is the specific discipline W3 broke.
