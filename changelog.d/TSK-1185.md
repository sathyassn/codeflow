### Fixed

<!-- codeflow:release-impact patch -->
- **A line sync orders a superseding task record by its ancestry.** A sync
  was refused with "none of them is newer than the others" when a planning
  edit's branch forked before a merge on the receiving line that only kept
  the older criteria. `codeflow ci` and the pre-push hook now order the two
  versions by which commits wrote them, on the fetched history, and read no
  branch name or date. A sync still refuses when both lines wrote their own
  criteria, when the merge saw the other version, or when it kept its version
  against something the other version was built on.
