### Fixed

<!-- codeflow:release-impact patch -->
- **A sync of `main` into a line keeps a home that `main` moved and the
  line already names.** The import check (`work.import_authority`) now
  follows every home move of an imported task on `main`'s trusted
  first-parent history since the line's merge base with `main`. It accepts
  a move made by a reviewed planning merge of records when the line already
  names the new home, or when a `todo` or `blocked` task's line copy is
  unchanged since that merge base. A later change on `main` no longer
  clears an earlier refused move. A record that newly names the line as its
  home must prove its history on `main`. A line's adoption of `main`'s
  version counts only through its own records-only planning merge of the
  exact bytes. The refusal names every refused record, not only the first.
