### Changed

<!-- codeflow:release-impact patch -->
- **Shipped guidance describes capacity admission, not one gate at a time.**
  The discipline rules, the worktree rules, the orchestrator and method
  skills, the Codex seat config and the harness posture doc no longer say that
  heavy work takes a gate lock, that only the primary runs gates, that a
  landing has priority over new builds, that a coordinator caps heavy builds or
  that lanes defer heavy runs. They describe the rule instead: every heavy run is admitted at once with a coordination slot and a share of the host's job budget (cores and free memory, or `execution.host_jobs`), a busy host gives a run a smaller job count and never makes it wait, and a target declared `host_exclusive: true` runs alone, so it and ordinary heavy runs can delay each other within the run's bound. Landing one candidate at a
  time on a line tip, and waiting for the tip's hosted verdicts before the
  next landing, stay, and neither stops building or testing in parallel.
  `codeflow update` carries the new text into adopters' rules and skills.
