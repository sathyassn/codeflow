### Changed

<!-- codeflow:release-impact patch -->
- **Shipped guidance describes capacity admission, not one gate at a time.**
  The discipline rules, the orchestrator and method skills, the Codex seat
  config and the docs no longer say that heavy work takes a gate lock, that
  only the primary runs gates, that a landing has priority over new builds, or
  that lanes defer heavy runs. They describe the rule instead: every heavy run
  takes one free slot of the host's capacity, a run with no free slot uses a
  smaller job count and never waits, and a target declared
  `host_exclusive: true` runs alone on the host. Landing one candidate at a
  time on a line tip, and waiting for the tip's hosted verdicts before the
  next landing, stay, and neither stops building or testing in parallel.
  `codeflow update` carries the new text into adopters' rules and skills.
