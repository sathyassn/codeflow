### Fixed

<!-- codeflow:release-impact patch -->
- **Qualification peers in a task worktree.** The repository's qualification
  runner refused every peer seat a subject opened outside the fixture root,
  so a subject that put its review seat in its task worktree, as the method
  prescribes, made the trial invalid. It now also accepts a registered linked
  worktree of the fixture at `.worktrees/<name>`, checked against the
  fixture's own git metadata and git's own listing, pinned per launch and
  rechecked before every key and on every poll; anything else still refuses.
  Under the default `--codex-hook-trust=review` a Codex peer in a new
  worktree still stops at its hook review, so launch such trials with
  `--codex-hook-trust=bypass`.
