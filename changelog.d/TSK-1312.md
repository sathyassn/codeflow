### Added

<!-- codeflow:release-impact minor -->
- **A sync of `main` into an integration line lands without a task of its
  own.** A pull request into a line that names the line's epic
  (`Task: EPC-NNN`) is admitted as a sync when every commit it brings
  beyond the line and `main` is a two-parent merge of a commit on `main`
  whose tree is the conflict-free merge of its parents, so its net diff may
  carry `main`'s release notes. `main`'s commit is the one `--policy-from`
  names, never a ref name. A commit of its own, a resolved conflict, a merge
  of anything not on `main` or a run with no `--policy-from` is refused,
  naming the cause. The frozen-criteria and record checks still apply, and a
  sync cannot change the line's own records.
