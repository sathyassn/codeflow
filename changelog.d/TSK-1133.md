### Added

<!-- codeflow:release-impact minor -->
- **Create capability records with `codeflow capability new`.** Capability
  numbers come from the shared registry when work tracking is enabled and
  remain reserved locally otherwise. Allocation counts legacy fences and
  folder records. Init, update, orient, status and doctor show inventory
  separately from declared evidence; status and recall read folder records.
  `codeflow ids retarget CAP-NNN` renumbers an unlanded capability record,
  preserving its uid and recording its former id.
