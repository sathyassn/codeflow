### Fixed

<!-- codeflow:release-impact patch -->
- **Two lines that share one planning merge push in either order.** The
  import check no longer refuses the first push of an integration line when
  both lines hold the same planning merge, for example after branching both
  lines from it or after two identical merges of the planning branch. Only a
  task record that merge introduced, still unchanged on the pushed line and
  carrying no completion or acceptance evidence, is exempt. A completion,
  evidence or criteria change that exists only on the other, unpublished
  line is still refused, even when the pushed line branched from the commit
  that landed it.
