### Fixed

<!-- codeflow:release-impact patch -->
- **Shared planning merges and planning moves of a task's home no longer
  read as foreign imports.** Two integration lines that hold the same
  planning merge can each make their first push, in either order, and a
  line whose reviewed planning merge moved a not-yet-complete task to
  another line passes `codeflow ci` into the default branch. Either
  exemption needs a clean merge on the checked line's own first-parent
  history that changes only plain record and plan files, and a task
  record that is `todo` or `blocked` with no acceptance block. A
  completion, evidence or criteria change that exists only on another,
  unpublished line, a move made through any other landing, and a move
  that reaches the line only through a merge's second parent are still
  refused.
