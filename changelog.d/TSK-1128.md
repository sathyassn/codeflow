### Fixed

<!-- codeflow:release-impact minor -->
- **A quick run under `codeflow reserve` uses the reserve's lock.** On
  Unix, `codeflow reserve -- codeflow test --mode quick` refused its own
  heavy targets as "another heavy gate", because the nested run could not
  tell that its parent held the lock. The reserve now hands its lock
  descriptors only to the same `codeflow` executable (by device and
  inode), which makes them close-on-exec and uses them only after the OS
  lock shows they are the holder's own; a value set any other way falls
  back to ordinary admission. A nested run's target groups stay on the
  holder's record while they run, so a surviving target keeps the slot
  busy. Full and essential gates under a reserve take a slot of their own
  (TSK-1145).
