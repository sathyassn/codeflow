### Fixed

<!-- codeflow:release-impact minor -->
- **A quick run under `codeflow reserve` uses the reserve's lock.** On
  Unix, `codeflow reserve -- codeflow test --mode quick` refused its own
  heavy targets as "another heavy gate", because the nested run could not
  tell that its parent held the lock. The reserve now hands its lock
  descriptors to a `codeflow` program, which makes them close-on-exec and
  uses them only after the OS lock shows they are the holder's own; a
  value set any other way falls back to ordinary admission. Full and
  essential gates under a reserve still refuse. `codeflow test
  --wait-seconds N` lets heavy quick targets wait up to N seconds for the
  lock, replacing `execution.heavy_wait_seconds` for that run.
