### Added

<!-- codeflow:release-impact minor -->
- **`codeflow status` reports progress for a task, an epic or a line.**
  `--task`, `--epic` (repeatable) and `--line` scope the view, and `--json`
  prints versioned facts (`report-v1.schema.json`, installed under
  `.codeflow/schemas/status/`): each task's stage and percent on a printed
  ladder, criteria verified against total, its location from `main` to a
  branch bound to the ref it was read from, the order of work with the
  critical path, the lanes carrying it, a window per item with its basis,
  the issues with their unblockers, the operator-owned set, and pace and
  intake over the last seven days. `--session` merges a coordinating
  session's facts, labelled `session`, and `--since` reports what changed
  since a previous snapshot. A fact it could not read says so rather than
  being dropped. The command writes nothing and never fetches; the text
  view with no flag is unchanged.
