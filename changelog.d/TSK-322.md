### Security

<!-- codeflow:release-impact minor -->
- **Blocked names and protected paths.** Pre-commit and `codeflow ci` now
  refuse secret-by-nature names at any depth by default: `.env`, `.env.*`,
  `*.env`, `*.pem`, `*.key`, `*.p12`, `*.pfx`, `*.jks`, `*.keystore`,
  `id_rsa*`, `id_dsa*`, `id_ecdsa*`, `id_ed25519*`, `.netrc`, `.pgpass`,
  `*.tfstate`, `*.tfstate.*`, `kubeconfig`, `.kube/config`,
  `.aws/credentials` and `application_default_credentials.json`.
  Projects tracking public certificates, example environment files or test
  keystores need a narrow, explained `git.blocked_names` `except_under`
  entry landed in the target policy before upgrading. Omitting a shipped
  name does not remove its denial. `git.protected_paths` refuses every
  change under a configured prefix, including deletion. Symlinks are
  checked without following them. CI checks every introduced commit and
  merge resolution, so adding then deleting a blocked name still refuses.
  Gate inputs remain editable, and deleting a blocked name passes with a
  reminder that its earlier content remains in history. `git.path_policy:
  off` disables only this gate; the existing secret scan stays separate.
