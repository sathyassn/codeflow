- **Inherited task criteria on diverged lines.** A task push can use its
  integration line's amended criteria when main still holds the criteria
  the line inherited and replaced, even if main edited the record's other
  text. Concurrent amendments and criteria restored by reverts,
  independent reintroductions or manual merge resolutions still compete,
  whatever text the restoring commit writes. History
  used to prove inheritance must be readable and complete, including older
  history below a recovered record. The check names the criteria authority
  and the held versions behind a refusal. Migration: none.
