### Fixed

<!-- codeflow:release-impact patch -->
- **One version parser for every pin check.** `codeflow update --pin` refuses
  a version that is not `MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH` with an optional prerelease and
  build, such as `3.2` or `3.2.0x`, before any download or write, and names the
  value. A hand-edited `scaffold_version` the parser rejects, such as `10.0`,
  is named by `update --pin` and by `codeflow doctor` instead of being ordered
  as text, even when both branches carry it or no target branch exists, and
  the behind-the-binary warning stays quiet for it. The `codeflow_older`
  function in the generic, GitLab and Bitbucket CI templates now orders pins
  as codeflow does (`rc.2` before `rc.10`, build metadata ignored, no
  overflow on large numbers) and refuses a version that is not
  `MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH` with an optional prerelease and build.
