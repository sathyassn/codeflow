### Added

<!-- codeflow:release-impact minor -->
- **`validate --docs` reads a capability catalog kept as one record per
  capability.** Records live as `docs/capabilities/CAP-NNN-<slug>.md`
  beside the legacy `docs/capabilities.md`, with areas from
  `.codeflow/project.toml` and a `docs/capabilities/vocabulary.yaml`. A
  checkout and a Git tree give the same records. The loader refuses unknown
  keys, duplicate ids and uids, values over 1024 characters, lists over 64
  items, more than 32 keys, inputs over 256 KiB, symlinks, executable
  record files and paths outside the folder, naming the file, and reports
  every other record's issues as well. YAML anchors and aliases cannot
  expand past these bounds. The schema and its limits are in
  `docs/capabilities.md`. A repository without the folder validates as
  before.
