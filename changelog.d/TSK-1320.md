- **A reviewed completion survives a landed planning move of its epic.**
  When a reviewed task branch cleanly merges its target after the review
  and that merge brings a planning pull request's move of the task to
  another epic, the acceptance binding now keeps the completion instead of
  asking for a fresh review. The one admitted edit is the `epic_id` line
  and plain lines added outside the front matter, the criteria, the
  acceptance blocks, the Deliverables and the Closeout, made by exactly one
  commit on the first-parent line of the target tip the run is judged
  against, with the new epic landing on the task's unchanged
  `integration_target`. Every other change, and any history the check
  cannot read, still refuses, and the refusal names the commit and the
  part that changed.
