### Fixed

<!-- codeflow:release-impact patch -->
- **The guards refuse what they cannot finish reading.** The reader that
  judges shell deletions stops at fixed limits on nesting, values and
  combinations. Reaching a limit could leave part of a command unjudged, and
  an unjudged part counted as nothing found. A stop now gives a refusal under
  `security.dangerous_commands`, which no policy setting lowers, and the
  message says to run the inner command directly with less nesting. A command
  nested past what the reader can follow is refused, whatever it does, and
  deeply nested text now gives a refusal where it could crash the guard. The
  shell startup guard likewise refuses a path behind more symbolic links than
  it follows, and a `find` that hands paths to an interpreter is refused when
  it can reach remote-tracking refs past what the guard lists.
