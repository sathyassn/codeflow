### Fixed

<!-- codeflow:release-impact patch -->
- **The dependency audit fails when osv-scanner reports a file it could not
  extract.** In the shipped CI workflow's security review job, a scan that
  reports an extraction error, or whose error output the step cannot read,
  now fails the job when either policy key is `block` and warns when they
  are `warn`, whatever the size of the scanner's output; before, that
  decision did not always hold. The gitleaks step decides whether its scan
  completed in the same way. Projects take the fix with `codeflow update`.
