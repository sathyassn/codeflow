### Added

<!-- codeflow:release-impact minor -->
- **The coordination timers are project settings.**
  `coord.check_interval_seconds` (default 600) and
  `coord.heartbeat_timeout_seconds` (default 1800) are read from the project's
  policy. An absent key keeps the default, and a value below 1 or of the wrong
  type refuses, naming the key and its bounds.
