### Changed

<!-- codeflow:release-impact minor -->
- **Claude sessions compact at 35 percent of the 1M window (issue 358).**
  The shared `.claude/settings.json` now sets
  `CLAUDE_AUTOCOMPACT_PCT_OVERRIDE` to 35 in place of 50, so a session on a
  1M-window model compacts near 350K tokens and a session limited to 200K
  near 70K. `codeflow update` moves a project still at the shipped 50, as
  its recorded baseline shows, to 35, and keeps a value the project chose
  and a person's own value in `.claude/settings.local.json`. The
  `cf-customize` context policy now states the tradeoff: per-turn input
  grows with the context carried, a lower point compacts more often, the
  point matters most for long orchestrator sessions, and the orient digest
  restores task, branch and next step after a compaction. The value is
  revisited once the adopter's measurement is posted on issue 358.
