### Fixed

<!-- codeflow:release-impact patch -->
- **Research pins survive a deleted line.** A research or decision
  dependency stays met after the producer's integration branch is deleted:
  `work next`, `work claim` and `work start` accept a pin that holds the
  producer's completed record when it is on the producer's target, the
  consumer's target or the default branch. Each target is read at its
  remote-tracking ref, such as `origin/main`, so a local `main` that is
  behind no longer refuses a landed pin, and one that is ahead no longer
  proves a landing. The local branch is read only where it has no
  remote-tracking ref. A missing object, an open or absent record at the pin
  and a pin on no target still refuse, and the refusal names the refs it
  read (#236).
