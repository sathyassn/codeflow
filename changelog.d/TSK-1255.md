### Changed

<!-- codeflow:release-impact minor -->
- **Present and portal pages are held to concrete copy limits.** The copy
  guide's new "Page limits" section sets them: at most two lines per text
  block outside a disclosure, one point per bullet, a figure as the main
  view of any flow, comparison or status, a unit on every number and names
  before identifiers. The design-system kit, `cf-present`,
  `cf-docs-portal` and `cf-editorial-review` point at it, and the writing
  rules add names before identifiers, whole context, times with a zone and
  entity views that open with what, for whom and why now.
- **`codeflow present check` warns on long blocks, cut context and bare
  times.** `copy-density` names a visible text block over two lines at the
  68-character reading measure, `copy-ellipsis` names text cut to "..." or
  an ellipsis, and `copy-time-zone` names a clock time without a zone. Code
  blocks and code spans are exempt, and collapsed detail is exempt from the
  length limit. They are warnings: the check still exits 0 on them, and
  `open` and `update` print them and proceed. `codeflow update` refreshes
  the skills and rules.
