### Changed

<!-- codeflow:release-impact minor -->
- **Present and portal pages are held to concrete copy limits.** The copy
  guide's new "Page limits" section sets them: at most 136 characters per
  text block outside a disclosure (about two desktop lines), at most three
  text blocks in a row, one point per bullet, a figure as the main view of
  any flow, comparison or status, a unit on every number and names before
  identifiers. The design-system kit, `cf-present`, `cf-docs-portal` and
  `cf-editorial-review` point at it, and the writing rules add names before
  identifiers, whole context, times with a zone and entity views that open
  with what, for whom and why now.

<!-- codeflow:release-impact minor -->
- **`codeflow present check` and `codeflow validate --portal` warn on long
  blocks, text walls, cut context and bare times.** `copy-density` names a
  text block over the 136-character budget or an HTML stage with more than
  320 characters of text, `copy-run` names more than three text blocks in a
  row, `copy-ellipsis` names text that may be cut to "...", and
  `copy-time-zone` names a clock time or range without a zone. Code,
  quotations and collapsed detail are exempt where the copy guide says so.
  They are warnings: both commands still exit as before, and `open` and
  `update` print them and proceed. `validate --portal` prints one line a
  page and a total, skips generated lookup pages, and lists each block with
  `--copy-detail`. `codeflow update` refreshes the skills
  and rules.
