### Fixed

<!-- codeflow:release-impact patch -->
- **A recursive copy or sync is judged by what it would write.** git-guard
  and exec-guard now read a recursive `cp`, `scp -r`, `rsync` or `ditto` by
  the files it would create, replace or delete under its destination, and
  refuse one that reaches an enforcement file or a checkout's `.git`,
  naming the file and its source. `rsync --del` and every `--delete`
  option are read as deleting. A source the guard cannot read, such as a
  missing, remote or run-time one, is refused when its destination holds
  enforcement files. Copies into ordinary folders, copies to a folder
  outside every checkout and dry runs pass as before. Migration: a copy
  that carries `.claude`, `.codeflow` or `.git` into a checkout now
  refuses; copy the files you need by name instead.
