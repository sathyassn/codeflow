### Added

<!-- codeflow:release-impact minor -->
- **Records state their Outcome, and epics their Deliverables.** The task
  and epic templates gain an `## Outcome` section for one line saying what
  someone can do once the work lands ("after this merges, <who> can <do
  what>"), and the epic template gains `## Deliverables`. `codeflow update`
  writes an adoption marker, `outcome_present_uids` in
  `.codeflow/project.toml`, once, listing the uid of every task and epic
  record present then; a fresh `codeflow init` writes it empty. From then
  `codeflow validate --docs` refuses an open task or epic created later
  without a filled Outcome, and warns for a listed record, which gains the
  section when next amended. Complete, cancelled and archived records are
  never flagged, and no existing record is edited. Without the marker every
  missing section only warns, and `codeflow doctor --check config` says
  `codeflow update` writes it. An open epic created after adoption warns
  when its Deliverables name nothing.
