### Fixed

<!-- codeflow:release-impact patch -->
- **`codeflow validate --docs` reads each record fewer times on large
  projects.** The check for coordination ledger ids (`FU-`, `LN-`, `WS-`)
  now runs on the bytes the ADR, epic and task checks already read, instead
  of listing and reading every record again; the record checks read and
  parse each record once for its identity and its validation; and record
  lists are sorted by a key computed once per path. Every check still runs,
  and the findings, their order and the exit code are unchanged.
