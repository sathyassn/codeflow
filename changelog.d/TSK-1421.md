### Added

<!-- codeflow:release-impact minor -->
- **A project can require extra sections in its task, epic and spec
  records.** The new policy keys `git.task_required_sections`,
  `git.epic_required_sections` and `git.spec_required_sections` each list
  heading names. `codeflow validate --docs`, `codeflow work start` and
  `codeflow ci` (and so the pre-push hook) refuse a new or changed record of
  that kind without one of them as its own `##` heading line, and
  `codeflow task new`, `epic new` and `spec new` write them into new
  records. CodeFlow's own required sections stay required whatever the
  lists say. The lists are read from the trusted policy (the range's base in
  `codeflow ci`, the landed policy elsewhere) joined with the working copy's,
  so a change cannot drop a section it is judged by. A record unchanged
  since that trusted commit gets a warning until it next changes. With no
  keys set, records are judged as before.
