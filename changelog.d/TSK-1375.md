### Fixed

<!-- codeflow:release-impact patch -->
- **The guards judge every reading of text a shell may read more than one
  way.** A double-parenthesis command may be read as arithmetic or as nested
  subshells, and an arithmetic expansion whose text is not arithmetic may be
  read as a command substitution. The reader that judges shell deletions
  took only the arithmetic reading, so a deletion inside an ambiguous
  arithmetic form was not judged. It now builds each reading, judges each
  one, and refuses under `security.dangerous_commands` when any reading
  deletes a protected location. Real arithmetic, such as counters and
  comparisons, stays allowed.
