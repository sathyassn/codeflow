### Changed

<!-- codeflow:release-impact minor -->
- **Heavy test runs share the host instead of waiting for one lock.** Full
  and essential gates, heavy quick targets and `codeflow reserve` each take
  a free slot in the lock folders, so gates in several worktrees and
  projects run at once, and quick and pre-push runs never wait for or skip
  their heavy targets because another run is active. The host has a job
  budget, its cores lowered to its available memory at 1 GiB per job, or
  the new `execution.host_jobs`; a run that finds more runs active than the
  budget holds starts at once with a smaller job count. Only a target that
  declares the new `host_exclusive: true` runs alone on the host, and
  `execution.heavy_wait_seconds` and `--wait-seconds` now bound the wait for
  such a target; the waiter queue that TSK-1128 added is gone, since no
  heavy run waits for another. Lock folders stay fail closed, and
  a quick run under `codeflow reserve` still uses the reserve's slot.
