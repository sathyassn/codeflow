### Changed

<!-- codeflow:release-impact minor -->
- **A planning landing may carry its decision documents.** A reviewed
  planning merge that adds or edits ADRs next to its task records and plans
  still counts as shared planning provenance and as a planning move of a
  task's home, so the lines that take it pass `work.import_authority` as
  they would for records and plans alone. A decision document is a regular,
  non-executable file named `docs/decisions/ADR-NNNN.md` or
  `docs/decisions/ADR-NNNN-<slug>.md`, four or more digits, directly in
  `docs/decisions/`; that layout is built into `codeflow`, and no policy
  value can widen it. Any other file in the landing, such as other
  documentation, `docs/decisions/README.md` or `template.md`, a document
  index, `AGENTS.md`, instructions, hooks, policy or CI, still keeps the
  landing out of both answers, and the import is judged as before.
