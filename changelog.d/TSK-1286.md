### Added

<!-- codeflow:release-impact minor -->
- **An epic can declare the integration line it lands on.** An epic record
  may set `parent_line: integration/EPC-NNN-<slug>`, the exact name of
  another epic's line. A task of that epic then targets the line as a task
  of the line's own epic does, in `codeflow ci`, pre-push and
  `codeflow work start`, and the line stays bound when every task on it
  belongs to a declaring epic. The declaration counts only from the
  trusted records the binding already reads (the protected branch or the
  line's `origin/<line>` tip): a `parent_line` added in the head binds
  nothing, and a head whose copy of the epic changes or removes it is
  refused, so the field is changed through a planning pull request into
  the line. A declaration that names a missing epic, another line, or a
  cancelled or archived epic binds nothing, and a task record that carries
  the field is refused. One level only: when the line's own epic itself
  declares a `parent_line`, a declaring epic's task is refused, and once
  the line's trusted records hold such a task the whole line refuses, its
  own epic's tasks and its landing included, naming the cause.
  `codeflow validate --docs` checks that the field is
  an exact epic line name, not the epic's own line, naming an epic that
  exists, and that no task record carries it.
