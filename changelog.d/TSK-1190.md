- **Target commands receive comparison inputs and can report skipped work.**
  Commands receive comparison commits, a per-run changed-paths file
  and the landing line in their environment. Exit 77 reports a skipped
  target and leaves the gate failed and incomplete, without pass or reuse
  credit. See the [target-command contract](docs/test-gate.md#inputs-and-exit-codes-for-target-commands).
