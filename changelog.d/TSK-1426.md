### Fixed

<!-- codeflow:release-impact patch -->
- **The edit guard maps NT namespace, verbatim and device paths on Windows,
  and refuses drive-relative ones.** NT namespace (`\??\`), verbatim
  (`\\?\`) and device (`\\.\`) paths are mapped by their prefix to the
  drive or network share they name, and NT namespace paths no longer get
  an exception. The guard refuses a drive-relative edit path such as
  `C:name`, mixed or incomplete prefixes, `/` inside a verbatim path,
  device roots and paths that climb out of a device, and a network share
  outside the folders the session chose. A verbatim path with a trailing
  dot is still refused, and shell startup files are checked on the
  resolved target.
