### Fixed

<!-- codeflow:release-impact patch -->
- **A killed gate keeps older gates out until its whole process tree has
  ended.** On Windows the OS freed a killed gate's hold on
  `full-gate.lock` while its targets were still ending, so an older
  CodeFlow gate could start beside them and share a target folder. While a
  run holds `full-gate.lock`, a watcher process outside each target's job
  object now holds it too. When the gate ends, the watcher ends the job, and
  it releases the lock only when the job reports no process left. A target
  whose watcher cannot start does not run. On Unix the watcher of a heavy
  quick target or a `codeflow reserve` command no longer ends with its own
  signal: it keeps the run's locks until the command's whole process group
  is gone, or until every process left in it is confirmed to be a zombie.
