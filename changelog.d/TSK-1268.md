### Changed

<!-- codeflow:release-impact minor -->
- **Ready pull requests land together on one candidate per line.** When a
  line lands, every pull request reviewed and ready at that moment goes on
  one candidate in dependency order, with one full gate; a pull request
  that becomes ready later joins the next candidate and is never held
  behind another pull request's landing. Each line has one candidate at a
  time, and lines land in parallel. A new always rule says every change
  that goes into a repository is part of a task, made by the session that
  owns that work, and that a session seeing a needed change in work it
  does not own tells the owner. `codeflow update` rewrites the managed
  block, rules and skills.
