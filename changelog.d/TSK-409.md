### Security

<!-- codeflow:release-impact patch -->
- **The docs portal starter takes the fixed `vite` release.** Three advisories
  in `launch-editor`, which `vite` 8.2.0 bundles, affect `vite` 8.2.0 through
  8.2.3 and are fixed in 8.2.4: GHSA-587h-7wmj-h5pm (CVSS 7.5),
  GHSA-698v-4r3x-3vcv (6.3) and GHSA-r825-w8mm-5pxw (6.3). They failed a
  blocking dependency audit in a project with the portal. The starter's
  `package.json` now pins `vite` to 8.2.4, and its lockfile moves `vite`,
  `rolldown`, `postcss` and their packages to the matching releases.
  `codeflow update` installs both files; the portal's recorded runtime
  scripts are unchanged.
