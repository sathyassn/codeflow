### Changed

<!-- codeflow:release-impact major -->
- **Dependency audit exceptions require validated triage.** The base
  commit's `.codeflow/audit-exceptions.sh` now prints one JSON object per
  line with `id`, `package`, `ecosystem`, `owner`, `rationale` and `expiry`.
  High or critical findings also require `reachability: "not-reachable"`
  and `evidence`. Replace the old bare tab-separated output with these
  entries before adopting the workflow. File entries in
  `.codeflow/audit-triage.json` use the same validator. Invalid entries
  fail closed, including at warn. An expired entry fails while its advisory
  appears in the completed head scan; otherwise a notice names the entry
  and expiry. A missing or unparseable scan never proves absence. Renewing
  an expired entry while its advisory remains live still fails because
  triage comes from base: recovery requires an operator merge of the renewal
  over the failing check. Alternatively, remove the vulnerable dependency
  and obtain a complete scan that no longer reports it. A hook can no longer waive a
  finding without ownership, expiry or reachability evidence.
<!-- codeflow:release-impact major -->
- **The audit compares the base and head scans.** New advisories fail at
  every severity. Existing high or critical advisories need trusted
  not-reachable triage, medium advisories need trusted triage, and low or
  informational advisories are reported. Trusted unexpired suppressions
  still apply first. Each `osv-scanner.toml` suppression now requires a
  nonempty `reason` and valid `ignoreUntil`, formerly optional. Add both
  fields on the base before adopting the workflow; missing or malformed
  fields fail the job at warn and block. There is no dev-only exemption. At warn, failing
  advisory rows warn; scan errors always fail. Long scanner diagnostics
  cannot turn an error into a pass under pipefail.
  Scanner version and checksum come from the base workflow. Both scans use
  the same configuration and isolated committed dependency inputs; head
  scripts, executables and scanner configuration are excluded. Unsupported
  custom input names need a reviewed input-list update before adoption.
<!-- codeflow:release-impact major -->
- **Style defaults warn.** Description length, body shape and unlisted
  non-AI trailers, policy characters and PR Summary shape are advisory.
  Commit type, breaking-change syntax, subject length, attribution, emoji,
  branch names, required PR sections and the Task line keep their checks.
  Required footers such as DCO keep a separate blocking default under
  `git.commit_footer_required`, even when body style is warn or off.
  Projects with explicit blocking style levels keep them until changed.
<!-- codeflow:release-impact minor -->
- **Policy reports omit unused controls.** Older files still load with a
  notice for each retained unused key. `codeflow policy` prints effective
  values and their sources without needing the `show` subcommand.
