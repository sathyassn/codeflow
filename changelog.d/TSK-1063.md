### Changed

<!-- codeflow:release-impact patch -->
- **Windows test partitions in CodeFlow's own CI.** CodeFlow's own Windows CI
  now spreads tests evenly across its 12 partitions, from refreshed
  measurements, so no partition nears its 30 minute limit. Tests the
  measurements do not cover are spread over the partitions, a check fails
  before the partitions run when the measurements have drifted from the
  suite, and a partition that ends without its report is named as not
  finished instead of reported as failing tests. This repository's CI scripts
  and data only; the `codeflow` binary and the shipped scaffold are unchanged.
