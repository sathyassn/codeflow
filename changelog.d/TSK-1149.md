### Fixed

<!-- codeflow:release-impact patch -->
- **A task branch can push its own line sync before review.** The
  pre-push hook no longer refuses a task branch whose merge of its own
  target line needed hand-resolved conflicts. Pushing keeps the work
  durable; `codeflow ci` at ready and at landing still refuses the
  hand-resolved hunks until a review of the head is recorded. A merge of
  anything the line does not hold, a branch that carries no task, a
  protected branch and the line's own push keep the refusal.
