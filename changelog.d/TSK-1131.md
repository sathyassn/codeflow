### Changed

<!-- codeflow:release-impact minor -->
- **Sessions start at each model's recommended effort.** The shipped model
  catalog no longer binds the host, the planning primaries, the executor and
  the reviewers at high. Each seat starts at its model's vendor-recommended
  default (medium for Opus, Sonnet and the Codex lines; high for Fable and
  Grok) and rises to high only on a trigger or a named need such as a hard
  build, a security review or a stuck diagnosis. The highest-cost reasoning
  model is kept for review, analysis and direction, and no execution route
  binds it (ADR-0095).
