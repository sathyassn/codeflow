### Fixed

<!-- codeflow:release-impact patch -->
- **One acceptance sequence for blocked, reopened and closed work.** A task
  that was blocked after its review, then completed, now passes its pull
  request's `codeflow ci`: the `## Blocker` section `task status` writes and
  removes is not reviewed scope, while any other text in that section, the
  criteria and the rest of the record stay reviewed (issue #198). A task
  reopened more than once keeps one chronological archive of superseded
  acceptance blocks, which `task status` and `codeflow ci` accept against
  the line and against `main` alike; a reordered, dropped or rewritten
  archived block is refused (issue #217). A pull request that reopens its
  task may merge its target cleanly after the review, as any task pull
  request may, so a later planning change on the line no longer forces a
  new review. A waiver naming a records-only commit in the task's own pull
  request is accepted after a reopen of a completion that never landed and
  on a branch whose name carries no task id; after a reopen of a landed
  completion the refusal says the waiver must name a planning commit on
  the target that amends the criterion (issue #278).
