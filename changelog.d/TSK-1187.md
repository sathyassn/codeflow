### Fixed

<!-- codeflow:release-impact patch -->
- **A refused copy names the operand it rests on.** When git-guard refuses
  a `cp`, `mv`, `install`, `rsync`, `ln` or `tee` because a destination is
  filled in at run time on a line that names an integrity path, the
  message now names that destination and says whether the integrity path
  is the source. A literal integrity destination is named as the
  destination. The verdicts are unchanged. Migration: none.
