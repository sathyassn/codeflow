### Changed

<!-- codeflow:release-impact minor -->
- **Any other model family counts as a cross-family review.** Codex is the
  preferred cross-family seat, not a required one: a review or plan
  challenge by any model family other than the author's now counts as
  cross-family in the managed `AGENTS.md`, the workflow rules,
  `cf-model-orchestrator`, `cf-plan`, `cf-develop`, `cf-editorial-review`,
  `cf-method` and the `cf-security-reviewer` agent. When Codex is
  unavailable, another family's seat, such as Grok, takes the review or
  challenge as a recorded reassignment. Reduced assurance now means that no
  other family was available, so only the same-family fallback ran. The
  shipped routing policy's `default_review` is `cross-family`, and two
  extra-family review triggers are renamed to match.
