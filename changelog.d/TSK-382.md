### Fixed

<!-- codeflow:release-impact patch -->
- **Amended criteria keep their tags.** An indented `- Amended ...` or
  `- Moved ...` note under a criterion no longer hides a `(journey)`,
  `(serves EPC-NNN AC-n)` or `(after release)` tag that closes the
  criterion's head line, so `codeflow ci` accepts a task with an amended
  journey criterion, or one serving an amended epic journey criterion.
  When another sub-bullet still hides the tag, the journey refusal names
  that note and says to move the tag to the start (issue #199).
