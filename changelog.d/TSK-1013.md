### Added

<!-- codeflow:release-impact minor -->
- **An optional time-box profile gives each planned task a band.**
  `cf-estimate` describes the profile beside the scenario forecast: one
  clock from the claim to the bound approval or the end of verification,
  bands by rubric grade (Easy 1 to 2 hours, Medium 2 to 4, Hard 4 to 6, Very
  Hard 6 to 8 and split before claim), and a stop rule at the band's top
  that is never a gate. The task, epic and spec templates carry optional,
  commented Estimate and Delivery size blocks; a project that leaves them
  out validates as before. `cf-plan` offers the profile once at the first
  breakdown and records a decline, and `cf-ship` writes the epic's outcome
  record before the line is deleted.

<!-- codeflow:release-impact minor -->
- **Planning sizes tasks by one outcome with its own proof.** `cf-plan`,
  `cf-method` and `cf-estimate` state the sizing test as guidance: siblings
  that share a cause, a code path or a proof merge, and no number caps a
  task's size or a plan's task count.

### Changed

<!-- codeflow:release-impact minor -->
- **The per-hour pace rule is replaced by the task's band.** The build row
  and "Planned work keeps to its band" in the `cf-method` lifecycle
  reference hold each task to its frozen band, and a claim means work
  begins now.

<!-- codeflow:release-impact patch -->
- **The estimation rubric is version `codeflow-agentic-2`.** It gains
  boundary clarifications and a never-counts list; points and cuts are
  unchanged, so grades recorded under `codeflow-agentic-1` stand.
