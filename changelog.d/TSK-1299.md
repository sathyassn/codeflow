### Added

<!-- codeflow:release-impact minor -->
- **Every CodeFlow limit is on one generated page.** The new
  [limits page](docs/limits.md) lists each timeout, retry, count, size, wait,
  parallel number and word limit with its key, default, bounds and class. All
  of them are declared once, in the limits registry, which also generates
  their policy schema entries. A check fails on a literal limit anywhere else
  in product code.
<!-- codeflow:release-impact minor -->
- **Projects set limits through the policy file.** Operational limits have a
  floor where correctness needs one and no ceiling. Security limits have a
  floor and a ceiling, and a value outside them refuses, naming the key and
  its bounds. A limit that is not settable says why on the page.

### Changed

<!-- codeflow:release-impact minor -->
- **A worker budget a project sets is used as set.** `execution.worker_budget`
  above the memory-derived host budget used to be lowered to it. It now runs at
  the value set, with a warning. A project's own `execution.host_jobs` still
  bounds it.
<!-- codeflow:release-impact minor -->
- **`.codeflow/ci-wait.json` waits have no ceiling.** The poll interval keeps
  its floor.
<!-- codeflow:release-impact minor -->
- **The id sync's registry fetch is bounded.** It stops at
  `git.remote_fetch_timeout_seconds` and reports the remote as offline, in
  place of waiting as long as git does.
