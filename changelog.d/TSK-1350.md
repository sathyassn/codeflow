### Added

<!-- codeflow:release-impact minor -->
- **A project can require the ticket footer on landing merges.** The new
  policy key `git.commit_ticket_merges` (default `off`) judges every merge
  commit for a ticket trailer from `git.commit_ticket_keys`, matching
  `git.commit_ticket_pattern` when one is set, at its own `warn` or `block`
  level. The commit-msg hook judges a real merge whatever its subject, git's
  default `Merge branch ...` included, and `codeflow ci` judges each merge in
  the range, so a merge made by a hosting service's merge button is caught on
  the line push. The refusal names the merge commit, the rule and the keys
  that satisfy it. With the key off, merges stay exempt as before.
