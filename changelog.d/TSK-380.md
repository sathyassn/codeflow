### Fixed

<!-- codeflow:release-impact patch -->
- **Search patterns are not write targets.** exec-guard no longer refuses
  a read-only search whose pattern looks like a path, such as
  `rg --files | rg '/CHANGELOG.md$'`, as a shell startup write. The pattern
  words of `rg`, `ag`, `grep`, `egrep` and `fgrep` (the first operand when
  no `-e`, `--regexp`, `-f` or `--file` supplies the pattern, and every
  `-e` or `--regexp` value) are left out of the judgment, called directly
  or run by `xargs`, `parallel` or `find -exec`. git-guard's `xargs` and
  `find` rules no longer read such a pattern as the line naming an
  enforcement path. A path operand, a `-f` pattern file, `rg --pre`, a
  redirect and any call with an option the guard does not know are judged
  as before, so `rg x > ~/.zshrc` is still refused (issue 242).
