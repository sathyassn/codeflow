### Fixed

<!-- codeflow:release-impact patch -->
- **A task reopened after its completion was edited in place can be completed again.**
  Before, the reopen check and the history check named different blocks as the
  previous completion, so `codeflow task status` and `codeflow ci` refused the
  re-completion whichever block was archived. They now agree: once the archive
  holds the completion as made and then as edited, the re-completion passes.
  The reopen verb archives only the edited block, so add the as-made block
  ahead of it by hand, in its own commit, before completing again. A
  completion never edited in place is judged as before. A redaction combined
  with an in-place edit is not covered, and one commit that both reopens and
  archives the two blocks is still refused.
