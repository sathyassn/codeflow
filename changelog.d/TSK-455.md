### Changed

<!-- codeflow:release-impact minor -->
- **Agents check changeable state live before they state or act on it.**
  Seat availability, usage limits, tools, CI runs, rulesets, PR and line
  state, task status, host locks and other agents' progress are checked at
  the moment of a claim, routing decision or action, or reported as
  unverified with the time of the last check. An earlier result is a lead,
  never a fact, also after a resume or a compaction. A fresh check before a
  later decision is not a retry, and a fact bound to a commit SHA or a
  merged PR's merge commit needs no re-check. The rule has one home
  in `workflow-discipline.md` "Check changeable state live", and the
  evidence always rule carries a short clause. The orchestrator's preflight
  availability now expires: seats are probed again before each routing
  decision, a seat recorded unavailable is probed again before every later
  assignment and review, a recorded route exclusion is probed again at the
  next routing decision, and reduced assurance is recorded only after a
  live probe fails, naming the probe and its time. Status reports name
  live-checked facts. `codeflow update` rewrites the managed block, rules
  and skills.
