### Fixed

<!-- codeflow:release-impact patch -->
- **A sync of `main` passes when `main` reopened and recompleted a task the
  line synced earlier.** An integration line that had merged `main`'s first
  completion of a task, and then merged `main` again after `main` reopened
  the task with another criterion and completed it again, was refused in
  every form of `codeflow ci` ("none of them is newer than the others" and
  "a reopened task keeps its criteria"). The earlier sync no longer counts
  as the line deciding the task's criteria, and a record the sync imports
  unchanged from a verified landing on `main` is no longer compared with
  the line's older copy. A sync that edits the record by even one byte,
  resolves a conflict on it, or cannot prove the import is still judged as
  the line's own change (issues #456 and #464).
