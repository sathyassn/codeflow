### Fixed

<!-- codeflow:release-impact patch -->
- **A helper that keeps git's error output open costs one wait.** When an
  ssh or credential helper kept git's error output open after git had
  exited, the destination lookup waited out its whole timeout and then
  retried, up to three times. The helper now gets a short grace and is then
  ended with git's process group, so the lookup returns in one wait and
  leaves no process behind. A lookup that failed still refuses.
