### Changed

<!-- codeflow:release-impact minor -->
- **A push gets its verdict sooner, and the hook says where the time went.**
  The pre-push hook runs the cheap checks first and stops at the first
  refusal: each `codeflow ci` it runs stops after the commit, added-line,
  conflict-marker, workflow and path checks when one of them blocks, and
  again after the record and branch-name checks, and a refusal skips the
  later branches' checks and the release preflight. The output
  says what did not run. A push that passes still runs every check, and the
  printed rerun always runs every check. `codeflow ci --timings` prints the
  time each check took, and a push over its budget names the slowest step
  and, for a `codeflow ci` step, its three slowest checks. The budget is the
  new policy key `git.push_budget_seconds` (default 60, a whole number of at
  least 1), read from the default branch's committed policy; it only
  reports and never refuses a push. The record checks read each record once
  per commit instead of once per record they judge, so a records-only push
  spends much less time in `codeflow ci`.
