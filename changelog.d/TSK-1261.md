### Added

<!-- codeflow:release-impact minor -->
- **Changed-line coverage with an explicit comparison base.** A new report
  names added executable lines and their coverage, states an
  80% floor and 90% target, and fails below the floor with a comparison base.

  Pull requests and pushes require an explicit resolved base. A standalone
  local run without one reports `unavailable: no comparison base` and
  enforces nothing. The aggregate 90% gate is unchanged.

  Shard inputs require matching revision, run, attempt, target roster and
  report digest. LCOV, Cobertura and llvm-cov per-line inputs are supported.

  Changed-line exemptions live in one validated list, with an owner, reason
  and removal condition for every entry.

  Migration: the hosted wiring requires the reviewed TSK-1261 operator
  patch. The report runs where coverage runs; integration pull requests
  receive no report after the planned coverage-placement sync.
