### Fixed

<!-- codeflow:release-impact patch -->
- **Default-branch lookup failures name the remote cause.** `codeflow ci`
  and pre-push report one `ci.destination_unavailable` refusal for a timeout,
  authentication failure, malformed advertisement or unusable default branch,
  with network and remote repair advice. Timeouts get up to two retries with
  a short backoff. Pre-push shares the final lookup across pushed refs and
  child checks. Unanswered destinations still refuse; local tracking refs
  never supply a replacement answer (TSK-305, issue 161).
