### Added

<!-- codeflow:release-impact minor -->
- **Merge reviewed heads without rebasing.** `codeflow integrate <head>
  --into <line> --merge` gates a no-fast-forward merge commit, then advances
  the local line while keeping the reviewed head and its acceptance binding
  unchanged. Merge mode runs from a linked worktree and names both branches
  in the merge subject. Rebase and fast-forward remain the default. A merge
  conflict or failed gate leaves the line unchanged. Neither mode pushes
  (TSK-293, issue 150).
