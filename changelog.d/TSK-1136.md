### Added

<!-- codeflow:release-impact minor -->
- **New projects check capability declarations at the standard and full
  tiers.** `codeflow init` and `codeflow update` write
  `git.pr_capability_impact` at `warn` for standard and full projects and at
  `off` for minimal ones, and keep any level a project sets. The key is
  written only once the project's pinned codeflow reads it, so an update that
  raises the pin from 3.x adds it on the next run and says so. The pull
  request template gains a `## Capability impact` section, and the new guide
  `.codeflow/rules/capabilities.md`, installed at every tier, explains what to
  declare and what the check passes and refuses.
