### Fixed

<!-- codeflow:release-impact minor -->
- **A slow destination no longer refuses an ordinary push.** The pre-push
  hook, `codeflow ci`, `task status` and the id sync asked the destination
  for its default branch with a fixed 10 s per attempt, and GitHub answered
  in 11.3 s and 11.6 s, so pushes refused with `ci.destination_unavailable`.
  Three new policy keys set the waits: `git.remote_query_timeout_seconds`
  (default 30), `git.remote_query_attempts` (default 3) and
  `git.remote_fetch_timeout_seconds` (default 120, the earlier fixed fetch
  wait). Each takes a whole number of at least 1 with no other ceiling; any
  other value refuses at load, naming the key. They are read from the
  default branch's committed policy, never from a pushed head. A destination
  that does not answer in time still refuses, and the message names the
  wait, the attempts, the time measured and the keys. The session guards
  keep their own 10 s network budget inside their 20 s deadline: when the
  remote's tracking refs are missing and the remote does not answer in that
  time, the guard refuses and names `git fetch <remote>`, after which it
  reads local refs and needs no network.
