### Added

<!-- codeflow:release-impact patch -->
- **A full gate run records its coverage totals and floor.** A target's
  `coverage` block accepts the `llvm-cov-json` format, the export
  `cargo llvm-cov --json` writes, with or without `--summary-only`. Each
  coverage entry in a full run's `run.json` now carries `totals` (lines,
  and functions and regions where the format measures them) and `floor`,
  the highest minimum of the target's `global` rules. An llvm-cov export whose totals
  differ from the sum of its files is refused. The gate removes a target's
  coverage artifact before the target runs, so a figure left by an earlier
  run is never recorded for this one: a target that writes none reports
  its coverage as missing, which fails the gate when its rules enforce a
  threshold. `codeflow test` prints the totals and floor under each
  coverage line.
