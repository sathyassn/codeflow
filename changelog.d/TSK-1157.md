### Changed

<!-- codeflow:release-impact patch -->
- **A free-disk check for the coverage shards in CodeFlow's own CI.** The
  new `scripts/disk-margin.py` watches a shard's free disk and keeps its
  lowest sample. At the end of the job it fails when the free disk then, or
  the lowest sample, is under 10 GiB, or when the watch left no record or
  its latest sample is stale. `scripts/gate-parity.py` pins both steps on
  the coverage shard job (`COVERAGE_JOB`): the watch right after checkout,
  and the check as the last step. The hosted workflow gains the two steps
  when the operator applies this task's workflow patch; until then the
  hosted shards do not run them. The local gate-parity command in
  `.codeflow/test-config.json` also runs the new tests, and the task record
  holds the findings. The `codeflow` binary and the shipped scaffold are
  unchanged.
