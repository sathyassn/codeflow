### Changed

<!-- codeflow:release-impact minor -->
- **Each completed fact has one record.** `codeflow ci` derives a completed
  task's Reviews row from its acceptance block and prints it, so a PR body
  may leave the row out or point at the record; a row naming another commit
  or verdict now warns instead of blocking. A task PR may amend its own
  task's criteria and follow-ups. New always-blocking refusals: a criterion
  it removes without a line in the PR body giving the reason
  (`work.criteria_removal`), and any change to another task's or an epic's
  record (`work.criteria_frozen`); give the reason, or move that change to a
  planning amendment. The docs
  validation runs once per `codeflow ci` run, as `work.valid_graph`. Once a
  code PR is ready, its Testing section cites `codeflow test --mode quick`
  for the head tree where `codeflow test` is configured, reported as a
  warning; a draft owes none. The shipped PR and task templates say so.
