### Added

- **Start a first capability catalog with `codeflow capability bootstrap`.**
  The command creates one epic and four tasks for inventory, classification,
  evidence and review, and a rerun continues from the first open stage. A
  project without work tracking at the minimal tier gets a four-stage
  checklist instead. Init, update and doctor offer the command while the
  inventory is incomplete and create nothing themselves.
