  unchanged completion bound to its verified home landing when a line or
  task branch merges it. Later receiving work and target policy changes
  no longer force unrelated source tasks to reopen. Imported record and
  criteria changes stay attributed to their source; altered records,
  unpublished home history, unreviewed source resolutions and unprovable
  history still refuse. A receiving-line merge's product changes outside
  verified source history require a reviewed task head, including ranges
  with no completion.
