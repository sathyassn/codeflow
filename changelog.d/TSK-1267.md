### Fixed

<!-- codeflow:release-impact patch -->
- **`codeflow ci` says when its destination lookup needs a credential.**
  Deciding whether a range is judged under the release rules asks the
  destination for its default branch and that branch's policy. On a
  private repository whose checkout kept no credential that lookup fails,
  and the error now says so and names the fix: give the job read access to
  the destination. `codeflow ci --help` documents the need. Judging a
  private repository without any credential is not yet supported (issue
  #527).
