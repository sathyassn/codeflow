- **Local history checks refuse rewritten parents.** Acceptance binding,
  journey scope, reopen detection, landing checks, reviewed stacks and
  range selection share a guarded history reader. A graft file or replace
  ref produces `cannot verify`; a walk that reaches a shallow boundary
  also refuses. Pre-push, `work start` and local `codeflow ci`, including a
  task-less branch without a PR body, no longer accept parent links supplied
  by those overlays. Each read-only judgment checks its history metadata
  once, keeping long parent walks from repeatedly scanning every ref.
  Remove the overlay or use a clean clone; fetch the full history when a
  shallow cut prevents the check. No policy or record migration is needed (TSK-287, issue 122).
