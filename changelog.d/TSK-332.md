### Fixed

<!-- codeflow:release-impact patch -->
- **A killed docs portal run no longer blocks the next one.** The workflow
  lease for `check`, `build`, `dev` and `preview` now records its owner, the
  coordinator process, each writer it starts and, on POSIX, the process group
  each writer leads. When the coordinator and every writer group on the same
  host have exited, for example after `kill -9` or a timed-out gate target,
  the next run reclaims the lease at once and prints one line naming the dead
  owner. A live coordinator or writer always blocks, whatever the lease age,
  and its process ids are named. Anything the lease cannot prove safe keeps it
  until the four-hour age rule: a coordinator killed while starting a writer,
  another host, an older lease without an owner, a failed liveness check, and
  on Windows any lease that names a writer, which the finished workflow
  therefore leaves in place. An owner record that keeps changing refuses
  until a later attempt sees a stable record. A run that cannot stop a
  writer, or cannot prove its group gone, fails and keeps the lease.
