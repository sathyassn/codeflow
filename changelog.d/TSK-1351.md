### Added

<!-- codeflow:release-impact minor -->
- **A landing candidate branch can be pushed and is judged against its
  line.** A branch named with `git.candidate_prefix` (default `candidate/`)
  is now an allowed branch name. Its push is judged by the pre-push hook and
  by `codeflow ci` as the line's next tip, with the line's fetched tip as the
  base and the line as the target, when its first-parent chain reaches the
  fetched tip of exactly one epic line within `git.candidate_search_depth`
  commits, the remote advertises that tip, the records at the default
  branch's tip and at the line tip bind the line, and every commit after the
  tip is a clean two-parent merge. `codeflow ci` binds the candidate again
  from its own reads. A push of the line to a candidate's tip must bind that
  candidate and be a fast-forward, so a batch lands on its line in one push.
  Any other case, a direct commit or an evil merge on the candidate
  included, is refused as `work.landing_candidate` with its cause; a branch
  without the prefix, and every other line push, is judged as before.
