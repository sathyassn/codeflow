### Added

<!-- codeflow:release-impact minor -->
- **A landing candidate branch can be pushed and is judged against its
  line.** A branch named with `git.candidate_prefix` (default `candidate/`)
  is now an allowed branch name. Its push is judged by the pre-push hook and
  by `codeflow ci` as the line's next tip, with the line's fetched tip as the
  base and the line as the target, when its first-parent chain reaches the
  fetched tip of exactly one epic line within `git.candidate_search_depth`
  commits, the remote advertises that tip, the records at the default
  branch's tip and at the line tip bind the line, every commit after the
  tip is a clean two-parent merge, and each member head is the current head
  of an open pull request into the line with the default branch's trusted
  admission checks green at that exact commit, each written by the policy
  workflow's own `pull_request_target` run for that pull request, as the
  forge records it (a same-named check from another workflow or event does
  not count; this proves admission at the head, not a person's review; an
  unreadable forge or a project with no admission check refuses).
  `codeflow ci` binds the candidate again from its own reads, its own
  read of the destination included, whose default branch's policy decides
  whether the pushed ref is a candidate: the destination must be `origin`'s
  push URL, and a hook advertisement that differs from that read refuses
  (fetch and push again), so a local `codeflow ci` of a candidate needs
  the destination to be readable. A push of the line to a candidate's tip must bind that
  candidate and be a fast-forward, so a batch lands on its line in one push.
  Any other case, a direct commit or an evil merge on the candidate
  included, is refused as `work.landing_candidate` with its cause; a branch
  without the prefix, and every other line push, is judged as before.
