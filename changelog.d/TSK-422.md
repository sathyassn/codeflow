### Added

<!-- codeflow:release-impact minor -->
- **Coordination ledger and follow-ups.** `codeflow follow` keeps an
  orchestrating session's open commitments as follow-ups (`FU-NNN`) in the
  clone's common git directory (`codeflow/coord/`), shared by every worktree
  and never committed. Each follow-up is a full record with typed links, a
  stream, a holder and a next check. It closes on its own only when a
  `fulfils` link is done on its target branch, never on a feature branch.
  One append-only log takes one sequence under a lock, syncs before it
  reports success, repairs a torn tail, and turns read-only with a
  diagnostic on damage. `follow list` reads only. Free text is size-bounded
  and scanned for secrets. The schema is in `docs/coordination-ledger.md`.
<!-- codeflow:release-impact minor -->
- **Recovery inventory.** `codeflow work status` lists, per task, each
  worktree with uncommitted changes, unpushed commits or commits on no
  branch, and names lost work as not recoverable.
<!-- codeflow:release-impact minor -->
- **Ledger ids stay out of records.** `codeflow validate --docs` flags a
  task, epic or ADR record that cites an `FU`, `LN` or `WS` id, and
  `codeflow ci` blocks a pull request body that does. The guards refuse an
  agent's direct edit under the ledger directory.
