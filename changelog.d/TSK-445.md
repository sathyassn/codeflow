### Changed

<!-- codeflow:release-impact minor -->
- **The `cf-present` skill teaches one method for anchored, figure-led
  documents.** A document opens with an anchor and a picture of the whole,
  then follows the order understanding grows (the familiar problem, the new
  model, how it behaves, the decision). Each part opens with a `## ` section
  titled by its topic in plain words, never a question, and a one or two
  line lead. Every concept is drawn as a figure in its grammar family, with
  its governing path in the accent-bearing marks; tables sit in a disclosure
  as reference. The figure grammar adds "Composing a figure" (draw the
  mechanism, draw the difference, verbs on arrows, one claim per figure,
  real names and numbers), and the example document and specimens are
  rebuilt to it.
<!-- codeflow:release-impact minor -->
- **`codeflow present check` warns on question titles and mannered prose.**
  A document, section or block title that ends in a question mark, and
  prose with a known mannered pattern (ceremonial framing, self-narration, a
  colon reveal, a "not just X, but Y" turn, stock puffery, or an em or en
  dash outside a numeric range), each print a warning. A `decision`, `form`
  or `feedback_prompt` asks the reader and is exempt. Warnings never refuse
  a document.
<!-- codeflow:release-impact minor -->
- **Present feedback is followed as one stream.** The skill's feedback loop
  now runs `codeflow present feedback <session> --follow --format v2` under
  the host's monitor, re-armed only when the monitor expires, so a review or
  answer sent after the first one is no longer left unread. The one-shot
  `--wait` loop stays for a harness with no monitor.
