### Fixed

<!-- codeflow:release-impact minor -->
- **Pre-push checks every pushed branch.** With `git.test_gate_on_push` at
  `block`, an unchecked new commit makes the pre-push hook refuse the push.
  Tree checks use the strictest gate from the working copy, HEAD and the
  destination authority, so an uncommitted policy edit cannot lower it.
  Dirty tracked files, hidden index flags, sparse checkouts and missing or
  mismatched submodules require a clean, complete checkout at the pushed
  commit. Initialized submodules now work with quick-target snapshots.
  Commits already on the destination need no tree checks, preserving
  `codeflow epic new --integration` from a planning branch. The hook reuses
  its cached destination advertisement. Lower levels name unchecked trees
  and the checks to run; `off` and `allow` name the inactive gate.
  Untracked files can still influence quick targets; the pass output
  states this limit. Tag and other non-branch pushes are unchanged.
