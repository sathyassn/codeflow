### Fixed

<!-- codeflow:release-impact minor -->
- **Pre-push checks every pushed tree.** With `git.test_gate_on_push` at
  `block`, a dirty tracked checkout, another pushed commit, a sparse checkout
  or a missing or mismatched submodule now refuses the push when its tree
  checks cannot run. Preserve tracked edits, use a clean, complete checkout
  of the pushed commit and push different commits separately. `warn` and
  `off` name unchecked trees and the checks to run. Untracked files can still
  influence quick targets; the pass output states this limit. The push set
  keeps its existing checks and one cached destination advertisement.
