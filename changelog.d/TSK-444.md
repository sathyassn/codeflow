### Changed

<!-- codeflow:release-impact minor -->
- **Present documents and portal pages open with their title and summary,
  and headings keep one size order.** A present document now shows its
  title, its `summary` and its provenance (task, spec, ADR) at the top of
  the document column, and the paragraph after a `## ` heading reads as
  that section's lead; portal pages show the page description under the
  title the same way. Both surfaces take one type scale from the design
  tokens (title 34, summary 19, section 24, subsection 18, block title 15,
  body 15, caption 13 px at the default size), so a comparison column,
  decision, form, callout or figure title never outranks the section that
  holds it, at the compact, default and large sizes.
- **Figures can tell named peers apart by colour.** The design tokens add
  two companion colours with tints (`--cf-fig-cat1`, `--cf-fig-cat2` and
  their `-soft` tints), cobalt and plum, the same in every skin of a mode
  and at 4.5:1 or better on every ground. A figure declaration may put a
  `tone` (`cat1`, `cat2`, or `accent` for the governing region) on a
  labelled layer, region, state, node, optional or denied mark; the tone
  changes hue only, so every rule of the figure grammar holds. Figures
  without tones draw exactly as before.
