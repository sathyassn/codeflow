# Design sourcing and revision

Read this reference only when external inspiration, a sourced or generated
asset, a material transformation, or a design revision affects the outcome.
Keep the evidence in the project's existing plan, task, research, or asset
record; do not create a parallel catalog or database.

## Distinguish evidence

- Inspiration can reduce visual or interaction uncertainty. It is not evidence
  of user need, preference, demand, accessibility, or product fact.
- For each material reference, retain its source and the specific quality being
  learned from. Public availability is not permission to copy protected
  expression.
- Prefer authoritative project evidence for audience and product claims. Label
  reversible inferences and surface unknown rights, consent, or provenance.

## Qualify assets proportionately

For a material sourced, commissioned, generated, or transformed asset, retain
the evidence needed for its risk and intended use:

- origin or provider and a stable source reference;
- rights, licence, or subject-consent basis;
- generation and material transformations;
- restrictions, attribution, expiry, or territorial limits where applicable;
- the product surface, state, mode, viewport, or platform in which it was
  actually checked.

Collapse this record for low-risk project-owned material whose authority is
already established. Unknown rights or consent blocks use; it is not filled in
by assumption. Provider names and current model releases belong in
project-owned qualified configuration, not CodeFlow doctrine.

Never upload private material, personal data, secrets, confidential product
content, or sensitive subjects to an external reference, asset, or generation
service without explicit authority for that service, data, and intended use.
When authority is absent, use an approved local path, sanitized substitute, or
ask the operator for the decision; do not weaken the boundary to preserve a
design option.

An asset is not accepted because a provider returned it. Inspect it in the
actual product context and applicable modes, states, sizes, languages, and
accessibility conditions. Check factual and brand fit, artefacts, legibility,
cropping, contrast, motion, rights obligations, and failure behavior only where
applicable, then retain claim-matched evidence.

## Refine and revise

After direction selection, name the unresolved choice before producing more
options. Offer two or three meaningfully different in-direction variants and a
recommendation only when that choice can materially alter the accepted result.
Stop when it is settled; do not produce a generic control panel or an endless
series of themes, fonts, images, colours, and motions.

For material feedback, record:

- the exact reviewed version or content digest;
- who supplied the feedback and what authority it carries;
- what was accepted or rejected and why; and
- the resulting approved Plan vN+1 and its required evidence.

Preserve the reviewed version and rationale through normal versioned project
records. A local correction that does not change accepted direction collapses
to the existing task and fidelity evidence.

A same-family design approval and a cross-family review are separate duties;
when both are configured, both are required. A project's committed design
co-owner gives the approval (`codeflow models resolve --duty
design-approval`); record it apart from the reviews, and never count it as
the independent review.
